# Phase 4 heuristic review 1

**As of:** 2026-10-28
**Reviewer:** subagent acting as a demanding UX director (Phase 4 gate, PROMPT.md "Gate")
**Scope:** the 26 clickable wireframes in `docs/design/wireframes/`, their 153 screenshots, and the specs `principles.md`, `board.md`, `flows.md`, `visual-system.md`, `learning-ui.md`, `accessibility.md`, `card-face-study.md` and `explanations.md`.

**Verdict.** The specs are strong and the wireframes are unusually complete for a Phase 4 pass. The battle arithmetic, the `whyNot` copy, the certainty labels and the honest phone screen are better than the competitors we studied. **Phase 4 is not gated:** 11 findings are severity 3. Most of them come from one cause. The 1280×720 rail was sized for the idle Main phase, and the three hardest moments (defending, lethal teaching, review) do not fit in it. Fix the space model once (C16, C21, UX-R6) and about half of the severity 3 findings close together.

## 1. Method

**Read in full:** PROMPT.md (Mission, Phase 4, Learning layer, Quality bar: Accessibility, Legal posture); the eight design documents listed above; `wireframes/NOTES.md` (22 conflicts); and the wireframe source (`wf.js`, `wf.css`, the per-screen HTML) where a screenshot needed explaining.

**Screenshots:**
- I viewed every 1280×720 shot at full size: 76 states, plus the phone and tablet shots. I went back to the board shots for a second look (`03-main`, `04-attack`, `05-defend`, `06-trigger`, `07-effect-choice`, `08-ai-turn`, `09-coach`).
- I viewed every 1920×1080 shot on 2×2 contact sheets at half scale, built with Playwright in the scratchpad. I viewed `03-main--idle-1920` at full size.
- Small-text judgements at 1920 come from the measurements below, not from the half-scale sheets.

**Measured with Playwright** (the repo's install, against the wireframe HTML):
- Tab order on `03-main#idle` at both sizes, recorded for the first 40 presses.
- Decision-panel overflow (`scrollHeight` against `clientHeight`) in every `05-defend` state, and coach-body overflow in `09-coach#count` and `#tier3`.
- Rendered text size, including CSS transform scale, on the board, the defence panel, the coach and the review's restored board.
- Interactive targets under 24 px.
- The accessible name and size of the board's disclaimer.

**Heuristics:** Nielsen's ten (H1 visibility of status, H2 match with the real world, H3 user control, H4 consistency, H5 error prevention, H6 recognition over recall, H7 flexibility and efficiency, H8 minimalist design, H9 error recovery, H10 help). Also principles P1–P10 from `principles.md`. Severity uses Nielsen's 0–4 scale. As `principles.md` requires, a failure of P1, P2, P3 or P7 on the game board is rated at least 3.

**Limits:**
- The wireframes implement no keyboard commands. `wf.js` binds only the backtick state switcher. So I judged keyboard behaviour from the tab order and the spec, not by playing.
- Motion and pacing are static, so I judged the timing tables on paper.
- The art is placeholder, so crop quality under the art-window treatment cannot be judged yet.
- No screen reader was run.
