# L screen-reader pass, first run: NVDA + Chrome

**Kind of pass:** scripted (Guidepup), not a human listening pass (accessibility.md §11.2, risk R9). First run of the §11.4 script (`app/a11y/src/l.ts`). The Firefox record is [l-nvda-firefox.md](l-nvda-firefox.md); finding numbers (L-SR) are shared by both.
**Result:** **PASS by the rule, with a large coverage gap.** No open severity 3 or 4 finding. But the script did not reach the guided count (4b, 4c), the detector turned on (5), the AI's defence (2, 2b) or the F15 re-check, because step 2's Enter never committed the attack (see L-SR1 and the harness notes). What was reached found five severity 2 problems (L-SR1 to L-SR5). Do not read this PASS as "the coach, the guided count and the detector were heard"; they were not. A second run needs the harness fix below.
**Date:** 2026-11-09.

## How it ran

- Steps F3 to 5 ran on `a11y-l` (Rookie as the AI); steps 6 to 9 reloaded on `lethalDemo`, conceded it, and opened the review (`l.ts`, README "The L script").
- **Harness assists:** step 3 pressed `L` again from the board three times (the harness's check looks for the coach's tier markup, and the coach was a strip; the keys themselves were spoken, so this is a harness false alarm). Step 6: the harness focused the Game menu button and the timeline. Step 7: the harness moved focus to the restored board. Steps 4b, 4c and 5: errors, "Tab did not reach" (see below).
- **Harness did not reach the intended state, from step 2 on.** After `M` focus is on the battle panel's heading, and the script pressed Enter there. NVDA's Enter on a heading clicks it, so the app's Enter handler never ran and the attack was never confirmed (L-SR1). Every later step ran with the attack still on the confirm panel: no Use prompt, no pick, no AI defence (steps 2, F15, 2b); the decision panel was tall, so the coach was only a strip (steps 3, 4), and 4b, 4c and 5 timed out looking for coach controls that were not there. I judge those steps by what was heard, and mark what was not reached as a harness note, not an app failure.
- Judged against §11.4 as amended by DECISIONS "2026-11-09 — L screen-reader script built; F3 and F15 re-checked, not changed" and STATUS "Not done" (what was not built is a Note, not a Fail).

## Harness notes (not app findings)

1. **Step 2 pressed Enter on the panel heading.** NVDA turns that into a click. The script should Tab to "Confirm attack" (it is two Tabs from the heading) or return to the target card and press Enter there, as step 9 does (that works: "You: Kaido attacks their Leader …").
2. Steps 3 to 5 then ran with the coach as a strip; the "L did not reach tier N" notes and the three "L was pressed again from the board" assists are false alarms (the keys were heard).
3. 4b, 4c and 5 depend on the coach being open and on the attack having been confirmed. They have never been heard; the guided count and the detector's "on" half are still unproven in a screen reader.
4. Step 6's `N` and arrows were judged with one flag and one turn, so "N to the flag" cannot be told apart from "N did nothing".

**What worked well:** the battle panel's facts (power, gap, counters needed) all spoken; `L`'s three sentences; the Concede dialog and the end-of-game announcements; the flag's text and certainty label in the timeline; Study's rail landing on a named button; the `+` DON!! dialog's live "your Leader 9000"; the puzzle's result announced politely.
