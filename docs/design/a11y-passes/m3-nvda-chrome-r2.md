# M3 screen-reader pass, re-run: NVDA + Chrome

**Kind of pass:** scripted (Guidepup), not a human listening pass (accessibility.md §11.2; risk R9). A re-run of [m3-nvda-chrome.md](m3-nvda-chrome.md) after the "M3 NVDA pass fixes" (DECISIONS 2026-11-05).
**Result:** **PASS.** No open severity 3 or 4 finding. F1 (the severity 3) is fixed in the speech. Open at severity 2: F2 (narrowed), F3, F7, and new F15. Caveat: the rewind list's commit (step 15) was not exercised, because the script's key path wandered out of the dialog (harness defect, see "How it ran").

## How it ran

- **Script, scenario, seed:** as in the first run (`app/a11y/src/m3.ts`, `a11y-m3`, turn 3, seed 11, AI pace Step). Keys go through NVDA; speech is NVDA's own, from the relay tap. The log's "Heard" column is the evidence for every verdict below; the "Live regions" column is what the page wrote.
- **Step order:** as before. Rookie attacked with its Leader first (step 11's [Trigger] hit), then with King at 7000 (step 10), so step 11 ran before 10; step 14's `order` decision came up inside step 12's attack.
- **Different position from run 1:** the Leader attack in step 12 now fails (5000 against 7000 after a Counter), because Kaido's -2000 and an AI choice differ. The steps still reach every decision type.
- **Harness assists:** none in steps 1 to 17. In step 18 the harness put focus on an attacker three times (as before, five).
- **Script moves that are not assists, but a user would not make:** step 2 starts from the skip link by pressing Shift+Tab 8 times from the first Tab stop (needed because of F3). Step 15 chose a move with a Shift+Tab loop written for the old list (see below).
- **Harness defect in step 15.** The script pressed ArrowDown twice (the radio group's selection moved to the third move), Tab (to "Cancel"), then Shift+Tab until focus matched the first radio. Arrow keys had changed which radio is checked, so the match never came. Focus walked back through "Close (Escape)" and out of the page into Chrome's toolbar (rows 11 to 14, "body (no accessible node)"), came back to "Rewind to before it", and the script then pressed Enter on "Cancel". So no move was chosen and committed. The speech for the list itself is real; the commit is untested. The script should Tab to "Rewind to before it" after the arrows.
- **Spec text in the log:** the log's "Expect" lines are the script's. Steps 8, 10, 13 and 14 still carry the old §11.3 wording ("E, then confirm", "change one counter's target", "1 selected of up to 2", "grab, move and drop"). I judged against the updated §11.3 in `docs/design/accessibility.md`.
