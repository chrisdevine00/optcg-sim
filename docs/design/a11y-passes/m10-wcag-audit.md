# M10 WCAG 2.2 A and AA audit

Written 2026-11-27 (M10 WP5). One row for each WCAG 2.2 success criterion at level A or AA, 1.1.1 to 4.1.3: how the product meets it, how it was checked and by whom, the evidence, and the result. A test (`app/test/a11y-sheet.test.ts`) fails if a criterion is missing from the table. The last section is the claim the project may make and its conditions.

## How to read the result column

There are four layers of checking. A row names the ones that judged it in the "Checked by" column.

- **automated**: axe (`wcag2a` to `wcag22aa`, zero violations) and the audit spec, run on 39 states at 1280×720 and 1920×1080 (78 runs): `docs/design/a11y-passes/m10-automated.md`, generated by `scripts/a11y-audit-report.ts` from `app/e2e/m10-audit.spec.ts`. Zero violations, zero fully obscured focus stops, every under-24 px target spaced.
- **manual (WP5)**: checks the WP5 agent made on 2026-11-27 against this branch's production build at 1280×720 (Chromium through Playwright 1.64.1), described under "Manual checks" and named P1 to P10 so a row can cite one. The probes were scratch scripts and are not committed; each is described so it can be re-run. A manual check is one agent's reading, not a second pair of eyes.
- **NVDA**: the scripted Guidepup pass (`app/a11y/src`, six scripts, Chrome and Firefox) on a Windows runner. Dispatched by the lead; **not yet run for the M10 script at this commit**, and the earlier scripts' records are in `docs/design/a11y-passes/README.md`.
- **VoiceOver**: the owner's pass on their Mac from `docs/design/a11y-passes/M10-listening-pass.md`. **Owed.**

"Pass" in the result column means every layer that has run found nothing. Where a row also names NVDA or VoiceOver, that layer is **owed** and nothing in the claim rests on it. "Fail" means a layer found a defect: it has a finding number (below) and is not hidden by the word "Pass" anywhere. Not applicable rows say why.

## Manual checks (P1 to P10)

All on this branch's production build, served by `vite preview`, in Chromium through Playwright 1.64.1, 1280×720, service workers blocked, art hosts refused so cards are text faces, on 2026-11-27. Scratch scripts, deleted before the commit.

- **P1 page facts.** 17 routes (`/`, `/play`, `/learn`, `/learn/L01`, `/meta`, `/meta/matchups`, `/drills`, `/rules`, `/progress`, `/decks`, `/cards`, `/reviews`, `/settings`, `/about`, an unknown path, `/tutorial`, a board). For each: `document.title`, `html lang`, the `h1`s, `main`, header and footer, the header links, inputs and their `autocomplete`, draggable elements, audio and video elements, the viewport tag. Found: 17 distinct titles; `lang="en"`; one `main` and one `h1` each; the same nine header links in the same order on the 15 page routes (the board and tutorial have no header); no audio, video or draggable element; no field about the user; the viewport tag has no scale limit.
- **P2 computed contrast.** Every element with its own text on those 17 routes, in the dark theme and the light, text colour against the composited background up its ancestors (opacity included), compared with 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text. 4,357 elements, none below. The lowest ratios: board card faces 6.02:1, text-face lines 8.07:1, plates 20:1. 512 elements have a gradient or background image somewhere above them and were measured against the solid colour beneath it.
- **P3 hover dock.** On the board, the pointer on a card: the dock opens (yes); Escape closes it (yes); pointer off and back opens it again (yes); the pointer moved in 25 steps from the card to the dock: closed before it arrived (no); the pointer resting on the card for 3 s: stays (yes).
- **P4 shortcut scope.** On the board with focus on a control outside the board and the decision panel, `q`, `e`, `a`, `?` and `u`: no status text, no dialog. With nothing focused, `q` spoke the status summary.
- **P5 label in name.** Every button, link, input, select and role-named control with an `aria-label` on 14 routes and the open shortcut sheet: the visible text (letters and digits) is inside the name. 0 mismatches.
- **P6 focus indicator.** On 14 pages, Tab through up to 45 stops: 316 stops, each with a computed outline of 2 px or more or a box shadow (0 without), and none changed the address.
- **P7 colour-vision screenshots.** The 24 under `.cache/m10/` (generated by `app/e2e/m10-audit.spec.ts`, not committed), four simulations, the board in Target and Defend and the review with a flag, at both sizes. Opened and read against [§6.1](../accessibility.md): achromatopsia on Target and Defend (the selected state is a tick and the word "Selected"; the target is a reticle and an arrow; the battle result is "Hit lands" with an ✕ or "Wins the game" with a ✓; whose turn is words and a chip; "Rested · attacking" is words; the Block step is marked by position and a word); protanopia on the review (flags are shapes with labels and a legend; the two Life lines are solid and dashed); tritanopia on Defend. The other 19 were not opened one by one.
- **P8 source reading.** `app/src` for media elements and APIs (none), `screen.orientation` and orientation queries (none), device motion (none), every `<img>` (four), `role="img"` (two), pointer handlers (click, hover, one hold-to-reveal on mouse down with an up-reversal, one drop zone), `@keyframes` (three: a ring pulse once for 1.1 s, a one-shot fade, a looping bar for the coach's "analysing" state; the pulse and the bar are off under reduced motion), `.animate(` (none).
- **P9 axe incompletes.** Axe run on the board in Explore: `color-contrast` is resolved by P2; `aria-prohibited-attr` is the rail notice (W5-4); `label-content-name-mismatch` is four icon-only buttons (≡, ⤢, ⌂ and the coach chevron) whose visible text is a symbol.
- **P10 scripted dry run of the M10 script.** `app/a11y/m10.dry.spec.ts` at this commit, Playwright keys and no screen reader, against the production build: steps A1, E1, S1, O1 and U1 reached what each needs (the first run, before the W5-3 patch, did not reach E1's last check). What it shows is the page's focus and live regions; what a screen reader says is the NVDA and VoiceOver layers'.

## The claim
