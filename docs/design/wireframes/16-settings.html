About

Unofficial fan tool. OPTCG Sim is a non-commercial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bandai, Shueisha or Toei Animation. One Piece and all card names, text and art belong to their respective owners. Card images load from the sources below at run time and are never stored in this project.

Sources

  • Card data and images: optcgapi.com, which says it is open for anyone to use. Text is normalised (restored minus signs); unverified cards say so in the inspector.
  • Official card list: en.onepiece-cardgame.com, read by a person to verify scripted cards; never fetched in bulk.
  • Rules: the Comprehensive Rules and Q&A, from the official site.
  • Decklists and results: Limitless TCG.
  • Tier: opmetagame.com (October 2026).
  • Strategy: Shonen TCG and TCGplayer articles, cited by tag in each guide.
  • Fonts: Inter (Rasmus Andersson) and Fraunces (Undercase Type), SIL Open Font License 1.1.
