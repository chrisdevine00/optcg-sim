Game

Opponent speed
How fast the AI's moves play. Step waits for you after each move. Attacks on you always keep a readable pause.
Always skip to my decisions
The AI's moves play instantly and the game stops at every Block, Counter or Trigger choice you have. Every move is still in the log.
Empty windows
Skip Block and Counter steps where you have nothing to do.
Warn when I end with attacks left
Adds the count to the End turn button. Never a dialog.
Ask me to order cards I put on the bottom
Off: they go in the suggested order.
Hotseat: full handoff for every defence
Hides the board between players for each Block and Counter choice.

Coach

Verbosity
Changes how much is open, never the words.
Lethal detector
"Ask me" runs nothing until you press L.
Tempo meter
Shows how your DON!! were used this turn, in the coach strip.
Blunder warning
A warning beside the commit button for clear mistakes. Never a dialog, never more than once per decision.
DON!! math on the board
"+4000 to stop" tags on targets while you choose.

Drills

Timed drills
Off by default. A timer never ends an item, and passing is judged untimed.
Time allowed
When timed drills are on. Pause any time with P.

Cards

Card faces on the board
Art window shows the clean top of the art, without the SAMPLE watermark. The full scan is always one hover away.
Art variant
Choose per card in the deck builder. Cosmetic, stored on this device.
Card text on the board
The full text is always in the preview.

About

Unofficial fan tool. OPTCG Sim is a non-commercial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bandai, Shueisha or Toei Animation. One Piece and all card names, text and art belong to their respective owners. Card images load from the sources below at run time and are never stored in this project.

Sources

  • Card data and images: optcgapi.com, which says it is open for anyone to use. Text is normalised (restored minus signs); unverified cards say so in the inspector.
  • Official card list: en.onepiece-cardgame.com, read by a person to verify scripted cards; never fetched in bulk.
  • Rules: the Comprehensive Rules and Q&A, from the official site.
  • Decklists and results: Limitless TCG.
  • Tier: opmetagame.com (October 2026).
  • Strategy: Shonen TCG and TCGplayer articles, cited by tag in each guide.
  • Fonts: Inter (Rasmus Andersson) and Fraunces (Undercase Type), SIL Open Font License 1.1.
