## Meta Explorer and matchup grid (WP1)

`/meta` (tiles), `/meta/:leaderId` (guide), `/meta/matchups` (grid; `M` switches). Data is parsed at build from the guide files (`import.meta.glob(?raw)`, a lazy chunk) and `meta-lists.json`: front matter `asOf`, `confidence`, `sources`; the sections of learning-ui §5.2 in file order; each *(inference)* claim and each `[TAG]` rendered as "our reasoning" or a link resolved through `sources.md`. Header: "English Standard · as of {asOf} · {events} online events, {from} to {to}" with the counting method in a disclosure. Tile: Leader art (runtime, `CardFace`), disambiguated name, colours in words, opmetagame tier with its source, share and share since the ban, "Lists: 8 to 13 Oct" or "Lists may be out of date (N days)" past 60 days (clock injected; test at 59/60/61), "N of 50 cards automated" from the scripts index, and the consequence when not. Actions: Play this deck (`/play?p1=<deckId>`), Play against this deck (`?p2=`), "Try the mulligan trainer" (`/drills/mulligan?archetype=`), each sourced list with its link. Matchup grid: one `<table>` with row and column headers (rows "you play"), 12 × 12 from the guides' Matchups tables (132 cells plus 12 "mirror"); cell marker is the sourced record or a "plan" glyph, never a percentage; the popover carries the plan, tag or inference mark, the record's owner ("Rocks W–L") and **Play this matchup**; where two guides give different records for one pairing both show with "Sources disagree". A parse test fails on a guide with other than 11 opponent rows, an unknown leader id, or a tag missing from `sources.md`.

## Drills (WP2, WP3, WP4)

One shell (flows F19, learning-ui §7): start (filters, count, timer list), item, answer (`Explanation`, Next), summary against the lesson's untimed exit. No answer is pre-focused or pre-styled. Banks are deterministic: generated with fixed seeds and node caps (never milliseconds), committed as card-id JSON in `app/src/drills/banks/`, and re-verified by the solver in CI (a changed engine or script that breaks an item fails the test). No art is stored.

**Hindsight label and the harder hand (carried).** A puzzle cut at a Main of an `any-hand` flag is rebuilt as a scenario (new engine `scenarioFromState`) whose opponent hand is `harderHand`: real cards from `priorFor(leader)` that maximise their defence, face-down Life swapped for inert fillers. The public proof dominates every real hand, so the solver re-check passes (tested). A flag that cannot be expressed as a scenario (a counter step, a hidden-card effect on the stack) or a Hindsight flag keeps the actual hand and shows "Their real hand: one way to see it". **Review flags enter the bank** as `{gameId, flagId}` references (the record stays in the games store, so no 500 KB copies); "From your game, turn 9" items come first under the "from my games" filter; a deleted game drops its items with a one-line count. Practice for DON!! unused, attack ordering (target) and mulligan flags comes with these drills (`don-ledger`, `target-best`, and the mulligan trainer item).

## Rules reference (WP5)

`/rules`, `/rules/cr/7-1-4-1`, `/rules/RL-18`. Sources are the two researched files, parsed at build from `?raw` into a lazy chunk: the digest's 137 numbered items (already paraphrased; every `[engine note]` stripped) with their CR numbers, and the rulings RL-01 to RL-52 with question, answer, confidence and sources (the `Engine test` paragraph dropped). Search is a hand-written token index over title, number and text (no dependency); typing a rule number jumps to it; glossary terms link in. The tutorial card's "Rule: CR 7-1-4-1" becomes a `RuleLink` opening the entry in a new tab so the step is not lost (carried F2 step 1). **Show me**: 24 demos (`app/src/rules/demos/`), one per row of the digest/ruling families the tutorial and meta lists lean on (battle tie, Counter step, Blocker timing RL-18, Trigger, Rush, Double Attack RL-20, Banish RL-21, DON!! turn-only RL-13, first-turn attack ban RL-28, deck-out RL-26, full Character area RL-04, Stage replace RL-07, "cannot be K.O.'d" RL-14 to RL-16, negative power, "up to 0", DON!! −X RL-24, and so on). A demo is a `ScenarioSpec` plus an allowed-action filter (it narrows the engine's actions, never adds one), a done predicate and two sentences, run on the real board at `/rules/:id/show` through the tutorial's `allow` seam. **Validation:** a data-driven test replays each demo's scripted `line` (predicates over `legalActions`) with the real engine and asserts its `expect` (queries on the final state); a second test asserts every demo's cited rule number exists in the digest, and a mutation check changes one expectation to see the test fail.
