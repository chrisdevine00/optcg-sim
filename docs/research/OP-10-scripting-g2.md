# OP-10 scripting, block 3: OP-10 group 2 of 4

Cards OP10-036, 037, 038, 043, 044, 045, 046, 047, 048, 049, 051, 052, 053, 055, 056, 057, 058, 062, 063, 065, 066, 069, 071, 072, 074 (25 scripted, all `verified: true`, none left out). Gaps from the block 3 audit, mapped through `m6b-runtime-audit.md` §2: B3-G7 = G1 (own-Character costs, wave 1: OP10-047, OP10-056), B3-G4 = G11 (`ko` replacement with a cause, wave 2: OP10-074), B3-G25 = G24 (`rested` timing, wave 3: OP10-036). All merged; no wave 5+ gap and no design item D1 to D9 was needed. Readings, one line each, with the card text. Open questions M6b-Q1..Q59 are not settled here.

## Readings

- **OP10-036 Perona**, "[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] If a Character is rested by your effect, set up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active." The "If" is the trigger condition, so it is a `rested` timing (`owner: 'any'`, a Character, `by: 'yourEffect'`) rather than an `if` step. A Character of either player counts when your effect rests it; a rest paid as a cost, by an attack or a block, a Leader, an already rested Character and the opponent's effects do not (M6b-Q30, Q41 open). The Once Per Turn is spent by the first such rest, even when you set no DON!! ("up to 1" asks a count).
- **OP10-037 Lim**, "[Once Per Turn] If this Character would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may rest 1 of your "ODYSSEY" type Characters instead." A `leaveField` replacement (cause `opponentEffect`) on this card; the cost is a rest of an active {ODYSSEY} Character, which may be Lim herself (she then stays on the field rested). "[End of Your Turn] Set up to 1 ... as active" is a mandatory auto with an "up to 1" pick over {ODYSSEY} Characters.
- **OP10-038 Roronoa Zoro**, "[Opponent's Turn] If you have 2 or more rested Characters, this Character gains +2000 power." A permanent with `opponentsTurn` and a count of your rested Characters (Zoro himself counts when rested; the opponent's do not).
- **OP10-043 Moocy**, "You may rest 1 of your "Dressrosa" type Leader or Stage cards: Up to 1 of your [Monkey.D.Luffy] Characters gains [Banish] during this turn." The cost is a rest of an active {Dressrosa} Leader or Stage (`type` read on the Leader/Stage); [Monkey.D.Luffy] is an exact name; a rested Luffy is still a valid target.
- **OP10-044 Cub, OP10-048 Sai**, same cost, "Return up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 1 or less to the owner's hand." "Cost" is the current cost; the cost is paid even if you pick nothing.
- **OP10-045 Cavendish**, "[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand." The trash is mandatory and may take a card just drawn; it is asked even with an empty hand beforehand (the 2 drawn cards are there).
- **OP10-046 Kyros**, "[On Play] Return up to 1 Character with a cost of 5 or less to the owner's hand." Either player's Character, including your own; it goes to its owner's hand.
- **OP10-047 Koala**, "[When Attacking] You may return 1 of your "Revolutionary Army" type Characters with a cost of 3 or more to the owner's hand: This Character gains +3000 power during this turn." The cost is a G1 `move` from your Characters (current cost, exactly 1); Koala (cost 2) cannot pay with herself; a single candidate is returned without asking.
- **OP10-049 Sabo**, "If your Character with a base cost of 7 or less other than [Sabo] would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may return this Character to the owner's hand instead." A `leaveField` replacement for the matched Characters (printed cost, `not name Sabo`: every [Sabo] is excluded, Sabo himself too); no Once Per Turn is printed. The payload is a `move` of this card to the hand, as an effect (not a cost), so it also works if the removal is a return to the hand.
- **OP10-051 Hack**, "[DON!! x1] [When Attacking] Look at 3 cards ...; reveal up to 1 "Revolutionary Army" type Character card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." `donGiven: 1` is an ability condition (a bracketed [DON!! xN]); the "Then" has no "If" and follows the take whether or not a card was taken.
- **OP10-052 Bartolomeo**, "[On Play] Place up to 1 Character with a cost of 1 or less at the bottom of the owner's deck." Either player's Character, current cost; it goes to the bottom of its owner's deck.
- **OP10-053 Bian**, "If you have a "The Tontattas" type Character other than [Bian], this Character gains [Blocker]." A permanent conditional grant (colon-less "If" on a continuous effect is a condition of the effect); "other than [Bian]" is by name, so a second [Bian] does not count. [Blocker] is gained, not printed, so `keywords` is empty.
- **OP10-055 Marco**, "[On K.O.] Return up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less to the owner's hand." Any K.O. (battle or effect) triggers it; a bounce does not.
- **OP10-056 Mansherry**, "You may rest 1 of your "Dressrosa" type Leader or Stage cards, and return 1 of your "Dressrosa" type Characters with a cost of 4 or more to the owner's hand: Return up to 1 ..." Two costs paid together (both or neither); Mansherry (cost 2) cannot return herself. Current cost for the return cost and the target.
- **OP10-057 Leo**, "[On Play] You may rest your Leader or 1 of your Stage cards: If your Leader is [Usopp], look at 5 ...; reveal up to 2 "Dressrosa" type cards other than [Leo] and add them to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order, and trash 1 card from your hand." The cost is paid whether or not the Leader is [Usopp] (the "If" is after the colon); the bottom placement and the trash are inside the Leader guard ("Then" after a failed "If" does not resolve, M4b-5). Any category of {Dressrosa} card may be taken; "other than [Leo]" is by name. The trash of 1 card is mandatory even with an empty hand after the take.
- **OP10-058 Rebecca**, "[On Play] If there is a Character with a cost of 8 or more, draw 1 card. Then, reveal up to 2 "Dressrosa" type Character cards with a cost of 7 or less other than [Rebecca] from your hand. Play 1 of the revealed cards and play the other card rested if it has a cost of 4 or less." The whole effect is inside the cost-8 guard (the "Then" does not resolve after a failed "If", M4b-5); the check counts a Character of either player. Scripted as a `select` of up to 2 from hand (bound), a pick of exactly 1 of the bound cards to play, then the other bound card played rested when its cost is 4 or less (otherwise it stays in the hand). The "play 1" pick is asked even with one revealed card; there is no engine reveal event for the hand (cosmetic). The hand selection is asked at max 0 with no candidate (hidden-gate rule).
- **OP10-062 Violet**, "[On K.O.] DON!! -1: If your Leader has the "Donquixote Pirates" type, add up to 1 purple Event from your trash to your hand." The DON!! -1 is paid whether or not the Leader has the type (the "If" is after the colon); any purple Event, any cost.
- **OP10-063 Vinsmoke Sanji**, "[On Play] If your Leader's type includes "GERMA", look at 5 ...; reveal up to 1 card with a type including "GERMA" ... Then, place the rest at the bottom" A colon-less "If", so an `if` step; the bottom placement is inside the guard. "A type including" is a substring of a type element ({GERMA 66}, {Kingdom of GERMA}); any category.
- **OP10-065 Sugar**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards and this Character: Look at 5 ...; reveal up to 1 "Donquixote Pirates" type card ..." One cost of both parts on an [Activate: Main] (activating is already the choice), so it cannot be used while Sugar is rested or with no active DON!!; any category of {Donquixote Pirates} card.
- **OP10-066 Giolla**, "[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may rest 2 of your DON!! cards: Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less." Needs 2 active DON!!; the cost is paid even if no Character is chosen.
- **OP10-069 Fighting Fish**, "[DON!! x1] [When Attacking] DON!! −1 (...): K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 1 or less." The optional DON!! -X cost (RL-52) with a `donGiven: 1` condition.
- **OP10-071 Donquixote Doflamingo**, "[On Play] DON!! −1: Play up to 1 "Donquixote Pirates" type Character card with a cost of 5 or less from your hand." and "[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards: Add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active." "Add up to 1 DON!!" asks a count; with an empty DON!! deck the rest cost is still paid and nothing is added.
- **OP10-072 Donquixote Rosinante**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 Event from your hand: Draw 2 cards. [End of Your Turn] If you have 7 or more DON!! cards on your field, set up to 2 of your DON!! cards as active." The trash cost is a hidden-hand cost (accept is offered and illegal with no Event). The colon-less "If" is an `if` step; DON!! on the field is the cost area plus the DON!! given to cards; "set up to 2" asks a count.
- **OP10-074 Pica**, "[Once Per Turn] If this Character would be K.O.'d by your opponent's effect, you may rest 2 of your active DON!! cards instead." A `ko` replacement with cause `opponentEffect` (a battle K.O. and your own effects are not replaced); the cost is 2 active cost-area DON!!.
