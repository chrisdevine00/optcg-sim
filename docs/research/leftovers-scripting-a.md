# Leftovers scripting, group A (boosters and EB)

Cards: EB02-030, EB03-018, OP09-033, OP09-045, OP09-086, OP10-070, OP10-104, OP11-006, OP11-023, OP11-046, OP12-021, OP12-024, OP12-027, OP12-028, OP12-034, OP12-101, OP13-025, OP15-099 (18 scripted, none left out). Source: `docs/research/m6b-audit/leftovers.md` ("ready", group A); every card is `verified: true`. Shapes: G3 (`attribute`), G6 (scoped K.O. protection), G2 (`cannotBeRested`), G11 (replacement payload), G23 (`div`), G8/G68 (Life face-down cost, OP15-099), D6 (`addReplacement`, EB02-030, wave 4). No card needs a wave 5+ gap or a design item. Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. Official text conventions: the printed `"X" type` is the exact type {X}, `type including "X"` is a substring match (CR 2-4-3-1); an activated [Trigger] whose condition fails sends the card to the trash.

- **EB02-030 And That's When Somebody Makes Fun of Their Friend's Dream!!!!**, "[Counter] If any of your Characters would be K.O.'d in battle during this turn, you may trash 1 card from your hand instead. [Trigger] Draw 1 card." `addReplacement` (D6): the replacement lasts to the end of the turn and is offered for every battle K.O. of a Character of yours that turn, each time (M6b-Q54, the lead's reading); each acceptance trashes 1 more card. It is also offered with an empty hand, where it cannot be accepted (the prompt is a public fact, as for every optional cost). It does not cover a K.O. by an effect, a Leader attack (no Character is K.O.'d), or a battle K.O. before the Event is played.
- **EB03-018 Tashigi**, "[Opponent's Turn] This Character cannot be K.O.'d by your opponent's effects and gains [Blocker]. [End of Your Turn] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards and trash 1 card from your hand: Set this Character as active." One permanent under `opponentsTurn` with both parts: `cannotBeKOd { opponentEffect }` stops only the opponent's effects (your own effect and a battle K.O. still work); [Blocker] only on their turn. The [End of Your Turn] cost is two parts (an active DON!! and a hand card), asked as one optional use that cannot be accepted when either is missing; it fires at the end of your turn only.
- **OP09-033 Nico Robin**, "[On Play] If you have 2 or more rested Characters, none of your "ODYSSEY" or "Straw Hat Crew" type Characters can be K.O.'d by effects until the end of your opponent's next turn." The quoted types are exact; the "If" has no colon, so it is an effect condition (the ability fires and nothing happens with fewer than 2); active Characters and Robin herself (just played, active) are not counted; "by effects" is either player's effect (a cost included) and not battle; the protection goes to the Characters on the field at resolution (a Character played later gets none) and lasts through the opponent's next turn (tested to the start of your next turn).
- **OP09-045 Cabaji**, "If you have a [Buggy] or [Mohji] Character, this Character cannot be K.O.'d in battle." Live permanent over your Characters by exact name ([Mohji & Cabaji] does not count, nor an opposing [Mohji]); battle only (an effect still K.O.s him); the protection ends as soon as the named Character leaves.
- **OP09-086 Jesus Burgess**, "This Character cannot be K.O.'d by your opponent's effects. If your Leader has the "Blackbeard Pirates" type, this Character gains +1000 power for every 4 cards in your trash." Two permanents: the first stops the opponent's effects only; the second reads the Leader's exact type and your own trash only (G23 `div`: 3 cards +0, 4 +1000, 8 +2000), live on both turns.
- **OP10-070 Trebol**, "[Blocker] [On Play] All of your Characters with 1000 base power or less cannot be K.O.'d by your opponent's effects until the end of your opponent's next turn." Reads BASE power at resolution (1000 in, 2000 out; a DON!! given does not remove it); only Characters on the field then; opponent's effects only; lasts through the opponent's next turn.
- **OP10-104 Caribou**, "[DON!! x1] If your Leader has the "Supernovas" type and your opponent has 3 or more Life cards, this Character cannot be K.O.'d in battle." One permanent with three live conditions (`donGiven 1`, the Leader's exact type, the opponent's Life, 3 in and 2 out); battle only.
- **OP11-006 Zephyr**, "[DON!! x1] [When Attacking] Give up to 1 of your opponent's <Special> attribute Characters −5000 power during this turn." The sign is −5000 (fixed in the override); the printed attribute; opposing Characters only (an opposing Leader with <Special> is not a candidate); "up to 1" can be declined; with no <Special> Character nothing is asked (the board is public).
- **OP11-023 Arlong**, "If your Leader has the {Fish-Man} type, you have 3 or less Life cards and your opponent has 5 or more rested cards, give this card in your hand −3 cost. [Trigger] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less." One hand-zone permanent with three conditions (exact Leader type, Life 3 in and 4 out, rested cards counted as M6b-Q25: the opponent's rested field cards plus their rested DON!!, as OP12-118 and ST16-003); the discount is gone once he is played. The [Trigger] rest is "up to 1" on cost 4 or less (current cost).
- **OP11-046 Vinsmoke Yonji**, "[Blocker] If you only have Characters with a type including "GERMA", this Character cannot be K.O.'d or rested by your opponent's effects." The condition is `none` of your Characters lacking a type including GERMA (substring: {GERMA 66} counts); Yonji is {GERMA 66} himself, so it is never vacuous (M6b-Q21 does not apply); live, so a non-GERMA Character leaving restores it; both protections stop the opponent's effects only (your own effects, costs, attacks and blocks pass).
- **OP12-021 Ipponmatsu**, "If your Leader has the <Slash> attribute and you have 6 or more rested DON!! cards, this Character cannot be rested by your opponent's effects. [Blocker]" Rested DON!! are the cost-area ones (3 rested plus 6 active is not enough; 6 in, 5 out); the opponent's effects only (your own effect rests him, and a block, which rests him, still works).
- **OP12-024 Gyukimaru**, "If this Character is active, this Character cannot be K.O.'d by your opponent's effects. [When Attacking] If you have a total of 3 or more given DON!! cards, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 6 or less." The protection needs him active (a rested one can be K.O.'d) and covers the opponent's effects only. "A total of 3 or more given DON!!" is the sum over your Leader and Characters, read when the effect resolves; the "If" has no colon (an effect condition: with 2 the ability fires and nothing is asked); the target's BASE cost (6 in, 7 out); "up to 1" can be declined.
- **OP12-027 Koushirou**, "If your <Slash> attribute Character with a cost of 5 or less other than this Character would be K.O.'d by your opponent's effect, you may rest this Character instead. [Blocker]" Replacement on `ko` with `cause: opponentEffect`, `isSelf: false`, optional, payload `restSelf` (G11); current cost 5 in, 6 out; never covers himself; not offered while he is rested; own effects and battle are not covered.
- **OP12-028 Kouzuki Hiyori**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards and this Character: If your Leader is [Roronoa Zoro], look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 <Slash> attribute card or green Event and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." The "If" follows the cost colon, so it is an effect condition: with another Leader the DON!! and the Character are rested and nothing is looked at (DECISIONS 2026-11-24, B3A-R1). <Slash> is any category with that printed attribute; "green Event" is an Event whose color list has green; "up to 1" may take nothing; the order of the rest is the owner's (asked).
- **OP12-034 Perona**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the <Slash> attribute, look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 <Slash> attribute card or green Event and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." Same search as OP12-028 under a colon-less "If" (an effect condition: with another Leader the ability fires and nothing is looked at).
- **OP12-101 Jewelry Bonney**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: Your "Supernovas" type Leader gains +1000 power until the end of your opponent's next turn. [Trigger] If your Leader has the {Supernovas} type, play this card." The [Trigger] text came from the override. The cost is paid with any Leader and the effect does nothing when the Leader is not {Supernovas} (the target is absent); the +1000 lasts through the opponent's turn (tested to your next turn). The [Trigger] "If" is an effect condition: activated with another Leader it fails and the card goes to the trash (not played, not added to the hand); not activating adds it to the hand.
- **OP13-025 Koby**, "[Blocker] [On Play] If your Leader has the "FILM" type or the "Strike" attribute, set up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active." Either condition is enough (tested with a Strike non-FILM and a FILM non-Strike Leader); the "If" is an effect condition; "set up to 1" asks a count (0 or 1).
- **OP15-099 Urouge**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 {Supernovas} type card from your hand: This Character gains [Rush] during this turn. [Activate: Main] You may turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-down: Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." The On Play cost filters the hand by exact type {Supernovas}; the optional use is asked whatever the hand holds (it cannot be accepted without such a card), so the prompt reveals nothing. [Rush] is for this turn only. The Activate cost is the G68 `turnLifeFaceDown { n: 1, from: 'top' }`: it needs the top card face-up (a face-down top card with a face-up card below it is not payable). "Give up to 1 rested DON!!" is the Leader-or-Character pick with `upTo: 1` (as OP13-005): the DON!! may be left after the cost is paid; with no rested DON!! nothing is asked.
