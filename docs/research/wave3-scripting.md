# Wave 3 cards: scripting readings

Cards unblocked by engine wave 3 (`docs/research/m6b-wave3.md`): OP15-002, -003, -010, -012, -017, -023, -026, -028, -059, OP16-041, -074, -100, ST32-003 (13 scripted, each with `engine/test/cards/<ID>.test.ts`). OP16-024 is written and tested but held back (see the last section). One line per reading; the open questions stay open.

- **OP15-002 Lucy** "[When Attacking]/[On Your Opponent's Attack] You may trash any number of Event or Stage cards from your hand. This Leader gains +1000 power during this battle for every card trashed." The "any number" pick is the decline (choosing none is +0), so no separate accept prompt. The pick sits behind an `exists` guard (an Event or Stage in hand): without it the engine asks an empty "trash up to 0" choice on every attack, which broke other tests that use Lucy as a stand-in Leader.
- **OP15-002 Lucy** "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If you have activated an Event with a base cost of 3 or more during this turn, draw 1 card." The "If" has no colon, so it is an effect condition: the Once Per Turn is spent when it is false (tested). Base cost is the printed cost, read on activation from hand (M6b-Q31 family).
- **OP15-003 Alvida / OP15-017 Morgan** "You may give 1 of your opponent's rested DON!! cards to 1 of your opponent's Characters: Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to its owner's Leader or 1 of their Characters." The DON!! are the opponent's own rested cost-area DON!! (M6b-Q24, Q44). The cost needs a rested DON!! and an opposing Character; with a single opposing Character the cost target is taken without a question.
- **OP15-003 Alvida** "If this Character would be K.O.'d, you may trash 1 Character card with a power of 6000 or less from your hand instead." Replacement for itself only, any cause; "power" is the printed power in hand. With no eligible card the prompt is still asked (the hand is hidden) but only "decline" is legal.
- **OP15-010 Nezumi, OP15-012 Buggy** "Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to its owner's Leader or 1 of their Characters." Same reading; "up to 1" is the target pick (choose nobody to give nothing).
- **OP15-023 Arlong** "[On K.O.] Up to 2 of your opponent's rested cards will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase." "Cards" read as the opponent's rested field cards (Leader, Characters, Stage); rested DON!! are not targets (block 4 audit Q5). The effect lasts through that one Refresh Phase only (tested over two of their turns).
- **OP15-023 Arlong** "Give up to 1 DON!! card from its owner's cost area to its owner's Leader or 1 of their Characters." `state: 'any'` (M6b-Q44): the giver picks the kind only when both active and rested are there.
- **OP15-026 Jango** "[Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: Give up to 1 of your opponent's rested DON!! cards to 1 of your opponent's Characters." Characters only (the text names no Leader); not Once Per Turn; the cost is only trashing itself, so it is always offered.
- **OP15-028 Meowban Brothers** "give up to 1 DON!! card from your opponent's cost area to 1 of your opponent's Characters": active or rested (M6b-Q44), Characters only.
- **OP15-059 Amazon** "[On Your Opponent's Attack] You may rest this Character: Your opponent may return 1 of their active DON!! cards to their DON!! deck. If they do not, give up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Character cards -2000 power during this turn." Built as `chooseOne` for the opponent (return 1 active DON!!, or the -2000). With no active DON!! (M6b-Q45) they cannot return one, so the -2000 happens with no choice offered (an `if` on their active DON!! count, with the -2000 as the `else`). The -2000 target is picked by Amazon's controller after the opponent's choice, and may be nobody.
- **OP16-041 Buggy (Leader)** "[DON!! X1] [Once Per Turn] This effect can be activated when your {Impel Down} type Character card is removed from the field. Play up to 1 [Prisoner of Impel Down] card from your hand." `removed` timing, owner `you`, Characters of type {Impel Down} (M6b-Q17, Q42, Q43): any move off the field counts (K.O., bounce, trash, either player's effect, battle). [DON!! x1] is the `donGiven` condition. The play sits behind an `exists` guard for the same empty-prompt reason as Lucy.
- **OP16-074 Magellan** "your opponent returns 1 (on play, with an {Impel Down} Leader) / 4 (on K.O.) DON!! cards from their field to their DON!! deck": `returnDon` for the opponent, who chooses which DON!! (cost area or given) and returns as many as they have (M6b-Q45).
- **OP16-100 Hallowed Glacier Slash** "[Main] You may rest 2 of your DON!! cards: If your opponent's Character has been K.O.'d during this turn, set your Leader [Yamato] as active." `koedThisTurn: 'opponent'` (any K.O. of their Character this turn, battle or effect, whoever did it; block 5 audit Q19). The cost is paid when the condition is false. The "If" is after the colon, so it is an effect condition.
- **ST32-003 Dracule Mihawk** "[Your Turn] When this Character becomes rested, draw 1 card and trash 1 card from your hand." Timing `rested` on itself with a `yourTurn` condition: every cause of resting counts (attack, cost, effect, either player's) as long as it is your turn (M6b-Q30, Q41); "draw 1 and trash 1" is not optional, the trash is exactly 1. [On Play] play up to 1 Character with a cost of 5 or less that is [Perona] or has the <Slash> attribute (the `exists` guard again).

## Cross-card notes

- **Empty "up to" prompts.** A `pick: { upTo }` or `{ any }` on hand cards with no candidate still asks a `choose` with min 0, max 0 ("play up to 1 of your cards in hand", "trash up to 0"). Three scripts here guard it with an `exists` condition so no dead prompt appears. Engine behaviour, not changed.
- **Lucy as a stand-in.** `engine/test/cards/OP15-057.test.ts` used OP15-002 as an inert Leader (its Event or Stage in hand now trigger the real ability): it now uses OP15-039. `engine/test/independent/cards/op15/OP15-056.test.ts` control test declines the new trash choice. `helpers-op15g2.ts` still says LUCY is "unscripted, inert".
