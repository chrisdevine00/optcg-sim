# OP-15 scripting, group 3 of 3: readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP15-084, 085, 086, 087, 090, 091, 094, 095, 096, 097, 098, 100, 102, 103, 104, 105, 106, 109, 110, 111, 115, 117 (22 cards, all scripted; none needed a wave 3+ gap or a design item). Scripts in `cards/effects/OP-15/`, tests in `engine/test/cards/OP15-*.test.ts` (helpers in `helpers-op15g3.ts`). B4-G1 (action mill) is unified G4, wave 1, merged. One line per reading; "no ruling" means the reading is the scripter's.

- **OP15-098 Monkey.D.Luffy (Leader)**, "If your {Sky Island} type Character with 6000 base power or more would be removed from the field **by your opponent**, you may add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand instead." The data text has no "effect" after "opponent" (the sibling OP15-105 and every similar card say "by your opponent's effect"); read as `cause: 'opponentEffect'`, so a battle K.O. is not replaced (tested). If the printed card really says "by your opponent" without "effect", it would also cover being K.O.'d in battle, which the DSL can only express together with your own effects (`cause: 'any'`); worth one check against the physical card. The replacement is the cost form `lifeToHand` (RL-41: cannot be used with 0 Life, not damage, no [Trigger]).
- **OP15-105 Jewelry Bonney**, same shape with 7000 base power or less, any Character of yours (she is covered herself); `basePower`, inclusive at 7000.
- **OP15-090 Perona**, "you may trash 1 card from your hand instead", 7000 base or less, opponent's effect only. The cost is a hand trash, so the offer is shown even with an empty hand (`costIsSecret`) but accepting is illegal (tested); Perona (2000) protects herself.
- **OP15-094 Roronoa Zoro**, "If your {Straw Hat Crew} type Character other than this Character would be removed ... you may trash this Character instead": `isSelf: false` in the replaced selector, `trashSelf` as the payload. Zoro himself being removed is not replaced; battle K.O.s are not replaced.
- **OP15-095 Gum-Gum Storm / OP15-097 I Find It Embarrassing...**, trash thresholds (15, 10) count the Event itself when it is played from hand: an Event is trashed before its effect resolves (CR 2-7-3, 8-4-2, digest 74/123), so 14 other cards make 15. Tested at both boundaries. For the [Trigger] of OP15-097 the card is in no area while it resolves (CR 10-1-5-3, QA-OP14), so it does not count: the Trigger needs 10 other cards (tested at 10 and 9).
- **OP15-095**, "[Main] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards: If you have 15 or more ...": `costOptional` (Event, B-1); the "If" follows the colon, so the DON!! is rested even when the check fails. "up to 1 of your {Straw Hat Crew} type Leader or Character cards" is read as `type: Straw Hat Crew` on Leader and Characters. The [Counter] "If you have 15 or more" is an effect-level `if` (leading "If" without a colon, read at resolution as in ST30-015); its target is any Leader or Character.
- **OP15-096**, [Main] same cost shape, "If your Leader has the {Straw Hat Crew} type, trash 5 cards from the top of your deck": a deck of exactly 5 or fewer is milled empty and loses at once (RL-26, as ST35-003, OP16-099). [Counter] "You may trash 1 card from your hand:" is `costOptional` with a hand trash (the Event itself is already trashed, so it cannot pay for itself; with no other card the accept is illegal, tested).
- **OP15-084 Dr. Hogback**, [On K.O.] "If you have 6 or less cards in your hand, draw 1": read at resolution; Hogback is on the field, so he is not in the count. Inclusive at 6 (tested at 6 and 7).
- **OP15-085 Tony Tony.Chopper**, "[Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: If your Leader has the {Straw Hat Crew} type, add up to 1 ...": the trash is the cost (paid even with another Leader type; the "If" follows the colon); "other than [Tony Tony.Chopper]" is `not name`, so any printing of Chopper and Chopper herself (she is in the trash by then) are excluded (tested with ST14-005).
- **OP15-086 Nami**, "play up to 1 ... from your trash. The Character played with this effect gains [Rush] during this turn": the played card is bound (`bind`) and given [Rush] by `ref`; with no card played nothing is granted. "If your Leader has the {Straw Hat Crew} type" gates both steps (the grant is part of the same effect).
- **OP15-087 Nico Robin (Black)**, "If you have 10 or more cards in your trash, this Character gains [Blocker]": a live permanent condition on your own trash only. "Draw 2 cards and trash 2 cards from your hand": the drawn cards are candidates; exactly 2 (mandatory).
- **OP15-091 Margarita**, "Place up to 1 card from your opponent's trash at the bottom of the owner's deck": `move` from the opponent's trash to `deck bottom`; the owner is the opponent (G-merged B4 shape, tested: it lands under the opponent's deck, not yours).
- **OP15-100 Kamakiri**, "You may trash this Character and add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand: K.O. ...": cost list `trashSelf` + `lifeToHand`; with 0 Life the cost is public and unpayable, so the optional effect is not offered at all (tested). "up to 1" K.O. may be declined after the cost.
- **OP15-102 Gan.Fall**, "If you have a {Sky Island} type Character with 7000 power or more, give this card in your hand -3 cost." The source text lost the minus sign (`textFixes` records "−3 cost"; audit Q2); script: hand-zone permanent cost -3 while you have such a Character, current power (not base), inclusive at 7000, Characters only. [On Play] "cost equal to or less than the number of your opponent's Life cards": `cost lte {life: opponent}` read at resolution, a Character of cost 0 needs 0 Life (inclusive tested at 2 Life and cost 2).
- **OP15-103 Genbo / OP15-104 Conis / OP15-106 Octoballoon / OP15-115 Impact Dial / OP15-117 Heso!!**, [Trigger]s. Genbo "Draw 1 card. Then, if you have 2 or less Life cards, play this card": the draw is unconditional, the play is its own `if`, read after the damage card left Life (as OP16-111). Octoballoon's "Then, play up to 1 yellow Character or Stage card with a cost of 2 or less from your hand" is independent of the draw and may play the drawn card; the prompt is asked (max 0 with no candidate) so the hand stays hidden. Conis [On Play]: "If you have less Life cards than your opponent" is strict `<` (0 vs 1 counts).
- **OP15-109 Nico Robin (Yellow)**, "You may add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand: If your Leader has the {Straw Hat Crew} type, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards. Then, play up to 1 {Sky Island} ... cost 5 or less from your hand": the Life card is the cost (paid even with another Leader type); the "Then" is inside the "If" (M4b-5 / RD 121-122), so with another Leader neither step runs.
- **OP15-110 Braham**, [On K.O.] "If your Leader has the {Shandian Warrior} type, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards": `type` exact element (a {Sky Island}-only Leader does not qualify, tested); "up to 1" is an optional prompt.
- **OP15-111 Mont Blanc Noland**, "[DON!! x1] [When Attacking] Up to 1 of your [Kalgara] cards gains [Rush] during this turn": `donGiven: 1` ability condition; "[Kalgara] cards" is read as Characters named Kalgara on the field (a Leader or a card in hand gaining [Rush] has no effect). The grant is a timed `grant`; tested to let a Kalgara played this turn attack and to end with the turn.
- **OP15-115 Impact Dial**, [Main] "K.O. up to 1 ... Then, add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand": independent "Then" (the Life card is taken even when no K.O. was chosen; nothing with 0 Life); the [Trigger] is the K.O. alone.
- **OP15-117 Heso!!**, "give up to 1 rested DON!! card to 1 of your {Sky Island} type Leader or Character cards": `giveDon n: 1 rested` to exactly 1 target (as OP16-094). The DON!! rested to pay for the Event is a rested DON!! and may be the one given (tested). [Trigger] "If your Leader has the {Sky Island} type, draw 2 cards" is an effect-level `if`.
