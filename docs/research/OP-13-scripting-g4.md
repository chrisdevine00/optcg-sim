# OP-13 group 4 of 4: scripting readings

Cards: OP13-089, 091, 092, 093, 094, 095, 096, 097, 098, 099, 100, 102, 104, 108, 109, 110, 112, 114, 115, 116, 117, 120 (22 cards, all `verified: true`, all scripted). None needed a wave 4+ gap, a design item or `[DON!! xN] [On K.O.]`: the audit's only gap for the group (B4-G39, G18, wave 2) is merged. One line per reading.

- OP13-089 / 091 ("If you have 7 or more cards in your trash, this Character cannot be removed from the field by your opponent's effects and gains [Blocker]"): one live permanent with the trash count as its condition and two effects (`cannotBeRemovedByOpponentEffects`, grant Blocker). The threshold is exactly 7; battle K.O.s and your own effects still remove it. `keywords` is empty (Blocker is conditional).
- OP13-091: "You may trash 1 card from your hand:" is an optional cost; the hand pick is asked even with a single card (hand size is public). Base cost 5 or less, inclusive.
- OP13-092: "play up to 1 'Mary Geoise' type Stage card with a cost of 1 from your trash": `cost eq 1` (current cost), `category Stage`; the Life condition (3 or less, so 0 counts) is a public count gating the ability.
- OP13-093: "Draw 2 cards and trash 2 cards from your hand": the trash is `exactly 2`, a plain unconditional step ("and" is not "if you do"); drawn cards are candidates.
- OP13-095 / 097: "If you only have / the only Characters on your field are 'Celestial Dragons' type Characters" is `exists` a Character and `none` that is not Celestial Dragons, so with no Characters it is false (OP13-097 can have none; Rosward is always on the field itself). 097 [Main] "You may rest 5 of your DON!! cards:" is a `costOptional` `restDon 5` on the Event (the Event's own cost is paid separately and the 5 DON!! are asked after).
- OP13-096 / 116 ("reveal up to 1 ... other than [this card]" / "place the rest at the bottom in any order"): 096 excludes by printed name and trashes the rest (no order); 116 asks the owner an order for the rest. 116 takes a Character only; 096 takes any category with the type. [Trigger] is `activate` of the card's own [Main].
- OP13-098: the leading "If your Leader is [Imu]" follows the colon, so the DON!! is rested even without Imu (condition is an effect `if`, name match `Imu`). Stage K.O. is `cost eq 7` (current cost) on the opponent's Stage (G18). [Counter] "If your Leader is [Imu], up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +4000" has the same `if`.
- OP13-099 [Your Turn] power: permanent with `yourTurn` and trash >= 19. [Activate: Main] "You may rest this card and 3 of your DON!! cards:" has no `costOptional` (not an Event; activation is the choice). "cost equal to or less than the number of DON!! cards on your field" is `donOnField` read after the 3 are rested (rested and given DON!! still count, rule: the field includes the cost area), black {Five Elders} Characters only.
- OP13-100 (a Leader, not a Character): "[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] This effect can be activated when you play a Character with a [Trigger]": `cardPlayed` by you, filter Character with `hasTrigger`, `yourTurn` condition, Once Per Turn. "Give up to 2 rested DON!! cards to 1 of your Leader or Character cards": `giveDon n 2 rested` to exactly 1 target; the engine asks the count (2, 1, 0) first, then the target. The DON!! paid to play the Character are already rested and can be given. Open: whether Once Per Turn is spent when the DON!! count 0 is chosen (spent at resolution, as every once-per-turn here).
- OP13-102: "If the number of your Life cards is equal to or less than the number of your opponent's, draw 1 card. Then, rest up to 1 ...": the draw is conditional, the rest is a separate sentence after "Then" and runs regardless (reading; contrast OP14-088 where "and" joined the two). The trash is a plain `trashSelf` cost (an [Activate: Main], no `costOptional`). [Trigger] has no condition.
- OP13-104: "[On K.O.] You may trash 1 card from your hand: If your Leader is multicolored, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards": the Leader check follows the colon (cost paid with a mono-colour Leader). "Up to 1" of a single top card is asked as a yes/no. Deck-top card goes face-down to the top of Life.
- OP13-108 [On Play]: "If your Leader has the 'Egghead' type, this Character gains [Rush] during this turn. Then, your opponent adds 1 card from the top of their Life cards to their hand": the Rush is gated, the Life step is not ("Then"); mandatory `exactly 1`, to the opponent's hand, nothing with an empty Life area. [Trigger] "If you have 1 or less Life cards" is an `if` inside the effect (see engine note: trigger `conditions` are not evaluated); at the Trigger the damage card has already left Life, so 1 or less counts the remaining cards (tested at 0, 1 and 2 left).
- OP13-109: "If this Character would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-up instead": `replacement` `leaveField` cause `opponentEffect`, optional, `turnLifeFaceUp 1` (cannot be paid, so no prompt, when the top card is already face-up; battle K.O.s are not replaced). Covers K.O., bounce and the like.
- OP13-110: "play up to 1 Character card with a cost of 5 or less and a [Trigger]": `hasTrigger` is the printed [Trigger]; the Egghead check gates the play (public Leader fact, never a hidden-zone gate).
- OP13-112: "a total of 2 or more given DON!! cards" is `donGiven you` (all your cards, Leader included) >= 2.
- OP13-114: "[On Play]/[When Attacking] You may turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-up:" is an optional `turnLifeFaceUp` cost, "Give up to 1 ... -2000 power" up to 1 target, no K.O. at 0. [Trigger] "You may trash 1 card from your hand: Play this card.": the player decides at the Trigger decision (activate or add to hand); once activating, the hand pick is mandatory and activating is not offered with an empty hand. `play { from: 'self' }` is safe here since the cost moves a hand card, not the source.
- OP13-115: "Then, if your opponent has 2 or less Life cards" is independent of the first pick; each "up to 1 of your Leader or Character" is its own pick (the second may be the same card, tested). Inclusive at 2.
- OP13-117: [Main] "You may turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-up:" `costOptional` on the Event, base cost 6 or less inclusive.
- OP13-120: "+2 cost until the end of your opponent's next turn" is `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` (resolved on your own turn, so it ends with the opponent's End Phase). "Then, give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader" runs whether or not a Character was chosen; the engine asks a target choice (min 0) even with the Leader as the only candidate, so the player can give none.

## Engine notes

- `TriggerAbility` inherits `conditions` from `AbilityBase`, but `engine/src/effects/run.ts` (~line 175) only evaluates `ab.conditions` for `kind === 'auto'`: a trigger's `conditions` are silently ignored. Failing case: give OP13-108's [Trigger] `conditions: [{ cmp: [{ life: 'you' }, '<=', 1] }]` and take damage with 2 Life remaining: the rest is still asked. The script uses an effect-level `if` instead. The DSL validator accepts the field, so a lint (reject `conditions` on `trigger`) or an evaluation is worth adding.
