# OP-16 scripting, group 4 of 4: readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP16-092 to 111, 114, 115, 116, 118 (24 cards; 23 scripted, OP16-100 left unscripted). Scripts in `cards/effects/OP-16/`, tests in `engine/test/cards/OP16-*.test.ts`, helpers in `engine/test/cards/helpers-op16g4.ts`. One line per reading; "no ruling" means the reading is the scripter's, not an official answer.

- **OP16-092 Nico Robin**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 Character card with a cost of 8 or more from your hand: Draw 2 cards." An optional cost (trash from hand, a Character card only: an Event of cost 8 does not qualify); with none in hand the cost cannot be paid.
- **OP16-093 Bartholomew Kuma**, "Draw 2 cards and trash 2 cards from your hand. Then, give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." "Then" is not "If you do": the give happens whatever the draw and trash did. "Give up to 1" is the engine-wide `giveDon` shape with a target pick that may be left empty (the engine asks "up to 1"). Kuma costs 3, so rested DON!! always exist when she is played.
- **OP16-094 Portgas.D.Ace**, "[On K.O.] Your opponent trashes 2 cards from their hand." The opponent chooses which 2 (`chooser: 'opponent'`); with fewer cards they trash what they have. "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to 1 of your {Land of Wano} type Leader or Character cards": a type filter on the receiver (the Leader must be {Land of Wano} too).
- **OP16-095 Monkey.D.Luffy**, "Up to 1 of your black {Land of Wano} type Characters gains [Unblockable] during this turn." Luffy himself is black and {Land of Wano}, so he is a legal target.
- **OP16-096 Yamato**, "[On K.O.] Play up to 1 [Yamato] with a cost of 6 or less from your trash." Printed [Unblockable] is in `keywords`. The card itself (cost 8) is in the trash when the effect resolves and is not eligible.
- **OP16-097 Yamato**, "[On Play] Add up to 1 {Land of Wano} type Character card with a cost of 6 or less from your trash to your hand. Then, play up to 1 Character card with a cost of 2 or less from your hand." "Then" is not "If you do": the play step happens even when nothing was added; the card just added can be the one played.
- **OP16-098 Yamato**, "[Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: Play up to 1 black [Yamato] with a cost of 8 from your trash." The trash cost is `trashSelf` (paid even when no target exists); "a cost of 8" is an exact match (`cost eq 8`), so OP16-098 itself (cost 6) and a cost 5 Yamato are not targets.
- **OP16-099 I've Come Here... To Cut Those Chains!!!**, "[Main] You may rest 6 of your DON!! cards: Trash 5 cards from the top of your deck. Then, play up to 1 {Land of Wano} type Character card with a cost of 6 or less from your trash." The target is chosen after the mill, so a milled card can be played. Mill uses wave 1's `mill` action; a deck of 5 or fewer cards loses the game on the spot (RL-26, wave 1 reading 3), which the test records. [Counter] +3000 to the Leader, no target choice.
- **OP16-100 Hallowed Glacier Slash**: NOT scripted. Needs gap **G26** (per-turn history, "an opponent's Character has been K.O.'d during this turn"; audit B5-G22, wave 3 or later). The [Counter] alone is expressible but the card stays whole until the condition exists.
- **OP16-101 Mahoroba**, "[Main] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +3000 power during this turn. Then, if you have 10 or more cards in your trash, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 2 or less." "Then, if" is a second step with its own condition, not gated on a power target having been chosen; the trash is counted at that moment, with the Event itself already in the trash (the engine moves a played Event to the trash before it resolves). [Trigger] "Add up to 1 [Yamato]": exact name.
- **OP16-102 Avalo Pizarro / 103 Van Augur / 106 Sanjuan.Wolf / 109 Doc Q / 110 Vasco Shot / 114 Laffitte**, "[Trigger] Activate this card's [On K.O.] effect." Wave 1's `activate: { ability: 'onKO', of: 'self' }`. The [On K.O.] runs from the Trigger with the Leader-type and [Opponent's Turn] conditions re-checked as it starts.
- **OP16-102 Avalo Pizarro**, "Draw 1 card, then play up to 1 [Fullalead] from your hand or trash." "then" after a comma is a sequence, not "If you do": the play is offered after the draw whatever it did. [Fullalead] is any card with that name (OP09-099 and OP17-057 both).
- **OP16-103 Van Augur**, "[Opponent's Turn] [On K.O.] If your Leader has the {Blackbeard Pirates} type, draw 1 card and give up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Character cards −3000 power during this turn." [Opponent's Turn] is an ability condition (`opponentsTurn`); the Leader type is an `if` inside. Not tested on its own turn: no scripted card in the repo K.O.s your own Character on your own turn without a type restriction (the attacker is never K.O.'d by losing a battle), so the negative case there is not reachable with real cards.
- **OP16-104 Catarina Devon**, "[When Attacking] Select up to 1 of your opponent's Characters. This Character's base power becomes the same as the selected Character's power during this turn." `select` with `bind` then `setBasePower` with `refStat power`, guarded by an `exists` so that selecting nothing changes nothing. The value is read when the effect resolves (the selected Character's power with its modifiers) and then fixed; no ruling on a later change of the selected Character's power. The [Trigger] cost-1 filter is the printed cost in the trash.
- **OP16-105 Gecko Moria**, "[Trigger] If you have 1 or less Life cards, play up to 1 [Absalom], up to 1 [Dr. Hogback], and up to 1 [Perona], with a cost of 4 or less from your trash." Three independent plays (each "up to 1", each may be declined); the Life count is read when the Trigger resolves, after the damage card has left Life (audit gave this shape).
- **OP16-106 Sanjuan.Wolf**, "draw 1 card, then up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards' base power becomes 7000 during this turn": the setBasePower is inside the Leader-type `if` (as the draw), and not gated on the draw. A Character with higher base power would be lowered ("becomes"); not tested.
- **OP16-107 Jesus Burgess**, "[On K.O.] Add up to 1 card from the top of your opponent's Life cards to the owner's hand." The engine asks an `optional` ("add the top card?") for this "up to 1" Life move. "[Trigger] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Play this card." A Trigger cost: with an empty hand the Trigger cannot be activated.
- **OP16-108 Shiryu**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Add up to 1 {Blackbeard Pirates} type card with a cost of 6 or less from your trash to the top of your Life cards face-up." "a ... type card": any category. The Life card goes on top face-up (`faceUp: true`).
- **OP16-109 Doc Q**, "draw 1 card and K.O. up to 2 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 1 or less": both inside the Leader-type `if`, "up to 2" is a pick of up to 2.
- **OP16-111 Boa Sandersonia**, "[Trigger] If you have 2 or less Life cards, play this card." Printed [Blocker] in `keywords`. The Life count is read after the damage card was taken (so 3 Life before damage counts), the threshold is inclusive.
- **OP16-115 Black Vortex**, "[Main] If your Leader has the {Blackbeard Pirates} type, add up to 1 card with a [Trigger] other than [Black Vortex] from your trash to your hand." `hasTrigger` filter with `not name`; once played, Black Vortex itself is in the trash and excluded by name. [Trigger] negates up to 1 opposing Leader or Character this turn (a timed `negate`).
- **OP16-116 Zehahahahaha!**, "[Main] If you have 10 DON!! cards on your field, play up to 1 [Marshall.D.Teach] from your hand. Then, add up to 1 card from the top of your opponent's Life cards to the owner's hand." Audit Q10 convention: "Then" is inside the DON!! `if`, so below 10 DON!! neither step happens. The audit notes the stronger reading may be that the Life step needs no condition; this one deserves a Q&A check. With 10 DON!! the Life step does not depend on a Teach having been played. DON!! on the field counts the cost area (rested too) plus given.
- **OP16-118 Portgas.D.Ace**, "The counter of all of your Character cards with 8000 power in your hand becomes +2000." A permanent while Ace is on the field with target zone `hand` (`power eq 8000`, the card's own power; no modifiers apply in hand). "[On Play]/[On K.O.] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 [Monkey.D.Luffy] or up to 1 card with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates" and add it to your hand": one card in total (the text says "add it"), `name` or `typeIncludes` as an `any` filter; the rest go to the bottom in any order.

## Observations (not bugs)

- Drawing the last card of a deck ends the game at once (deck-out), as does milling the whole deck; tests keep 2 or more cards in the deck after a draw.
- An "up to 1" step with no candidate usually opens no decision, but OP16-116 with 10 DON!! and no [Marshall.D.Teach] in a non-empty hand still shows an empty `choose` (min 0, max 0) before the Life step; the test answers it with `done`. The same shape in OP16-097 (play from hand, no candidate) showed none, so the engine is inconsistent here (harmless).

## Not scripted

- **OP16-100 Hallowed Glacier Slash**, "[Main] You may rest 2 of your DON!! cards: If your opponent's Character has been K.O.'d during this turn, set your Leader [Yamato] as active." needs gap **G26** (a per-turn K.O. record read by a condition; audit B5-G22, with open question Q19). Stats-only until that wave.
