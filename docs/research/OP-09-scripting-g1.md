# OP-09 scripting, group 1 of 4: readings

Cards: OP09-001, 002, 003, 004, 005, 007, 009, 010, 011, 012, 013, 014, 015, 017, 023, 024, 026, 027, 028, 029, 030, 042 (22 cards, all `verified: true`, none left unscripted). Gaps used (all merged): G1 (OP09-030 cost), G10 (OP09-014), G11 (OP09-012). No wave 5+ gap and no design item D1 to D9 was needed. One line per reading, with the card text.

- **OP09-001 Shanks (Leader)** "[Once Per Turn] This effect can be activated when your opponent attacks. Give up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Character cards −1000 power during this turn." `onOpponentAttack` with Once Per Turn; the target is chosen when it resolves (the attacker is a candidate, and so is any other Character or the Leader); "up to 1" may be declined; the lowered power counts in the battle that follows and lasts the turn.
- **OP09-002 Uta** "Look at 5 cards ...; reveal up to 1 "Red-Haired Pirates" type card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." As EB02-017: the quoted type is the exact type {Red-Haired Pirates}; the search may take nothing (CR 8-4-4-2).
- **OP09-004 Shanks** "Give all of your opponent's Characters −1000 power. [Rush]" No timing on the first sentence, so a permanent while he is on the field (both turns, also Characters the opponent plays later); the opposing Leader is untouched. Two Shanks stack.
- **OP09-005 Silvers Rayleigh** "[On Play] If your opponent has 2 or more Characters with a base power of 5000 or more, draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand." Base power (a buff does not count, a lowered 5000-base Character still counts); opponent Characters only. "Draw 2 and trash 1" is one sequence inside the guard; the trash is exactly 1 and may be a drawn card.
- **OP09-007 Heat** "[On Play] Up to 1 of your Leader with 4000 power or less gains +1000 power during this turn." The 4000 is the Leader's current power when the effect resolves (so it needs a lowering effect: no Leader has a printed power of 4000 or less); the pick is on the Leader only and is asked only when the Leader qualifies.
- **OP09-009 Benn.Beckman** "[On Play] Trash up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 6000 power or less." A move to the trash (not a K.O.: no [On K.O.], no K.O. replacement); the limit is current power.
- **OP09-010 Bonk Punch** "[On Play] Play up to 1 [Monster] from your hand." By name; the pick is on public facts only (the name), so it is asked even with no [Monster] in hand (an "up to" at max 0). "[DON!! x1] [When Attacking] This Character gains +2000 power during this turn." `donGiven: 1` means 1 or more.
- **OP09-011 Hongo** "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: If your Leader has the "Red-Haired Pirates" type, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn." The If is after the cost colon, so it is an effect condition (B3A-R1): with another Leader the ability is still offered, Hongo is rested and nothing happens.
- **OP09-012 Monster** "If your Character [Bonk Punch] would be K.O.'d by an effect, you may trash this Character instead." A `ko` replacement with `cause: 'effect'` (G11): an effect of either player, a cost included; not a battle K.O.; not a bounce or trash. It protects the Character named [Bonk Punch] (not [Bonk Punch & Monster], not Monster himself); `trashSelf` as the payload; no Once Per Turn on the text.
- **OP09-013 Yasopp** "[On Play] Up to 1 of your Leader gains +1000 power until the end of your opponent's next turn." The plain duration `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`. "[DON!! x1] [When Attacking] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −1000 power during this turn." Characters only.
- **OP09-014 Limejuice** "[On Play] Your opponent cannot activate up to 1 [Blocker] Character that has 4000 power or less during this turn." G10 `cannotActivateBlocker` on the chosen Blocker. "[Blocker] Character" is a Character with [Blocker] now (printed or gained); the target is fixed when the effect resolves (a snapshot of current power at 4000 or less; a later buff does not free it). Other Blockers still block.
- **OP09-015 Lucky.Roux** "[On K.O.] If your Leader has the "Red-Haired Pirates" type, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base power of 6000 or less." No colon: an effect `if`. Base power (a buff or a lowering does not move a Character in or out). Fires on a K.O. of any cause, not on a bounce.
- **OP09-017 Wire** "[DON!! x1] If your Leader has 7000 power or more and the "Kid Pirates" type, this Character gains [Rush]." A permanent with `donGiven: 1` and a Leader filter on current power (given DON!! and buffs count; exactly 7000 qualifies) and the exact type.
- **OP09-023 Adio** "[On Play] If your Leader has the "ODYSSEY" type, set up to 3 of your DON!! cards as active." The engine's count prompt (3, 2, 1, None); a Leader of another type: nothing. "[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards: Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +2000 power during this battle." Optional cost `restDon: 1`; Adio may boost himself; the boost ends with the battle.
- **OP09-024 Usopp** "[On Play] If you have 2 or more rested Characters, draw 2 cards and trash 2 cards from your hand." Your own rested Characters only (not a rested Leader); Usopp himself is active. Exactly 2 are trashed, drawn cards included. As OP10-025.
- **OP09-026 Sakazuki** "[On Play] If you have 2 or more rested Characters, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less." Current cost; Characters only. As OP10-024.
- **OP09-027 Sabo** "[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] If you have 3 or more rested Characters, draw 1 card." No colon, so an effect `if`: the ability fires and Once Per Turn is spent with fewer than 3. Sabo is rested by attacking and counts himself.
- **OP09-028 Sanji** "[On K.O.] You may add 1 card from the top or bottom of your Life cards to your hand: Play up to 1 "ODYSSEY" or "Straw Hat Crew" type Character card with a cost of 4 or less from your trash rested." `lifeToHand` with `topOrBottom` (not damage: no [Trigger]); the Character is played rested; Sanji himself (cost 5) is in the trash but not a candidate; with no Life the cost is unpayable. "Up to 1" may be left empty after the cost was paid.
- **OP09-029 Tony Tony.Chopper** "[End of Your Turn] Set up to 1 of your "ODYSSEY" type Characters with a cost of 4 or less as active." Chopper (ODYSSEY, cost 3) is a candidate himself; an already-active Character may be picked (the text does not require rested).
- **OP09-030 Trafalgar Law** "[On Play] You may return 1 of your Characters to the owner's hand: Play up to 1 "ODYSSEY" type Character card with a cost of 3 or less other than [Trafalgar Law] from your hand." G1 cost: any of your Characters, Law himself included (a lone candidate is taken without a pick); the play is offered after the cost, and a Character just returned can be the one played back; a second [Trafalgar Law] (or Law returned to the hand) is excluded by name; no prompt depends on the hand (the "up to 1" is asked at max 0).
- **OP09-042 Buggy (Leader)** "[Activate: Main] You may rest 5 of your DON!! cards and trash 1 card from your hand: Play up to 1 "Cross Guild" type Character card from your hand." Costs `restDon: 5` and a hand trash of exactly 1 (so not offered with 4 active DON!! or an empty hand); the trashed card may be a {Cross Guild} Character; "up to 1" may be left empty after the cost; no Once Per Turn on the text.

Test notes: the {ODYSSEY} Leader [Lim] (OP09-022) is the only Leader of that type; the tests that need it clear its text with `withAbilities` (`mkOdyssey`), so a later script of OP09-022 cannot change them. The {Red-Haired Pirates} Leader is ST05-001 and the {Kid Pirates} Leader ST02-001 (an [Activate: Main] only, never used).

No engine bugs found. Open questions touched: none new (the colon-less If readings follow DECISIONS 2026-11-24, B3A-R1).
