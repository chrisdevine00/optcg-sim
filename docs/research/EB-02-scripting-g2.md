# EB-02 scripting, group 2 of 2: readings and ambiguities

Cards: EB02-007, 008, 020, 031, 038, 040, 041, 044, 045, 046, 047, 048, 049, 050, 051, 054, 055, 056, 058, 059, 060, 061 (22 scripted, none left out; every card is `verified: true` in `cards/overrides/`). Gaps from `docs/research/m6b-audit/block3.md` mapped through `m6b-runtime-audit.md` §2: B3-G1 = G4 (`mill`, EB02-046), B3-G19 = G17 (`lookAtTop` `to: 'play'`, EB02-056), B3-G29 = G9 (`returnDon` of active DON!! only, EB02-061); all in waves 1 to 3, merged. No wave 5+ gap and no design item D1 to D9 in this group. Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. No new rulings were invented; M6b open questions stay open.

No prompt is gated on a hidden-zone condition: the plays from hand (EB02-038, 048, 059, 056) are plain "up to 1" picks asked at max 0 when the hand holds no match (tests pin it with raw `g.act`); the only gates read public facts (Life count, the opponent's hand size and Character count, DON!! counts, the Leader).

- **EB02-007 Cloven Rose Blizzard**, "[Main] Up to a total of 3 of your Leader and Character cards gain +1000 power during this turn. Then, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 3000 power or less. [Trigger] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 4000 power or less." One pick of up to 3 across Leader and Characters (a card at most once); "Then" is sequential, the K.O. does not depend on a card having been boosted. "3000 power or less" is the current power at resolution (a +1000 effect on the target counts; the opponent's own DON!! give no power on your turn). With no qualifying target nothing is asked (the board is public).
- **EB02-008 The Peak, EB02-020 We Are!, EB02-031 Hope, EB02-040 BRAND NEW WORLD, EB02-050 Kokoro no Chizu, EB02-058 UUUUUS!**, "[Main] Look at 4 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 card with a cost of 4 or more and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Trigger] Activate this card's [Main] effect." One script shape for all six (same text). Any card type with a printed cost of 4 or more qualifies (a Character, Event or Stage); "up to 1" may take nothing even with a match (CR 8-4-4-2); the rest go to the bottom in an order the owner picks. The take is asked even when no card qualifies (a hidden deck: the prompt must not tell the opponent whether the top 4 held a match). The Trigger runs the [Main] effect with no cost to pay.
- **EB02-038 Magellan**, `[On Play] Play up to 1 "Impel Down" type Character card with a cost of 2 or less from your hand.` The quoted type is the exact type {Impel Down}; the cost is the printed cost in hand; boundary 2 in, 3 out. Asked at max 0 with no match.
- **EB02-041 Merry Go (Purple Stage)**, `[On Play] If your Leader has the "Straw Hat Crew" type, draw 1 card. [Activate: Main] You may rest this Stage: If the number of DON!! cards on your field is equal to or less than the number on your opponent's field, up to 1 of your "Straw Hat Crew" type Characters gains +2 cost until the end of your opponent's next turn.` The On Play "If" has no colon: an `if` step. The Activate "If" follows the colon: the Stage is rested either way. "DON!! cards on your field" counts the cost area plus DON!! given to cards (rules-digest), on both sides, read when the effect resolves (the Stage's rest does not change it). "Until the end of your opponent's next turn" is `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` (it covers the whole of their turn, as in EB03-017). The Stage can be activated the turn it is played.
- **EB02-044 Sengoku**, `[Blocker] [On Play] Play up to 1 black "Navy" type Character card with a cost of 4 or less from your trash rested.` Printed keyword Blocker in the override. "Black" is a color filter (a multicolor card containing black qualifies); {Navy} exact type; cost boundary 4 in, 5 out; the card enters rested. The trash is public, so with no match nothing is asked.
- **EB02-045 Trafalgar Law**, `[Blocker] [On Play] You may place 2 cards from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order: Choose one: • Draw 1 card. • If your opponent has 5 or more cards in their hand, your opponent trashes 1 card from their hand.` The 2 cards are an exact optional cost (not offered with fewer than 2 in the trash); the owner picks them and their order. The second mode may be chosen and do nothing (the same reading as OP12-060): the hand size is public, read when the mode resolves; boundary 5 acts, 4 does nothing. The opponent chooses the card they trash (`chooser: opponent`).
- **EB02-046 Hildon**, `[On Play] Trash 2 cards from the top of your deck and give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −1 cost during this turn.` `mill` (G4) then a cost change; "and" joins two independent instructions, so the cost change does not depend on the trash. The trash is mandatory (as many as the deck holds). A cost cannot go below 0 as shown in the view (a -1 on a cost 0 Character does nothing visible).
- **EB02-047 Blueno**, `[Activate: Main] You may trash 1 card from your hand and trash this Character: Play up to 1 Character card with a type including "CP" and a cost of 5 or less other than [Blueno] from your trash.` Both costs are paid first; Blueno and the hand card are in the trash when the play resolves, so the hand card may be the Character played, and Blueno (and any other card named [Blueno]) is excluded by name. `typeIncludes: 'CP'` is a substring of a type (CP0, CP9...). Not offered with an empty hand.
- **EB02-048 Brook**, `[On Play] Add up to 1 [Laboon] from your trash to your hand. [On K.O.] Play up to 1 [Laboon] with a cost of 4 or less from your hand.` [Laboon] is the exact card name (any of the Laboon cards). The text says "add", not "reveal": nothing is revealed (the trash is public anyway). The On K.O. play is asked at max 0 when the hand has no [Laboon] (hand is hidden); no printed [Laboon] has a cost above 4, so the cost boundary cannot be pinned with a real card.
- **EB02-049 Monkey.D.Garp**, `[On Play] Give up to 2 rested DON!! cards to 1 of your Leader. [Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: If your Leader is [Monkey.D.Garp], K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 1 or less.` "1 of your Leader" is read as the Leader (the only possible target); "up to 2" asks a count when 2 or more DON!! are rested (as EB03-014). The Activate's "If" follows the colon: the rest cost is paid and nothing happens with another Leader. The Leader check is by name [Monkey.D.Garp].
- **EB02-051 Three-Pace Hum Soul Notch Slash**, `[Main] Choose one: • K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 2 or less. • Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −4 cost during this turn.` The mode is chosen first, then its "up to 1" target (as OP11-050); either mode may pick nothing.
- **EB02-054 Sanji**, `[Blocker] [On Play] If you have 2 or less Life cards, draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand.` Blocker in the override. The "If" has no colon: an `if` step on the Life count, read as the effect resolves; "2 or less" includes 0; the trash is mandatory and may take a card just drawn.
- **EB02-055 Jinbe**, `[Trigger] If your Leader has the "Fish-Man" or "Merfolk" type and you have 2 or less Life cards, play this card.` No effect text. A colon-less "If" is an effect condition (B3A-R1): the Trigger may be activated and does nothing when the condition is false. The Life count is read after the damaged card has left the Life area (so the card revealed by a hit is not counted: 3 Life before the hit is 2 after it). The Leader check is `any` of the two types.
- **EB02-056 Vegapunk**, `[Blocker][On Play] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; play up to 1 "Scientist" type Character card with a cost of 5 or less other than [Vegapunk]. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order and if your opponent has 2 or less Characters, trash 1 card from your hand. [Trigger] Draw 1 card.` G17 (`lookAtTop` to play), no `reveal` (the text says play). "Other than [Vegapunk]" excludes every card named [Vegapunk] (the printed Vegapunk with cost 2 also). The last clause is read as a second, independent effect-level "if" evaluated after the look and the placing (an empty opposing board counts as 2 or less); it does not depend on a Character having been played, and the trash is mandatory (an empty hand does nothing). The Life of the opponent plays no part.
- **EB02-059 Without Your Help I Can't Become the King of the Pirates!!!!**, `[Counter] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +1000 power during this battle. Then, if you have 1 or less Life cards, play up to 1 of your yellow "Straw Hat Crew" type Character cards or [Sanji] with a cost of 5 or less from your hand.` "Yellow" is read as qualifying only the {Straw Hat Crew} Characters: any Character named [Sanji] with a cost of 5 or less is playable whatever its color or type (a red or yellow [Sanji] in the tests). If "yellow" also applied to [Sanji] the red Sanji would be excluded: open: no ruling found, reading noted, not settled. "Then, if" is an effect condition (an `if` step) read after the power resolves, not "If you do": the play happens even when no power was given.
- **EB02-060 Merry Go (Yellow Stage)**, `[Activate: Main] You may rest this Stage and turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-up: Up to 1 of your "Straw Hat Crew" type Characters gains +1000 power until the end of your opponent's next turn.` Both costs (rest, and the top Life card turned face-up, `turnLifeFaceUp`) are paid together; not offered with no Life card. "Until the end of your opponent's next turn" is the plain duration.
- **EB02-061 Monkey.D.Luffy (Purple)**, `If your Leader is multicolored and your opponent has 5 or more DON!! cards on their field, this Character gains [Rush]. [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may return 2 of your active DON!! cards to your DON!! deck: Set this Character as active. Then, add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand.` A permanent grant of [Rush] with two conditions (the opponent's DON!! on the field counts the cost area plus DON!! given to their cards). The cost takes active cost-area DON!! only (G9; rested ones do not count, so it is not offered with fewer than 2 active). Once Per Turn is spent on use, so the re-attack by the now active Luffy offers nothing. "Then" is sequential: the Life card is added even though setting active cannot fail; with no Life nothing is added (the DON!! stay returned). The Life card is added to the hand, not revealed.

Engine bugs: none found. Observations: a cost change shows a floor of 0 (a -4 on a cost 3 Character reads 0, not -1).
