# OP-17 scripting, group 2 of 3: readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP17-025 to 034, 036, 037, 038, 041, 043, 047, 053, 057, 064, 066 (20 cards; 19 scripted, 1 left unscripted). Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. No new rulings were invented; where the text is silent the nearest reading on file is used.

- **OP17-025 Building Snake**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your [Shanks] Leader." The target is the Leader named [Shanks], a pick of exactly 1 (the engine still asks for it); the DON!! is taken from the rested ones. "Up to 1" is on the DON!! card, so with none rested nothing is given.
- **OP17-026 Fugar**, "[When Attacking] If your Leader has the {Red-Haired Pirates} type, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 2 or less." The Leader check is an `if` step, checked as the effect resolves.
- **OP17-027 Benn.Beckman**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Red-Haired Pirates} type, draw 1 card and rest up to 2 of your opponent's Characters." One sentence: both the draw and the rest are inside the `if`. [Rush: Character] is a printed keyword (override), so it can attack Characters the turn it is played, not the Leader.
- **OP17-029 Hongo**, "Set up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active. Then, rest up to 2 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 2 or less." "Then" is not "If you do" (m4b audit §7, the EB-05 review): the rest does not depend on a DON!! card having been set active. With rested DON!! cards all interchangeable, the engine sets one active without asking.
- **OP17-030 Monkey.D.Luffy**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If you have 5 or less cards in your hand, set up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active." The "If" opens the ability, so it is an activation condition (the EB04-007 reading): with 6 or more cards the ability cannot be activated and Once Per Turn is not spent. "[On Play] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards:" is an optional cost; with no active DON!! left after the card's own cost it cannot be paid.
- **OP17-031 Yasopp**, "[On Play] Draw 1 card and rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 8 or less." Both unconditional. "[End of Your Turn] Set up to 1 of your Characters with a type including \"Red-Haired Pirates\" as active": a substring match (CR 2-4-3-1); Yasopp himself is a candidate.
- **OP17-032, OP17-033, OP17-037** "reveal up to 1 card with a type including \"Red-Haired Pirates\"": `typeIncludes`, so a card with {Red-Haired Pirates Allies} qualifies (tested with OP17-026). A Look at the top cards always shows the choice, even with no card to take (an empty choice answered with "done"). The rest go to the bottom in the order picked (DECISIONS 2026-11-16).
- **OP17-033 Lucky.Roux**, "[On Your Opponent's Attack] You may trash this Character: Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Characters." One target in total over Leader and Characters (usual "Leader or Character" wording, block5 Q13). The attacker itself may be rested; the attack continues.
- **OP17-034 Rockstar**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If your opponent's Leader has 6000 power or more, set up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active. Then, your {Red-Haired Pirates} type Leader's base power becomes 6000 until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." The "If" opens the whole ability, so it is an activation condition and nothing needs a "Then" reading (block5 Q10 does not arise): the base-power step runs whenever the ability was activated, even with a Leader that is not {Red-Haired Pirates} (no target) or no rested DON!! to set active. The opponent's Leader power is current power, read at activation.
- **OP17-036 Withdraw Now and Allow Me to Save Face**, "[Main] You may rest 6 of your DON!! cards: Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters. Then, K.O. up to 2 of your opponent's rested Characters with a cost of 6 or less." An optional cost on an Event (DECISIONS B-1): the Event's own cost is paid first, so 7 active DON!! are needed. "Then" is not "If you do": the K.O. step runs even if nothing was rested, and a Character just rested by the first step is a legal K.O. target.
- **OP17-037, OP17-038** "You may rest 1 / 4 of your cards:": read as OP15-035 and EB05-019 do: the Leader, a Character, the Stage or an active DON!! card (only active cards can be rested). The Leader that then receives the power may be one of the rested cards. With fewer restable cards than the cost asks for, the cost cannot be paid (OP17-038 [Main] tested).
- **OP17-041 Wang Zhi**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Place all of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 1 at the bottom of the owner's deck in any order of the owner's choosing." No "up to": every match moves. The base-cost filter reads the printed cost. "In any order of the owner's choosing" is the order the cards are placed in (DECISIONS 2026-11-16, bottom placement); the cards are identical to the owner in practice, so no separate order decision is modelled. Rejected: asking the opponent for an order.
- **OP17-043 Ganzui, NOT SCRIPTED.** "If this Character would be removed from the field, you may trash 2 cards from your hand instead." The verified text has no "by your opponent's effect" qualifier (resolves block5 Q3 in favour of the unqualified reading), so it needs a `leaveField` replacement with any cause: gap **G14** of the unified list (B5-G21), wave 2. Left for after engine wave 2.
- **OP17-047 Shiki**, "[End of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less cards in your hand, your opponent places 1 card from their hand at the bottom of their deck." An `if` checked as the effect resolves; the opponent chooses from their own hand (chooser `opponent`); with an empty hand nothing happens. The opponent's draw for their next turn follows the effect.
- **OP17-053 Barbell**, "[On K.O.] Your opponent places 2 cards from their hand at the bottom of their deck in any order." No "up to", so the opponent places 2 (all they have when fewer); the order is the order picked. "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand:" is an activation cost (B-1): with an empty hand it cannot be activated.
- **OP17-057 Fullalead**, "Up to 1 of your Leader with a type including \"Rocks Pirates\" or up to 1 of your Characters with a type including \"Rocks Pirates\"" is one target in total (block5 Q13, as EB05-008). "You may rest this Stage and trash 1 card from your hand:" both parts form one cost: with an empty hand the prompt is shown but cannot be accepted.
- **OP17-064 King**, "[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand:" Once Per Turn is spent on activation; a second attack in the same turn is not offered it. The power goes to the Leader or any Character (King included).
- **OP17-066 Kurozumi Orochi**, "[On Play] DON!! -1: If you have a Character with a cost of 10 or more, draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand." The `if` follows the cost, so the DON!! is returned even when the condition fails (cost paid, no effect). "Draw 2 and trash 1" is one sentence inside the `if`; the trashed card may be one just drawn. The engine asks which DON!! (active or rested) to return.
