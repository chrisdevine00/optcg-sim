# OP-15 scripting, group 1 of 3: readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP15-004, 005, 006, 007, 009, 011, 013, 019, 020, 021, 024, 029, 033, 034, 036, 037, 039, 040, 041, 042, 043, 044, 045 (23 scripted, none left out). Every gap the audit listed for these cards (B4-G2 = G2 `cannotBeRested` for OP15-024 and OP15-029, B4-G17 = G1 own-Character costs for OP15-039 and OP15-041) is wave 1, merged. Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. No new rulings were invented.

- **OP15-004 Sea Cat**, "[On Play] If your Leader has 0 power or less, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -3000 power during this turn." An auto with a `leader: { power: lte 0 }` condition: the Leader's CURRENT power (given DON!! and timed modifiers count), checked at resolution. Tests plant the Leader's reduction with `applyOp` (no real Leader or scripted card here lowers its own Leader that far).
- **OP15-005 Cabaji**, "[When Attacking] If your opponent has any DON!! cards given, this Character gains +2000 power during this turn." `cmp donGiven(opponent) >= 1`: DON!! given to the opposing Leader or any Character; DON!! in the cost area do not count.
- **OP15-006 Cavendish**, "If you have 4 or more Events in your trash, this Character gains +2000 power." A permanent with a `count` of your trash Events (category Event only; the opponent's trash is not counted).
- **OP15-007 Gin**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {East Blue} type, play up to 1 Character card with a cost of 5 or less from your hand." Leading "If" on an [On Play] is an auto condition. The played Character may be of any type (the text names no type).
- **OP15-009 Koby**, "If your Character with 7000 base power or less would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may give your Leader -2000 power during this turn instead." A `leaveField` replacement with cause `opponentEffect`, as OP15-035 Laboon (same wording): Koby may protect himself, base power is read (a boosted 8000 Character with 7000 base is covered), every removal by the opponent's effect (K.O., bounce, trash) is replaced, a battle K.O. and your own effects are not. The -2000 is applied even when the Leader's power goes to 0 or below.
- **OP15-011 Pearl**, "[Opponent's Turn] If your Leader has the {East Blue} type, this Character gains [Blocker] and +2000 power. [On K.O.] If your Leader has the {East Blue} type, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 6000 base power or less." The permanent carries two conditions (opponent's turn, Leader type), granting [Blocker] and +2000. The [On K.O.] reads the Leader type at resolution and 6000 BASE power.
- **OP15-013 Pincers**, "If your Leader has 0 power or less, give this card in your hand -2 cost. [Blocker]". A `zone: 'hand'` permanent as OP16-005 Thatch; [Blocker] is the printed keyword.
- **OP15-019 Barrier Bulls**, "[Main] Draw 1 card and your Leader gains +1000 power until the end of your opponent's next End Phase. [Trigger] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -4000 power during this turn." Draw 1 is mandatory; duration `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` (as OP16-009 Speed Jil).
- **OP15-020 Fire Fist**, "[Main] Your Leader gains +3000 power during this turn and give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -8000 power until the end of your opponent's next End Phase. Then, you may trash 2 cards from your hand. If you do, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 0 power or less." The "Then, you may trash 2 cards ... If you do" is a `may` step with `ifDid` (not a cost), guarded by "hand has at least 2 cards" (the OP16-035 pattern), so a hand of 0 or 1 never reaches the K.O. The K.O. target is chosen after the -8000 and reads CURRENT power: a Character left at exactly 0 qualifies, one left at 1000 does not, and a Character already at 0 or below (another one, or the same one) qualifies. A "no legal target" pick is never offered.
- **OP15-021 Just Watch Me, Ace!!!**, "If you have 4 or more Events in your trash, give this card in your hand -3 cost. [Main]/[Counter] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -3000 power during this turn." Two abilities: a hand permanent (-3 cost) and one `activate` with `when: ['main', 'counter']` (one ability id `main` for both timings).
- **OP15-024 Usopp**, "[Opponent's Turn] This Character cannot be rested by your opponent's Leader and Character effects and gains [Blocker]. [On K.O.] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Character cards with a cost of 7 or less." Two readings for review. (1) "by your opponent's Leader and Character effects" is scripted as the engine's `cannotBeRested: { by: 'opponentEffect' }`: any effect of the opponent, so an opposing Event or Stage effect would also be stopped; the engine does not tell the source card type apart (the M6b-Q30 family). Blocking, attacking and costs are not "effects" so they still work. (2) "Leader or Character cards with a cost of 7 or less": a Leader has no cost, so the `cost` filter never matches the opposing Leader and only Characters can be rested. If the Leader is meant to be eligible whatever the cost (the qualifier binding to Character only), the script needs `any: [category Leader, cost lte 7]`. Open.
- **OP15-029 Bartholomew Kuma**, "[On Play] Up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less cannot be rested until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." A timed unscoped `cannotBeRested` (G2): the Character cannot attack, activate [Blocker] or be rested by any effect or cost until that End Phase ends (rules-digest 57).
- **OP15-033 Hody Jones**, "[On Play] Set your {Fish-Man} type Leader as active. Then, add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand." Two steps, no `ifDid`: "Then" is not "If you do", so the Life card is added whether or not the Leader was set (an active or non-{Fish-Man} Leader does not stop it). Mandatory (no "up to"); with 0 Life nothing is added. The Life card goes to the hand as an effect (no [Trigger]).
- **OP15-034 Yorki**, "[Your Turn] [On Play] Up to 1 of your [Brook] cards gains +2000 power during this turn." The [Your Turn] is an auto condition. "[Brook] cards" is read as the Leader or Characters named [Brook] (the Leader OP15-022 is one).
- **OP15-036 Ryuma**, "[On Play]/[When Attacking] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with a cost of 4 or less." One auto on two timings (`when: ['onPlay', 'whenAttacking']`).
- **OP15-037 The Outcome Will Tell Us Who's Strong and Who's Weak**, "[Main] Look at 5 cards ...; reveal up to 1 {East Blue} type card other than [The Outcome ...] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Trigger] Draw 1 card." `lookAtTop` 5, take up to 1 with an `all` filter of type and `not name`; the rest `bottomAnyOrder`. The engine asks the (empty) take choice even with no candidate; the Event itself is never in the deck here when played from hand (the test plants a copy in the deck to pin the name exclusion).
- **OP15-039 Rebecca (Leader)**, "This Leader cannot attack. [Activate: Main] You may rest this Leader and return 1 of your {Dressrosa} type Characters to the owner's hand: Play up to 1 {Dressrosa} type Character card with a cost of 3 from your hand." A permanent `cannotAttack` on self; an `activate` with the costs `restSelf` and a G1 `move` to hand (a single {Dressrosa} Character is returned automatically, several are chosen). No `costOptional` (an Activate on a non-Event; the optional-cost check forbids it). The returned Character is in hand when the play is chosen, so a cost-3 {Dressrosa} Character may replace itself. The play is "up to 1" and free.
- **OP15-040 Viola / OP15-044 Koala**, "Look at 3 cards ...; reveal up to 1 {Dressrosa} type card (Event) and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." As OP15-037 (the owner orders the rest). Koala's filter is `type Dressrosa` plus `category Event`; a {Dressrosa} Character is not offered.
- **OP15-041 Orlumbus**, "[On K.O.] Draw 1 card. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may place 1 of your Characters at the bottom of the owner's deck: This Character gains [Rush] during this turn." The cost is a G1 `move` from `characters` to the deck bottom (exactly 1), and it may be Orlumbus himself (the [Rush] then has no target). A rested Orlumbus can still pay.
- **OP15-042 Kyros**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: If your Leader is [Rebecca], this Character gains [Rush] during this turn. [On K.O.] Add this Character card from your trash to your hand." The "If" is after the colon, so it is an `if` step: the card is trashed even with a non-[Rebecca] Leader (the engine offers the optional with accept illegal on an empty hand). [On K.O.] is a mandatory `move` of the card itself from the trash to the hand (`isSelf` filter on `trash`: a `move` from `self` is not implemented). "Leader is [Rebecca]" is the exact name.
- **OP15-043 Kelly Funk**, "[On Play] Play up to 1 [Bobby Funk] from your hand." Name filter, "up to 1", free play. The empty take choice is asked even with no [Bobby Funk] in hand.
- **OP15-045 Sai**, "[Blocker] [On Play] You may trash 1 Event from your hand: Draw 2 cards." Optional cost with an Event filter; [Blocker] from the override. With no Event in hand the engine still shows the optional with accept illegal.

## Engine notes (no bug found)

- A single-candidate "exactly 1" cost choice (OP15-039, OP15-041 with one Character) is made without asking.
- An "up to" take with zero candidates still asks an empty choice (min 0, max 0): the same on lookAtTop and play. Cosmetic.
