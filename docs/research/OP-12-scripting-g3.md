# OP-12 scripting, group 3 of 3: readings and ambiguities

Cards scripted (24, all `verified: true`): OP12-078, 079, 084, 085, 086, 087, 089, 090, 091, 093, 094, 095, 096, 097, 098, 100, 102, 104, 105, 106, 107, 116, 118, 119. Left unscripted: OP12-081 Koala (Leader), see below. No card needed a wave 5+ gap or a design item. Gap map (audit `block3.md` to unified ids): B3-G1 = G4 `mill` (OP12-084, 096, 098 wave 1), B3-G4 = G11 `ko` replacement with a cause and G14 `leaveField` (OP12-102 wave 2), B3-G45 = G25 `cardPlayed` `via` (OP12-081, wave 3). Scripts in `cards/effects/OP-12/`, tests in `engine/test/cards/OP12-*.test.ts` (helpers in `helpers-op12g3.ts`). One line per reading; "no ruling" means the reading is the scripter's.

- **OP12-081 Koala (Leader), NOT SCRIPTED, engine gap**: "When this Leader attacks your opponent's Leader, if you have 2 or more Characters with a cost of 8 or more, draw 1 card." The `attack` timing takes `attacker` and `target` selectors, but `attackMatch` (`flow.ts` ~1373) reads `target` only for the defending side and `attacker` only for the attacking side, so a script that names both (`attacker` = own Leader, `target` = opponent Leader) draws when the Leader attacks an opponent's Character too. Failing case: scripted as `when: [{ event: 'attack', attacker: { from: 'leader', owner: 'you' }, target: { from: 'leader', owner: 'opponent' } }]`, with 2 cost-8 Characters, Koala attacking a rested opponent Character still draws (the repro script and its 9 tests, the one that fails being "attacking one of the opponent's Characters does not trigger the draw", are kept outside the repo; the second ability, "[Once Per Turn] when your opponent plays a Character with a base cost of 8 or more, or plays a Character using a Character's effect: your opponent adds 1 top Life card to their hand", scripted with `via: 'characterEffect'` passed all its tests). Fix needed: an `attack` timing whose `attacker` AND `target` must both match when both are given (or a `battleTarget` condition). Not worth a half-script: the card stays stats-only.
- **"Then" inside the "If" (OP12-078, 118)**: OP12-078 "If the number of DON!! ... equal to or less than ..., draw 1 card. Then, give up to 1 ... -3000" and OP12-118 "If you have 8 or more rested cards, draw 2 ... and trash 1. Then, set up to 1 of your DON!! as active" put the "Then" clause inside the guard (M4b-5): when the condition fails, nothing resolves (tested).
- **OP12-078 Brochette Blow**, "the number of DON!! cards on your field is equal to or less than ... your opponent's": cost area (active and rested) plus DON!! given, for both players (rules-digest); the Event's own rested DON!! still count. Read when the Event resolves, after the cost.
- **OP12-079**, "If your Leader is [Sanji]": the exact Leader name. "add up to 1 card" is not a reveal. The rest go to the bottom in any order.
- **OP12-084 / 096 / 098 "trash N cards from the top of your deck"**: `mill`, trashed one at a time in deck order; a deck shorter than N trashes what is there.
- **OP12-085 Karasu, 087 Nico Robin, "your opponent has 5 or more cards in their hand"**: gated on the hand size, a public fact, so the hidden-gate rule holds; the opponent chooses what to trash. OP12-087's "If" follows the colon, so the cost (1 card from your hand) is paid first and the check is read afterwards (declining is the only way out; tested).
- **OP12-085/089/093/095 "+3 / +4 cost"**: a permanent stage on the card's own cost, live with the Leader's type, so effects that read `baseCost` (OP12-089's K.O. filter) are unaffected and effects that read `cost` see 8.
- **OP12-086 Koala**, "reveal up to 1 "Revolutionary Army" type card other than [Koala] or up to 1 [Nico Robin]": one pick of up to 1 card that is either; [Nico Robin] of any type qualifies. Reveals, then trashes the rest.
- **OP12-087 Nico Robin**, "If your Leader is [Koala] or [Monkey.D.Luffy]": the Leader's exact name, either one. (Test note: ST01-001 is named [Monkey.D.Luffy], so it qualifies; the "no effect" case uses another Leader.)
- **OP12-090 Belo Betty**, "You may trash 2 cards from the top of your deck:": a `mill` cost, optional by construction; unpayable with a deck of fewer than 2 cards (not offered). "-2 cost" reads 0 at the floor (the view floors at 0; no ruling).
- **OP12-091 Poker**, "place 3 cards from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order": a cost that moves exactly 3 chosen cards (an order is asked), payable only with 3 or more in the trash. "Up to 2 of your SMILE type Characters" includes Poker herself.
- **OP12-094 Dragon**, "You may place 3 "Revolutionary Army" type cards ... If your Leader has the type, play up to 1 Character with a cost of 6 or less from your trash": the cost is paid whatever the Leader; the "If" is read after it, so the cards paid cannot be the one played. Cost 6 is inclusive, by printed cost in the trash. With exactly 3 qualifying cards the cost has nothing to choose and goes straight to the order.
- **OP12-096 Ursa Shock / M6b-Q8**: "you may select ... cost 6 or less instead" is scripted as a condition (a Character of cost 8 or more: K.O. cost 6 or less, otherwise cost 4 or less), the same as the superset reading; "a cost of 8 or more" is current cost (Karasu at 5 +3 qualifies). The [Trigger] "Draw 1 card and trash 1 card from the top of your deck" is two plain steps (draw, mill 1).
- **OP12-098 Hair Removal Fist**, "that card gains an additional +2000": the card chosen for the first boost (bound), so choosing nothing gives no second boost; the cost-8 Revolutionary Army Character check is current cost, read after the first boost. Same shape as EB03-020.
- **OP12-100 Sabo**, "You may add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand: Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand": a `lifeToHand` cost (not damage, no [Trigger]); with no Life the cost is not offered. The permanent "3 or less Life" is live.
- **OP12-102 Shirahoshi**, "If your Character with a base cost of 6 or less would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-up instead": a `leaveField` replacement (opponent's effect, any removal: K.O., return, trash). "Your Character" includes Shirahoshi herself. The payload is the `turnLifeFaceUp` cost, which the engine can pay only when the top Life card is face-down: with a face-up top card the removal goes through (turning a card already face-up is not an action, CR 1-3-2-1; no ruling, M6b-Q5 neighbour). Not once per turn. "[Opponent's Turn] If you have no other [Shirahoshi] with a base cost of 2, all of your "Neptunian" type Characters gain +2000": `none` over your Characters named [Shirahoshi] with base cost 2 other than this card (an opposing one does not count); the bonus covers Shirahoshi too if she were Neptunian (she is Merfolk).
- **OP12-104 Sentomaru**, "[Trigger] K.O. up to 1 ... cost of 4 or less": current cost; no effect text. A trigger-only script.
- **OP12-105 Trafalgar Lammy**, "[Your Turn] [On Play] Up to 1 of your [Trafalgar Law] cards gains +2000 power": "cards" read as the Leader and Characters on your field (the cards that have power), exact name; the Leader counts when it is [Trafalgar Law]. `yourTurn` condition.
- **OP12-107 Donquixote Doflamingo**, "[Opponent's Turn] [On K.O.] Add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards": "up to 1" of a single known card is asked as a yes/no (the engine's `optional`); the Leader condition `opponentsTurn` is read when it resolves. [Rush] at 2 or less Life is live (tested by playing and attacking).
- **OP12-116**, "reveal a total of up to 2 "Shandian Warrior" type Character cards or [Mont Blanc Noland]": one pick of up to 2 cards in any mix; [Mont Blanc Noland] by exact name (any category); Shandian Warrior Events are out. The rest go to the bottom in any order.
- **OP12-118 Jewelry Bonney / M6b-Q25**, "If you have 8 or more rested cards": rested Leader, Characters and Stage plus rested DON!! in the cost area (the audit reading; Q25 is open, and DON!! given to cards are not counted). Bonney herself enters active. "Set up to 1 of your DON!!" asks a count (1 or 0, most first).
- **OP12-119 Bartholomew Kuma**, "You may trash 1 card from your hand: Add up to 1 ... Then, this Character gains +2 cost until the end of your opponent's next End Phase": "Then" is not "If you do": once the hand card is trashed the +2 cost applies even if the Life card is declined or the deck is empty (tested). Plain `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` duration (the engine knows it is the opponent's next End Phase).
