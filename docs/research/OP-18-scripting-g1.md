# OP-18 scripting: group g1 readings

Cards OP18-001, -022 (Leaders), then -002, -004 to -019, -023, -024, -025, -028. Each text was compared with the official OP-18 series page (`?series=569118`, read by hand 2026-11-27) and the override cites it (`verified: true`). About 5 author tests per card in `engine/test/cards/<ID>.test.ts`; scenery in `helpers-op18-g1.ts`. "Qn" refers to `docs/research/m6b-audit/OP-18.md` section 4: the proposed reading was followed.

- **OP18-001 Karoo (Leader).** All of your Characters with both the {Animal} and {Alabasta} types gain [Rush] and +1000 power. [Once Per Turn] When your {Alabasta} type Character is K.O.'d, draw 1 card. `permanent` grant Rush + power on Characters with both types (the Leader itself is not a Character); `auto` `koed` (own Alabasta Character, any cause) OPT draw 1.
- **OP18-002 Igaram.** [On Play] You may trash 1 {Alabasta} type card from your hand: Draw 2 cards. `auto` onPlay, trash cost from hand, draw 2. The prompt is not gated on the hidden hand (accepting is illegal when no {Alabasta} card is held).
- **OP18-004 Karoo (Q14).** Apply each of the following effects based on this Character's power: 5000+ [Rush]; 6000+ [Banish]; 7000+ [Unblockable]. Three `permanent` abilities with `self power gte`, cumulative; power is the current power including given DON!! and other effects (the Leader OP18-001's +1000 counts).
- **OP18-005 Kung Fu Jugon (Q1).** [Your Turn] +1000 power for every [Kung Fu Jugon]. `permanent` `yourTurn`, `mul count 1000` over your own Characters named [Kung Fu Jugon], this one included.
- **OP18-006 Koza (Q2, Q12).** If you have [Nefeltari Vivi], this Character gains [Rush] and +1000 power. `permanent` with `exists` over your Leader and Characters named [Nefeltari Vivi] (a Vivi Leader counts). The reminder text after the effect is not an effect.
- **OP18-007 Smoker.** If your Leader has the {Navy} type, gains [Banish] and +1000 power. `permanent` `leader type Navy`.
- **OP18-008 Chaka.** If your Leader has the {Alabasta} type, gains [Rush]. [On Play] You may trash 1 Character card with 5000 power or less from your hand: Draw 2 cards and this Character gains [Banish] during this turn. `permanent` + `auto` onPlay with trash cost; the power is the card's (printed) power in hand.
- **OP18-009 Super Spot-Billed Duck Troops.** [Rush]; [Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: If you have 9 or more DON!! cards on your field, play up to 1 [Karoo] from your hand. Keyword `Rush`; `activate` with `trashSelf` cost; the condition has no colon before it, so it is an effect condition (the cost is paid even with fewer than 9 DON!!). DON!! on the field = cost area (active and rested) plus given.
- **OP18-010 Tony Tony.Chopper.** [On K.O.] Add up to 1 Character card with 4000 power or less from your trash to your hand. `auto` onKO `move` trash to hand.
- **OP18-011 Nefeltari Vivi.** All of your Characters with both the {Animal} and {Alabasta} types gain +1000 power. [Opponent's Turn] This Character gains [Blocker] and +6000 power. Two `permanent` abilities; Vivi herself is only {Alabasta}, so the first does not touch her.
- **OP18-012 Pincers.** [When Attacking] Up to 1 of your Characters with both the {Animal} and {Alabasta} types gains +1000 power during this turn. `auto` whenAttacking, `power` thisTurn (Pincers may choose itself).
- **OP18-013 Pell.** If your Leader has the {Alabasta} type, gains [Rush]. [On Play] You may trash 1 Character card with 5000 power or less from your hand: Draw 2 cards and trash up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 3000 base power or less. `permanent` + `auto` onPlay; the opponent Character is moved to the trash (not K.O.'d, as ST19-003), filter `basePower`.
- **OP18-014 Portgas.D.Ace (Q12).** [When Attacking] Up to 1 of your {Alabasta} type Characters gains [Rush] during this turn. `auto` whenAttacking `grant Rush thisTurn`. Ace may choose himself.
- **OP18-015 Eyelashes.** [On Your Opponent's Attack] You may trash this Character: If your Leader has the {Alabasta} type, give up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Characters -3000 power during this turn. `auto` onOpponentAttack, `trashSelf` cost, `if leader type Alabasta` in the effect. Ruling needed (low risk): "If" follows the colon, so it is an effect condition and the cost is paid even when the Leader is not {Alabasta}.
- **OP18-016 Monkey.D.Luffy.** [Banish]; [On K.O.] Play up to 1 {Alabasta} type Character card with 6000 power or less from your hand. Keyword `Banish`; `auto` onKO `play`.
- **OP18-017 Roronoa Zoro (Q4).** [Once Per Turn] If your {Alabasta} type Character would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may give your Leader -2000 power during this turn instead. [When Attacking] Up to 1 of your {Alabasta} type Characters gains [Rush] during this turn. `replacement` OPT as OP13-017 (`leaveField`, `opponentEffect`); no "other than this Character", so Zoro covers himself. The -2000 is offered even when the Leader is already low.
- **OP18-018 Let's Go, Karoo!!** [Main] All of your {Alabasta} type Characters gain +1000 power during this turn. [Trigger] Up to 1 of your Leader or Characters gains +2000 power during this turn. `activate` main + `trigger`. The audit table listed the +2000 under [Main]; the card text has it only as the [Trigger] (the script follows the card text).
- **OP18-019 Quack!!!** [Main] If you have no Characters with 7000 base power or more, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -3000 power during this turn. [Counter] Your Leader gains +3000 power during this battle. `activate` main with `none` Characters of basePower >= 7000 (base power, so given DON!! does not count); `activate` counter, `power` battle.
- **OP18-022 Monkey.D.Luffy (Leader, Q15).** [DON!! x3] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may set this Leader as active. If you do, this Leader will not become active in your next Refresh Phase. `auto` whenAttacking, `donGiven 3`, OPT, `may setActive` then `ifDid skipRefresh` on the Leader. Tested over two Refresh Phases.
- **OP18-023 Gaimon.** [On Play] Up to 1 of your {East Blue} type Leader or Characters gains [Banish] during this turn. `auto` onPlay `grant Banish thisTurn` over Leader and Characters.
- **OP18-024 Kokoro.** [Blocker]; [End of Your Turn] You may trash this Character: Set your Leader as active. Keyword `Blocker`; `auto` endOfYourTurn with `trashSelf` cost, `setActive` Leader.
- **OP18-025 Gonbe.** [End of Your Turn] If this Character is rested, set your Leader as active. `auto` endOfYourTurn, `if self rested`.
- **OP18-028 Chimney.** [Activate: Main] You may rest 1 of your [Gonbe] and this Character: Set your [Monkey.D.Luffy] Leader as active. `activate` with `rest` (Gonbe, exactly 1) and `restSelf` costs; `setActive` Leader named [Monkey.D.Luffy]; a Leader with another name is not changed but the cost is paid.

Not scripted: none.
