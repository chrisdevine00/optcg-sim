# OP-13 scripting, group 1 of 4: readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP13-005, 006, 007, 008, 009, 012, 013, 014, 015, 016, 017, 019, 020, 021, 022, 023, 024, 026, 028, 030, 031 (21 scripted, none left out). Every gap the audit listed for these cards is merged: B4-G18 (OP13-007) = G7 cost `giveDon`, B4-G15 (OP13-008) = G11 `ko` replacement with a cause, B4-G17 (OP13-031) = G1 own-Character costs; all waves 1 and 2. No card needs a design item D1-D9 or `[DON!! xN] [On K.O.]`. Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. No new rulings were invented.

- **OP13-005 Inazuma**, "[On Play] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader." `giveDon` n 1 rested to the Leader with `pick: { upTo: 1 }`: the player is asked (min 0, max 1) so "up to" is honoured, the 4 DON!! that paid for Inazuma are the rested ones.
- **OP13-006 Woop Slap**, "[On Play] Give up to 2 rested DON!! cards to 1 of your [Monkey.D.Luffy] cards." A count (2, 1 or None) is asked first, then the target (exactly 1; Leader or Character named [Monkey.D.Luffy]); all the DON!! go to one card. With no such card nothing is asked.
- **OP13-007 Ace & Sabo & Luffy**, "[Activate: Main] You may give 1 of your active DON!! cards to 1 of your Leader or Character cards and trash this Character: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -3000 power during this turn." A `giveDon` cost (active, target exactly 1 of Leader or Characters) plus `trashSelf`; no `costOptional` (an Activate). With no opposing Character the cost is still paid. The DON!! may go to any of your Leader or Characters, this Character included (it is then trashed with it).
- **OP13-008 Emporio.Ivankov**, "If your "Revolutionary Army" type Character would be K.O.'d by your opponent's effect, you may trash this Character instead." A `ko` replacement with `cause: 'opponentEffect'`, `instead: trashSelf`. Ivankov is himself a Revolutionary Army Character, so he may trash himself instead of being K.O.'d (pointless but legal; read as the text, "your Character" includes him). A bounce or trash by the opponent is not a K.O. and is not replaced; battle K.O.s and your own effects are not covered.
- **OP13-009 Curly.Dadan**, "If you have a "Mountain Bandits" type Character other than this card, this Character gains [Double Attack]." A permanent `exists` condition on Characters with the type and `isSelf: false` (a second copy of Dadan counts for both). Only your own field counts, rested or not. [Double Attack] is conditional so the override's printed keywords stay empty.
- **OP13-012 Nefeltari Vivi**, "[On Play] Look at 4 cards ...; reveal up to 1 "Alabasta" or "Straw Hat Crew" type card with a cost of 2 or more and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." `lookAtTop` 4, filter `any` of the two exact types plus `cost gte 2`, the owner orders the rest. Cost, not base cost. A cost 1 [Sanji] ({Alabasta}{Straw Hat Crew}) is not offered.
- **OP13-013 Higuma**, "[On Play] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 0 power or less." `power lte 0` on CURRENT power (a boosted 0 base is not eligible; a Character lowered to 0 or below is). "Up to 1": a no-target pick is not offered.
- **OP13-014 Portgas.D.Rouge**, "[Trigger] Up to 1 of your [Portgas.D.Ace] cards gains +3000 power during this turn." A trigger; "[Portgas.D.Ace] cards" is read as the Leader or Characters with that exact name (as OP15-034 Yorki reads [Brook]).
- **OP13-015 Makino**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: Up to 1 of your [Monkey.D.Luffy] cards gains +2000 power during this turn." `restSelf` cost, Leader or Character named [Monkey.D.Luffy]; usable the turn she is played.
- **OP13-016 Monkey.D.Garp**, "[On Play] If your Leader is [Sabo], [Portgas.D.Ace] or [Monkey.D.Luffy], look at 4 cards ...; reveal up to 1 card with a cost of 3 or more ..." A leading "If" on an [On Play] is an auto condition on the Leader NAME (any of three exact names; a same-type Leader with another name does not count). Cost, not base cost. The condition is public, so it is not a hidden-zone gate.
- **OP13-017 Monkey.D.Dragon**, "[Once Per Turn] If your {Revolutionary Army} type Character would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may give this Character -2000 power during this turn instead." A `leaveField` replacement with cause `opponentEffect` (any removal by an opposing effect: K.O., bounce, trash), `oncePerTurn`. The effect text in the data is the Limitless text: the upstream feed dropped the minus sign and quoted the type (M6b-Q2; the override records it). Dragon may protect himself. The -2000 applies to Dragon even when his power drops to 0 or below. Once Per Turn is spent when the replacement is used (the test pins that a second removal in the same turn is not offered).
- **OP13-019 But Ace Here Said You Deserved It!!**, "[Main] You may rest 4 of your DON!! cards: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -3000 power during this turn. Then, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 3000 power or less. [Counter] Your Leader gains +3000 power during this battle." Two abilities. The [Main] is an Event with an optional cost (`restDon: 4`, `costOptional`): the 4 are on top of the Event's own cost, and with fewer than 4 active DON!! left the offer is shown with accept illegal (decline only). "Then" is not "If you do": the K.O. half runs even when the -3000 was skipped. The K.O. reads CURRENT power after the -3000 (a 6000 Character lowered to 3000 qualifies, 4000 does not).
- **OP13-020 Meteor Fist**, "[Main] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -5000 power during this turn. [Trigger] Activate this card's [Main] effect." The trigger is `activate: main of self`. Power may go below 0.
- **OP13-021 Gum-Gum Gatling Gun**, "[Main] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to 1 of your [Monkey.D.Luffy] cards. Then, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -2000 power during this turn. [Trigger] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -2000 power during this turn." The DON!! step has a target `pick: { upTo: 1 }` so "up to 1" can be declined (n 1 asks no count); "Then" is not "If you do", so the -2000 runs with no Luffy card or no DON!! given. The Event's own 1 cost leaves 1 rested DON!! to give.
- **OP13-022 Windmill Village**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Stage: Up to 1 of your Characters with 2000 base power or less gains +1000 power during this turn." `restSelf`, `basePower lte 2000` (base, not current), Characters only (no Leader).
- **OP13-023 Uta**, "[On Play] Set up to 2 of your DON!! cards as active. Then, you cannot play Character cards with a base cost of 5 or more during this turn. [On K.O.] Play up to 1 Character card with a cost of 5 or less from your hand rested." The restriction is `cannotPlay` of Characters with `baseCost gte 5` on the player for this turn (it also bars effect plays, as OP13-118). The [On K.O.] uses `cost lte 5` (not base cost), so the restriction can still bar a base cost 5 pick on the same turn (a reading, pinned in the test).
- **OP13-024 Gordon**, "[On Play] You may reveal 1 "Music" or "FILM" type card from your hand: Set up to 2 of your DON!! cards as active at the end of this turn." An auto with a `reveal` cost (exactly 1, type Music or FILM; the card stays in hand) and a `delayed: endOfThisTurn` set-active step. With no such card the offer is shown with accept illegal. The cost is a prompt on a public fact (the offer is always shown), so it passes the hidden-gate check.
- **OP13-026 Sunny-Kun**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards: This Character gains +2000 power until the end of your opponent's next turn." `restDon: 1` cost, `oncePerTurn`, timed `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`.
- **OP13-028 Shanks**, "[On Play] Set all of your DON!! cards as active. Then, you cannot play cards from your hand during this turn." Set active with `pick: { any: true }`. M6b-Q33: `cannotPlay` has no hand-only scope, so it is scripted as `cannotPlay` of every category (Character, Event, Stage) for this turn: cards played from hand are barred (what the text says) but so are plays by an effect from the trash or deck (not barred by the printed text). Open; no ruling invented.
- **OP13-030 Tony Tony.Chopper**, "[On Play] Set up to 2 of your DON!! cards as active." As OP13-027 Sanji: `setActive` from the cost area, up to 2.
- **OP13-031 Trafalgar Law**, "If you have 1 or less Life cards, this Character gains [Blocker]. [On Play] You may return 1 of your Characters to the owner's hand: Play up to 1 Character card with a cost of 5 or less from your hand rested." A permanent `life <= 1` grant (0 Life counts) and an auto with a G1 `move` cost (exactly 1 of your Characters to hand; Law himself is a legal pick). The returned Character is in hand when the play is chosen, so it may be played again. The play is "up to 1".

## Engine notes (no bug found)

- A `giveDon` whose `to` has no `pick` is asked as an "up to 1" choice (min 0, max 1); a single legal target is not auto-chosen, so OP13-005 uses an explicit `upTo: 1` and asks.
- An unpayable optional cost (OP13-019, OP13-024) is offered with accept illegal rather than skipped.
