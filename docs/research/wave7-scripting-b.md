# Wave 7 cards, group 2 of 2: scripting readings

Ten cards unblocked by engine wave 7 ([m6b-wave7.md](m6b-wave7.md)), scripted from the verified text in `cards/data` merged with `cards/overrides`: OP12-036, OP12-117, OP13-064, OP13-105, OP14-053, OP14-060, OP15-093, OP16-080, ST15-001 and ST20-003. Each reading is one line with the card text it settles; an open question is named, none is settled here.

- **OP12-036 Roronoa Zoro** (G63, G46; M6b-Q80, M6b-Q68). "This card in your hand cannot be played by effects." is a `zone: 'hand'` permanent: while in the hand an effect of either player cannot play it, a normal play is legal, and from the trash an effect may play it.
- **OP12-036** "If your Leader has the <Slash> attribute, this Character cannot be K.O.'d in battle by <Slash> attribute cards and gains +1000 power." is one permanent with the Leader's `attribute` filter as its condition, so both the protection and the power need the attribute; the attacker's printed attribute is read (a Leader or a Character); effect K.O.s and other attributes still K.O. it.
- **OP12-117 Slam Gibson** (G61; M6b-Q78). "[Main] You may rest 5 of your DON!! cards: If your Leader has the "Supernovas" type, add up to 1 Character with a cost of 9 or less to the top or bottom of the owner's Life cards face-down." The "If" follows the cost colon, so it is an `if` step: with the cost paid and a non-{Supernovas} Leader the 5 DON!! are rested and nothing moves. The cost is optional (an Event whose cost is worded "you may": asked even when it cannot be paid, with accept unavailable).
- **OP12-117** the type is the exact element (optcgapi prints the official {Supernovas} as "Supernovas"); "a cost of 9 or less" is the current cost of either player's Character; the card goes to its owner's Life, the controller choosing the end.
- **OP13-064 Gol.D.Roger** (G66; M6b-Q83). "Your Leader and all of your Characters that do not have a type including "Roger Pirates" have their effects negated." The type clause is read as covering the Leader as well as the Characters, so a {Roger Pirates} Leader (OP13-003) and a {Former Roger Pirates} one (OP12-001) are not negated, nor a Character whose type merely includes the words (substring, printed list). The other reading (the Leader always negated) would make the card unplayable in the Roger Leader deck it names; it is a one-line change to the selector if the official ruling says otherwise.
- **OP13-064** "[On Play] DON!! −3: Your Leader gains +2000 power until the end of your opponent's next End Phase. Then, give all of your opponent's Characters −2000 power until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." The cost is the optional DON!! −3; "Then" is plain sequence (both run; the first cannot fail); the −2000 reaches the Characters present when it resolves. The negated Leader still receives the power (it comes from Roger's own effect, which is never negated by itself).
- **OP13-105 Kouzuki Momonosuke** (G64; M6b-Q81). "[On Play] Look at all of your Life cards and place them back in your Life area in any order." The cards stay in Life with their face state; no Life card is "removed"; only you see them and remember the order; nothing happens with no Life.
- **OP14-053 Vista** (G67; M6b-Q84). "[Opponent's Turn] If you have 7 or less cards in your hand, this Character's base power becomes the same as your Leader's base power." A permanent `setBasePower` with `{ statOf: leader, stat: 'basePower' }`; the Leader's base power is its printed power (an effect that raises the Leader does not carry over); at 8 cards it falls back to 4000; never in your own turn. [Blocker] is printed.
- **OP14-060 Donquixote Doflamingo** (D3; M6b-Q85, QA-OP12 OP12-020, QA-OP16 OP16-080). "[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] DON!! −1: Select your Leader or 1 of your {Donquixote Pirates} type Characters. Change the attack target to the selected card." An auto with a cost (optional by construction); the selection is made on resolution, after the DON!! is returned; active or rested, and the card already attacked may be chosen; a Character attack redirected to the Leader is an attack on a Leader; the timings are not read again. With no DON!! on the field it is not asked.
- **OP15-093 The Risky Brothers** (G59; M6b-Q76). "[Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: If you have 15 or more cards in your trash, up to 1 of your [Monkey.D.Luffy] Characters gains [Rush: Character] and the "Slash" attribute during this turn." The "If" follows the cost colon (an `if` step): the cost is paid and the Brothers go to the trash first, then the trash is counted, so 14 other cards are enough and 13 are not. One `grant` gives the keyword and the attribute to the same chosen card; "up to 1" may be declined.
- **OP16-080 Marshall.D.Teach** (D3; M6b-Q85). "[Opponent's Turn] All of your Characters gain +1 cost." is a permanent with the `opponentsTurn` condition. "[On your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card with a [Trigger] from your hand: Change the target of that attack to this Leader or to one of your {Blackbeard Pirates} type Character cards." The cost is a hand trash with `hasTrigger: true`. The question is asked even with no [Trigger] card in hand (accept unavailable), so the opponent learns nothing about the hand (ADR 0005). The target is "this Leader or one of your {Blackbeard Pirates} Characters", chosen on resolution; an attack on a Character may be redirected to the Leader.
- **ST15-001 Atmos** (G60; M6b-Q77, M6b-Q31). "[When Attacking] If your Leader is [Edward.Newgate], you cannot add Life cards to your hand using your own effects during this turn." The "If" has no colon, so it is an effect condition (`if` step: the ability fires and nothing happens with another Leader); the restriction is the player's, lasts the turn, bars your own effects taking your own Life into your hand and not an effect that takes the opponent's Life.
- **ST20-003 Charlotte Brulee** (G64, G57; M6b-Q81). "[Trigger] Look at up to 1 card from the top of your or your opponent's Life cards, and place it at the top or bottom of the Life cards. Then, add this card to your hand." The look is "up to 1" (choose whose Life, or none; the end is asked when the Life has more than one card); "Then, add this card to your hand" does not depend on the look, so the card goes to the hand even when none is looked at (as OP12-109). Declining the [Trigger] adds it to the hand by the damage rule.

## Not scripted

None: all ten cards were `verified: true` and needed only waves 5, 6 and 7 gaps (G46, G57, G59, G60, G61, G63, G64, G66, G67, D3).

## Tests that used one of these cards as scenery

OP14-060 and OP16-080 are Leaders that earlier tests used as inert scenery (their text only matters on an opponent's attack, or as a {Blackbeard Pirates}/{Donquixote Pirates} Leader). Scripted, they now ask for a redirect when attacked and add +1 cost, so those tests were switched to another unscripted Leader; see the list in the commit message.
