# OP-12 scripting, group 2 of 3 (block 3): readings and ambiguities

Cards (25 scripted, none left out; all `verified: true` in `cards/overrides/`): OP12-040, 042, 043, 044, 046, 047, 048, 050, 051, 053, 054, 056, 057, 059, 060, 062, 063, 065, 066, 069, 070, 072, 073, 074, 077. Audit gaps (`docs/research/m6b-audit/block3.md`, B3-G14 = wave-2 G10 for OP12-051, B3-G20 = G23 `div` for OP12-070, B3-G53 = G22 `trashedFromHand` for OP12-040) are all merged. No card needed a wave 5+ gap or a design item D1 to D9. Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. Open questions stay open.

- **OP12-040 Kuzan (Leader)**, "When a card is trashed from your hand by your "Navy" type card's effect, draw cards equal to the number of cards trashed." `trashedFromHand` with `by: you`, `sourceFilter: {type: Navy}`, `includeCosts: true` and `draw {eventCount}`. Costs count (the owner decision on M6b-Q31, a cost is part of the effect), which resolves audit Q4 the other way: Kuzan Character OP12-043, Sengoku OP12-047, Garp OP12-056 and Ice Block OP12-057 all trash as a cost and feed the draw. One instruction is one event, so Zephyr's 2-card trash draws 2, not 1 twice. A Navy Leader or Event is a "Navy type card" too. The opponent's effects do not count (`by: you`).
- **OP12-042 Alvida**, "If you have 2 or more Characters with a base cost of 5 or more, this Character gains +1 cost." Permanent `count` of your Characters by `baseCost >= 5`. Alvida is base cost 4 and never counts toward herself, so the condition cannot be satisfied by her own +1. [On Play] bottom-decks up to 1 opposing Character with base cost 1 or less (an owner's deck bottom; one card, nothing to order).
- **OP12-043 Kuzan**, "If you have 5 or more cards in your hand, this Character gains +1 cost." Permanent on the hand size (public). [On Play] auto with a "you may trash 1" cost; the lock is `cannotAttack` with `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` on an opposing Character ("up to 1"), which on your own turn lasts through the opponent's next turn. With an empty hand the optional is shown but cannot be accepted.
- **OP12-044 Sakazuki**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the "Navy" type, draw 2 cards. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." `leader: {type: Navy}` is an `if` step. The give is `giveDon` n 1 rested with an `exactly 1` target (as OP13-081, OP14-005): once the cost is paid the target pick can still end with `done`, so it can be declined. The cost card is always asked, even with one card in hand.
- **OP12-046 Zephyr(Navy)**, "[On Play] Trash 2 cards from your hand. [Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: Return up to 1 Character with a cost of 5 or less to the owner's hand." The [On Play] is not optional and not "up to" (`trashFromHand` exactly 2; with 1 card the 1 card goes, with none nothing). "Return up to 1 Character" names no owner, so either side's Characters qualify (current cost <= 5), including your own, never Zephyr (already in the trash). The Activate has no Once Per Turn and its "You may" is the activation itself (no `costOptional`, as OP14-029).
- **OP12-047 Sengoku**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 2 "Navy" type cards other than [Sengoku] and add them to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." One `take` of up to 2, any category, `{type: Navy}` and not the name Sengoku (another copy of the name is excluded, any other Navy card is not). Rest to the bottom in an order the owner chooses.
- **OP12-048 Donquixote Rosinante**, "[Opponent's Turn] If your blue "Navy" type Character would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may rest this Character and trash 1 card from your hand instead." `replacement` on `leaveField` with `cause: opponentEffect`, condition `opponentsTurn`, payload `restSelf` plus a hand trash. Rosinante is a blue Navy Character himself and may protect himself (he rests as the payment and is not removed). Not offered while he is rested. With an empty hand the replacement is still shown but cannot be accepted. A removal by your own effect or a battle K.O. is not replaced. Which cards count as blue: any card with the blue colour (a multicolored one too). M6b-Q6 (several simultaneous removals) is open.
- **OP12-050 Jaguar.D.Saul**, "[Blocker]". Keyword only (`keywords: ['Blocker']`, as the override).
- **OP12-051 Hina**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character and trash 1 card from your hand: Up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 4 or less cannot activate [Blocker] during this turn." Costs `restSelf` and a hand trash, `restrict ... 'cannotActivateBlocker'` this turn on an opposing Character of base cost <= 4 (printed cost, a +cost does not move a Character in or out). The restriction covers a [Blocker] gained as well as printed. This replaces the audit's G14 shape: `cannotActivateBlocker` already restricts a given card, no new primitive.
- **OP12-053 Borsalino**, "[Once Per Turn] If this Character would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may trash 1 card from your hand instead. [Opponent's Turn] If your Leader has the "Navy" type, this Character gains [Blocker] and +1000 power." The first sentence carries no [Opponent's Turn] tag, so the replacement is not gated by turn (only an opposing effect can cause it; it applies on either turn). Once Per Turn is spent when used; a second removal in the same turn is not replaced. The second sentence is a permanent with `opponentsTurn` and a Navy Leader; [Blocker] is gained, so `keywords` stays empty.
- **OP12-054 Marshall.D.Teach**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the "The Seven Warlords of the Sea" type, return up to 1 Character with a cost of 1 or less other than this Character to the owner's hand." Leading "If" on an auto is an `if` step. "Return up to 1 Character" names no owner: either side, current cost <= 1, never Teach himself (also cost 1).
- **OP12-056 Monkey.D.Garp**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Play up to 1 blue "Navy" type Character card with 8000 power or less other than [Monkey.D.Garp] from your hand." The cost is chosen first and cannot be the card played. The play is an unguarded "up to 1" pick from the hand (hidden-zone rule: asked even when nothing qualifies). Power is the printed power of the card in hand; 8000 is allowed, 10000 not. Played active (the text does not say rested). Another copy of Garp is excluded by name.
- **OP12-057 Ice Block Pheasant Peck**, "[Counter] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +4000 power during this battle. Then, trash 1 card from your hand. [Trigger] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Draw 1 card." "Then" is not "If you do": the trash is mandatory and happens even when no target was chosen (with an empty hand nothing is trashed). The Trigger is a trigger ability with a hand-trash cost; with an empty hand it cannot be activated.
- **OP12-059 Concasser**, "[Main] If your Leader is [Sanji], draw 1 card. [Counter] If you have 4 or more Events in your trash, up to 1 of your Leader gains +4000 power during this battle." Leader name exactly [Sanji] (either Sanji Leader). The Event is in the trash when its [Counter] resolves (CR 8-4-2) and counts toward the 4, so 3 other Events suffice. The Counter's target is the Leader only.
- **OP12-060 Boeuf Burst**, "[Main] If your Leader is multicolored, choose one: Return up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less to the owner's hand / If you have 6 or less cards in your hand, draw 2 cards." The "If" gates the whole choice (nothing is asked with a mono-colored Leader). A mode may be chosen and do nothing (E-Q6), e.g. the draw with 7 cards in hand. The hand count is public and read at resolution, after the Event has left the hand (6 other cards is the boundary).
- **OP12-062 Vinsmoke Sora**, "[On Play] If your Leader is [Sanji] and the number of DON!! cards on your field is equal to or less than the number on your opponent's field, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and rest it. Then, draw 1 card." The "Then, draw" is inside the "If" (M4b-5, RD 121-122): a failed condition draws nothing. It is "Then", not "If you do": declining the DON!! (count 0) still draws. DON!! on the field is the cost area plus given, rested included, so playing Sora (cost 1) does not change the count.
- **OP12-063 Vinsmoke Reiju**, "If you have 4 or more Events in your trash, this Character gains +2000 power and +5 cost. [Blocker]". One permanent with two modifiers on the Events count; [Blocker] is printed.
- **OP12-065 Emporio.Ivankov**, "If you have 4 or more Events in your trash, this Character gains [Blocker]. [On K.O.] Add up to 1 Event from your trash to your hand." Permanent grant; the On K.O. move is a plain "up to 1" pick of an Event in the trash (public zone).
- **OP12-066 Carne**, "If you have 4 or more Events in your trash, this Character gains [Blocker]." The parenthesis is the [Blocker] reminder; no printed keyword.
- **OP12-069 Crocodile**, "[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] DON!! −1: If your Leader's type includes "Baroque Works", up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +2000 power during this battle." The DON!! -1 is an optional cost (RL-52). The "If" follows the colon, so the DON!! is returned and the Once Per Turn used even when the Leader has no matching type, and then nothing is boosted. `typeIncludes` is a substring of a type element ("Baroque Works", "Former Baroque Works"). Not offered with no DON!! on the field.
- **OP12-070 Sanji**, "This Character gains +1000 power for every 5 Events in your trash. If this Character would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may return 1 DON!! card from your field to your DON!! deck instead." Power `mul [div [count Events, 5], 1000]` (steps at 5, 10, 15). The replacement is not Once Per Turn and not gated on whose turn it is (there is no [Opponent's Turn] tag); the payload is `returnDon 1` (active or rested), not offered with no DON!! on the field. M6b-Q6 open for simultaneous removals.
- **OP12-072 Zeff**, "When a DON!! card on your field is returned to your DON!! deck, if your Leader is [Sanji], this Character gains [Rush] during this turn." `donReturned` for your DON!! (any cause, either turn) with an intervening-if on the Leader name as an ability `condition`. The opponent's DON!! returning does not count. [Rush] is gained, so `keywords` stays empty.
- **OP12-073 Trafalgar Law**, "[On Play] If the number of DON!! cards on your field is equal to or less than the number on your opponent's field, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active. Then, all of your [Donquixote Rosinante] and "Heart Pirates" type Characters gain +1000 power until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." "Then" inside the "If" (M4b-5): a failed condition gives no power. "Then", not "If you do": declining the DON!! still gives the power. The set is every Character of yours on the field when it resolves with the name [Donquixote Rosinante] or a {Heart Pirates} type (Law himself included); your Leader is not a Character; a Character played later is not covered.
- **OP12-074 Patty**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 Event from your hand: If your Leader is [Sanji], add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active." The Leader check follows the colon, so the Event is trashed even with another Leader and then nothing is added. The cost can only be paid with an Event in hand (the optional is shown but cannot be accepted otherwise).
- **OP12-077 The "Extinguishes All Sound Created by Your Influence" Technique**, "[Main] Select up to 1 of your [Trafalgar Law] cards and that card gains +2000 power during this turn. Then, if the selected card attacks during this turn, your opponent cannot activate [Blocker]. [Trigger] Draw 1 card." `select` binds up to 1 Law (Leader or Character), then +2000 this turn and `cannotActivateBlockerAgainstIt` this turn on the bound card (it applies to every attack that card makes this turn). The "Then" is independent of the selection being made: with none selected both steps do nothing.

## Engine notes (no bug found)

- An optional cost that cannot be paid is handled two ways by the engine: for a hand-trash cost the `optional` decision is shown with `accept` illegal (OP12-043, 048, 053, 074), for a DON!!-based one (OP12-069 DON!! -1, OP12-070 return DON!!) it is not offered at all. Both are consistent with what the opponent can see (the hand size and DON!! count are public), and are pinned by the tests.
- A single-candidate "exactly 1" hand-trash cost on an Activate is still asked (OP12-044).
