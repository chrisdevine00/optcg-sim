# OP-16 scripting, group 2 of 4: readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP16-031 to 045, 047 to 057 (26 listed; 25 scripted). Not scripted: OP16-041 (needs G29, the `removed` / `leftField` timing, wave 5). Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. No new rulings were invented; where the text is silent the nearest reading on file is used.

- **OP16-031 Buggy (Green)**, "[On K.O.] Play up to 1 [Prisoner of Impel Down] card from your hand." An `onKO` auto with a play of up to 1 by exact name; the Prisoner comes in active.
- **OP16-032 Boa Hancock**, "[Unblockable] [On Play] Up to 1 of your opponent's Characters other than [Monkey.D.Luffy] cannot be rested until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." Unscoped `cannotBeRested` (rules-digest 57: it also cannot attack, block or pay a rest cost), `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`. "Other than [Luffy]" is a `not name` filter on the pool.
- **OP16-033 Morley**, "If this Character would be K.O'd, you may rest 2 of your cards instead." A `ko` replacement of itself (any cause, battle included), optional, paying "rest 2 of your cards" as the Leader, Characters, Stage or active DON!! (CR 1-3-2-1, as OP15-035 / EB05-019). Morley is usually already rested when K.O.'d in battle, so he does not count for himself; the replacement is not offered with fewer than 2 restable cards.
- **OP16-034 Monkey.D.Luffy**, "[DON!! x1] [Your Turn] This Character gains +1000 power for each of your Characters with a different card name." `count` with `distinctNames` over your Characters, Luffy included (M6b-Q9 open: "different card name" read as the number of distinct names, not "differs from Luffy's"). Two Characters with the same name count once. The [On Play] is a look at 3, up to 1 {Impel Down} card revealed, the rest to the bottom in any order.
- **OP16-035 Roronoa Zoro**, "[On Play] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's cards. Then, you may trash 1 card from your hand. If you do, give up to 3 rested DON!! cards to your Leader." "Cards" is read as the opposing Leader, Characters, Stage or active DON!!. "Then, you may ... If you do" is part of the effect, not a cost (OP05-038 pattern): a `may` trash and an `ifDid` give; with an empty hand the question is not asked. "Up to 3" is the engine's count question (most first).
- **OP16-036 Mr.2.Bon.Kurei(Bentham), Green**, "[On Play] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less. [When Attacking] This Character's base power becomes the same as your opponent's Leader during this turn." "The same as your opponent's Leader" is read as the Leader's power (the Blue copy says "Leader's power"): the Leader is bound and its current power is read as the effect resolves; the base is replaced, DON!! bonuses still add.
- **OP16-037 Mr.3(Galdino), Green**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Impel Down} type, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less." An `if` on the Leader type, checked as the effect resolves.
- **OP16-038 Let's Go!! To the Navy Headquarters!!**, "[Main] You may rest 6 of your DON!! cards: If you have 5 {Impel Down} type Characters with different card names, set your Leader and all of your Characters as active." Event with `costOptional` (the "you may" cost is asked after the card is played, B-1). The condition is `count` with `distinctNames` >= 5; the field holds at most 5 Characters so >= and = are the same. Cost 6 is paid even when the condition fails. [Counter] is a plain +3000 to the Leader.
- **OP16-039 Gum-Gum Twin Jet Pistol**, "[Main] Up to 1 of your [Monkey.D.Luffy] cards gains [Double Attack] during this turn. Then, if your Leader has the {Impel Down} type, rest up to 2 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 3 or less." "Your [Luffy] cards" is the Leader or a Character (the Leader is named [Monkey.D.Luffy] in OP16-022). "Then, if" is a separate `if`, checked even when no Luffy was chosen. [Trigger] rests the opposing Leader. The verified text reads "cost of 3 or less" (the scrape's "cost or 3" is an upstream defect, M6b-Q4).
- **OP16-040 Gum-Gum Hammer Rifle**, "[Main] If you have [Monkey.D.Luffy] and [Mr.3(Galdino)], up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with a cost of 6 or less will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase." "Have" is read as on the field: [Monkey.D.Luffy] as Leader or Character, [Mr.3(Galdino)] as a Character (exact name). Only rested Characters are candidates (EB05-018 pattern, `skipRefresh`).
- **OP16-042 Prisoner of Impel Down**, "Under the rules of this game, you may have any number of this card in your deck." The `anyNumberInDeck` rules clause (G12); `DECK_RULES` in `engine/src/defs.ts` already carries the entry and the drift test passes.
- **OP16-043 Usopp**, "[Blocker] [On K.O.] You may rest 1 of your {Dressrosa} type Leader or Stage cards: Return up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less to the owner's hand." Printed [Blocker] from the override. The cost is a `rest` of one Leader or Stage with the {Dressrosa} type (an auto with a cost is optional, CR 8-3-1-4); only an active one can pay. The audit row (OP16-043 "move opp Character cost<=5 to hand") omits this cost; the verified text has it.
- **OP16-044 Emporio.Ivankov**, keyword-only [Blocker]. Script with `keywords: ['Blocker']` and no abilities.
- **OP16-045 Crocodile**, "[Blocker] [On Play] You may return 1 of your Characters with a cost of 2 or more to the owner's hand: Play up to 1 {Impel Down} type Character card with a cost of 2 or less from your hand." Cost is the G1 own-Character return; "1 of your Characters" may be Crocodile himself (cost 4), and the Character just returned may be played back (cost 2 or less). The play is filtered to Characters by category.
- **OP16-047 Donquixote Doflamingo**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: If your opponent has 8 or more cards in their hand, they place 2 cards from their hand at the bottom of their deck in any order." The "If" is after the colon, so it is an effect `if` (the Character is rested even with 7 cards). The opponent chooses their own 2 cards (OP17-053 pattern); "in any order" does not need a decision here (the order of two hidden cards is not observable).
- **OP16-048 Buggy (Blue)**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Impel Down} type, draw 1 card and play up to 1 [Prisoner of Impel Down] card from your hand. [Once Per Turn] This effect can be activated when your opponent attacks. Up to 1 of your [Prisoner of Impel Down] cards gains [Blocker] during this turn." Read as two abilities: the [On Play] and an `onOpponentAttack` auto, Once Per Turn, giving [Blocker] to up to 1 [Prisoner] Character for the turn (the audit reads it the same way). Draw and play share one `if`. Open: rulings.md RL-19 notes a QA-OP16/QA-OP17 answer on OP16-048 about a Leader gaining [Blocker]; the verified text names only [Prisoner of Impel Down] cards (Characters), so a Leader cannot be chosen. If that Q&A means the card can reach a Leader, the target filter is wrong. Worth a reviewer check.
- **OP16-049 Portgas.D.Ace**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: Draw 1 card." `restSelf` cost; not Once Per Turn.
- **OP16-050 Miss Olive**, "[Blocker] [On Play] You may return 1 of your Characters with a cost of 2 or more to the owner's hand: Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand." As OP16-045 for the cost; she may return herself (the effect still resolves).
- **OP16-051 Mohji & Cabaji**, "[On Play] If you have 5 or less cards in your hand, draw 2 cards." Read as the hand after the card was played (it has left the hand).
- **OP16-052 Monkey.D.Luffy (Blue)**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." `giveDon` rested, target a pick of exactly 1 over Leader and Characters; "up to 1" is the engine's own (none when no rested DON!!, or `done` on the target).
- **OP16-053 Roronoa Zoro (Blue)**, "[When Attacking] If you have 6 or less cards in your hand, draw 1 card." An `if` as the effect resolves; the leading "If" follows the tag, so it is an effect condition, not an activation condition.
- **OP16-054 Mr.1(Daz.Bonez)**, "[DON!! X1] [Your Turn] If you have 5 or more cards in your hand, this Character gains +3000 power. [On Play] Draw 1 card." A permanent with `donGiven: 1`, `yourTurn` and a hand count of 5 or more (live).
- **OP16-055 Mr.2.Bon.Kurei(Bentham), Blue**, "[On Play] Draw 1 card. [DON!! x1] [When Attacking] This Character's base power becomes the same as your opponent's Leader's power during this turn." `donGiven: 1` is an ability condition (CR 8-3-2-3). The opposing Leader's current power is read on resolution (DON!! on it count only on its owner's turn).
- **OP16-056 Mr.3(Galdino) (Blue)**, "[Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: Draw 2 cards, and up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 9 or less cannot attack until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." `trashSelf` cost; the draw and the `cannotAttack` (untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn) are two steps. No vanilla with a cost of 9 exists in the data, so the test pins 8 (in) and 10 (out) and relies on the filter for the exact 9.
- **OP16-057 Captain Buggy's Our Savior!!**, "[Counter] If you have 2 or more [Prisoner of Impel Down] cards, up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +4000 power during this battle. [Trigger] Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand." "Cards" counted as Characters on your field (a Prisoner is a Character); the condition is checked as the effect resolves.

## Notes for review

- Tooling: `cards/src/optional-cost.ts` does not find the text unit of OP16-048's second ability (its text carries no [On Your Opponent's Attack] tag, only "This effect can be activated when your opponent attacks") nor OP16-055's [When Attacking] (it follows a [DON!! x1] tag after another ability without a blank line, so the splitter keeps it in the [On Play] unit). Both are checker limits, not script errors: `cards/test/optional-cost.test.ts` fails for exactly these two ("no text unit found") until `optional-cost.ts` is patched (a two-hunk change, checked against every script; left to the caller because `cards/src` was out of scope for this group).
