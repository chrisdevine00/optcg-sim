# Wave 5 cards, group 2 of 2

Cards: OP10-033, OP11-005, OP11-066, OP11-071, OP11-073, OP11-074, OP11-079, OP11-081, OP11-092, OP13-002, OP14-003, PRB02-018. One line per card: the verified text, then the readings taken (open questions M6b-Q65 to Q69 and Q1..Q64 are not settled here). All twelve are unblocked by engine wave 5 ([m6b-wave5.md](m6b-wave5.md)); none needed a wave 6+ gap or a design item.

- **OP10-033 Nami**: "[On Play] If you have 2 or more rested "ODYSSEY" type Characters, up to 1 of your opponent's rested DON!! cards will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase." Readings: the "If" follows no colon, so it is an effect condition read when the effect resolves; Nami is played active and does not count herself; the DON!! is held as "up to 1, as many as they have" when the effect resolves (G48, M6b-Q69) and the hold ends with that Refresh Phase; nothing is asked (the opponent's rested DON!! is public, no prompt depends on a hidden card).
- **OP11-005 Smoker**: "[Blocker] [DON!! x1] This Character cannot be K.O.'d by effects of Characters without the <Special> attribute." Readings (G46, M6b-Q68): `cause: 'effect'` is an effect of either player, a K.O. cost included (RL-16); the source must be a Character (a Leader, Stage or Event is not "a Character without <Special>", so the effect K.O.s it) whose printed attribute is not <Special>; a K.O. with no source card is not stopped; battle K.O.s are not effects; [DON!! x1] is one DON!! given to this Character, checked when the K.O. happens.
- **OP11-066 Charlotte Oven**: "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: Choose a cost and reveal 1 card from the top of your opponent's deck. If the revealed card has the chosen cost, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 3 or less. Then, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and rest it." Readings (G44, M6b-Q67): the number 0 to 10 is named before the reveal and shown to both players; the revealed card stays on top of the deck and is public; it matches by its printed cost (a card with no cost, or an empty deck, never matches); the "Then" follows the If-clause so it is inside the guard (M4b-5): a miss adds no DON!!, a match adds it even when no Character is K.O.'d (a Then is not an If you do); "up to 1" DON!! asks a count; "base cost of 3 or less" is the printed cost; "You may rest" on an [Activate: Main] is the player's choice to activate (no `costOptional`).
- **OP11-071 Charlotte Perospero**: "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Choose a cost and reveal 1 card from the top of your opponent's deck. If the revealed card has the chosen cost, draw 1 card and add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active." Readings: as OP11-066 for the choice, the reveal and the match; the trash is the cost (not offered with an empty hand), paid before the number is named; Once Per Turn is spent on a miss too; the draw and the DON!! are both inside the guard.
- **OP11-073 Charlotte Linlin**: "If your Leader has the "Big Mom Pirates" type, this Character gains [Rush]. [On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] DON!! −5: Choose a cost and reveal 1 card from the top of your opponent's deck. If the revealed card has the chosen cost, up to 1 of your Leader gains +2000 power during this turn." Readings: [Rush] is a continuous grant while the Leader has the type; DON!! −5 is an optional cost (an auto with a cost, and DON!! −N alone is always optional, RL-52); not offered with fewer than 5 DON!!; the +2000 lasts "during this turn", the whole turn and not only this battle (checked: it is still there after the battle); "up to 1 of your Leader" is asked as a pick of the single Leader that may be declined.
- **OP11-074 Streusen**: "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] DON!! −1, You may rest this Character: Choose a cost and reveal 1 card from the top of your opponent's deck. If the revealed card has the chosen cost, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less." Readings: "You may rest this Character" is read as part of the cost with the activation being the player's choice: DON!! −1 and the rest are both paid or the ability is not activated (an [Activate: Main] carries no `costOptional`, `cards/src/optional-cost.ts`); a variant where the DON!! is paid and the rest declined while the effect still runs is not modelled; "a cost of 4 or less" is the current cost (no "base"); only Characters, never the Leader.
- **OP11-079 When Two Men Are Fighting the Last Thing I Need Is Some Half-Hearted Assistance!!!!**: "[Counter] Choose a cost and reveal 1 card from the top of your opponent's deck. If the revealed card has the chosen cost, up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +5000 power during this battle. / [Trigger] Draw 1 card." Readings: as OP11-066 for the choice and match (an Event on top matches its own printed cost); the number is named after the Counter is paid and the boost is only asked on a match; the +5000 ends with the battle; the [Trigger] is a plain draw.
- **OP11-081 Cognac Mama-Mash**: "[Main] Choose a cost and reveal 1 card from the top of your opponent's deck. If the revealed card has the chosen cost, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 8 or less. / [Trigger] Add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active." Readings: as OP11-066; "base cost of 8 or less" reads the printed cost (a Character raised to 9 stays a target, one with a printed 9 lowered to 8 does not); the tests use the unscripted [Yamato] OP04-112 as the inert printed-cost-9 Character (no vanilla of cost 9 exists); the [Trigger] DON!! comes in active, "up to 1" asks a count.
- **OP11-092 Helmeppo**: "[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Draw 1 card and play up to 1 "SWORD" type Character card with a cost of 8 or less other than [Helmeppo] from your trash. Then, place the 1 Character played by this effect at the bottom of the owner's deck at the end of this turn." Readings (G39, M6b-Q38): the draw comes first, then the play; "other than [Helmeppo]" excludes every card with that name; the cost 8 limit reads the printed cost in the trash (the test fixture for the boundary is [Koby] OP11-119 at 8; no {SWORD} Character of cost 9 exists to test the other side, the limit is also pinned at 7 by the mutation check); the played Character is bound and placed at the deck bottom at the end of the turn if it is still on the field, otherwise nothing happens; the delayed step is queued even if nothing was played (it then finds nothing).
- **OP13-002 Portgas.D.Ace**: "[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Character cards −2000 power during this battle. / [DON!! x1] [Once Per Turn] When you take damage or your Character with 6000 base power or more is K.O.'d, draw 1 card." Readings (G43, M6b-Q66): "when you take damage" fires once per damage, after its processing, the [Trigger] included, has ended (rulings RL-02), so the draw is queued after a [Trigger] and a Double Attack is one event; the damage and the K.O. timings share one Once Per Turn; the K.O. timing reads the printed power of the K.O.'d Character (the `koed` filter reads values where the card now is, M6b-Q20), so a buff or debuff on the field changes nothing, and a K.O. of any cause counts; the first ability's cost is optional, offered while the hand is empty only to be declined (the hand size is public, the engine asks anyway, as for OP14-009); the -2000 target is a Leader or Character of the opponent, "up to 1".
- **OP14-003 Capone"Gang"Bege**: "This Character cannot be K.O.'d by effects of your opponent's Characters with 5000 base power or less." Readings (G46, M6b-Q68): `cause: 'opponentEffect'` with a source filter: only an opponent's Character is a source that counts, read by its printed power (5000 is the boundary: a 5000 Character cannot, a 6000 one can; a buffed 4000 one still cannot, a debuffed 6000 one still can); effects of the opponent's Leader, Stage or Event, your own effects and battle K.O.s are not stopped.
- **PRB02-018 Portgas.D.Ace**: "[On Play] If you have a face-up Life card, play up to 1 [Sabo], [Portgas.D.Ace], or [Monkey.D.Luffy] with a cost of 2 from your hand or trash." Readings: the "If" follows no colon: an effect condition read when the effect resolves; the face-up count is public (G41), so the gate leaks nothing (hidden-gate); the cost of 2 applies to each of the three names, as the printed cost of a Character card in the hand or trash; one pick over the hand and the trash together, "up to 1", asked on the hand whatever it holds.

## Engine notes

No engine bug found. Two behaviours worth knowing when reviewing the tests:

- An auto ability whose cost cannot be paid is not offered when the engine can see that (OP11-073 with 4 DON!!: straight to the block decision), but one whose cost is a trash from the hand is still offered with an empty hand and can only be declined (OP13-002, as OP14-009 already did). The hand size is public, so the prompt is needless, not a leak.
- Mutation check (one clause changed, its test file run, restored): every clause of the 12 scripts was killed except two equivalents: OP13-002 `basePower` read as `power` for the `koed` filter (the filter reads printed values off the field, so they are the same, M6b-Q20), and the `upTo` of OP11-073's single-Leader pick.
