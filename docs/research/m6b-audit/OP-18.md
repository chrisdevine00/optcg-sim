# OP-18 runtime audit (runbook `docs/runbooks/new-set.md`, step b)

Written for the OP-18 "The Dominance of God" import (ADR 0015), using the method of `docs/research/m6b-audit/block5.md` (sections 1 to 3). Inputs: every card of `cards/data/sets/OP-18.json` (119 cards, text from Limitless card pages, checked against the official card list; all `verified: false`, none scripted when the audit started), `cards/src/dsl.ts` (the vocabulary), `cards/src/dsl-validate.ts` (`IMPLEMENTED`, `cost()`, `moveShape`, `zonesIn`), the runtime in `engine/src/effects/` (`run.ts`, `eval.ts`, `layers.ts`), `engine/src/flow.ts` (`runKo`, `collectReplacements`, `offerReplacement`), `engine/src/ops/index.ts`, `engine/src/actions.ts`, `docs/research/effect-dsl.md` §4.15, and the scripted cards of other sets with the same wording (`cards/effects/**`, found by phrase). `cards/data/coverage-report.json` tags were not used. Nothing here scripts a card.

Reading note: in the data a card's second text block is its [Trigger] with the "[Trigger]" prefix already removed (Limitless layout, ADR 0015). Every "Trigger" below is that block.

## 1. Result

- **119 cards audited**: 92 Characters, 16 Events, 5 Stages, 6 Leaders (OP18-001, -021, -022, -041, -060, -079). 7 have no text (OP18-003, -035, -043, -062, -073, -090, -102), 5 are a bare keyword line (OP18-098, -104, -106, -111, -112; the override must carry `keywords`, which is data, not engine).
- **108 cards need nothing new**: every shape they use is in `IMPLEMENTED` and the code path was read (precedents named in the table). 20 cards have a [Trigger]; every one is expressible except OP18-105's (G6).
- **11 cards need at least one gap** (OP18-117 needs two), **7 distinct gaps**, `OP18-G1` to `OP18-G7`. G1 is done and G2 was being made in the working tree while this audit ran (see below). Only G7 is large.
- **No card is unexpressible**. OP18-105 (activate the [Trigger] of a revealed card) and OP18-117 (play from the deck, then shuffle) are the two design-sized pieces.
- The engine wave is therefore small: five open primitives (G3 to G7), two of size S, two of size M and one of size L.
- **The Franky and Luffy deck slice** (section 5, 16 cards) is clean except OP18-021 (G1, done) and OP18-066 / -072 (G2).
- Several texts need a ruling before they are scripted (section 4); none blocks the engine work.
- Two cards print block X on the official list (OP18-031, OP18-065; `cards/overrides/`); that is data, not a rules gap.
- State of the tree when the audit ran: G1 (`engine/src/actions.ts` `counterCard`, `engine/src/effects/eval.ts` `counterOf`) and the validator half of G2 (`cost()` `ko` now accepts `stage`, `costText` in `run.ts`) were present as uncommitted edits, with scripts for the slice in `cards/effects/OP-18/` and tests in `engine/test/cards/`. Those were written by another session; this audit did not rely on them.

## 3. Gap list

Sizes as in block5: S = a few lines in 2 or 3 files plus a planted test, M = a new runtime path or a change to a shared flow, L = new engine state or a new decision. Gaps are ordered by the order the slice and the number of cards need them.

### OP18-G1 a Stage in hand with an effect-given Counter (1 card, S, done)

"All Stage cards in your hand have a +3000 Counter" (OP18-021). A Stage in hand with a Counter is trashed in the Counter Step like a Character, and `counterOf` computes Counters for Stages in hand. Files: `engine/src/actions.ts` (`counterCard`, the `counter` legality and the legal-action list), `engine/src/effects/eval.ts` (`counterOf`). The continuous `counter` with a `hand` selector already validated. Card: OP18-021. Status: made in the working tree (owner's note, and visible there); the planted test is `engine/test/cards/OP18-021.test.ts`. Ruling noted: CR 7-1-3-1-1 names Character cards; the card has no other use.

### OP18-G2 cost `ko` on a Stage (3 cards, S)

"You may K.O. 1 of your Stages with a cost of 5 or less:" as a cost. `cost()` in `dsl-validate.ts` limited `ko` to `characters`; the runtime already supports it: `fieldCost` in `run.ts` pushes `pushKo` with the cause `cost`, `runKo` in `flow.ts` takes a Stage (G18), `payable` in `eval.ts` counts the pool, and a protected Stage fails the cost (RL-16). Needed: `zonesIn` in `cost()` `ko` accepts `stage` (and the `costText` string), a planted test for a K.O.'d Stage (trash, no [On Play] side effects) and for "cannot be K.O.'d" blocking the payment. Files: `cards/src/dsl-validate.ts`, `engine/src/effects/run.ts` (`costText`). Cards: OP18-063, OP18-066, OP18-072. Status: the validator and `costText` edit were in the working tree at audit time, with `engine/test/cards/m6b-op18/g1-g2.test.ts`; confirm and mark done.

### OP18-G3 move from hand to the top or bottom of Life, face-up (3 cards, S)

"Add up to 1 card with a type including "CP" from your hand to the top or bottom of your Life cards face-up" (OP18-099, -100, -101). Today a hand or trash card moves to the top of Life only (`moveShape` in `dsl-validate.ts`: "a card enters Life at the top", `pos !== 'top'` is an error), and `move()` in `run.ts` calls `placeCards`, which reads `position === 'bottom' ? 'bottom' : 'top'`. The Character-into-Life path already asks the end with `lifeEnd` (G61). Needed: accept `position: 'topOrBottom'` for hand and trash sources into Life (with `faceUp`), ask the end once for the chosen card with `lifeEnd`, then `moveCard(..., 'life', { position, faceUp })`. A face-up Life card is public, so the identity is revealed (EB03-059 precedent). Files: `cards/src/dsl-validate.ts` (`moveShape` Life branch), `engine/src/effects/run.ts` (`move`, `placeCards`). Cards: OP18-099, OP18-100, OP18-101.

### OP18-G4 "all" for the Life-count costs (2 cards, S)

"You may add all of your Life cards to your hand:" (OP18-020) and "You may trash all of your Life cards:" (OP18-117). `lifeToHand` accepts only `n: 1` (`cost()` in `dsl-validate.ts`: "only n = 1 is implemented"; `lifeToHandCost` in `run.ts` loops to `spec.n`), and `trashLife` is an integer >= 1. Needed: `{ lifeToHand: { from: 'top', n: 'all' } }` and `{ trashLife: 'all' }`, paid for every Life card the player has (zero Life is a legal payment, Q3, Q8). Both are not damage: no [Trigger], `lifeRemoved` watchers fire per card, `cannotAddLifeToHandByOwnEffects` still blocks the hand version. Files: `cards/src/dsl.ts` (`Cost`), `cards/src/dsl-validate.ts` (`cost()`), `engine/src/effects/eval.ts` (`payable`: `lifeNeeds`), `engine/src/effects/run.ts` (`payCost`, `lifeToHandCost`, `costText`). Cards: OP18-020, OP18-117.

### OP18-G5 permanent play-cost reduction on cards in hand (1 card, M)

"If your Leader has the {Celestial Dragons} type, the cost of playing {Holy Knights of God} or {Five Elders} type Character cards with a cost of 2 or more from your hand will be reduced by 1" (OP18-097, a Stage on the field). `Continuous` has `playCost` but `IMPLEMENTED.continuous` omits it (effect-dsl.md §4.15 lists "a permanent `playCost`" as not implemented) and the validator's `continuous()` rejects it; the existing `playCost` is a one-shot window modifier on the Leader (`playCostOf` in `eval.ts` sums only `leaderMods(..., 'playCost')`). The `cost` continuous cannot do it: its target must be on the field (the `zone: 'hand'` form changes the card's own cost from its own ability, OP15-013) and it changes the card's cost, which also moves "cost of 2 or more" and every other cost reader; the text names the cost of playing. Needed: a permanent `{ playCost: { target: Selector (from hand), amount: Num } }` read by `playCostOf` (and so by `playable`, the play frame and the AI's cost view) as a delta that never applies to the filter itself, floored at 0, stacking with the one-shot ones. Files: `cards/src/dsl.ts` (the `Continuous.playCost` type member already exists in `dsl.ts`, only the validator and runtime reject it), `cards/src/dsl-validate.ts` (`IMPLEMENTED.continuous`, `continuous()`), `engine/src/effects/layers.ts` or `engine/src/effects/eval.ts` (`playCostOf`), `engine/src/actions.ts` (legality, already through `playCostOf`). Card: OP18-097.

### OP18-G6 activate the [Trigger] of a card in hand (1 card, M)

"Reveal up to 1 card with a type including "CP" other than [Spandam] from your hand. Activate the [Trigger] effect of the revealed card" (OP18-105, itself a [Trigger]). `Action.activate` has `of: 'self'` for `main`, `onPlay`, `onKO`, `counter`, and `of: Selector` for an Event's `main` only (`activateEvent` in `run.ts`). Needed: `{ activate: { ability: 'trigger', of: Selector } }` where the selector is a hand card (use `reveal: true`, `bind`); the effect resolves with the revealed card as its source (`pushEffect` with that card's `trigger` ability), its conditions checked when it resolves, its optional cost asked, the card staying in the hand afterwards unless its own effect moves it. "this card" inside such a Trigger ("Play this card", "Activate this card's [Main]") needs a rule for where the card is (the `selfZone` check in the `play` action is `resolving` for a Trigger and `trash` otherwise): Q7 asks the ruling; the 20 OP-18 Triggers are all independent of it ("this card" appears only in OP18-058 and OP18-095, which are Events: [Main] is activated, a plain run of the Event's own effect, and neither is a CP card). Files: `cards/src/dsl.ts` (`Action.activate`), `cards/src/dsl-validate.ts` (`action()` `activate` case), `engine/src/effects/run.ts` (`activate`, `pushEffect`), `engine/src/effects/eval.ts` (the revealed card's [Trigger] lookup). Card: OP18-105.

### OP18-G7 play from the deck, and shuffle the deck (1 card, L)

"Play up to a total of 5 {Vice Admiral} type Character cards with different card names from your deck or trash. Then, shuffle your deck" (OP18-117). The `Zone` list has no `deck` (only `deckTop`, which `play` accepts only for a revealed top card, G53); `IMPLEMENTED.zones` omits it; there is no `shuffle` action (the shuffle is never needed elsewhere: no scripted card in block 4 or later plays from the deck, and OP-01 and OP-08 "play from your deck, then shuffle" cards are not in the meta). Needed: (a) Zone `deck` as a selector source for `play`, a search that shows the controller the whole deck (a decision whose candidates are hidden from the opponent, like a hand pick, with the deck order not told), accepted together with `trash` in one selector (`from: ['deck', 'trash']`) with `pick: { upTo: 5, distinctNames: true }` (the OP13-082 shape for the trash alone); (b) `{ shuffleDeck: P }` action using the seeded RNG, with a `DeckShuffled` event that tells the opponent nothing about the order (and clears the "known top card" state that `reveal` keeps, `run.ts`); (c) the usual rule that searching a deck without finding is allowed ("up to"). The rest of the card is expressible: "trash all Characters" is a `move` of every Character of both sides (`owner: 'any'`, no pick), the Leader check is an `if` after the cost. Files: `cards/src/dsl.ts` (`Zone`, `Action`), `cards/src/dsl-validate.ts` (`IMPLEMENTED.zones`, `IMPLEMENTED.actions`, `action()` `play` zones, `moveShape` is not needed), `engine/src/effects/run.ts` (`play`, `select`, a `shuffleDeck` branch), `engine/src/ops/index.ts` (shuffle), `engine/src/api.ts` (event, view redaction of the deck order), `engine/src/derive.ts` and the player view (info hidden), the AI's choice of a search target. Card: OP18-117.

## 4. Open questions

Readings that need an official ruling or a text check, each with the proposed reading. None is invented as a ruling.

- **Q1. OP18-005 Kung Fu Jugon, "[Your Turn] This Character gains +1000 power for every [Kung Fu Jugon]."** Where are the copies counted, and does the count include this Character? Proposed: Characters on your own field, this one included (a lone Jugon is +1000), as `count` over `characters`, `owner: 'you'`, name filter. The reading with both fields or only the others changes the number by one or more.
- **Q2. OP18-006 Koza, "If you have [Nefeltari Vivi]".** Proposed: a Character named [Nefeltari Vivi] on your field (OP18-011 is a Character). A Leader with that name exists (OP18-020's text says "your Leader is [Nefeltari Vivi]") and "have" could include it; proposed to count both (`from: ['leader', 'characters']`) as the safer reading for the player, pending a ruling.
- **Q3. OP18-020 Stop Fighting!!!, "You may add all of your Life cards to your hand:".** Proposed: the cost is payable at any Life count including 0, adds every Life card (face-up ones too), is not damage (no [Trigger]), and the condition "If your Leader is [Nefeltari Vivi]" is checked after the cost is paid. With 0 Life a player can still choose to "pay"; a ruling is needed on whether a cost of "all" with nothing to move is payable (CR 8-3-1-3 says a cost must be paid in full).
- **Q4. Replacements that cover the card itself (OP18-026 Sanji, OP18-031 Nico Robin, OP18-069 Sodomu & Gomora, OP18-017 Zoro, OP18-071 Trebol, OP18-054 Mr.8).** Unlike OP15-094 these have no "other than this Character". Proposed: the pool includes this card, so Sanji removed to the hand by the opponent's effect may trash himself instead, Robin may rest herself, Sodomu may rest himself (if active). `collectReplacements` (`flow.ts`) already allows it (`id === card` ranks first). A ruling would confirm that "instead" is allowed when the payload is the replaced card itself. OP18-031 also: "[Opponent's Turn]" is a condition of the whole replacement, and a rested Robin cannot pay.
- **Q5. OP18-070 Tom, "DON!! −1, you may trash 1 card from your hand:" and OP18-075 Thousand Sunny, "DON!! −2, you may rest this Stage:".** Proposed: both parts are one optional cost list paid in full (the "you may" restates that the whole cost is optional), as the scripts written for the slice do. The other reading (DON!! −N is paid and the second half is separately declinable) would let the effect run after paying only the DON!! −N cost; it is rare to want it, and no ruling was found.
- **Q6. OP18-080 Van Augur, "When a card is trashed from your hand by your [Marshall.D.Teach] Leader's effect".** The Teach Leaders in the card pool (OP09-081, OP16-080) trash their hand card as a cost. Proposed: a cost counts (a cost is part of the effect, RL-16), so `trashedFromHand` with `includeCosts: true`, `by: 'you'`, `sourceFilter: { name: 'Marshall.D.Teach' }` (the source card is the Leader). M6b-Q31 (do costs count?) is still open and this card depends on it. "No other [Van Augur]" is read on your Characters and excludes this card.
- **Q7. OP18-105 Spandam, "Activate the [Trigger] effect of the revealed card."** Open: the card stays in the hand when the Trigger resolves; a Trigger that says "Play this card" or "add this card to your hand" then acts on a card in the hand; the revealed card is shown to the opponent; "other than [Spandam]" stops a chain. Proposed: the effect runs with the revealed card as its source, the card stays where it is unless the effect moves it ("this card" = that card in the hand), and a Trigger whose cost cannot be paid does nothing. Only the CP cards of OP-18 matter now (all independent of "this card"), but the engine rule must be set.
- **Q8. OP18-117 Buster Call.** (a) "You may trash all of your Life cards:" with 0 Life, as Q3. (b) "trash all Characters" is both players' Characters (the text has no "your"). (c) "up to a total of 5 ... with different card names from your deck or trash": the 5 are chosen across both zones as one pick, names all different, and the deck is searched. Proposed: the deck is shuffled whenever the effect resolves past the cost, even when no card was found. (d) The Leader must be [Spandam] only at resolution; the Life is gone either way.
- **Q9. OP18-097 Abyss Pentagram, "the cost of playing ... Character cards with a cost of 2 or more from your hand will be reduced by 1".** Proposed: the "cost of 2 or more" is read on the card's current cost before the reduction (so a cost 2 card costs 1), the reduction applies only to a play from the hand (not an effect that plays it from the trash), several reductions stack, and the play cost never goes below 0. The Stage's own cost is 1 and plays at the normal price.
- **Q10. OP18-099, -100, -101 (CP Characters), "to the top or bottom of your Life cards face-up".** Proposed: the player chooses the end for the card (no other choice), the card arrives face-up and public, Life count rises, no [Trigger]. Nothing is asked when no CP card is in hand ("up to").
- **Q11. OP18-051 Mr.4 and OP18-064 Saint Killingham, "up to 1 Character ... and up to 1 Character ...".** `pick.groups` is not implemented; the pattern is two selects, the second excluding the first with `not ref` (effect-dsl.md §4.15). For OP18-064 it is exact. For OP18-051 the text adds "in any order of the owner's choosing": two separate `move` steps cannot ask the owner to order two cards of one owner (the engine orders only within one move, `bottomOrder` in `run.ts`). If the two Characters belong to the same player, the script puts them in the order picked. Either accept that (low stakes), or add `groups` to selectors (S to M; not counted above as no card needs more than this).
- **Q12. Text defects in the Limitless data (read before scripting).** OP18-071 "this Characters gains [Blocker]" (plural s), OP18-014 and OP18-006 carry the reminder text after the effect lines, OP18-105 has an empty first line (its only text is the [Trigger]). The text is "reliable" per the import, but each should be checked on the official card list at verification (PROMPT.md M1) and any change recorded as a `textFixes` entry (kind `cleanup`).
- **Q13. OP18-082 Catarina Devon, "This Character's base power becomes the same as your opponent's Leader's power during this turn."** Proposed: read once as the effect resolves (a snapshot: `statOf` of the opponent's Leader, current power), not a continuing link, and the Leader's power includes its given DON!! and modifiers.
- **Q14. OP18-004 Karoo, "Apply each of the following effects based on this Character's power".** Proposed: cumulative `tiers` (5000 Rush, 6000 Banish, 7000 Unblockable all at once at 7000), power = current power including given DON!! and effects such as OP18-001's +1000 (the grant stage reads the power stage).
- **Q15. OP18-022 Monkey.D.Luffy Leader, "You may set this Leader as active. If you do, this Leader will not become active in your next Refresh Phase."** Proposed: the Leader attacking is rested, becomes active, and the Refresh skip is a modifier on the Leader that ends after your next Refresh Phase whether or not the Leader is rested then (as `skipRefresh` already does for OP14 and OP15 cards).
- **Q16. OP18-037 Yokozuna, "Rest up to 1 of your opponent's cards."** Proposed: Leader, Characters, Stage and active cost-area DON!!, as for OP14-024 Kin'emon (M6b-Q5).
- **Q17. OP18-085 Cerberus, "gains [Rush], [Banish] or [Unblockable] during this turn".** Proposed: the player chooses one keyword (a `chooseOne` of three grants), and the target is chosen inside the chosen branch; the return-to-hand cost is paid first, so a Cerberus that returned itself leaves nothing else to cancel.
- **Q18. OP18-060 Saint Gunko Leader, "When a Character is played from your trash".** Proposed: any Character of yours played from your trash, by any effect (including your opponent's), once per turn; a Character played from the Life or deck does not count.
