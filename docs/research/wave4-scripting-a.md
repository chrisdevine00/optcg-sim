# Wave 4 scripting, group A (11 cards)

Cards: OP15-001, OP15-008, OP15-015, OP15-018, OP15-025, OP15-027, OP15-031, OP15-038, OP15-116, OP13-046, OP14-051. All scripted; none left over. Features: G34 (`donGiven` filter, `donGivenToTarget`, `refStat donGiven`), G30 (Life to trash), G14(b) (`replaces` list), and `[DON!! xN] [On K.O.]` via the last-known given DON!!. Giving the opponent's DON!! (G20) and `delayed` + `skipRefresh` were already in.

## Readings (one line each, with the card text)

- **OP15-001 Krieg (Leader)**, "[DON!! x1] [Opponent's Turn] If the only Characters on your field are {East Blue} type Characters, give all of your opponent's Characters -2000 power." A permanent with `donGiven: 1`, `opponentsTurn`, an `exists` of your Characters and a `none` of those that are not {East Blue}; false with no Characters (same open question as OP16-022, "the only Characters ... are" with none).
- **OP15-001**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters that has 2 or more DON!! cards given." `donGiven: { gte: 2 }`; "up to 1" is asked at max 0 when none qualifies.
- **OP15-008 Krieg (Character)**, "[On Play] Give up to 3 of your opponent's rested DON!! cards to 1 of your opponent's Characters. Then, this Character gains [Rush] during this turn." `giveDon { n: 3, state: 'rested', from: 'opponent' }` asks a count (most first) then the Character; the Rush is a separate step ("Then", not "if you do") and is granted even when nothing was given.
- **OP15-008**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If this Character was played on this turn, give all of your opponent's Characters -1000 power during this turn for every DON!! card given to that Character." Reading M6b-Q55: "that Character" is each affected Character in turn (`donGivenToTarget`). No colon, so the If is an effect condition (`playedThisTurn` on self): the activation is offered and Once Per Turn is spent when it is false.
- **OP15-015 Higuma**, "[On Play] Give up to 1 of your opponent's rested DON!! cards to 1 of your opponent's Characters. Then, give -1000 power during this turn to up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a DON!! card given." The -1000 is not conditional on the give; a Character that was just given a DON!! qualifies.
- **OP15-018 Mohji**, "[When Attacking] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 3000 power or less with a DON!! card given." Current power (so a -1000 brings a 4000 Character in range); the opponent's given DON!! add no power on your turn.
- **OP15-025 Kuro**, "[Blocker] [On Play] Give up to 2 DON!! cards from your opponent's cost area to 1 of your opponent's Characters. Then, at the end of this turn, up to 1 rested Character with 3 or more DON!! cards given will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase." Active or rested DON!! (M6b-Q44 reading, as OP15-023). The Character of the second half is read as the opponent's (the text says "your opponent's next Refresh Phase"; one of yours cannot refresh then); rested state and the DON!! count are read at the end of the turn (`delayed`), and the step is not conditional on the give.
- **OP15-027 Dracule Mihawk**, "[On Play] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a DON!! card given." `donGiven: { gte: 1 }`.
- **OP15-031 Purinpurin**, "[On Play] Select up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters. If the chosen Character has a cost equal to the number of DON!! cards given to it, K.O. it." `select` bound, then `refStat cost = refStat donGiven` (current cost; a cost-0 card with no DON!! would match, no such Character exists).
- **OP15-038 It's an Order! Do Not Defy Me!!!**, "[Main] Up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with a cost of 8 or less that has 2 or more DON!! cards given will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase. [Counter] Up to 1 of your [Krieg] cards gains +4000 power during this battle." `skipRefresh` on a filtered pick; the Counter targets your Leader or Characters named Krieg.
- **OP15-116 Gum-Gum Golden Rifle**, "[Main] If your Leader has the {Straw Hat Crew} type, trash 1 card from the top of your Life cards. Then, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards and trash 1 card from your hand." The whole Main is inside the Leader guard (a "Then" after a failed "If" does not resolve, M4b-5); the Life trash is G30 (not damage, no [Trigger]); "Then" is not "if you do": with no Life the add and hand trash still happen. The gate is public (the Leader's type); the hand trash is mandatory and the Event is already in the trash.
- **OP13-046 Vista**, "[Once Per Turn] If this Character would be K.O.'d or would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may trash 1 card with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates" from your hand instead." `replaces` as a list of two entries (G14 b), one ability, one Once Per Turn. **M6b-Q6 stays open**: this is the "one use covers both events" reading (using it for a K.O. leaves none for a later removal the same turn); the two-ability reading would give two uses. The offer is made even when the hand holds no {Whitebeard Pirates} card but only declining is legal (the cost cannot be paid); the K.O. entry has no cause, so a battle K.O. is covered, and the removal entry is the opponent's effects only.
- **OP14-051 Hatchan**, "[DON!! x2] [On K.O.] Draw 1 card." `donGiven: 2` on an `onKO` auto; reads the last-known given DON!! (M6b-Q53), so 2 or more draws 1 and 1 or 0 does not.

## Engine notes

No engine bug found. Observed (not a bug): a given DON!! returns to its owner's cost area at that owner's Refresh Phase, so a DON!! given by Higuma, Krieg or Kuro adds no power on the opponent's next turn; the -1000/-2000 effects here are the lasting part.

## Tests

One file per card in `engine/test/cards/` (OP15-001, 008, 015, 018, 025, 027, 031, 038, 116, OP13-046, OP14-051), helpers in `engine/test/cards/helpers-w4sa.ts`. Each has the effect, the exact thresholds (DON!! count, power, cost), an "up to" decline and a must-not-fire case; interactive prompts are driven with raw `g.act` where an empty pick matters.
