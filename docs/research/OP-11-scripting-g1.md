# OP-11 scripting, group 1 of 3 (block 3): readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP11-001, 002, 004, 007, 008, 009, 010, 013, 014, 016, 018, 019, 020, 021, 022, 024, 025, 027, 028, 029, 030, 034, 035, 036 (24 scripted, none left out; every card is `verified: true` in `cards/data/sets/OP-11.json` and has an override in `cards/overrides/`). Gaps from `docs/research/m6b-audit/block3.md`, mapped through `m6b-runtime-audit.md` §2: B3-G9 = G32 (OP11-010, 014; wave 4, `canAttackActive`), B3-G14 = G10 (OP11-013; wave 1, `cannotActivateBlocker`), B3-G12 = G28 (OP11-024, 035; wave 3, `koCause`), B3-G13 = G2 (OP11-034; wave 1, `cannotBeRested`); all merged. Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. No new rulings were invented; M6b open questions stay open.

Method notes: no prompt is gated on a filtered hidden-zone condition. The plays from hand (OP11-022, 024, 035) and the deck searches (OP11-004, 030, 036) are plain "up to 1" picks, asked even when nothing matches. The only count gates read public facts (OP11-021: the hand size is public). A trash cost from hand (OP11-008, 024) is a real cost, so with an empty hand the engine still asks "pay the cost?" and only "no" is legal (the answer must not reveal the hand).

- **OP11-001 Koby**, "Your "SWORD" type Characters can attack Characters on the turn in which they are played. [Once Per Turn] If your "Navy" type Character with 7000 base power or less would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may place 3 cards from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order instead." The first sentence is `Rush: Character` granted to every {SWORD} Character (the Leader is not a Character). The second is a `leaveField` replacement (cause `opponentEffect`: a K.O., bounce, trash or deck move by the opponent's effect; battle and your own effects are not), Once Per Turn when used, exact cost "3 cards from the trash" (not offered with fewer), the owner orders them. The filter is {Navy} and BASE power <= 7000 (M6b-Q6 is about the K.O.-plus-removal list; here the text says only "removed", so the single `leaveField` entry covers an opponent-effect K.O. too, as OP15-035).
- **OP11-002 Ain**, "[On Play] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −1000 power during this turn. Then, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 0 power or less." "Then" is sequential, not "If you do": the K.O. is chosen even when no power was given. "0 power or less" is CURRENT power, so the Character just lowered and a base-0 Character qualify (and one at 1 power does not).
- **OP11-004 Kujyaku**, "[On Play] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Navy" type card other than "Kujyaku" and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: Up to 1 of your Characters gains +1000 power during this turn." The look takes any category of {Navy} card; the name filter is the exact name (a second copy is excluded). The Activate's cost trashes Kujyaku first, so the +1000 cannot target herself.
- **OP11-007 Tashigi**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: If your Leader has the "Navy" type, up to 1 of your "Navy" type Characters gains +2000 power during this turn." The "If" is after the colon, so an effect condition, not an activation condition: the rest cost is paid even with a non-Navy Leader and the effect does nothing. Tashigi may boost herself.
- **OP11-008 Doll**, "[Blocker] [On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: If your Leader has the "Navy" type, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −6000 power during this turn." Same shape: cost first, then the Leader check as an effect condition. [Blocker] is printed (keyword from the override).
- **OP11-009 Nico Robin**, "[DON!! x2] [When Attacking] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power until the end of your opponent's next turn." `donGiven: 2` (2 or more); the plain duration `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` (still lowered on their turn, restored when it ends).
- **OP11-010 Hibari**, "[On Play] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn. [When Attacking] This Character gains +1000 power during this turn. Then, up to 1 of your "Navy" type Leader can also attack active Characters during this turn." The Leader grant is its own "up to 1" selection of a {Navy} Leader (G32 `canAttackActive`), not conditional on the +1000 ("Then"); with a non-{Navy} Leader no choice is offered. "Also": attacks on rested Characters and the Leader stay legal.
- **OP11-013 Prince Grus**, "[When Attacking] All of your opponent's Characters with 2000 power or less cannot activate [Blocker] during this turn." `cannotActivateBlocker` (G10) on every matching Character, chosen when the effect resolves on CURRENT power (a snapshot: a Character lowered to 2000 afterwards, or played afterwards, can still block). 2001 can block, 2000 cannot.
- **OP11-014 Borsalino**, "[Blocker] [Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: Up to 1 of your "Navy" type Leader or Character cards can also attack active Characters during this turn." A selector over Leader and Characters filtered to {Navy}; Borsalino rests as the cost, so he can be chosen only for no effect (he is rested).
- **OP11-016 Roronoa Zoro**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." As OP13-081 and OP14-005: `giveDon` n 1, state rested, one target; no count is asked at n 1, and with no rested DON!! nothing is given (the use is spent).
- **OP11-018 Honesty Impact**, "[Main] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −4000 power during this turn. Then, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 6000 power or less." / "[Trigger] K.O. up to 1 ... 6000 power or less." "Then" is sequential; the K.O. reads CURRENT power, so a 9000 Character lowered to 5000 can be K.O.'d and 6000 is the exact boundary. The Event counts itself in the trash once it resolves (no condition reads it).
- **OP11-019 Glorp Web!!**, "[Counter] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +2000 power during this battle. Then, if your opponent has a Character with 6000 power or more, up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +1000 power during this turn." / "[Trigger] ... +1000 power during this turn." The "if" is an `if` step read after the first boost resolves, on CURRENT power of an opponent's Character (6000 counts; the attacker itself counts). The second boost is a separate "up to 1" pick that may name a different card. The +2000 ends with the battle, the +1000 with the turn.
- **OP11-020 X Calibur**, "[Main] Give up to 2 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn. Then, up to 1 of your "Navy" type Characters gains +1000 power during this turn." / "[Trigger] K.O. up to 1 ... 4000 power or less." "Up to 2" may be 0, 1 or 2; the {Navy} boost does not depend on the first step (not "If you do").
- **OP11-021 Jinbe (Leader)**, "[End of Your Turn] If you have 6 or less cards in your hand, set up to 1 of your "Fish-Man" or "Merfolk" type Characters and up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active." A public hand-size condition on the ability (6 exact; 7 does not fire), then two independent steps: a Character pick, and the DON!! count ("up to 1": asked as a count with 1 or None, as OP14-R2).
- **OP11-022 Shirahoshi (Leader)**, "This Leader cannot attack. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards and turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-up: Play up to 1 "Neptunian" type Character card or "Megalo" with a cost equal to or less than the number of DON!! cards on your field from your hand." `cannotAttack` on self (a permanent restriction); the Activate's costs are `restDon 1` and `turnLifeFaceUp 1` (the top Life card). "The number of DON!! on your field" is read when the play resolves, after the rest, and counts cost-area DON!! (rested too) and given DON!! (rules-digest: the field includes the cost area). The filter is a Character that is {Neptunian} or named exactly [Megalo], cost <= that number; the pick is "up to 1", asked without looking at the hand. The cost is paid even if nothing is played. M6b-G8's convention for an already face-up top card: the cost needs a face-down top Life card (an already face-up top card makes `turnLifeFaceUp` unpayable, M4b-3), not merely a Life card.
- **OP11-024 Aladine**, "When this Character is K.O.'d by your opponent's effect, you may trash 1 card from your hand and rest 1 of your DON!! cards. If you do, play up to 1 "Fish-Man" or "Merfolk" type Character card with a cost of 6 or less from your hand." An `onKO` auto with `koCause: opponentEffect` (G28: the other player's effect, a cost of theirs included; not battle, not your own effect). Both costs are paid together (an auto with a cost is optional); "If you do" is the cost-then-effect order. The trashed card is gone before the play (it cannot be played), and the play is an unguarded "up to 1" pick.
- **OP11-025 Ishilly**, "[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards and this Character: Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +1000 power during this battle." Cost `restDon 1` + `restSelf`; Ishilly may boost herself. Once Per Turn counts when used.
- **OP11-027 Bulge-Eyed Neptunian**, "If your Leader is [Shirahoshi], this Character can attack Characters on the turn in which it is played." A permanent `Rush: Character` on self with the Leader exactly named [Shirahoshi]. Rush: Character means rested Characters only on the turn it is played, never the Leader.
- **OP11-028 Lord of the Coast**, "[On Play] Up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase." / "[Trigger] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with a cost of 3 or less." `skipRefresh` on a rested Character (Characters only, not DON!!); only the next Refresh is skipped. The Trigger's filter is rested and current cost <= 3.
- **OP11-029 Charlotte Praline**, "[Blocker] [On Play] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 1 or less." Current cost; Characters only.
- **OP11-030 Shirahoshi**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards and this Character: Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Neptunian" or "Fish-Man Island" type card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." Costs `restDon 1` + `restSelf`; both types are exact elements; any category.
- **OP11-034 Hatchan**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: If your Leader has the "Fish-Man" or "Merfolk" type, up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 3 or less cannot be rested until the end of your opponent's next turn." The "If" is after the colon (an effect condition, the cost is paid regardless). The restriction is the full `cannotBeRested` (G2): it cannot attack or block and cannot be rested by your effects either (verified with a planted rest effect). It ends with the end of the opponent's next turn.
- **OP11-035 Fisher Tiger**, "When this Character is K.O.'d by your opponent's effect, you may rest 1 of your DON!! cards. If you do, play up to 1 "Fish-Man" or "Merfolk" type Character card with a cost of 4 or less from your hand. [On Play] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters." As OP11-024 with a single rest cost; the [On Play] rest takes any Character (Characters only, any cost).
- **OP11-036 Spotted Neptunian**, "[On Play] If your Leader is "Shirahoshi", look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Neptunian" type card or "Shirahoshi" and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." A leading "If" on an auto without a colon is an effect condition (an effect-level `if`, made consistent with OP11-041 in the B3A fix round), so with another Leader nothing is looked at. "Shirahoshi" in the take is the exact card name (OP11-030 or any other [Shirahoshi]); the {Neptunian} type is an exact element.
