# EB-04 scripting, group 1 of 2: readings and ambiguities

Cards: EB04-001, 003, 004, 005, 006, 008, 009, 010, 011, 012, 013, 014, 015, 016, 017, 018, 019, 020, 021, 022, 024, 025, 026, 027 (24 scripted, none left out; every card is `verified: true` in the card data, cross-checked on 2026-11-24). EB04-048 is unverified (B4-V1) and not in this group. Gaps from `docs/research/m6b-audit/block4.md`, mapped through `m6b-runtime-audit.md` §2: B4-G18 = G7 (EB04-009, wave 1, the `giveDon` cost), B4-G35 = G21 (EB04-016, wave 3, `cannotSetDonActive`); all merged. No card needs a wave 4+ gap, a design item D1-D9, `[DON!! xN] [On K.O.]` or a double-event replacement (EB04-022's `[DON!! x1]` is on a [When Attacking]). Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. No new rulings were invented; M6b open questions stay open.

Method notes: no prompt is gated on a filtered hidden-zone condition. The plays from hand (EB04-015, 017, 025, 027) are plain "up to 1" picks, asked even when nothing matches; the gates read only public facts (Life count, the opponent's hand size, field Characters, the Leader's type). Leading "If" on an auto is an ability `conditions` entry only for EB04-016's [When Attacking]; the "If" after a colon or after a bare timing tag with no colon is an effect `if` step.

- **EB04-001 Jewelry Bonney (Leader)**, "[Opponent's Turn] If you have 1 or less Life cards, this Leader gains +2000 power. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -1000 power during this turn. Then, if you have 2 or more Life cards, you may add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand." A permanent with `opponentsTurn` and a live Life count (0 counts as "1 or less"). The "Then, if" is gated inside an `if` (M4b-5), checked after the power step; the Life card goes to the hand with no [Trigger] (not damage); the optional step is asked even with no opposing Character (the power is "up to 1").
- **EB04-003 Smoker & Tashigi**, "[Rush] [Opponent's Turn] Your {Navy} type Leader's base power becomes 7000." `setBasePower` on the {Navy} Leader as a permanent under `opponentsTurn`; later +power still adds (RL-33: with several base-power effects the highest applies). "Becomes 7000" would also lower a Navy Leader printed above 7000 (the effect sets the value); no such Leader is expected.
- **EB04-004 Zeff**, "[When Attacking] Your Leader's base power becomes 7000 until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." `setBasePower` on your Leader, plain duration `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` (the engine splits own-turn and opponent-turn creation). No "up to", no type: always set (also down from a higher printed power). Given DON!! still add on top on your turn.
- **EB04-005 Trafalgar Law**, "This Character cannot attack unless your opponent has 2 or more Characters with a base power of 5000 or more." A permanent `cannotAttack` on self while the opposing count of base power >= 5000 is below 2. BASE power: a boosted 4000 does not count, a lowered 5000 does; the Leader never counts; rested Characters count.
- **EB04-006 Moda**, "[On Play] Look at 7 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 [Lulucia Kingdom] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." `[Lulucia Kingdom]` in square brackets is a card NAME (the Stage EB04-010), not the {Lulucia Kingdom} type (Moda herself and OP07-014 carry the type but not the name). Reading to review: if the printed card meant the type, the filter would be `type`. May take nothing.
- **EB04-008 Distorted Future**, "[Main] If you have 2 or less Life cards, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -3000 power during this turn. [Counter] Your Leader gains +3000 power during this battle." The "If" follows [Main] with no colon, so it is an effect `if`: the Event is played (cost paid) and does nothing with 3 or more Life. Boundary pinned at 2/3 (0 and 1 work).
- **EB04-009 It's My Student's Farewell. I Want It to Be Proper.**, "[Main] You may give 1 active DON!! card to 1 of your [Silvers Rayleigh]: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -2000 power during this turn. [Counter] Up to 1 of your Characters or [Silvers Rayleigh] gains +2000 power during this battle." G7 `giveDon` cost (active, exactly 1 target of the Leader or a Character named [Silvers Rayleigh]), `costOptional` (Event). When the cost cannot be paid (no Rayleigh, no active DON!!) the question is still asked but accepting is not legal. [Counter]: "Characters or [Silvers Rayleigh]" is any of your Characters, or a Rayleigh Leader; any other Leader is not a target.
- **EB04-010 Lulucia Kingdom (Stage)**, "[Opponent's Turn] All of your Characters with a base cost of 1 gain +5000 power. [On Play] Set the power of up to 1 of your opponent's Characters to 0 during this turn." The buff is a permanent under `opponentsTurn` on `baseCost` 1 (printed cost), covering Characters that arrive later. "Set the power to 0" is `setBasePower` 0 for the turn (M6b-Q9): later +power this turn still adds; an official ruling that later buffs do not apply would change this.
- **EB04-011 Scaled Neptunian**, "[Rush: Character] [On Play] Draw a card for each of your {Neptunian} type Characters. Then, trash the same number of cards from your hand." `draw` of a `count` and a `forEach` of the same count, one mandatory trash each (no "none" offered: "Then" is not "if you do"). The count includes this Character (on the field at resolution) and only your own {Neptunian} Characters. Printed keyword `Rush: Character` (from the override).
- **EB04-012 Kikunojo**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If this Character was played on this turn, set your {Land of Wano} type Leader as active." The "If" is not before a colon, so it is an effect `if` (`self` `playedThisTurn`): on a later turn the ability can be activated (and burns Once Per Turn) but does nothing. The Leader must be {Land of Wano}.
- **EB04-013 Carrot**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Minks} type, set up to 2 of your {Minks} type Characters and your Leader as active." Read as two groups: up to 2 {Minks} Characters, and the Leader, which is not an "up to" choice (it is never worse to set it). Both inside the `if`. Reading to review: the alternative "up to 2 cards in total across Characters and Leader" is not scripted.
- **EB04-014 Kouzuki Sukiyaki**, "[Blocker] [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your {Land of Wano} type Leader." `giveDon` n 1 rested to the {Land of Wano} Leader as a min-0 target pick (as OP14-005): once activated the give can be declined by picking nothing; a Leader of another type gets nothing (the activation is still used).
- **EB04-015 Jinbe**, "[Blocker] [On K.O.] You may rest 1 of your cards: If your Leader has the {Fish-Man} or {Merfolk} type, play up to 1 green Character card with a cost of 6 or less from your hand." Auto with a `rest` cost (Leader, Character, Stage or cost-area DON!!, active only). Jinbe is already in the trash, so he cannot pay it with himself. The Leader check is after the colon (effect `if`), so with another Leader the cost is paid and nothing is played; the play is an unguarded "up to 1" pick. With nothing active to rest the cost cannot be paid and the auto is dropped without a question. No {Merfolk} card exists in the card data, so only {Fish-Man} is tested.
- **EB04-016 Bird Neptunian**, "[Activate: Main] Set up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active. Then, you cannot set DON!! cards as active using Character effects during this turn. [When Attacking] If you have 3 or more {Neptunian} type Characters, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 8 or less." G21 `cannotSetDonActive` on the player, created after the set, with no condition on the set ("Then", not "If you do"). The Activate has no Once Per Turn, so a second use is possible and sets nothing; the lock covers every Character's effect for the turn. The [When Attacking] "If" is an ability condition (leading "If" on an auto), counting this Character and checked at the trigger and at resolution. The "up to 1" DON!! asks a count (most first, "0" last) when several are rested.
- **EB04-017 Mystoms**, "[Your Turn] If you have 3 or more {Minks} type Characters, give all of your opponent's Characters -1 cost. [On Play] If your Leader has the {Minks} type, play up to 1 {Minks} type Character card with a cost of 5 or less from your hand." The -1 cost is a permanent (`yourTurn` and a live count that includes this Character) and covers Characters that arrive later; a cost cannot go below 0. The [On Play] Leader check is an effect `if`, the play an unguarded "up to 1" pick.
- **EB04-018 Megalo**, "[On Play] You may rest this Character: K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with 8000 power or less." `restSelf` cost (optional by construction), `ko` of a rested opposing Character by CURRENT power <= 8000 (a boosted 8000 is out). With no candidate the prompt is skipped (public zone).
- **EB04-019 Eleclaw**, "[Main] You may rest 1 of your cards: If your Leader has the {Minks} type, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -3 cost during this turn. [Counter] Up to 1 of your {Minks} type Leader or Character cards gains +3000 power during this battle." `costOptional` with a `rest` of 1 of your cards; the Leader check after the colon is an effect `if` (the cost is paid, then nothing, with another Leader). [Counter]: a {Minks} Leader or Character; others are not targets.
- **EB04-020 Shark Brick Fist**, "[Counter] Up to 1 of your {Fish-Man} type Leader or Character cards gains +3000 power during this battle. Then, set up to 1 of your {Fish-Man} type Characters as active. [Trigger] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less." "Then" is independent of the power step (no "If you do"); the set-active targets Characters only (a {Fish-Man} Leader is not one). [Trigger]: cost <= 4, current cost.
- **EB04-021 Igaram**, "[On Play] If your Leader is [Nefeltari Vivi], draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." The Leader check is by card name; draw and trash are two mandatory steps inside the `if`. The Activate has an optional-by-construction cost (not `costOptional`: not an Event); with no hand card it is not offered, with no rested DON!! the cost is paid and nothing is given. As OP14-005 the give can be declined once activated.
- **EB04-022 Issho**, "[On Play] You may trash 2 cards from your hand: If your opponent has 6 or more cards in their hand, your opponent places 2 cards from their hand at the bottom of their deck in any order. [DON!! x1] [When Attacking] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -2000 power during this turn." The opponent's hand size is checked after your cost is paid; they choose which 2 and the order (the owner orders). With 5 or fewer cards nothing is placed (the cost stays paid). The hand-trash cost with too few cards: the question is still asked, accepting is not legal (hidden hand, so nothing is skipped). [When Attacking] uses `donGiven: 1`.
- **EB04-024 Terracotta**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character and trash 1 card from your hand: Up to 1 of your {Alabasta} type Characters gains [Unblockable] during this turn." Two costs (`restSelf`, hand trash); no Once Per Turn in the text, but the rest cost limits it to once per untap. She may target herself. The grant lasts this turn.
- **EB04-025 Nefeltari Vivi**, "[On Play] Play up to 1 {Alabasta} type Character card with a cost of 8 or less other than [Nefeltari Vivi] from your hand. Then, your opponent places 1 card from their hand at the bottom of their deck." "Then" is independent of the play (not "if you do"): the opponent places a card whether or not you played one; with an empty hand nothing is placed. The opponent chooses the card. The data text says "from their hand" after the override (typo fixed by the verification pass).
- **EB04-026 Bluegrass**, "[On Play] Place up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 1 or less at the bottom of the owner's deck. [When Attacking] Draw 1 card and trash 1 card from your hand." `move` to the deck bottom of the opposing Character (cost 1 or less, current cost, cost 0 included); the draw and the trash are two mandatory steps (the drawn card may be trashed).
- **EB04-027 Boa Hancock**, "[On Play] Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand. [Trigger] Play up to 1 Character card with 5000 power or less and a [Trigger] from your hand." The [Trigger] filter reads printed power in the hand and `hasTrigger`; the played Character's own [Trigger] is not used (it is played, not revealed from Life).

## Engine observations (not bugs, for the review)

- A `costOptional` Event or an `auto` with a hand cost is asked even when its cost cannot be paid, with accepting illegal; an auto with a rest cost and nothing active to rest is dropped without a question. Both are the engine's existing behaviour and neither leaks.
- The `Blocker` keyword on an unscripted card does not exist in tests (keywords come from scripts): a stand-in blocker must be a scripted card (ST10-010).
