# OP-13 scripting, group 2 (OP13-032 to OP13-058)

Readings and ambiguities, one per card. Scripts: `cards/effects/OP-13/`; tests: `engine/test/cards/OP13-0xx.test.ts`; helpers: `engine/test/cards/helpers-op13g2.ts`. Open questions stay open (M6b-Q6 for Vista).

Scripted (21): 032, 033, 034, 035, 038, 039, 041, 042, 043, 044, 045, 047, 050, 051, 052, 053, 054, 055, 056, 057, 058.

Left unscripted (1):

- **OP13-046 Vista** "[Double Attack] [Once Per Turn] If this Character would be K.O.'d or would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may trash 1 card with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates" from your hand instead." Needs **G14(b)** (wave 2 built only G14(a), `leaveField` with `cause: 'any'`): one replacement covering two events with ONE Once Per Turn. `replaces` is a single event (`engine/src/../cards/src/dsl.ts` `ReplaceEvent`), so two abilities would each get a use per turn, and `leaveField any` would over-cover (a removal by your own effect). Recorded in `docs/research/m6b-wave2.md` as not built; M6b-Q6 is the open question. The same reasoning applies to the optional hand-trash payload, which is fine in itself (OP16-018 Rockstar). Mapped gap: B4-G16 = unified G14.

Mapping of the audit's gaps for the scripted cards (all merged in waves 1 to 2): B4-G2 (OP13-032) = G2 `cannotBeRested` (wave 1); B4-G21 (OP13-039) = G5 `activate` own [Counter] (wave 1); B4-G15 (OP13-047) = G11 `ko` with a cause (wave 2); B4-G17 (OP13-053) = G1 cost on own Characters (wave 1). The other cards had no gap.

Readings:

- **OP13-032 Nico Robin**: "until the end of your opponent's next End Phase" is `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`, as OP14-033. The restriction is the unscoped `cannotBeRested`, which also stops the Character from attacking and from activating [Blocker] (rules-digest 57). The tests have no cost-9 vanilla, so the "8 or less" boundary is pinned at 8 (in) and 10 (out).
- **OP13-033 Franky**: "Rest up to 2 of your opponent's cards" rests Leader, Characters, Stage or DON!! (M4b-1, closed), `pick: { upTo: 2 }`. The controller of Franky chooses, even though the K.O. may have been an opponent's effect.
- **OP13-034 Brook**: "has the 'FILM' or 'Straw Hat Crew' type" is `type: FILM` or `type: Straw Hat Crew` on the Leader (an exact type element). DON!! are fungible, so which rested DON!! is set active is never asked.
- **OP13-035 Bepo**: "Set this Character or up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active" is a `chooseOne` at the end of your turn (a mode choice). Both modes are always offered, even if the Character is already active (harmless; the DON!! mode is "up to 1").
- **OP13-038 Gum-Gum Elephant Gun**: "Then, set up to 2 ... at the end of this turn" is a `delayed` `endOfThisTurn` step that is not gated on the rest ("Then" is not "if you do", as OP14-031). The [Trigger] has only the rest.
- **OP13-039 Gum-Gum Snake Shot**: the Event has only a [Counter]; the [Trigger] is `activate: counter of self`. Played from a [Trigger] there is no battle, and its effect (a K.O. with no "during this battle" modifier) resolves fully (wave 1 G5). "Rested Characters" is `state: rested`, a current state, so the attacking Character (rested by attacking) is a candidate if its cost is 4 or less.
- **OP13-042 Edward.Newgate**: "give your Leader and 1 Character up to 2 rested DON!! cards each" is a `select` of exactly 1 Character (bound), then `giveDon each` over the Leader plus the bound Character, `n: 2` rested. The player is asked a count for each target (2 down to 1, wave 2 G16); a target never gets "0" (the engine asks 1 to N per target; give-none is the limit of the `each` form, see wave 2 note 7). Edward himself can be the Character (it is "1 Character", not "1 other"), and with no other Character he is chosen. Trashing 1 card runs after the draw, so the drawn cards are candidates.
- **OP13-043 Otama / OP13-054 Yamato**: `cmp life <= 3` is public. Yamato's "Then, give ..." is inside the guard (M4b-5, closed: a "Then" after a failed "If" does not resolve). "give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader" is `giveDon n 1 rested` to the Leader.
- **OP13-044 Curiel**: "Leader with a type including X or 1 Character with a type including X" is one `giveDon` target pick (`upTo: 1`) over Leader and Characters with `typeIncludes: Whitebeard Pirates` (substring of a type element, CR 2-4-3-1). Curiel counts as a Character with the type, so he may give to himself. [On K.O.] draws 1 unconditionally.
- **OP13-045 Haruta / OP13-055 Rakuyo**: "4 or less cards in your hand" is `hand <= 4` at resolution (public). Rakuyo's +1000 is a no-pick `power` step over your Characters with the type (itself included), this turn only; a Character played after the attack gets nothing.
- **OP13-047 Fossa**: a `ko` replacement with `cause: opponentEffect` for your Characters with `typeIncludes Whitebeard Pirates`, payload `trashSelf`. Battle K.O.s, your own effects and removal other than K.O. (a bounce) are not covered. Reading: Fossa herself has the type, so her own K.O. is covered and she may be trashed in place of being K.O.'d (pointless, but the text says "your Character with ..."); no `isSelf` exclusion. Not Once Per Turn (the text has none).
- **OP13-050 / OP13-052**: "If your Leader is [Boa Hancock]" is `leader: { name: 'Boa Hancock' }`, any Leader of that name. The hand pick is "up to 1" over a hidden zone, gated only on the public Leader name. [Boa Hancock] filter is `name` exact plus category Character, cost 3 and 6 boundaries.
- **OP13-051 Boa Hancock**: "[Boa Hancock] or multicolored" is `any: [name, multicolor]` on the Leader.
- **OP13-053 Marshall.D.Teach**: "You may trash 1 of your Characters with a type including ...:" is an optional auto cost over your Characters (Teach himself qualifies; with no other candidate the cost is paid without a choice). The draw and the [Banish] grant (this turn, to Teach) follow the cost; if Teach trashed himself the grant does nothing.
- **OP13-057 / OP13-058**: the Events' "You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards:" is `restDon: 1` with `costOptional`. 057's condition after the colon is an effect-level `if` on Life (`<= 1`), so the cost is paid even when the condition fails. "Whenever your Leader attacks" is the `cannotActivateBlockerAgainstIt` restriction on the Leader this turn (wave 1 G27), so a Character's attack can still be blocked. 058 uses current `power <= 3000` and the bottom of the owner's deck, a single card (no order). [Counter] +3000 is "during this battle".
