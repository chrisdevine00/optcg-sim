# ST-18 to ST-21 scripting: readings and ambiguities

Cards (24 scripted): ST18-001 to 005, ST19-001 to 005, ST20-001, 002, 004, 005, ST21-001, 002, 004, 007, 009, 010, 011, 012, 015, 016. All 24 assigned cards were scripted; none needed a wave 5+ gap or a design item. Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. Gaps used (all merged): G11 `ko` replacement with a cause (ST20-002), G30 Life to trash (ST20-002, ST20-005), G10 `cannotActivateBlocker` (ST21-016), `ev.don` for `[DON!! xN] [On K.O.]` (ST21-004), `from: 'self'` triggers (ST20-002).

- **ST18-001 Uso-Hachi**, "[On Play] If you have 8 or more DON!! cards on your field, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less." The "If" has no colon, so it is an `if` step (B3A-R1). DON!! "on your field" is the cost area plus DON!! given to cards (rules digest); 8 passes, 7 does not. The cost limit is the current cost.
- **ST18-002 O-Nami**, "[On Play] If you have 8 or more DON!! cards on your field, trash 1 card from your hand and draw 2 cards." Both instructions sit inside the `if`. "Trash ... and draw" is read as two independent instructions (not "if you do"): with an empty hand the 2 cards are still drawn. The trash is mandatory (exactly 1), asked of the owner.
- **ST18-003 San-Gorou**, "[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] If you have 8 or more DON!! cards on your field, draw 1 card." Colon-less "If": an effect-level `if`, Once Per Turn spent even when the check fails (B3A-R1). Each copy has its own Once Per Turn.
- **ST18-004 Zoro-Juurou**, "[On Play] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 purple \"Straw Hat Crew\" type card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." One `lookAtTop` (5, take up to 1 revealed, `bottomAnyOrder`). The filter names no category, so a purple {Straw Hat Crew} Event or Stage is takeable; a card that merely contains purple (multicolor) counts as purple.
- **ST18-005 Luffy-Tarou**, "[On Play] DON!! −1 (You may return the specified number of DON!! cards from your field to your DON!! deck.): Play up to 1 purple \"Straw Hat Crew\" type Character card with a cost of 5 or less from your hand." DON!! −1 is the optional cost (accept prompt, then which DON!!); the cost filter is the printed cost in hand; the Character comes in active. The hand is hidden, so the pick is asked even when nothing in the hand qualifies (at a maximum of 0).
- **ST19-001 Smoker**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 black \"Navy\" type card from your hand: Up to 2 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less cannot attack until the end of your opponent's next turn." Cost: a black {Navy} card of any category. "Until the end of your opponent's next turn" is the plain `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` duration (the lock lasts the whole of their next turn and expires at its End Phase). Cost limit read when the targets are chosen. With no black {Navy} card in hand the prompt is still shown (the hand is hidden) but only declining is legal.
- **ST19-002 Sengoku**, "[On Play] You may trash 2 black \"Navy\" type cards from your hand: If your Leader has the \"Navy\" type, draw 3 cards." The "If" is after the cost colon, so it is an effect condition: the cost is paid and, with a non-{Navy} Leader, nothing is drawn.
- **ST19-003 Tashigi**, "[On Play] If your Leader is [Smoker], give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −4 cost during this turn. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If this Character was played on this turn, trash up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 0." Both "If" clauses have no colon, so both are effect-level `if` steps (B3A-R1): the Activate is always offered and spends Once Per Turn even when Tashigi was not played this turn. "Cost of 0" is the current cost (the -4 can create a target; a printed-0 Character never exists). The trash is a move to the trash, not a K.O. Open: if the Activate were meant as an activation condition (the colon-less rule being wrong for an Activate), it would not be offered when Tashigi was not played this turn.
- **ST19-004 Hina**, "[DON!! x1] [Opponent's Turn] This Character gains +4 cost. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may place 1 card from your trash at the bottom of your deck: Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." The cost bonus is a permanent with `donGiven: 1` and `opponentsTurn`. The give is "up to 1" of a single rested DON!!, so no count is asked; with no rested DON!! the cost is still paid and nothing is given. A single card to the deck bottom needs no order.
- **ST19-005 Monkey.D.Garp**, "[Blocker] [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may place 1 card from your trash at the bottom of your deck: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −1 cost during this turn." Only Characters are targets (not the Leader); a cost-1 Character goes to 0, never below.
- **ST20-001 Charlotte Katakuri**, "[Blocker] [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-up: Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." Follows M6b-Q59: turning the top card face-up needs a face-down top card, so the Activate is not offered when the top Life card is already face-up (or there is no Life). Open: a ruling that allows the cost on an already face-up card would make it always payable with Life.
- **ST20-002 Charlotte Cracker**, "[Once Per Turn] If this Character would be K.O.'d by an effect, you may trash 1 card from the top of your Life cards instead. [Trigger] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Play this card." "By an effect" is the `cause: 'effect'` replacement (an effect of either player, a cost included, not battle). The replacement payload is `trashLife` (face up, not damage, no [Trigger]); it is not offered with no Life, and the K.O. then happens. The Trigger is a hand-trash cost (asked as a pick, not an accept prompt) then `play` from `self`.
- **ST20-004 Charlotte Pudding**, "[On Play] You may add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand: Set up to 1 of your \"Big Mom Pirates\" type Characters with a cost of 3 or less as active. [Trigger] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 3 or less." Pudding can set herself active (she is a cost-3 {Big Mom Pirates} Character). The Life card goes to the hand as a cost (no [Trigger]). The Trigger's target is a Character of cost 3 or less (current cost).
- **ST20-005 Charlotte Linlin**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Your opponent chooses one: • Your opponent trashes 2 cards from their hand. • Trash 1 card from the top of your opponent's Life cards." Modeled as OP17-049 (`chooseOne`, chooser opponent, neutral log labels). The opponent may choose the trash mode with fewer than 2 cards (they trash what they have) and the Life mode with no Life (nothing happens). The Life card is trashed, not damaged (no [Trigger]). Open: whether an opponent may pick a mode that cannot do anything (same reading as OP17-049, QA-OP17).
- **ST21-001 Monkey.D.Luffy (Leader)**, "[DON!! x1] [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Give up to 2 rested DON!! cards to 1 of your Characters." `[DON!! x1]` is an activation condition (`donGiven: 1`, at least 1 DON!! given to the Leader): with none the Activate is not offered and Once Per Turn is not spent. "Up to 2" asks a count; the target is a Character only (not the Leader).
- **ST21-002 Usopp**, "[DON!! x2] [Opponent's Turn] This Character gains +2000 power." A permanent with `donGiven: 2` and `opponentsTurn`, on this Character only.
- **ST21-004 Jewelry Bonney**, "[DON!! x2] [On K.O.] Draw 1 card." The same text and script as OP14-051 (M6b-Q53): the DON!! given are read as they were when she was K.O.'d.
- **ST21-007 Sentomaru**, "[Blocker]." Keyword only (`abilities: []`).
- **ST21-009 Nami**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Give up to 2 rested DON!! cards to 1 of your {Straw Hat Crew} type Leader or Character cards." One pick over Leader and Characters filtered by type, so a non-{Straw Hat Crew} Leader is not a target; Nami herself is.
- **ST21-010 Nico Robin**, "[DON!! x2] [When Attacking] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 4000 power or less." `donGiven: 2` as an ability condition; "4000 power" is the current power when the target is chosen (not base power).
- **ST21-011 Franky**, "[DON!! x2] [Opponent's Turn] All of your {Straw Hat Crew} type Characters with 4000 base power or less gain +1000 power." A permanent on every matching Character (Franky included, 4000 base). The limit is the printed base power: a buffed 4000-base Character still gets +1000, a 5000-base one never does.
- **ST21-012 Brook**, "[When Attacking] Give up to 2 rested DON!! cards to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." As OP14-012: a count (most first), all the DON!! to one card, which may be Brook.
- **ST21-015 Roronoa Zoro**, "[DON!! x2] This Character gains [Rush]. [On K.O.] Play up to 1 red Character card with 6000 power or less other than [Roronoa Zoro] from your hand." The `[DON!! x2]` covers the Rush line only (a permanent grant); the [On K.O.] is its own line and needs no DON!!. The power limit is the printed power of the card in hand; the card is excluded by name.
- **ST21-016 Gum-Gum Dawn Whip**, "[Main] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +1000 power during this turn. Then, up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 4000 power or less cannot activate [Blocker] during this turn. [Trigger] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 4000 power or less." "Then" is not "If you do": the Blocker lock resolves even when no +1000 target was picked. The 4000 power is the current power at selection (a snapshot, M6b wave 1 G10).
