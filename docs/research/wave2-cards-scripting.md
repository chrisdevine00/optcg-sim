# Wave 2 cards: scripting readings

Five cards that became expressible with M6b-2 wave 2 ([m6b-wave2.md](m6b-wave2.md)). Texts are the verified ones in `cards/data`. One line each.

- **EB05-052 Gloriosa**, "If you would take damage, you may trash this Character instead." (D4): `replacement` with `replaces: { damage: 'you' }`, `optional`, `instead: [{ trashSelf: true }]`. The damage does not happen at all (no Life card, no [Trigger]), as one replacement for a whole Double Attack hit, also at 0 Life (m6b-wave2 D4; M6b-Q5 and Q8 stay open as questions). [Trigger] "Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand": draw 2, then a mandatory trash 1 (the text is a sequence, not an "if you do").
- **OP17-018 The Power to Destroy the World** and **OP17-116 Fulgora**, "[Main] You may rest 2 of your DON!! cards: K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Stages." (G18): optional cost `restDon 2` (`costOptional`, Event), `ko` from `stage`; it is a real K.O., so "cannot be K.O.'d" protects the Stage. "[Counter] If you have 2 or more Characters with ...": a condition on resolution (`if` around the effect, so the Event is still playable and its cost spent when the condition fails), then up to 1 of your Leader or Characters gets +4000 for the battle. 018 filters `basePower >= 8000` (printed power); 116 filters `hasTrigger` (printed [Trigger]).
- **OP17-020 Shanks**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand or rest 1 of your DON!! cards: Up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase." (G19): `cost: [{ oneOf: [[trash 1 from hand], [restDon 1]] }]`. "You may" is the player choosing to activate, so no `costOptional` (the optional-cost check forbids it on a non-Event Activate: Main). `skipRefresh` on up to 1 rested opponent Character, picked on resolution; with none rested the cost is still paid.
- **OP17-043 Ganzui**, "If this Character would be removed from the field, you may trash 2 cards from your hand instead. [On Play] Your Leader's base power becomes 6000 until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." (G14): `leaveField` with `cause: 'any'` (the verified text has no "by your opponent's effect", audit Q3), payload trash exactly 2 from hand; not offered with fewer than 2 cards in hand. A rule trash for Character-area room is not covered (m6b-wave2 reading 4). [On Play] is `setBasePower` 6000 on the Leader, `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`; it is a set, so it lowers a Leader printed above 6000.

## Tooling finding (not fixed, `cards/src` is out of scope here)

`cards/src/optional-cost.ts` `unitFor` finds a replacement's text unit with `/would be/`; "If you would take damage" does not match, so `cards/test/optional-cost.test.ts` reports `EB05-052.damage-shield: no text unit found for this ability`. One-line fix: `/would (?:be|take)/`.
