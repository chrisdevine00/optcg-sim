# OP-11 scripting, group 3 of 3 (block 3)

30 cards, OP11-072 to OP11-119, all `verified: true`. All 30 scripted; none left unscripted. Gaps used (all merged in waves 1 to 4): G4 `mill` (OP11-082, -084, -098), G8 `turnLifeFaceDown` (OP11-100, -103, -104, -107, -108; the other B3-G11 users), G32 `canAttackActive` (OP11-082, -084, -119). Scripts are in `cards/effects/OP-11/`, tests in `engine/test/cards/OP11-*.test.ts`, helpers in `engine/test/cards/helpers-op11g3.ts`.

One line per reading, with the card text it concerns.

- OP11-072 Charlotte Mont-d'or: "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] DON!! −1, You may rest this Character: Your opponent places 2 cards from their trash at the bottom of their deck in any order. Then, add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand." The opponent chooses the 2 cards and the order; with fewer than 2 they place all they have. "Then" is not "if you do": the Life card is added regardless. The Life card is mandatory (`exactly: 1`), not "up to".
- OP11-075 Jaguar.D.Saul: "If your Leader is [Nico Robin] and you have 7 or more DON!! cards on your field": DON!! on the field = cost area plus given, so rested DON!! count. The [Trigger] runs the [On Play] with its condition re-checked.
- OP11-076 Hannyabal: Leader "has the "Impel Down" type" is the exact type element; the played Character is an "Impel Down" type Character with cost 3 or less.
- OP11-077 Randolph: "[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When a DON!! card on your field is returned to your DON!! deck": any return of your own DON!! (a DON!! −X cost or an effect of either player) on your turn; the opponent's own returns do not count. Randolph is a "Big Mom Pirates" type himself and may raise himself. The +2 cost lasts until the end of your opponent's next turn.
- OP11-080 Gear Two: "[Main] You may rest 2 of your DON!! cards: If your Leader's colors include blue, add up to 1 DON!! card ... and rest it." The condition is the effect's own `if` (after the colon), so with a non-blue Leader the cost is paid and nothing is added. A blue/purple Leader counts. "Add up to 1" asks the count. Observation: with fewer than 2 active DON!! left, the optional cost prompt is still shown but only "decline" is legal.
- OP11-082 Aramaki: "You may trash this Character: If your Leader has the "Navy" type, up to 1 of your "Navy" type Characters can also attack active Characters during this turn. Then, trash 2 cards from the top of your deck." The "Then" is inside the Leader guard (M4b-5), so with a non-Navy Leader no deck cards are trashed. Aramaki is in the trash when the Character is chosen, so he cannot be chosen.
- OP11-083 Caribou: "[On Play] Trash 2 cards from your hand": mandatory, as many as possible (1 or 0 cards trashes what is there).
- OP11-084 Kuzan: "[When Attacking] Up to 1 of your "Navy" type Leader or Character cards": the Leader is eligible with the "Navy" type; the grant lasts the turn.
- OP11-085 Kurozumi Orochi: "a "SMILE" type card with a cost of 5 or less from your trash": any category with the SMILE type (the cost filter excludes nothing else); cost 5 is allowed, 6 is not.
- OP11-086 Coribou: "[Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: Play up to 1 [Caribou] with a cost of 4 or less from your trash": the activation is offered whenever the cost can be paid, even with no [Caribou] in the trash. Coribou is not named [Caribou], so he never returns himself.
- OP11-090 Briscola: keyword-only [Blocker].
- OP11-091 Berry Good: "Your opponent places 3 Events from their trash at the bottom of their deck in any order": the opponent chooses which Events and the order; with fewer than 3 Events they place all they have; mandatory.
- OP11-095 Monkey.D.Garp: "You may place 3 "Navy" type cards from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order: Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to 1 of your Leader. Then, if there is a Character with a cost of 9 or more, K.O. ..." The order is asked even when the three cards are forced. The target of the DON!! is the Leader (asked with min 0, "up to 1"). "if there is a Character with a cost of 9 or more" counts either player's Characters by current cost; Garp himself (cost 8) does not. The "Then, if" is independent of whether a DON!! was given (it is not guarded by a failed "If").
- OP11-096 Ripper: "If you have a black "Navy" type Character other than [Ripper]": by name, so a second [Ripper] does not count. The Leader is not a Character, so a black Navy Leader alone does not give [Blocker]. Keywords on the card are empty (the [Blocker] is gained).
- OP11-097 After All These Years I'm Losing My Edge!!!: "if you have 10 or more cards in your trash": the Event is in the trash while it resolves and counts itself (9 + 1). The "Then" part is independent of the power target ("up to 1" may be empty). Cost 3 or less is by current cost; cost 4 is not a candidate.
- OP11-098 Blue Hole: "You may trash 3 cards from the top of your deck: K.O. ...": `mill` cost; the [Trigger] is a separate +1000 power this turn on up to 1 Leader or Character. Observation: with fewer than 3 cards in the deck the optional prompt is shown but cannot be accepted.
- OP11-099 I'm Gonna Be a Navy Officer!!!: "reveal up to 1 "Navy" type card other than [I'm Gonna Be a Navy Officer!!!] and add it to your hand. Then, trash the rest": the "other than" is by name; the rest are trashed, not placed back; the [Trigger] activates the [Main] (no cost).
- OP11-100 Otohime, OP11-108 Neptune, OP11-104 Shirley: the leading "If your Leader is [Shirahoshi]" precedes the "you may ... :" cost and is therefore checked before the cost is offered (OP11-104 has no Leader condition at all). The card to turn is the top Life card and it must be face-up, so with the top card face-down the cost cannot be paid and no prompt appears.
- OP11-103 Long-Jaw Neptunian, OP11-107 Topknot Neptunian, OP11-117 Fish-Man Island: "[Activate: Main] If your Leader is [Shirahoshi], you may ...:" the leading "If" before the colon is an activation condition, so with another Leader the ability is not offered. OP11-107 is Once Per Turn; the set-active happens at the end of the turn through `delayed`, whether or not the Character is rested by then. OP11-117 turns the top Life card face-up (it must be face-down) and its power goes to "Neptunian", "Fish-Man" or "Merfolk" type Characters (Characters only).
- OP11-104 Shirley: "place the rest at the top or bottom of the deck in any order": `topOrBottomAnyOrder` (all on top or all on the bottom, in an order the owner chooses), as other "top or bottom" looks.
- OP11-109 Pappag: "If you have [Camie]": a [Camie] on your Leader or Character area, any card of that name; one in hand, trash or on the opponent's side does not count. "draw 2 cards and trash 2 cards" are both inside the guard.
- OP11-110 Fukaboshi: "If this Character would be K.O.'d, you may rest 1 of your [Fish-Man Island] or your [Shirahoshi] Leader instead": the replacement covers every K.O. cause (battle included). "[Fish-Man Island]" is the Stage named so; the Leader is named [Shirahoshi]. The rested card must be active. The [On Play] cost takes the top or bottom Life card (the owner chooses).
- OP11-112 Megalo: "[Opponent's Turn] If your Leader is [Shirahoshi], this Character gains +4000 power": a permanent with both conditions.
- OP11-114 Gum-Gum Fire-Fist Pistol Red Hawk: "If you and your opponent have a total of 5 or more Life cards": both Life counts added; "base cost of 5 or less" uses the printed cost (`baseCost`).
- OP11-115 You're Just Not My Type!: "[Counter] If your Leader is [Shirahoshi], up to 1 ...": the condition is in the effect (the Event is still played and spent when it fails). The [Trigger] K.O. has no Leader condition.
- OP11-118 Monkey.D.Luffy: "Return up to 1 Character with a cost of 4 or less to the owner's hand. Then, give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters": the Character may belong to either player; "Then" is not "if you do", so the DON!! is given even if nothing was returned. The DON!! given is "up to 1" (asked with min 0).
- OP11-119 Koby: "[On Play] Up to 1 of your Characters can also attack active Characters": Characters only (the Leader is not offered). "[When Attacking] You may place 2 cards from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order": the +1000 lasts until the end of the opponent's next turn.

Engine observations (not bugs in scope, nothing in `engine/src` changed): an optional cost that cannot be paid (a `restDon` or `mill` cost on an Event, a hand-`trash` cost on an Activate or When Attacking) still shows the "optional" prompt with only "decline" legal, whereas an unpayable `move` (trash to deck bottom) or `turnLifeFaceDown` cost shows no prompt. Harmless, but inconsistent.
