# OP-12 scripting, group 1 of 3: readings and ambiguities

Cards (19 scripted, none left out): OP12-001, 003, 004, 007, 008, 009, 012, 013, 014, 016, 017, 019, 022, 026, 029, 030, 031, 033, 039. All are `verified: true` in `cards/data/sets/OP-12.json`. Every gap the audit listed for them is merged and in waves 1 and 2: B3-G16 (OP12-016, 017, 019) = G7 cost `giveDon`; B3-G31 (OP12-001) = G12 `deckRestriction`. No card needs a wave 5+ gap or a design item D1-D9. Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. No rulings were invented.

- **OP12-001 Silvers Rayleigh (Leader)**, "Under the rules of this game, you cannot include cards with a cost of 5 or more in your deck. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may reveal 2 Events from your hand: Up to 1 of your Characters with 4000 base power or less gains +2000 power during this turn." `rules: deckRestriction { cost gte 5 }` (printed cost, so the Leader and costless Events count as 0; the script equals `DECK_RULES` in `engine/src/defs.ts`, pinned by the test). The Activate has a `reveal` cost (exactly 2 Events, they stay in hand) and `oncePerTurn`; the target is `basePower lte 4000` (printed, not current; 4000 qualifies, 5000 does not), Characters only (not the Leader), "up to 1".
- **OP12-003 Crocus**, "[On K.O.] You may reveal 2 Events from your hand: Play up to 1 red Character card with 3000 power or less from your hand." An `onKO` auto with a `reveal` cost; the play filter is red, Character, printed `power lte 3000` (not cost), "up to 1". Only a K.O. fires it (trashed or bounced by an effect does not).
- **OP12-004 Kouzuki Oden**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may reveal 2 Events from your hand: This Character gains +2000 power during this turn." `reveal` cost, `oncePerTurn`; usable the turn it is played (no rest in the cost).
- **OP12-007 Shanks**, "[On Play] Up to 1 of your Characters with a type including "Roger Pirates" other than [Shanks] gains [Rush] during this turn." `typeIncludes: 'Roger Pirates'` (a substring, so {Former Roger Pirates} counts) and `not name Shanks`: read as every card NAMED [Shanks] (a second copy on the field is excluded), not as `isSelf`. "Up to 1": a no-target pick is not asked.
- **OP12-008 Shanks**, "[Blocker] [On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Character cards -2000 power during this turn." `onOpponentAttack` auto with a `trash` cost (exactly 1 of the hand; with an empty hand accepting is illegal), `oncePerTurn`, target "up to 1" over the opposing Leader and Characters (the attacker may be chosen). The trashed card is a cost: it is gone even when the target is skipped.
- **OP12-009 Jinbe**, "[On Play] You may reveal 2 Events from your hand: This Character gains [Rush] during this turn. Then, this Character gains +1000 power until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." `reveal` cost; two steps (Rush `thisTurn`, then +1000 `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`, the plain duration for "until the end of your opponent's next End Phase"). "Then" is not "If you do": both follow the paid cost with no further condition.
- **OP12-012 Buggy**, "[On Play] Up to 1 of your Characters with a type including "Roger Pirates" other than [Buggy] gains [Blocker] until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." As OP12-007 with `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`; excludes every card named [Buggy].
- **OP12-013 Hatchan**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character and reveal 2 Events from your hand: Give up to 2 rested DON!! cards to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." Costs `restSelf` and `reveal`; `giveDon` n 2 rested, to exactly 1 of the Leader or Characters (Hatchan himself is a legal target). "Up to 2" asks a count (None, 1, 2), then the target. With no rested DON!! the cost is still paid and nothing is asked; with one rested DON!! no count is asked.
- **OP12-014 Boa Hancock**, "[On Play] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 [Monkey.D.Luffy] or red Event and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: Give up to 2 rested DON!! cards to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." The look is as OP12-006 (one take of up to 1 of either kind, "reveal"). The Activate has a `trashSelf` cost, so Hancock is already gone when the target is chosen: a lone Leader is then given the DON!! without a pick (the engine auto-picks a single "exactly 1" candidate).
- **OP12-016 To Never Doubt--That Is Power!**, "[Main] You may give 2 active DON!! cards to 1 of your [Silvers Rayleigh]: Your opponent cannot activate [Blocker] when the card given these DON!! cards attacks during this turn. [Counter] Up to 1 of your Characters or [Silvers Rayleigh] gains +2000 power during this battle." Event `costOptional` with a G7 `giveDon` cost (2 active, exactly 1 Leader or Character named [Silvers Rayleigh]), the chosen card BOUND (`bind: 'rayleigh'`) and read back by `{ ref }` in the `cannotActivateBlockerAgainstIt` restriction (this turn). So the restriction follows the card that got the DON!!: with a Rayleigh Leader and a Rayleigh Character in play, giving them to the Character leaves the Leader blockable (the audit's note, a restriction on the Leader, is only right when the Leader is chosen). The [Counter] is as EB04-009: any Character or a Rayleigh Leader (any other Leader is not a target), "up to 1".
- **OP12-017 Color of Observation Haki**, "[Main] You may give 1 active DON!! card to 1 of your [Silvers Rayleigh]: Look at 4 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 red Event or up to 1 Character card with a cost of 3 or more and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." Reading: ONE card in all ("add it"), either a red Event or a Character with cost 3 or more (current cost, as the card is in the deck: printed); a single take of up to 1 with an `any` filter. A reading of "up to 1 of each" would allow two cards; the singular "it" decides it. A red Event of any cost qualifies.
- **OP12-019 Color of Arms Haki**, "[Main] You may give 1 active DON!! card to 1 of your [Silvers Rayleigh]: Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +1000 power during this turn. [Counter] Up to 1 of your Characters or [Silvers Rayleigh] gains +2000 power during this battle." The [Main] target is any Leader or Character (not only a Rayleigh), "up to 1", and the Leader's given DON!! add their own +1000 on your turn (pinned: a Rayleigh Leader is 7000 with 1 DON!! and this boost). The [Counter] is as above.
- **OP12-022 Inuarashi**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: Up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with a cost of 5 or less will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase." `restSelf` cost, `skipRefresh` of one opposing rested Character (`state rested`, current `cost lte 5`), "up to 1". Only the next Refresh is skipped (pinned across three turn ends).
- **OP12-026 Kuina**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 4 or less. Then, give up to 3 rested DON!! cards to your [Roronoa Zoro] Leader." `restSelf` cost; `rest` by `baseCost lte 4`; then `giveDon` n 3 rested to the Leader named [Roronoa Zoro] (exactly 1, so a Leader with another name makes the step a no-op and no count is asked). "Then" is not "If you do": the DON!! go out when nothing was rested. A count (None to 3, capped by the rested DON!! available) is asked; the Leader is the only candidate so it is not asked again.
- **OP12-029 Shimotsuki Kouzaburou**, "[On Play] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 2 or less. Then, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with a base cost of 1 or less." Two steps. The rest uses current `cost lte 2`, the K.O. uses `state rested` and `baseCost lte 1`. "Then" is not "If you do": the K.O. step is asked even if the rest was skipped, and a base cost 1 Character rested by the first step is a legal K.O. target. A just-rested cost 2 is not (base cost 2).
- **OP12-030 Dracule Mihawk**, "[Blocker] [On Play] Set up to 4 of your DON!! cards as active. Then, you cannot play Character cards with a base cost of 7 or more during this turn." As OP13-023 Uta: `setActive` from the cost area "up to 4" (a count, most first), then `cannotPlay` Characters with `baseCost gte 7` for this turn. The restriction is not conditioned on any DON!! being set (count 0 still applies it). It bars every play of such a Character this turn including by effect (the text is "cannot play", not "from hand"; M6b-Q33 concerns the hand-only wording and does not arise).
- **OP12-031 Tashigi**, "[On Play] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 6 or less. Then, give up to 3 rested DON!! cards to your [Roronoa Zoro] Leader." As Kuina's two steps with `baseCost lte 6`.
- **OP12-033 Helmeppo**, "[Blocker] [On Block] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less." `onBlock` auto, `rest` by current `cost lte 5`, "up to 1". The attacker being blocked is a legal target when its cost is 5 or less.
- **OP12-039 Luffy Is the Man Who Will Become the King of Pirates!!!**, "[Main] Set your [Roronoa Zoro] Leader as active. [Trigger] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +1000 power during this turn." The [Main] is `setActive` on the Leader named [Roronoa Zoro] (no pick: it is that Leader or nothing); the [Trigger] is a plain "up to 1" power over Leader or Characters. An Event with no cost, no `costOptional`.

## Engine notes (no bug found)

- A cost `giveDon` selector may carry a `bind`: the bound card is readable in the effect through `filter: { ref }` (OP12-016 relies on it and the test pins that a Rayleigh Character, not the Leader, is the one that ignores Blockers).
- A `reveal` cost over the hand is asked as a hidden-pool pick each time, and an unpayable optional cost is offered with accept illegal (as OP12-015).
- A `giveDon` to a single legal target with `exactly: 1` does not ask the target.
