## Open questions touched

- M6b-Q51 (swap with another base-power setting): OP14-001, OP14-009, OP14-017 are built as the wave states and the question is not settled here.
- No new open question; the "up to 2" split in EB03-055 is an engine shape limit, noted in the script's `rulings`.

## Engine notes (no bug found)

- An auto with an unpayable cost still raises the `optional` decision with only "decline" legal when the cost is a `trash` (OP14-009 with 1 card in hand, EB04-044's replacement with an empty hand), but when the cost is `trashLife` with 0 Life (EB03-054, EB03-055 [On Play]) the ability is skipped without a question. Both are safe (the unpayable path cannot be taken), the difference is only in the prompt.
