# Leftovers group B scripting (ST-22, ST-23)

Cards scripted (16): ST22-001, 002, 003, 005, 006, 007, 011, 012, 015, 016, 017; ST23-001, 002, 003, 004, 005. All have `verified: true` overrides. None needs a wave 5+ gap or a design item. Gaps used (all merged): G1 (own-Character cost, ST22-005), G11 and G14 (replacements, ST22-005, 012), G45 (Life top or bottom to hand, ST22-015), hand-zone permanents (ST23-001, 002), `lookAtTop` (ST22-002). Drafts follow `docs/research/m6b-audit/leftovers.md` rows; the audit's own open questions for this group are none (M6b-Q87 to Q90 belong to other cards).

Readings, one line each, with the card text:

- **ST22-001 Ace & Newgate**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may reveal 1 card with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates" from your hand: Draw 1 card and place the revealed card at the top of your deck." The cost `reveal` binds the card (`bind: 'shown'`); the effect draws first (the order of the text), then moves the bound card from the hand to the deck top. The cost-named card does reach the effect (the audit flagged this as unproven; the card's own test is the check). A card drawn is never the revealed one unless the deck was empty. With no {Whitebeard Pirates} card in hand the ability is not offered.
- **ST22-002 Izo**, "[On Play] Look at 5 cards ...; reveal up to 1 card with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates" other than [Izo] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." `lookAtTop` 5, one take (up to 1, `typeIncludes` and not named Izo, revealed), `bottomAnyOrder`. "Other than [Izo]" is by name, so any card named Izo is excluded. The take is asked even with no match (hidden zone), at max 0. "[On Your Opponent's Attack] You may trash this Character: Draw 1 card and place 1 card from your hand at the bottom of your deck." Cost `trashSelf` (optional by construction); the placement is mandatory and may take the card just drawn; the hand pick is always asked (even with one card).
- **ST22-003 Edward.Newgate / ST22-006 Jozu**, "Reveal 1 card from the top of your deck. If that card's type includes "Whitebeard Pirates", draw 2 cards [and trash 1 card from your hand]." As ST17-001 (RL-44): the revealed card stays on top on a miss and is the first card drawn on a hit. The "If" is colon-less: the reveal always happens. Jozu's trash is inside the hit ("draw 2 cards and trash 1"), mandatory, and may take a card just drawn.
- **ST22-005 Kouzuki Oden**, "If this Character would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may trash 2 cards from your hand instead." A `leaveField` replacement with `cause: 'opponentEffect'`. A K.O. by the opponent's effect is read as a removal from the field (QA-OP02 OP02-027 reads "removed from the field" as K.O., trash, hand and deck moves); a battle K.O. and the owner's own effects are not replaced. Not Once Per Turn (the text says none). With fewer than 2 cards in hand only declining is legal. "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may rest 3 of your DON!! cards and return 1 of your Characters other than this Character to the owner's hand: Set this Character as active." Costs `restDon 3` and a field `move` of one other own Character to the hand (`isSelf: false`); effect `setActive` on self. An active Oden can use it too (cost paid, nothing changes).
- **ST22-007 Squard**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Reveal 1 card from the top of your deck. If that card's type includes "Whitebeard Pirates", give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." The "If" is an effect condition: the Once Per Turn is spent and the card shown even on a miss. As OP14-005 Killer, "up to 1 rested DON!!" asks only a target (no count for 1); with no rested DON!! nothing is asked.
- **ST22-011 Whitey Bay**, "[Your Turn] [On Play] You may reveal 2 cards with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates" from your hand: Up to 1 of your Leader with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates" gains +2000 power during this turn." The cost clause is missing in optcgapi and was verified from Limitless (DECISIONS 2026-11-24, "Open decisions", a clear upstream defect, not a reading; the override records it). Built as an auto `onPlay` with `yourTurn` and the reveal cost (`exactly: 2`, optional by construction); the target is the Leader filtered by type, "up to 1" asked at max 1 with a matching Leader and nothing to choose otherwise. The cost is still paid when the Leader does not match. Played by the opponent's effect on their turn it does not fire.
- **ST22-012 Marco**, "[Once Per Turn] If this Character would be K.O.'d by your opponent's effect, you may trash 1 card from your hand instead." `ko` replacement `cause: 'opponentEffect'`, Once Per Turn; battle and own effects are not replaced. "[When Attacking] Reveal 1 card from the top of your deck. If that card's type includes "Whitebeard Pirates", this Character gains +1000 power until the end of your opponent's next turn." `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`, so the bonus outlasts the battle.
- **ST22-015 I Am Whitebeard!!**, "[Main] If your Leader's type includes "Whitebeard Pirates", play up to 1 [Edward.Newgate] from your hand. Then, you may add 1 card from the top or bottom of your Life cards to your hand. If you do, up to 1 of your Leader gains +2000 power until the end of your opponent's next turn." The "Then" and the Life `may` sit inside the Leader guard (M4b-5: "Then" after a failed "If" does not resolve), so a non-{Whitebeard Pirates} Leader gets nothing. The Life step is not tied to the play ("Then", not "If you do"): it is offered even when nothing was played. It is gated on a public Life count (1 or more); the end (top or bottom) is a blind choice and the card goes to the hand with no [Trigger]. The play filter is Character named [Edward.Newgate] ("up to 1", asked at max 0 with no match).
- **ST22-016 Take That Back!!**, "[Counter] Reveal 1 card from the top of your deck. If that card's type includes "Whitebeard Pirates", up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +4000 power during this battle." The reveal always happens (colon-less "If"). "[Trigger] Draw 1 card."
- **ST22-017 Fire Fist**, "[Main] You may reveal 2 cards with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates" from your hand: Draw 1 card. Then, place up to 1 Character with a cost of 5 or less at the bottom of the owner's deck." `costOptional`, reveal `exactly 2`; the Character is chosen after the draw, either side, current cost, "up to 1". "[Trigger] Return up to 1 Character with a cost of 3 or less to the owner's hand": either side, current cost.
- **ST23-001 Uta**, "If you have a Character with 10000 power or more, give this card in your hand -4 cost." A `zone: 'hand'` permanent, your Characters only, current power (given DON!! and buffs count, rested Characters count). [Blocker] is a printed keyword.
- **ST23-002 Shanks**, "If your opponent has a Character with 8000 base power or more, give this card in your hand -3 cost." Opposing Characters, base power (printed; buffs and given DON!! ignored). "[On Play] If your Leader has the "Red-Haired Pirates" type or is [Uta], your Leader gains +2000 power until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." The printed `"Red-Haired Pirates" type` is an exact type element (the user's note: optcgapi prints `{X} type` as `"X" type`), so `{ type }`; "is [Uta]" is the Leader's name. Duration `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`.
- **ST23-003 Benn.Beckman**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: If your Leader has the "Red-Haired Pirates" type, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 4000 base power or less." The "If" follows the cost colon, so it is an effect condition: the card is trashed even with another Leader and nothing is K.O.'d. Exact type again; base power (buffs ignored).
- **ST23-004 Monkey.D.Luffy**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards and this Character: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -1000 power during this turn." Costs `restDon 1` and `restSelf`; not Once Per Turn (resting Luffy stops a second use). Characters only, "up to 1" may be declined.
- **ST23-005 Yasopp**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." As OP14-005 Killer.

Type matches. Every "a type including" in ST-22 is `typeIncludes` (a substring of a type element), so "Whitebeard Pirates Allies" (Squard, Doma) and "Former Whitebeard Pirates" count; the tests pin both. The ST-23 "Red-Haired Pirates" type (printed with quotes) is exact.

Hidden-zone gates: none. The reveal costs (ST22-001, 011, 017) pay from the hand with no gate on the prompt (same as OP12-013); the deck-top reveals make the card public, so the `if` over it is not a gate; ST22-015 reads only the public Life count. `cards/test/hidden-gate.test.ts` passes.

Engine bugs: none found.
