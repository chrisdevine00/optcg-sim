# OP-14 scripting, group 1 of 4: readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP14-002, 004, 005, 006, 010, 011, 012, 013, 014, 015, 016, 018, 021, 023, 024, 025, 026, 027, 028, 029, 032, 033, 034 (23 scripted, none left out; every card is `verified: true` in `cards/overrides/`). Gaps from `docs/research/m6b-audit/block4.md`, mapped through `m6b-runtime-audit.md` §2: B4-G37 = G17 (OP14-010, wave 2), B4-G7 = G24 (OP14-021, 027, 028, 032, wave 3), B4-G2 = G2 (OP14-033, wave 1), B4-G15 = G11 (OP14-034, wave 2); all merged. Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. No new rulings were invented; M6b open questions stay open.

Method notes from the coordinator's correction: no prompt is gated on an `exists` over a hidden zone (hand, deck, Life). The plays from hand (OP14-014, 018, 025, 033) are plain "up to 1" picks, asked even when nothing matches. Gates read only public facts (OP14-021 gates on the Life count).

- **OP14-002 Urouge**, "[When Attacking] If this Character has 5000 power or more, draw 1 card and K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 3000 base power or less." Leading "If" on an auto is a condition; `self` power is CURRENT power (given DON!!, timed boosts), checked at the trigger and at resolution. "Draw 1 and K.O." are two mandatory steps (the draw happens even with no target); the K.O. reads BASE power.
- **OP14-004 Cavendish**, "If this Character has 5000 power or more, this Character gains [Rush]." A permanent with a `self` power condition; [Rush] is gained, not printed. A boost that reaches 5000 lets a Character played this turn attack.
- **OP14-005 Killer**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." `giveDon` n 1, state rested, target exactly 1 of Leader or Characters. The engine asks no count at n 1, so with a rested DON!! and a target the DON!! is given (the player cannot choose 0 once activated; activating is already the choice). Review: n 1 "up to" cannot be declined.
- **OP14-006 Shachi & Penguin**, "[When Attacking] If this Character has 5000 power or more, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn." As OP14-002 for the condition.
- **OP14-010 Basil Hawkins**, "[On K.O.] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; play up to 1 {Supernovas} type Character card with 2000 power or less other than [Basil Hawkins]. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." `lookAtTop` take `to: 'play'` (G17), filter Character, {Supernovas}, power <= 2000, not the name. "Power" is read as the printed power (a card in the deck has no modifiers). Played active (the text says nothing about rested). The rest are ordered by the owner.
- **OP14-011 Bartolomeo**, "[DON!! x2] This Character gains [Blocker]." A permanent with `donGiven: 2` (2 or more given); [Blocker] is gained, so `keywords` stays empty.
- **OP14-012 Bepo**, "[When Attacking] If this Character has 5000 power or more, give up to 2 rested DON!! cards to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." `giveDon` n 2, rested, one target; a count 2 / 1 / 0 is asked when 2 or more are rested, nothing is asked for 1 (see OP14-005).
- **OP14-013 Monkey.D.Luffy**, "[On Play] Look at 5 ...; reveal up to 1 {Supernovas} type card other than [Monkey.D.Luffy] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom ... [When Attacking] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −1000 power during this turn." As OP15-037 for the look. Any {Supernovas} card of any category qualifies ("card"). The power of this card is 0; it is a normal attacker.
- **OP14-014 Eustass"Captain"Kid**, "[Blocker] [On Play] If your Leader has the {Supernovas} type, play up to 1 red Character card with 2000 power or less from your hand." Leading "If" on an auto: the Leader check is an `if` step; the play itself is an unguarded "up to 1" pick (leak rule). "Power 2000 or less" is the printed power in hand; a 0-power Character is eligible.
- **OP14-015 Roronoa Zoro**, "[Rush] ... [When Attacking] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −1000 power during this turn." [Rush] is printed (keyword from the override).
- **OP14-016 X.Drake**, "[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] If your {Supernovas} type Character would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may give your Leader −2000 power during this turn instead. [DON!! x1] [When Attacking] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn." A `leaveField` replacement (cause `opponentEffect`: K.O., bounce, trash all replaced; battle and your own effects not), conditioned on the opponent's turn, with Once Per Turn. The data text prints −2000 (the audit's M6b-Q3 expected a lost sign, so +2000: the printed −2000 is used). A {Supernovas} Drake covers himself. The Leader's power may fall to 0 or below. Review: whether declining the offer consumes the Once Per Turn is the engine's behaviour (not pinned here); the second removal after a used replacement is pinned as not replaced.
- **OP14-018 Time for the Counterattack**, "[Counter] If there is a Character with 8000 power or more, up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +4000 power during this battle. [Trigger] Play up to 1 red Character card with 2000 power or less from your hand." The "If" follows the timing tag with no colon, so it is an `if` step: the Counter is played and does nothing without such a Character. "A Character" is any Character on either side, CURRENT power (8000 exact counts; a boosted 7000 counts). The Leader never counts (it is not a Character).
- **OP14-021 Issho**, "[Your Turn] When this Character becomes rested, you may add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand. If you do, up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters or Stages will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase." Timing `rested` (G24) with `isSelf`, `yourTurn` condition; a `may` + `ifDid` (the text has no colon, so no cost), wrapped in a public Life-count gate so the prompt is never shown with 0 Life. Every rested transition on your turn counts (an attack declaration, a cost, an effect: M6b-Q10, Q30, Q41). The Life card goes to the hand with no [Trigger]. Targets are the opponent's rested Characters and Stage (not the Leader, not DON!!; audit Q5), "up to 1" may be nothing; the effect covers the opponent's next Refresh only.
- **OP14-023 Kikunojo**, "[End of Your Turn] Set this Character as active." `endOfYourTurn` `setActive` on self, as OP13-037.
- **OP14-024 Kin'emon**, "[On Play] Set up to 3 of your DON!! cards as active. Then, you cannot play Character cards during this turn. [On K.O.] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's cards." "Then" is not "If you do": the restriction is applied even when no DON!! changed. The restriction (`cannotPlay` Character, this turn) covers every Character play, by effect included (audit Q14). "Your opponent's cards" are scripted as Leader, Characters, Stage and cost-area DON!! (as M4b E-Q3, Laboon; M6b audit Q5); an already rested card cannot be a candidate in practice (the engine only offers the legal ones). Review: DON!! as targets is the broad reading.
- **OP14-025 Kuro**, "[On Play] If your Leader is [Kuro], play up to 1 {East Blue} type Character card with a cost of 6 or less from your hand." Leader name exactly [Kuro]; ungated "up to 1" pick; boundary cost 6 allowed, 7 not.
- **OP14-026 Kouzuki Oden**, "[Opponent's Turn] If this Character is rested, this Character gains +2000 power." A permanent with `opponentsTurn` and a `self` rested condition.
- **OP14-027 Shanks**, "[Your Turn] When this Character becomes rested, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 7000 base power or less. [Opponent's Turn] If this Character is rested, give all of your opponent's Characters −1000 power." First ability as OP14-021 (G24). The second is a permanent power effect on all the opponent's Characters (no duration is printed, so it is continuous while he stays rested on their turn; Characters that arrive later are covered; the Leader is not). Review: could be read as a one-shot when he is rested on their turn; the continuous reading was chosen.
- **OP14-028 Johnny**, "[Your Turn] When this Character becomes rested, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with a cost of 2 or less." G24 as above; the K.O. target is rested and cost <= 2.
- **OP14-029 Tashigi**, "[Opponent's Turn] If this Character would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may rest 1 of your cards instead. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may rest 2 of your cards: This Character gains +2000 power until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." A `leaveField` replacement of herself only (opponent's turn, cause `opponentEffect`), payload "rest 1 of your cards"; and an Activate with a rest-2 cost (no `costOptional`: it is an [Activate: Main] on a Character) and `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`. "Your cards" are Leader, Characters, Stage and active cost-area DON!! (as OP15-035 Laboon); Tashigi may rest herself for either cost. The replacement is not offered when nothing active can be rested.
- **OP14-032 Humandrill**, "[Your Turn] When this Character becomes rested, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less." G24 as above. An already rested Character is a legal (no-op) target since the text does not require an active one.
- **OP14-033 Perona**, "[On Play] Up to 2 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less cannot be rested until the end of your opponent's next End Phase. [On K.O.] You may rest 1 of your cards: Play up to 1 green Character card with a cost of 5 or less from your hand." `cannotBeRested` (G2) with `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` also stops attacking and [Blocker] (rules-digest 57). The [On K.O.] is an auto with a rest-1 cost (Leader, Characters, Stage, active DON!!; Perona is already gone), so with a single active candidate the cost is paid without a choice; the play is an unguarded "up to 1" pick.
- **OP14-034 Monkey.D.Luffy**, "[Your Turn] All of your green {Straw Hat Crew} type Characters with a base cost of 4 or more gain +1000 power. [Once Per Turn] If your {Straw Hat Crew} type Character would be K.O.'d by your opponent's effect, you may rest 1 of your Characters instead." A permanent on your turn (this Luffy is cost 3 and not in his own boost; the `color: Green` filter also matches a green multicolor card, and `baseCost` is read) and a `ko` replacement with cause `opponentEffect` (G11), Once Per Turn, payload "rest 1 of your Characters" (may be himself or the K.O.'d one). A bounce or trash is not replaced (the card says K.O.'d); battle and own effects are not.

- Unscripted Events in hand cannot be played (no Main ability): tests use Characters.
