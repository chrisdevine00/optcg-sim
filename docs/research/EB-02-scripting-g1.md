## Engine notes

- A `giveDon` to a single Leader (`to: { from: 'leader', owner: 'you' }`, `n` 1) with exactly 1 rested DON!! available asks "give DON!! to up to 1 of your Leader" (min 0): EB02-006 and EB02-011 tests pick the Leader. With 2 or more rested DON!! the same shape asks a count first, as OP13-054 does.
- A prompt that depends on the hand (a `play` from hand after a search) is asked at max 0 when the hand holds no eligible card (EB02-013, EB02-014, EB02-016, EB02-028, EB02-032), so the opponent cannot tell.
