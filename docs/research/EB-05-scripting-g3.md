# EB-05 scripting, group 3 of 3: readings and ambiguities

Cards: EB05-043 to EB05-061 (17 cards, all scripted; none left unscripted). Scripts in `cards/effects/EB-05/`, tests in `engine/test/cards/EB05-*.test.ts`. One line per reading; "no ruling" means the reading is the scripter's, not an official answer.

- EB05-043 Perona, "[On K.O.] K.O. or rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 6 or less": a `chooseOne` (K.O. or rest) first, then the target (M6b-Q23, no ruling). The choice is offered even if no Character is a legal target.
- EB05-044 Ms. Father's Day, "If your Leader's type includes \"Baroque Works\" and your opponent has a Character with a cost of 0": "a cost of 0" reads the current cost (`cost: { eq: 0 }`), so a Character lowered to 0 counts. No printed Character has cost 0, so the test lowers one with a planted effect.
- EB05-045 Ms. Monday, "[On Play] K.O. up to 1 of your Characters with a type including \"Baroque Works\"": Ms. Monday is herself a candidate (the text says "your Characters", not "other"); K.O.ing herself fires her [On K.O.]. No ruling.
- EB05-045 Ms. Monday, "[On K.O.] Look at 3 ...; reveal up to 1 ... Then, trash the rest": `lookAtTop` with `rest: 'trash'`; the look still shows (a `max 0` choice) when no card matches.
- EB05-046 Yamato, "[On Your Opponent's Attack] You may trash 1 card from your hand: If you have 10 or more cards in your trash ...": the trash count is read after the cost is paid, so the trashed card counts (9 before + 1 = 10 works). The condition is an `if` step in the effect, not an ability condition, so it is evaluated after the cost.
- EB05-046 Yamato, "If your opponent has a Character with 8000 base power or more, this Character gains [Blocker] and +3000 power": a permanent ability with a condition on the opponent's Characters (base power); applies on either player's turn.
- EB05-047 Ripley, "This Character gains +12 cost": field only (RL-43, as OP17-089); the cost card trashed for [On Play] can itself be played back if it is a Character with cost 2 or less (it is already in the trash when the effect resolves). No ruling on the second part.
- EB05-048 Hedgehog Stinger, "You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards and K.O. 1 of your Characters ...: All of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 0 cannot activate [Blocker] during this turn": both costs are one optional `costOptional` cost (B-1, as OP14-096); the affected Characters are fixed when the effect resolves (wave 1 reading 6, G10), so a Character that reaches cost 0 later in the turn can still block. The K.O. is a real K.O. (RL-16), so [On K.O.] fires.
- EB05-049 Special Hollow, "You may K.O. 1 of your {Thriller Bark Pirates} type Characters: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -3 cost": the K.O. cost is a K.O. (RL-16); the cost is paid before the target is chosen, and "up to 1" may then choose nothing. The cost -3 is not clamped in the script; the engine reports the current cost (a cost 4 Character shows 1).
- EB05-050 Atlas, "[Trigger] Activate this card's [On Play] effect": `activate: onPlay of self`; the card is not played (it goes to hand after resolving), as OP13-113.
- EB05-051 Ahiru, "cost equal to or less than the total of your and your opponent's Life cards": `Num.life 'both'` read when the effect resolves, after Ahiru is played; no ruling.
- EB05-053 Charlotte Praline, "[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] If your opponent has 9 or more cards in their hand, add up to 1 card from your hand to the top of your Life cards": the card is added face-down (no "face-up" in the text); the condition is an ability condition, so it is checked when the attack is declared.
- EB05-053 and EB05-055 [Trigger], "You may trash 1 card from your hand: If you have 2 or less Life cards, play this card": Activating the [Trigger] is the choice; the cost (trash 1) is paid and then the Life count is read, so with 3 or more Life cards left the cost is lost and the card is not played. No ruling on the lost-cost case beyond RD 120 and the cost-before-colon order.
- EB05-054 Charlotte Brulee, "Play up to 2 {Big Mom Pirates} type Character cards with 4000 power from your hand": power is read as printed in hand (`power: { eq: 4000 }`).
- EB05-055 Nami, "[Your Turn] [On Play] If your Leader has the \"Wisdom\" attribute, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards": `yourTurn` is an ability condition, so a Nami played by [Trigger] on the opponent's turn adds no Life card. The add is offered as a yes or no (one card).
- EB05-056 Nico Olvia, "If your Leader is [Nico Robin]": the Leader's name is `Nico Robin` exactly; the cost is paid even when the Leader is not Robin (text order: "You may trash ...: If ...").
- EB05-057 Nojiko, "Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to 1 of your Leader or Character cards with the \"Special\" or \"Wisdom\" Attribute": as OP14-114, one target chosen (the DON!! count is "up to" but the engine gives 1 when a rested DON!! and a legal target exist); Nojiko, a Wisdom Character, is a legal target.
- EB05-057 Nojiko [Trigger], "play up to 1 Character card with 6000 power or less and a [Trigger] from your hand": the Life condition is an `if` step; power is the printed power in hand.
- EB05-059 Charlotte Pudding!!, "all of your Characters with 4000 base power and the {Big Mom Pirates} type cannot be K.O.'d in battle until the end of your opponent's next End Phase": a timed `cannotBeKOd` with cause `battle` (G6), snapshotted at resolution (a Character played later this turn is not covered; M6b-Q18 family, no ruling); does not stop effect K.O.s (RL-14). Duration `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`.
- EB05-060 Take Care of Lilith!!!, "[Main] You may trash 1 of your {Egghead} type Characters with a cost of 5 or more": a trash cost, not a K.O. (RL-15); "cost" reads the current cost. A single legal Character is chosen automatically.
- EB05-060 Take Care of Lilith!!!, "[Counter] You may turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-up": with the top Life card already face-up the cost cannot be paid (G8's neighbour, `turnLifeFaceUp` needs a face-down top card); the cost is asked and may be declined (B-1).
- EB05-061 Nami, "[Once Per Turn] If one of your Characters with 6000 base power or less would be removed from the field by your opponent ...": read as "by your opponent's effect" like OP15-035 (M6b-Q7, no ruling), so a battle K.O. is not replaced; the `replaces.leaveField` shape cannot express "by your opponent" including battle. If the printed meaning includes battle K.O.s this needs a `cause` that covers battle (the DSL has only `opponentEffect` for `leaveField`; `ko` replacements are not cause-scoped). Flagged for review. The Once Per Turn is the ability's (`oncePerTurn: true`), used only when the replacement applies.
- EB05-061 Nami, "add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand instead": not damage, so no [Trigger] (RL-41 / OP12-061); not available with 0 Life.
