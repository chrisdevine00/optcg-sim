# EB-05 scripting, group 2: readings and ambiguities

Runbook `docs/runbooks/new-set.md` step d. Cards: EB05-021 to EB05-025, 027 to 030, 033 to 040, 042 (18 cards, all scripted, none left stats-only). One line each: the card text, then the reading chosen. No new rulings were invented; where a reading rests on a ruling it is cited.

- **EB05-021 Alvida**, "[On Play] Draw 2 cards and play up to 1 {Cross Guild} type Character card with a cost of 8 or less from your hand. Then, you cannot play Character cards during this turn." Reading: the restriction is unconditional (it applies even when nothing was played, and it is not gated on the play); it covers Character cards only, so Events stay playable. The play is not a "play" of the restricted kind because the restriction is created after the effect's own play.
- **EB05-021 Alvida**, "[When Attacking] Negate the effect of up to 1 of your opponent's Characters during this turn." Reading: a timed `negate` with duration `thisTurn` on a chosen opposing Character; no target means nothing happens.
- **EB05-023 Osome**, "[On Play] You may trash 2 Events from your hand: Draw 3 cards." Reading: an auto with a cost is optional by construction (CR 8-3-1-4). With fewer than 2 Events the engine still asks, but only declining is legal.
- **EB05-024 Little Sadi**, "If your Leader has the {Impel Down} type, this Character gains [Blocker] and +2 cost." Reading: one permanent ability, one condition, two `apply` entries (grant Blocker, cost +2 on itself). Not an [On Play]: it follows the Leader's type live.
- **EB05-025 Domino / EB05-037 Black Maria**, "Look at 3 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 {type} card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." Reading: `lookAtTop` with `take` up to 1 and `bottomAnyOrder`; the player may take nothing even with a match (CR 8-4-4-2, the same reading as EB04-002 and OP13-113).
- **EB05-027 Hibari**, "[On Play] Draw 3 cards and trash 2 cards from your hand. Then, place up to 1 Character with a cost of 2 or less at the bottom of the owner's deck." Reading: the Character may belong to either player (`owner: 'any'`, "the owner's deck"), including your own Characters; trashing exactly 2 cards uses the whole hand (the drawn cards are eligible). The "Then" is not gated by anything.
- **EB05-028 Boa Hancock**, "[On Play] If your opponent has 9 or more cards in their hand, they trash 4 cards from their hand." Reading: the opponent chooses which 4 (`chooser: 'opponent'`); the count is checked once, when the effect resolves.
- **EB05-029 Kimono Sleeve Cage**, "[Main] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Negate the effect of up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 6 or less during this turn, and return it to the owner's hand." Reading: the optional cost is the activation cost of the [Main] (B-1, DECISIONS 2026-10-29); the target is chosen once (`select` with `bind`), negated, then returned. The negation is real but unobservable once the Character is in hand, so the test checks the return only. "Up to 1" lets you pay the cost and choose nothing. [Trigger] "Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand" is a plain trigger.
- **EB05-030 Take Care, Everybody. ♡**, "[Main] If your Leader is [Nami], return up to 1 Character with a cost of 5 or less to the owner's hand." Reading: either player's Character (`owner: 'any'`); with a non-Nami Leader the Event is played and does nothing. "[Counter] Draw 1 card, and up to 1 of your Leader or Characters gains +1000 power during this turn." Reading: duration `thisTurn` exactly as printed (not "during this battle"), so the boost outlasts the battle.
- **EB05-033 Nico Robin**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Straw Hat Crew} type and the number of DON!! cards on your field is at least 6 less than the number on your opponent's field, draw 3 cards and trash 2 cards from your hand. Then, add up to 4 DON!! cards as rested from your DON!! deck." Reading: the "Then" sits inside the "if" (M6b audit §7 Q10; m4b audit, effect-dsl §4.10). The DON!! difference is read after Robin's own cost is paid (rested DON!! stay on the field, so it does not change the count). Written as `donOnField(you) <= donOnField(opponent) - 6`.
- **EB05-034 Sugar**, "[On Play] DON!! −2: If you have 7 or more DON!! cards on your field, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −4000 power during this turn." Reading: the "7 or more" check is part of the effect, so it is made after the DON!! −2 has been returned (nine DON!! before paying, seven after).
- **EB05-035 Speed**, "[On Play] DON!! −1: Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 2 {SMILE} type cards and add them to your hand, then place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. Then, play up to 2 {SMILE} type Character cards with 5000 power or less from your hand." Reading: the play step is not gated by the search; it can play a {SMILE} Character just added or already in hand (printed power is the filter, `power` on a hand card is its printed power). The engine shows the play prompt even with no candidate (a choice with max 0).
- **EB05-036 Tsuru**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Navy} type, draw 1 card, and add up to 1 DON!! card as rested from your DON!! deck." Reading: both steps inside the Leader-type `if`; the DON!! add is `upTo`, so an empty DON!! deck still draws.
- **EB05-038 Russian**, "[On Play] Up to 1 of your [Senor Pink] cards gains +3000 power during this turn." Reading: Leader or Character named [Senor Pink], yours only; "cards" is read as the field cards that can hold power (a Leader or Character).
- **EB05-039 Pink Hornet**, "[Main] DON!! −1: If your Leader has the {The Vinsmoke Family} type, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −4000 power during this turn. [Counter] DON!! −1: Your Leader gains +4000 power during this battle." Reading: DON!! −X on an Event is an optional cost (CR 8-3-1-4; QA-ST01–04 ST04-016, as for EB04-040). With the wrong Leader type the [Main] still pays its DON!! −1 and then does nothing.
- **EB05-040 You Need Me, Don't You?!!♡**, "[Main] You may return 1 of your active DON!! cards to your DON!! deck: If your Leader has the {Donquixote Pirates} type, draw 3 cards and trash 1 card from your hand. [Counter] Your Leader gains +2000 power during this battle." Reading: the cost is the wave-1 `returnDon` active-only form (G9), so rested and given DON!! cannot pay it (with none active only declining is legal); the Leader-type check is part of the effect, so the DON!! is lost when the Leader has another type.
- **EB05-022 Octopako, EB05-042 Shinobu**: no ambiguity ([On K.O.] draw 2; draw 2 then trash exactly 2, the drawn cards may be trashed).
