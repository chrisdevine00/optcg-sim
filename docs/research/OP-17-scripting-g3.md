# OP-17 scripting, group 3 of 3: readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP17-067, 068, 069, 071, 072, 078, 085, 092, 097, 099, 101, 103, 104, 105, 108, 115, 116, 117 (18 cards; 17 scripted, OP17-116 left unscripted). Scripts in `cards/effects/OP-17/`, tests in `engine/test/cards/OP17-*.test.ts`. One line per reading; "no ruling" means the reading is the scripter's, not an official answer.

- **OP17-067 Kurozumi Kanjuro**, "[On Play] DON!! −1: If you have a Character with a cost of 10 or more, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters." The condition is read after the DON!! −1 is paid (cost before the colon, "if" in the effect), on current cost; Kanjuro (cost 2) never satisfies it himself.
- **OP17-068 Sasaki / OP17-104 Charlotte Cracker / OP17-078 Drunken Dragon Bagua**, "You may trash 2 cards from your hand: If your Leader has the ... type, add up to 2 DON!! cards as rested". The Leader type is an `if` in the effect, so the cost is paid and wasted with another Leader type (no ruling needed: the text puts the "if" after the colon). "Add up to N DON!!" always adds the full amount (engine-wide, OPEN-QUESTIONS E-Q11).
- **OP17-069 Jack**, "DON!! −1: If your Leader has the {Animal Kingdom Pirates} type, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn." Same split: DON!! −1 is the cost, the Leader type an `if`. Printed [Rush: Character] is in `keywords`.
- **OP17-071 Who's.Who / OP17-103 Charlotte Katakuri / OP17-104 Charlotte Cracker / OP17-101 Caribou / OP17-108 Charlotte Brulee**, [Trigger] "Play this card." / "Rest up to 1..." / "You may trash 1 card from your hand: K.O. ...": a `trigger` ability; a cost the player cannot pay (empty hand) makes the Trigger not activatable (the test asserts `trigger activate` is illegal), the card then goes to the hand.
- **OP17-072 Black Maria**, "[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Up to 1 of your Leader or Characters gains +1000 power during this battle." Once Per Turn is spent on activation; "up to 1" is a choice that may be empty after the cost is paid (the text says "up to").
- **OP17-078 Drunken Dragon Bagua**, "[Main] You may rest 2 of your DON!! cards and trash 2 cards from your hand: ..." costs are one optional cost asked after the Event is played (DECISIONS 2026-11-09 B-1). The 2 DON!! must still be active after the Event's own cost is paid.
- **OP17-085 Dorry / OP17-092 Brogy**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Elbaph} type, play up to 1 [Brogy] with a cost of 5 or less from your hand or trash. Then, you cannot play Character cards during this turn." The +12 cost is field-only (RL-43 via QA-OP17 OP17-085), so the named card in hand or trash is judged by its printed cost 5. The "Then" clause is inside the Leader-type `if` (audit Q10 convention, no ruling) and is not gated on a card having been played. The fetched card's own [On Play] fires too (its Leader check passes) and finds no partner (the first is on the field); the engine still opens an empty "up to" choose for it, which the tests answer with `done`. The lock is applied after the play, so the fetched card's [On Play] can still play a third copy (this chain cannot occur with one Dorry and one Brogy in a deck of one each, but 4 + 4 copies allow it).
- **OP17-097 I'll Feed on This Rage...**, "[Main] Give all of your opponent's Characters −1 cost during this turn." The target set is a snapshot at resolution (no pick), like every "all of" target; costs bottom out at 0 for display (a cost 1 Character reads 0).
- **OP17-099 Charlotte Linlin (Leader)**, "[When Attacking] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Your opponent chooses one: • Trash 1 card from your hand. Then, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards. • Trash 1 card from your opponent's hand." (audit Q12): "your" is the Leader's controller (the opponent only picks the mode, a `chooseOne` with `chooser: 'opponent'`); mode 2 trashes from the opponent's hand with the controller choosing face-down (`blind`, QA-OP17 OP17-099). In mode 1 "Then" is not "If you do": the Life card is added (it is "up to 1", declinable) even when the hand was empty. The cost card is trashed before the opponent chooses, so mode 1 can empty the hand.
- **OP17-101 Caribou**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −3000 power during this turn." The Life card goes to the hand with no [Trigger] (it is a cost, not damage) and is taken even if no target is chosen. [Trigger] "You may trash 1 card from your hand:" is a Trigger cost (as EB05-055).
- **OP17-103 Charlotte Katakuri**, "[Your Turn] [On Play] If your Leader has the {Big Mom Pirates} type, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards. Then, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −3000 power during this turn." (audit Q10): "Then" is inside the Leader-type `if`; the −3000 does not depend on the Life card having been added. [Your Turn] is an ability condition, so a [Trigger] play on the opponent's turn does nothing.
- **OP17-105 Charlotte Chiffon**, "a [Trigger]" means a printed [Trigger] line (`hasTrigger`, as OP17-109). The Character to return is picked after the cost; with none it fizzles after the cost is paid.
- **OP17-115 Don't you know...**, "[Main] Your [Charlotte Linlin] Leader gains [Unblockable] during this turn." No "up to": applies to the matching Leader with no choice (review EB05-R1 lesson); with no such Leader nothing happens. "[Counter] Up to 1 of your [Charlotte Linlin] gains +4000" is read as your Leader or Characters with that name (no zone in the text).
- **OP17-117 Maser Saber**, "[Trigger] Your opponent may trash 3 cards from their hand. If they do not, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 6 or less." (audit Q11, no ruling): with 3 or more cards in hand the opponent chooses between trashing 3 (their pick) and letting the K.O. happen (a `chooseOne` with `chooser: 'opponent'`), before the K.O. target is picked; with fewer than 3 they cannot trash, so the K.O. happens with no choice. "Your opponent" in the K.O. clause is the opponent of the Event's owner, i.e. the player who owes the choice.

## Observations (not bugs)

- The engine offers an `optional` "use this effect" prompt for a cost it cannot pay and labels it "You can't pay its cost" (seen with OP17-072 on a hand it could not pay), so a test that wants "not offered" must decline.
- A hidden-zone "up to" search with no match (Dorry/Brogy fetching the missing partner) still opens an empty `choose` (min 0, max 0) for the player.

## Not scripted

- **OP17-116 Fulgora**, "[Main] You may rest 2 of your DON!! cards: K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Stages." needs gap **G18** (Action `ko` on a Stage; audit B5-G9, wave 2). Its [Counter] ("If you have 2 or more Characters with a [Trigger], ...") is expressible but the card stays unscripted until G18 lands.
