# OP-11 scripting, block 3: OP-11 group 2 of 3

Cards OP11-037 .. OP11-070 (25 scripted, all `verified: true`, none needed a wave 5+ gap or a design item). Readings and ambiguities, one line each, with the card text. Open questions M6b-Q1..Q58 are not settled here.

## Readings

- **OP11-040 Luffy (Leader)**: "This effect can be activated at the start of your turn. If you have 8 or more DON!! cards on your field, look at 5 ... Then, place the rest at the top or bottom of the deck in any order." A mandatory `startOfYourTurn` auto; the DON!! check is inside the effect and the "Then" is inside that guard (M4b-5). The count is read at the start of the turn, before the Refresh Phase and the DON!! Phase (cost area plus given, so 7 in the cost area and 1 given is 8). "Top or bottom" is one choice for all the cards (DECISIONS 2026-11-16).
- **OP11-041 Nami (Leader)**: "[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] This effect can be activated when a card is removed from your or your opponent's Life cards. If you have 7 or less cards in your hand, draw 1 card." The Once Per Turn is spent by the activation (the removal); the hand check is made on resolution, so a removal with 8 cards in hand uses the turn's activation and draws nothing. Counts Life removed by any means from either player, own turn only. The second ability, "[DON!! x1] [On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand: This Leader gains +2000 power during this turn", is an optional cost on an auto, gated on a DON!! given to the Leader.
- **OP11-039 Vagabond Drill**: "Up to 1 of your ... gains +3000 power during this battle. Then, rest up to 1 ..." The "Then" has no "If", so it does not depend on the boost target; with no {Fish-Man}/{Merfolk} card to choose the rest still resolves.
- **OP11-043 Ichiji**: "This effect can be activated when you only have Characters with a type including \"GERMA\"" is an activation condition (the Once Per Turn is not spent when it is false), false with no Characters, and Ichiji counts himself ({GERMA 66}). "Then, trash 2 cards from the top of your deck" is not "If you do": it follows the optional "up to 1" boost whether or not a card was chosen.
- **OP11-047 Reiju**: "If your Leader has the {The Vinsmoke Family} type, look at 5 ...; reveal up to 1 card with a type including \"GERMA\" and add it to your hand. Then, trash the rest." The trash is inside the Leader guard (M4b-5). "A type including GERMA" is a substring match, so {Kingdom of GERMA} qualifies.
- **OP11-048 Bege / OP11-070 Pudding**: the searched card may be of any category; "a cost of 2 or more" never matches a card with no cost.
- **OP11-049 Carrot**: "Look at 3 cards ... place them at the top or bottom of the deck in any order": all to the top or all to the bottom (same reading as OP17-050 Streusen, QA-OP17).
- **OP11-050 Gotti**: "Return up to 1 Character with a cost of 1 or less to the owner's hand or place it at the bottom of their deck." Scripted as a choose-one (mode first, then the "up to 1" target), as OP14-062; either player's Character, including your own.
- **OP11-051 Sanji**: the K.O. clause has no [On K.O.] tag; scripted as `onKO` with `koCause: 'opponentEffect'` (an effect of the other player, a cost of theirs included; battle and your own effects do not trigger it), as OP16-024. The played card is not revealed (the text says "play"). "5000 base power or less" reads printed power.
- **OP11-056 Brook, OP11-061 Jet Culverin [Main]**: "base cost" reads printed cost (a modified cost does not change eligibility); the Jet Culverin [Trigger] says "cost" and reads the current cost and either player's Character.
- **OP11-059 King Cobra**: "that card" is the card chosen for the first +2000 (no card, no second boost, as EB03-020). The hand is counted after the Event has left it (it is in the trash while it resolves), so "4 or less" means 4 other cards.
- **OP11-060 Let's Crash This Wedding!!!**: the "Then, place the rest at the bottom" is inside the multicolored-Leader guard (M4b-5); the [Trigger] activates the [Main] again, with the same guard.
- **OP11-062 Katakuri (Leader)**: "[When Attacking]/[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] DON!! -1: Look at 1 card from the top of your opponent's deck. Then, this Leader gains +1000 power during this battle." DON!! -1 is the optional DON!! -X cost (RL-52); the boost follows the look unconditionally; the Once Per Turn is shared by both timings.
- **OP11-063 Sadi, OP11-069 Brulee**: "COST: If your Leader has the type X, ..." pays the cost whether or not the Leader has the type (the "If" is after the colon).
- **OP11-065 Anana**: "a purple {Big Mom Pirates} type Character other than [Charlotte Anana]" excludes every Character named [Charlotte Anana] (a second copy does not count), not just this card.
- **OP11-067 Katakuri**: the "set up to 2 ... as active" targets any {Big Mom Pirates} Character with cost 3 or more, this card included; "add up to 1 DON!! card ... and rest it" asks a count (0 or 1).
- **OP11-070 Pudding**: "DON!! -1, You may rest this Character:" is one cost of both parts on an [Activate: Main] (activating is already optional), so it cannot be used while this Character is rested.
