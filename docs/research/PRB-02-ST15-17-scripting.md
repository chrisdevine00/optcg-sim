# PRB-02 and ST-15 to ST-17 scripting (25 cards)

Cards scripted: PRB02-001, 002, 003, 004, 006, 007, 008, 009, 010, 011, 012, 013, 014, 015, 016; ST15-002, 004; ST16-001, 003, 004, 005; ST17-001, 002, 003, 005. All have `verified: true` overrides. Gaps used (all merged in waves 1 to 4): G1 (own-Character cost, ST17-002), G24 (`rested`, PRB02-009), G31 (`topAnyOrder`, ST17-003), G37 (rest replacement, PRB02-006). PRB02 reprints did not match an already-scripted card's text exactly, so each shape was written from the closest existing script (OP13-017 for the power replacement, EB04-044 for the removal replacement, OP15-065/OP14-044 for the reveal, OP12-013 for the DON!! give).

## Readings (one line each, with the card text)

- **PRB02-001 Koby**, "[When Attacking] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 3000 base power or less. Then, if you have 6 or less cards in your hand, draw 1 card." The "if" after "Then" is its own condition, not "if you do": the draw happens with no K.O., and the hand is counted after the K.O. "3000 base power" is the printed power. The [Opponent's Turn] +1000 is a permanent gated on the opponent's turn and a {Navy} Leader.
- **PRB02-002 Trafalgar Law**, "[Once Per Turn] If this Character would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may give this Character −2000 power during this turn instead." A `leaveField` replacement (cause opponent's effect, so a bounce counts, a battle K.O. does not) with the power as the payload; no timing tag, so not gated on whose turn it is.
- **PRB02-003 Lucky.Roux**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 Character card with 6000 power or more from your hand: Draw 2 cards." An optional trash cost; the printed power in hand, 6000 qualifies. The prompt is shown whatever the hand holds (no hidden-zone gate); accepting is illegal without a qualifying card.
- **PRB02-004 Jewelry Bonney**, "[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] Set up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active." No cost: a count (None or 1) is asked when a rested DON!! exists (OP14-R2).
- **PRB02-006 Roronoa Zoro**, "[Opponent's Turn] If this Character would be rested by your opponent's Character's effect, you may rest 1 of your other Characters instead." G37: only an opponent Character's effect (not a Leader, Stage or Event; not a cost). The payload is written as a cost (`rest` of one other Character) so the offer needs a legal payment; **reading: the other Character must be active** (an already rested one cannot be rested again), so with no other active Character the offer is illegal to accept. Gated on the opponent's turn (a condition on the replacement), which only matters for an opponent Character effect that resolves during your turn (e.g. a [On Your Opponent's Attack]).
- **PRB02-007 Jinbe**, "[On Play] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 {The Seven Warlords of the Sea} type card other than [Jinbe] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [When Attacking] Place up to 1 Character with a cost of 1 or less at the bottom of the owner's deck." Cost is the current cost; the Character may be either side's (Jinbe's cost is 4 so he is never a candidate himself unless modified) and goes to its owner's deck.
- **PRB02-008 Marco**, "[On K.O.] Draw 2 cards." Fires on every K.O., battle included.
- **PRB02-009 Mr.3(Galdino)**, "This effect can be activated when this Character is rested by your opponent's effect. You may trash this Character and draw 2 cards." No colon: read as one optional unit, written as an optional `trashSelf` cost of the draw (nothing is drawn without trashing). "Rested by your opponent's effect" is a `rested` event by an opponent effect of any source; an attack, block or cost rest does not count (M6b-Q30, Q41).
- **PRB02-010 Charlotte Pudding**, "[On Play] DON!! −2: If your Leader has the "Big Mom Pirates" type and your opponent has 6 or more DON!! cards on their field, draw 2 cards. Then, play up to 1 "Big Mom Pirates" type Character card with 6000 to 8000 power from your hand." The "If" after the colon is an effect condition: with it false the DON!! −2 is still paid. "Then, play" is inside the guard (M4b-5). The opponent's DON!! count is the cost area plus given DON!!. 6000 and 8000 are inclusive; the printed power in hand.
- **PRB02-011 Donquixote Doflamingo**, "[On Play] If your Leader is multicolored, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and rest it." A count is asked when the DON!! deck has a card.
- **PRB02-012 Nami**, "[On Play] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Straw Hat Crew" type card other than [Nami] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." "Straw Hat Crew" is the exact type (a card of several types qualifies when one is it). [Trigger] "Play this card."
- **PRB02-013 Gecko Moria**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the "Thriller Bark Pirates" type, play up to 1 Character card with a cost of 4 or less from your trash rested. Then, give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." "Then, give" is inside the guard (M4b-5) and is not conditional on a Character having been played; the played Character is a legal target. "Up to 1 rested DON!!" is asked as an up-to-1 target (None allowed), as for ST17-004.
- **PRB02-014 Sabo**, "If you have 15 or more cards in your trash, give this card in your hand −3 cost." A hand-zone permanent; the count is read when the card is played; the reduction does not apply on the field.
- **PRB02-015 Shiryu**, "If your Leader has the "Blackbeard Pirates" type, this Character gains [Blocker] and +4 cost. [On K.O.] If your Leader has the "Blackbeard Pirates" type, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 4 or less." [Blocker] is gained, not printed (keywords stay empty). The [On K.O.] "If" is an effect condition read at resolution.
- **PRB02-016 Otama**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character and add 1 card from the top or bottom of your Life cards to your hand: Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +3000 power during this turn." The "You may" of an Activate is the player's choice to activate; top or bottom is asked. [Trigger] "Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less."
- **ST15-002 Edward.Newgate**, "[On Play] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters. [Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 5000 power or less." "5000 power or less" is the current power (a lowered 6000 qualifies, a raised 5000 does not).
- **ST15-004 Thatch**, "[On Play] If your Leader's type includes "Whitebeard Pirates", give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn. Then, add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand." "Then" is inside the guard (M4b-5); the Life card is mandatory (no "up to"/"may"), not damage, no [Trigger]. `typeIncludes` (a type containing the string).
- **ST16-001 Uta**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 "FILM" type card from your hand: Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." The trashed card is any category with the {FILM} type; the cost is paid even with no rested DON!!.
- **ST16-003 Charlotte Katakuri**, "If your Leader has the "FILM" type and you have 6 or more rested cards, this Character gains +2000 power." Rested cards = rested Leader, Characters and Stage plus rested DON!! in the cost area (M6b-Q25, as OP12-118); Katakuri counts himself when rested; the opponent's rested cards do not count.
- **ST16-004 Shanks**, "[On Play] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters."
- **ST16-005 Monkey.D.Luffy**, "If you have a rested [Uta], this Character gains +1000 power." **Reading: a rested Leader or Character of yours named [Uta]** (the text does not say Character); the opponent's does not count. Worth review: a Character-only reading would drop the Leader case.
- **ST17-001 Crocodile**, "[On Play] Reveal 1 card from the top of your deck. If that card is a "The Seven Warlords of the Sea" type card, draw 2 cards and place 1 card from your hand at the top of your deck." Exact type; a hit draws the revealed card first and the card placed on top is mandatory and may be one just drawn; a miss leaves the card on top.
- **ST17-002 Trafalgar Law**, "[On Play] You may return 1 of your Characters to the owner's hand: If your Leader has the "The Seven Warlords of the Sea" type, return up to 1 Character with a cost of 4 or less to the owner's hand." The "If" follows the colon: an effect condition, so the cost is paid even without the type. The cost may return Law himself; the effect may return any Character of either side with a current cost of 4 or less, Law included.
- **ST17-003 Buggy**, "[On Play] Look at 3 cards from the top of your deck and place them at the top of your deck in any order." G31 `topAnyOrder`.
- **ST17-005 Marshall.D.Teach**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may place 1 card from your hand at the top of your deck: Give up to 2 rested DON!! cards to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." As OP12-013: a count (None, 1, 2) then one target; the cost is paid even with no rested DON!!.
