# OP-10 scripting, group 1 of 4: readings

Cards: OP10-001, 002, 003, 004, 005, 006, 007, 008, 009, 011, 012, 015, 016, 017, 023, 024, 025, 028, 029, 030, 032, 034, 035, 042, 099 (25 cards, all `verified: true`, none left unscripted). Gaps used (all merged): G1 (OP10-002 cost), G11 (OP10-034 battle replacement), G21 (OP10-030), G29 (OP10-042). Open questions touched: M6b-Q20 (OP10-042), M6b-Q48 (OP10-035), M6b-Q30 family (none new). One line per reading, with the card text.

- **OP10-001 Smoker (Leader)** "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If you have a Character with 7000 power or more, set up to 2 of your DON!! cards as active." No colon, so an effect `if`: the ability is used and Once Per Turn is spent with no such Character. The 7000 is current power (a given DON!! or a buff counts on your turn); only your own Characters count.
- **OP10-001** "[Opponent's Turn] All of your {Navy} or {Punk Hazard} type Characters gain +1000 power." A permanent with an opponent's-turn condition on Characters only (not the Leader).
- **OP10-002 Caesar Clown (Leader)** "[DON!! x2] [When Attacking] You may return 1 of your {Punk Hazard} type Characters with a cost of 2 or more to the owner's hand: K.O. up to 1 ... 4000 power or less." The return is the G1 cost (exactly 1, chosen at payment; a lone candidate is taken without a pick); the K.O. target is chosen after it and its limit is current power.
- **OP10-003 Sugar (Leader)** "[End of Your Turn] If you have a {Donquixote Pirates} type Character with 6000 power or more, set up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active." Colon-less `if` (effect condition), current power, own Characters.
- **OP10-003** "[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When you activate an Event, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active." `eventActivated` by you with the opponent's-turn and Once Per Turn on the ability; on the opponent's turn only a [Counter] Event can be activated. "Up to 1" is the engine's count prompt (None offered).
- **OP10-042 Usopp (Leader)** "All of your "Dressrosa" type Characters with a cost of 2 or more gain +1 cost." The cost stage reads values from before itself, so the test is on the Character's cost before this effect: a cost-1 Dressrosa Character stays 1, a cost-2 becomes 3 (a cost-1 Character raised to 2 by another effect would not also get +1).
- **OP10-042** "[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] This effect can be activated when your "Dressrosa" type Character is removed from the field by your opponent's effect or K.O.'d. If you have 5 or less cards in your hand, draw 1 card." Two timings sharing one Once Per Turn: `removed` with `cause: 'opponentEffect'` (any move off the field by the opponent's effect, M6b-Q20) and `koed` (a K.O. of any cause, so a battle K.O. counts). One event that is both (the opponent's K.O. effect) is one activation. "If you have 5 or less" has no colon: an effect `if` checked when it resolves, after a returned Character is back in the hand (a bounce counts as a card in hand), and Once Per Turn is spent even at 6 cards.
- **OP10-099 Eustass"Captain"Kid (Leader)** "[End of Your Turn] You may turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-up: Set up to 1 of your "Supernovas" type Characters with a cost of 3 to 8 as active. That Character gains [Blocker] until the end of your opponent's next turn." `turnLifeFaceUp` cost; the Character is selected, set active and granted [Blocker] (also when it was already active); with no Life the cost is unpayable; "up to 1" may be left empty after the cost was paid.
- **OP10-004 Vergo** "Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 {Punk Hazard} type card other than [Vergo] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." Type filter, name exclusion (a [Vergo] is never offered); as OP11-004.
- **OP10-005 Sanji** "[Your Turn] This Character gains +3000 power. [On K.O.] Draw 1 card." Permanent on your turn; [On K.O.] on any K.O. cause, not on a bounce or trash.
- **OP10-006 Caesar Clown** "...reveal up to 1 [Smiley] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order and play up to 1 [Smiley] from your hand." One unconditional sequence: the play is offered whether or not a [Smiley] was added (a [Smiley] already in hand can be played). The empty "up to 1" take is asked at max 0 (no prompt depends on the hand).
- **OP10-007 Ceaser Soldier** "[On Play] Play up to 1 "Punk Hazard" type Character card with a cost of 2 or less from your hand." Printed cost, 2 or less; Characters only.
- **OP10-008 Scotch / OP10-017 Rock** "If you don't have [Rock] (or [Scotch]), play up to 1 [Rock] (or [Scotch]) from your hand." "Have" is a Character of that name on your field (a rested one counts; one in the trash does not); the condition is public so the play prompt is asked at max 0 if none is in hand. Playing Rock from Scotch does not loop (Scotch is already on the field when Rock's [On Play] resolves).
- **OP10-009 Smiley** "[On Play] If your Leader has the "Punk Hazard" type, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −3000 power during this turn." Leader type condition; "up to 1" may be declined.
- **OP10-011 Tony Tony.Chopper / OP10-012 Dragon Number Thirteen** printed [Blocker]; Chopper "[Opponent's Turn] This Character gains +2000 power" (an opponent's-turn permanent).
- **OP10-015 Mocha** "[On Play] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −1000 power during this turn." Characters only.
- **OP10-016 Monet** "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: Give up to 2 rested DON!! cards to your Leader or 1 of your Characters. Then, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −1000 power during this turn." "Then" is not "If you do": the −1000 happens even if no DON!! was given (none rested, or 0 chosen). "Up to 2" asks a count first (as EB03-014), then the target.
- **OP10-023 Issho** "[On Play] If your Leader has the "Navy" type, rest up to 2 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less." Characters only (the Leader is not a candidate); an already rested Character is not a useful target but is not excluded by the text.
- **OP10-024 Edward.Newgate** "[On Play] If you have 2 or more rested Characters, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less. Then, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with a cost of 3 or less." "Then" sits inside the guard (a failed If does not resolve it). The K.O. is chosen after the rest, so a cost-3 Character just rested can be K.O.'d. Edward himself is active when played and does not count; only your own rested Characters count (not rested Leaders, not the opponent's).
- **OP10-025 Enel** "[On Play] If you have 2 or more rested Characters, draw 3 cards and trash 2 cards from your hand." Exactly 2 are trashed, drawn cards included; the condition is public.
- **OP10-028 Kouzuki Momonosuke** "[Activate: Main] You may rest 2 of your DON!! cards and trash this Character: Look at 5 ...; reveal up to 2 "The Akazaya Nine" type cards and add them to your hand." Costs `restDon: 2` (active DON!!) and `trashSelf`; not offered with fewer than 2 active DON!!. Type filter on any card category.
- **OP10-029 Dracule Mihawk** "[On Play] If you have 2 or more rested Characters, set up to 1 of your rested "ODYSSEY" type Characters with a cost of 5 or less as active." Printed-type match, current cost 5 or less, rested only.
- **OP10-030 Smoker** "[Banish] ... [Activate: Main] Set up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active. Then, you cannot set DON!! cards as active using Character effects during this turn." The DON!! is set first (this Character effect is not yet barred), then the G21 player restriction applies for the turn: it bars Character effects only (Leader, Event and Stage effects and the Refresh Phase are not affected). No Once Per Turn and no rest cost on the text, so it can be used again (and then sets nothing).
- **OP10-032 Tashigi** "If you have a green Character other than [Tashigi] that would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may rest this Character instead." A `leaveField` replacement with `cause: 'opponentEffect'` (K.O., trash, return to hand or deck by the opponent's effect); it protects a green Character that is not named Tashigi (so not herself, not another Tashigi); not offered when she is already rested; no Once Per Turn on the text (resting herself is the natural limit). A battle K.O. and your own effects are not covered.
- **OP10-034 Franky** "[Once Per Turn] If this Character would be K.O.'d in battle, you may add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand instead." G11 `ko` replacement with `cause: 'battle'`; Life to hand is not damage (no [Trigger]); unavailable with no Life; Once Per Turn is spent only when used.
- **OP10-035 Brook** "[On K.O.] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Character cards with a cost of 5 or less." As OP15-024 (M6b-Q48, open): a Leader has no cost, so the limit restricts Characters only and the opposing Leader is always eligible.

No engine bugs found. Open questions: none new; M6b-Q20 (OP10-042) and M6b-Q48 (OP10-035) are the ones whose readings these scripts apply.
