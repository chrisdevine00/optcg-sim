# Wave 5 scripting, group 1 of 2: readings

Cards scripted from the verified text (`cards/data` merged with `cards/overrides`) after engine wave 5 ([m6b-wave5.md](m6b-wave5.md)): EB02-035, EB03-033, EB03-051, OP03-008, OP09-025, OP09-061, OP09-065, OP09-068, OP09-070, OP09-073, OP09-076, OP09-104, OP09-119. Open questions M6b-Q65 to Q69 are the wave's readings; nothing here settles them. One line per reading, with the card text.

- **EB02-035** "[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When 2 or more DON!! cards on your field are returned to your DON!! deck, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active." A DON!! −N cost counts as a return (Q31 family); the event carries the whole count of one instruction, so two returns of 1 never make 2. "[Your Turn]" is `yourTurn` as a condition on the ability (repo convention, as OP17-062).
- **EB02-035** "[On Play] If the number of DON!! cards on your field is equal to or less than the number on your opponent's field, draw 1 card." Colon-less "If": an effect condition. `donOnField` counts the cost area plus given DON!! (rested and active), after the play cost has been paid (the paying DON!! are still on the field, rested).
- **EB03-033** "[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When a DON!! card on your field is returned to your DON!! deck by your effect, if your Leader has the {Big Mom Pirates} type, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and rest it." `cause: 'yourEffect'` (M6b-Q65: a DON!! −N cost is part of the effect, so it fires). The inline "if" is read as an effect condition (colon-less rule): the Once Per Turn is spent by a first return even when the Leader has the wrong type. The Leader type is read when the effect resolves.
- **EB03-051** "[On Play] If you have a face-up Life card, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 2 or less. Then, turn all of your Life cards face-down." "Then" is not "if you do" and follows the "If", so it sits inside the guard: with no face-up Life card nothing happens, and with one the Life cards turn even if no Character was K.O.'d. The face-up count is public, so the guard is not a hidden gate. "Cost of 2 or less" never matches a Leader.
- **OP03-008** "This Character cannot be K.O.'d in battle by "Slash" attribute cards." `cannotBeKOd { cause: 'battle', by: { attribute: 'Slash' } }`: the attacker's printed attribute, Leader or Character; an effect K.O. or another attribute is not stopped. Read on the attacker where it stands when the K.O. is checked (Q68).
- **OP03-008** "[On Play] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 red Event and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." One `lookAtTop` with `bottomAnyOrder`. The pick is "up to 1" and is asked at a maximum of 0 when no red Event is among the 5, so the question does not show the deck; the owner orders the rest.
- **OP09-025** "If your Leader has the "ODYSSEY" type, this Character cannot be K.O.'d in battle by Leaders." Permanent with a `leader` condition (checked live: losing the Leader type lifts it) and `by: { category: ['Leader'] }`. A Character attacker, an effect, or a Leader without the type all still K.O. it. `"ODYSSEY" type` is the exact type (DECISIONS 2026-11-25).
- **OP09-061** "[DON!! x1] All of your Characters gain +1 cost." `donGiven: 1` on the Leader itself (at least 1, not per DON!!), +1 cost on every one of your Characters; the Leader and the opponent's Characters are not changed. A DON!! given to a Character does not switch it on.
- **OP09-061** "[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When 2 or more DON!! cards on your field are returned to your DON!! deck, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active, and add up to 1 additional DON!! card and rest it." Two `addDon` entries, each its own "up to 1" count: the second may be declined independently of the first (Q65 for the return itself).
- **OP09-065, OP09-068, OP09-070, OP09-073, OP09-076, OP09-119** "You may return 1 or more DON!! cards from your field to your DON!! deck:" is the cost `{ returnDon: { atLeast: 1 } }` on an auto ability (optional by construction): the owner names how many (1 up to every DON!! on the field, given DON!! included), then which if the places differ (G42, wave 5). One `donReturned` event of the whole count.
- **OP09-065** "This Character gains [Rush] during this turn. Then, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 6 or less." "Then" is not "if you do": the rest does not depend on the Rush. A Leader is never a target of a cost filter.
- **OP09-068** "[End of Your Turn] ... Set this Character as active. Then, this Character gains [Blocker] until the end of your opponent's next turn." The Blocker is gained even when the Character was already active. `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` is the plain duration.
- **OP09-070** "Give up to 2 rested DON!! cards to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." The DON!! count is asked first (as many as are rested, up to 2, or none), then the one card that receives them. The cost's returned DON!! are gone before the give, so with every DON!! returned nothing is rested to give.
- **OP09-073** "[When Attacking] ... Give up to 2 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn." Up to 2 Characters (never the Leader), each −2000 for the turn; the pick may be empty and the cost is already paid.
- **OP09-076** "Add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active." The return goes first, so the DON!! deck holds what was returned; "up to 1" asks a 0 or 1 count. With the DON!! deck empty and nothing returned there is nothing to add, but the cost always puts at least 1 back.
- **OP09-104** "[On Play] Add up to 1 "Revolutionary Army" type Character card from your hand to the top of your Life cards face-up. Then, if you have 2 or more Life cards, add 1 card from the top or bottom of your Life cards to your hand." The "Then, if" is an independent effect condition counted after the first step (Life 1 plus the added card is 2, so the card is taken; no card added with 1 Life takes nothing). The Life move is G45: blind, mandatory, not damage. The added card is face-up in the Life area and, if taken back from the top, goes to the hand. The hand pick is asked at a maximum of 0 with no match.
- **OP09-104** "[Trigger] If your Leader is multicolored, draw 2 cards." Colon-less "If": an effect condition, so the Trigger is activated and trashed (not added to the hand) and nothing is drawn with a single-colour Leader.
- **OP09-119** "[On Play] You may return 1 or more DON!! cards ...: Draw 1 card and this Character gains [Rush] during this turn." Two independent steps after the cost.
