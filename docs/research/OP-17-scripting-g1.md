# OP-17 scripting, group 1 of 3: readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP17-001 to 005, 007 to 016, 019, 021, 024 (18 scripted). Not scripted: OP17-018 (needs G18, `ko` on a Stage, wave 2) and OP17-020 (needs G19, cost alternatives `oneOf`, wave 2). Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. No new rulings were invented; where the text is silent the nearest reading on file is used.

- **OP17-001 Edward.Newgate (Leader)**, "[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Up to 1 of your Leader or Characters gains +4000 power during this battle." The cost is the optional "you may" (an auto with a cost is optional by construction); Once Per Turn is spent when the ability activates. "Up to 1" is a pick of up to 1 over Leader and Characters.
- **OP17-002 Atmos / OP17-009 Haruta**, "[Opponent's Turn] This Character gains +3000 power." A permanent with the `opponentsTurn` condition, applying to this Character only.
- **OP17-003 Izo**, "[On Play] If your Leader is [Edward.Newgate] or has the {Land of Wano} type, give up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters -6000 power during this turn." An `if` step (checked as the effect resolves, as EB05-001). "Rested" is read when the target is chosen.
- **OP17-004 Inuarashi & Nekomamushi**, "Up to 1 of your {Land of Wano} type Characters or up to 1 of your Characters with a type including \"Whitebeard Pirates\" gains [Rush]." Read as ONE pick of up to 1 over the union of the two sets ("or" chooses a set; the text does not say both sets may each get one). It may pick itself (it is {Land of Wano}). Open: a literal two-targets reading would let a player give Rush to one Wano and one Whitebeard Character.
- **OP17-005 Edward.Newgate (Character)**, "If your opponent has a Character with 10000 power or more, give this card in your hand -4 cost." A `hand` permanent, condition read on current power. "[On Play] Your monocolored Leader's base power becomes 8000": a Leader of two colors is not affected (`not multicolor`); the effect replaces base power (it can lower a base above 8000), DON!! bonuses still add. Duration `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` = until the end of the opponent's next End Phase.
- **OP17-007 Kouzuki Oden**, "play up to 1 {Land of Wano} type Character card or Character card with a type including \"Whitebeard Pirates\" with 6000 power or less from your hand." The verified text (override fix) puts "or Character card" after the first type, so the 6000 limit is read as applying to both alternatives (one `power <= 6000` filter on printed power in hand). Open: if the limit were meant for the second alternative only, a {Land of Wano} Character of any power would be playable.
- **OP17-008 Jozu**, "Your [Edward.Newgate] Leader's base power becomes 8000": target is the Leader filtered by name; no "up to", so it is automatic; with a different Leader nothing happens.
- **OP17-010 Fossa**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If your opponent has a Character with 10000 power or more and you have no other [Fossa], this Character gains [Blocker] and +2000 power until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." The "If" opens the sentence, so it is an ability condition: the activation is not offered when it is false and Once Per Turn is not spent (rulings.md: "with an activation timing, the condition must hold at that moment", QA-OP01 OP01-061). "No other [Fossa]" counts your Characters only (`isSelf: false`). The 10000 is current power, read at activation. Open: the engine treats the activation as unavailable rather than available-and-fizzling.
- **OP17-011 Blamenco**, "[DON!! x2] [When Attacking] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -4000 power during this turn." `donGiven: 2` as an ability condition (CR 8-3-2-3); the -4000 lasts the turn, not only the battle.
- **OP17-012 Blenheim**, "[On K.O.] Play up to 1 card with a cost of 1 and a type including \"Whitebeard Pirates\" from your hand." No `category` filter is written because the text says "card", and the engine only offers Characters and Stages to a `play` (a cost-1 Event is not a candidate; tested). The cost is the printed cost in hand (`cost: eq 1`).
- **OP17-013 Portgas.D.Ace**, "[On Play] If your Leader is [Edward.Newgate], give up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters -6000 power." Only the name counts here (no {Land of Wano} clause, unlike OP17-003); the hand cost reduction reads current power as OP17-005 does.
- **OP17-014 Whitey Bay**, "[On Your Opponent's Attack] You may trash this Character: Your Leader gains +1000 power during this battle." The cost is `trashSelf`, so the Character is trashed even if the +1000 is not needed; the target is the Leader with no pick. Not Once Per Turn (the text has no such clause). [On Play] K.O. filter is base power (printed), up to 1.
- **OP17-015 Marco**, "If one of your Characters would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may K.O. this Character instead." Read as the `leaveField`, cause `opponentEffect` replacement (as OP17-045 and EB05-061, M6b-Q7); a battle K.O. is not covered (tested). The substitute is a K.O. of this Character (`isSelf`, the validator rejects `from: 'self'` for a K.O.), which fires Marco's own [On K.O.]. Open (M6b-Q7 family): whether a Character removed by the opponent's effect "from the field" includes a Marco that is itself the removed one (the script lets Marco replace his own removal by his own K.O.).
- **OP17-015**, "[On K.O.] You may trash 1 card with a type including \"Whitebeard Pirates\" from your hand: Play this Character card from your trash." The play is `from: 'self'` (the Marco card now in the trash); the cost is optional and unpayable without a matching hand card. The card returns active (the text does not say rested).
- **OP17-016 Rakuyo**, "K.O. up to 2 of your opponent's Characters with 2000 base power or less." Pick of up to 2 on printed base power.
- **OP17-019 I Don't Have Time to Chat with Snot-Nosed Brats**, "[Main] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 card with a type including \"Whitebeard Pirates\" and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." One `lookAtTop` (5, take up to 1 revealed, rest `bottomAnyOrder`). [Trigger] "Your Leader gains +1000 power during this turn": no pick, the Leader is the only target.
- **OP17-021 Crone Oil**, "If your Character with a type including \"Red-Haired Pirates\" would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may rest 1 of your cards instead." The replacement is the `leaveField` form with the type filter; "1 of your cards" is read as in EB05-019 / OP15-035: the Leader, a Character, the Stage or an active DON!! (only active cards can be rested), so it cannot be used when all of those are already rested. Crone Oil is {Red-Haired Pirates Allies} and protects herself.
- **OP17-024 Howling Gab**, "[Banish] [On Play] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters." Keyword from the override; the rest is a pick of up to 1.
