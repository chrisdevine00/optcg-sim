# OP-09 scripting, group 2 of 4: readings

Cards: OP09-031, 032, 034, 035, 036, 037, 043, 044, 046, 047, 048, 050, 051, 052, 053, 054, 056, 064, 066, 069, 071, 074 (22 cards, all `verified: true`; all scripted). Gaps used (all merged): G28 (OP09-052), `donReturned` (OP09-074, as OP11-077). Block 3 audit gap B3-G12 (OP09-052) maps to the unified G28 (`koCause`, merged). No wave 5+ gap and no design item D1 to D9 is needed. One line per reading, with the card text.

- **OP09-031 Donquixote Doflamingo** "[Blocker] [End of Your Turn] If you have 2 or more rested Characters, set this Character as active." No colon, so an effect `if` (DECISIONS 2026-11-24, B3A-R1) checked when the ability resolves; a rested Doflamingo counts himself (1 rested: stays rested); only your own Characters count.
- **OP09-032 Donquixote Rosinante** "[Blocker] [On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] Set this Character as active." `onOpponentAttack` auto with Once Per Turn, resolving before the block step, so a rested Rosinante can block the attack that triggered it; blocking rests him again and the second attack of the turn does not set him active. Fires for an attack on the Leader or on a Character.
- **OP09-034 Perona** "[On Play] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 [Dracule Mihawk] or "Thriller Bark Pirates" type card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order and trash 1 card from your hand." `lookAtTop` 5, take up to 1 of (name [Dracule Mihawk] or exact type {Thriller Bark Pirates}), revealed; the "trash 1" is its own step with no "if you do" (it happens when nothing was taken), taken from the hand after the rest went to the bottom, so the card just added may be the one trashed; an empty hand trashes nothing.
- **OP09-035 Portgas.D.Ace** "[On Play] If you have 2 or more rested Characters, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less." Effect `if` (no colon); Ace is played active and does not count herself; "cost of 5 or less" is current cost, inclusive; the opponent's rested Characters do not count.
- **OP09-036 Monkey.D.Luffy** "[On Play] If you have 2 or more rested Characters, rest up to 1 of your opponent's DON!! cards or Characters with a cost of 6 or less." One pick over the opponent's Characters and cost-area DON!!; "with a cost of 6 or less" qualifies the Characters only (a DON!! card has no cost), as ST26-002 and EB03-012; only an active DON!! can be rested. Effect `if`.
- **OP09-037 Lim** "[On Play] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "ODYSSEY" type card other than [Lim] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [End of Your Turn] If you have 3 or more rested Characters, set this Character as active." The type is the exact {ODYSSEY}, any category; every card named [Lim] is excluded; the end-of-turn condition is an effect `if`, herself included.
- **OP09-043 Alvida** "[On K.O.] If your Leader has the "Cross Guild" type, play up to 1 Character card with a cost of 5 or less other than [Alvida] from your hand." Any K.O. (battle or effect); effect `if` on the Leader's exact type {Cross Guild}; Characters only; cost 5 inclusive; every card named [Alvida] excluded; played active. The Leader is public, so the condition does not gate a hidden-information prompt (the play pick itself is an unguarded "up to 1" over the hand).
- **OP09-044 Izo** "[When Attacking] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Land of Wano" type card or card with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates" and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order and trash 1 card from your hand." {Land of Wano} is an exact type, "a type including Whitebeard Pirates" a substring test (Former Whitebeard Pirates, Whitebeard Pirates Allies qualify, CR 2-4-3-1); the trash is unconditional, as OP09-034.
- **OP09-046 Crocodile** "[On Play] Play up to 1 "Cross Guild" type Character card or Character card with a type including "Baroque Works" with a cost of 5 or less from your hand." The cost limit qualifies both alternatives; Characters only; cost 5 inclusive; played active.
- **OP09-047 Kouzuki Oden** "[Double Attack] [On K.O.] Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand." Any K.O.; the trash is taken after the draw from the enlarged hand (a drawn card is a candidate). [Double Attack] comes from the override. (Oden has 10000 power: the shared battle-K.O. kit's 9000 attacker cannot K.O. him, so the battle test uses a 10000-power attacker.)
- **OP09-048 Dracule Mihawk** "[Blocker] [On Play] Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand." As OP09-047.
- **OP09-050 Nami** "[When Attacking] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 blue Event and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." An Event whose colours include Blue (a blue Character or a non-blue Event is not offered); no trash step.
- **OP09-051 Buggy** "[On Play] Place up to 1 of your opponent's Characters at the bottom of the owner's deck. Then, if you do not have 5 Characters with a cost of 5 or more, place this Character at the bottom of the owner's deck." The second step is an effect `if` of its own ("Then, if" is not "If you do", M4b-5 does not apply: the condition is a fact, not the result of the first step), so it is checked also when nothing was placed (up to 1, or no opposing Character). The count is your Characters with current cost 5 or more, Buggy (cost 10) included, so he stays only with 4 other such Characters (a board of 5). Self-placement is a `move` of the `isSelf` Character from `characters`: the engine's `move` does not support `from: 'self'` (see the report).
- **OP09-052 Marco** "[Opponent's Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand: When this Character is K.O.'d by your opponent's effect, play this Character card from your trash rested." The colon follows "You may trash 1 card from your hand", so the trash is the optional cost and "When ... K.O.'d by your opponent's effect" (G28 `koCause: opponentEffect`) with the opponent's-turn condition is the trigger; a battle K.O., a K.O. by your own effect, or an opponent's effect on your own turn does not trigger it. The cost trashes a hand card, not Marco, so `play { from: 'self' }` is safe (as ST30-008, OP14-120). With an empty hand the optional is offered but accepting is illegal (as ST30-008).
- **OP09-053 Mohji** "[On Play] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 [Richie] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order and play up to 1 [Richie] from your hand." The play is not conditioned on a reveal ("Then ... and play"): a [Richie] already in hand, or the one just added, can be played; matched by exact name, Characters only; asked as an "up to 1" pick (max 0 when none).
- **OP09-054 Richie** "[Blocker]" keyword only; no abilities (the keyword comes from the override).
- **OP09-056 Mr.3(Galdino)** "[On Play] Look at 4 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Cross Guild" type card or card with a type including "Baroque Works" other than [Mr.3(Galdino)] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." The "other than" applies to both alternatives; any category; every card named [Mr.3(Galdino)] excluded.
- **OP09-064 Killer** "[On Play] DON!! −1 (You may return the specified number of DON!! cards from your field to your DON!! deck.): Set up to 1 of your "Kid Pirates" type Leader as active." Optional DON!! -1 cost (rested or active DON!! can be returned); "up to 1" Leader, a Leader without the exact type {Kid Pirates} is never a candidate; the cost is paid even when nothing is set.
- **OP09-066 Jean Bart** "[On Play] If your opponent has more DON!! cards on their field than you, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 3 or less." DON!! on the field = cost area (active and rested) plus DON!! given to cards, not the DON!! deck; strictly more; both counts read when the effect resolves (after Jean Bart's cost was paid, which rests but does not remove DON!!); effect `if`; cost 3 inclusive, current cost.
- **OP09-069 Trafalgar Law** "[On Play] Look at 4 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Straw Hat Crew" or "Heart Pirates" type card with a cost of 2 or more and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." Reading: "with a cost of 2 or more" qualifies both types (a {Straw Hat Crew} card of cost 1 is not offered); any category (a {Straw Hat Crew} Event of cost 2 or more is); inclusive at 2. Worth a reviewer check: the other reading (cost only on {Heart Pirates}) would offer cost-1 {Straw Hat Crew} cards.
- **OP09-071 Nico Robin** "[Blocker]" keyword only; no abilities.
- **OP09-074 Bepo** "[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When a DON!! card on your field is returned to your DON!! deck, up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +1000 power during this turn." As OP11-077: `donReturned` for your own DON!!, any return (a DON!! -X cost or an effect), your turn only, once per turn (the first return of the turn); the target is chosen when the effect resolves (Bepo himself or the Leader are legal); the opponent's DON!! returning, or any return on the opponent's turn, does nothing.
