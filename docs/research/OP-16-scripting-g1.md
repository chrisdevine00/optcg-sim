# OP-16 scripting, group 1 of 4: readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP16-001 to 003, 005 to 015, 017 to 022, 025 to 027, 029, 030 (25 scripted). Not scripted: OP16-024 Inazuma, which needs B5-G16 = G28 ("[On K.O.] qualified by the K.O. cause", wave 3, "When this Character is K.O.'d by your opponent's effect"). Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. No new rulings were invented.

- **OP16-001 Portgas.D.Ace (Leader)**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Up to 1 of your [Monkey.D.Luffy] Characters or up to 1 of your Characters with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates", with 8000 power or more, gains [Rush] during this turn." Read as ONE pick of up to 1 over the union, with the 8000 clause on the Whitebeard branch only: the comma sets "with 8000 power or more" off from the second branch, and a 6000 Luffy (OP16-015) with its hand discount is the card this Leader is built around. The audit row read the 8000 as applying to both; the other reading is a one-line change (move the `power` filter outside the `any`). Open for review.
- **OP16-002 Izo / 007 Jozu / 010 Namule / 011 Vista**, "You may reveal 1 Character card with 8000 power from your hand". An auto with a `reveal` cost on `power: eq 8000` printed power in hand (exactly 8000: a 7000 or 9000 Character cannot pay). The card stays in hand; a `CardRevealed` event is emitted.
- **OP16-003 Edward.Newgate**, "[Your Turn] Your Leader gains [Double Attack] and +2000 power." A permanent with `yourTurn`, two modifiers on the Leader. "[On Play] You may reveal 2 Character cards with 8000 power": `reveal` with `exactly: 2`; "up to 1" opposing Character gets -6000 this turn.
- **OP16-005 Thatch**, "If you have a Character with 8000 power or more and a type including "Whitebeard Pirates" give this card in your hand -3 cost." A `hand` permanent; the condition reads current power on your field (7000 plus a given DON!! on your turn counts). Keyword [Blocker] from the override.
- **OP16-006 Shanks**, "[On Play] You may rest 2 of your DON!! cards: K.O. up to 1 ... 4000 power or less." `restDon: 2`; the K.O. filter reads current power (not base).
- **OP16-008 Squard**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 of your Characters with 10000 base power: K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 8000 power or less." Wave-1 G1 cost `trash` from `characters` on `basePower: eq 10000` (Squard itself is 7000 and cannot pay). The K.O. is current power.
- **OP16-009 Speed Jil**, "gains [Rush] and +2000 power until the end of your opponent's next End Phase": two steps on `self`, duration `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`.
- **OP16-010 Namule / OP16-011 Vista / OP16-013 McGuy**, "2000 base power or less" / "8000 base power or less": `basePower` filter (printed), so a 7000 Character with DON!! given is a legal target for McGuy; Vista's "up to 2" is a pick of up to 2. "[DON!! x1] [When Attacking]" is `donGiven: 1` as an ability condition.
- **OP16-012 Benn.Beckman**, "[On Play] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards: If your Leader has the {Red-Haired Pirates} type and you have 10 DON!! cards on your field, play up to 1 [Shanks] from your hand." The "If" is after the colon, so it is an `if` step (the cost is paid and may achieve nothing). "10 DON!! on your field" is `donOnField >= 10` (cost area plus given, rested and active; the rested cost DON!! counts). "Up to 1": the [Shanks] may be left in hand.
- **OP16-014 Marco**, as OP17-015 with "Character card with 8000 power" in the [On K.O.] cost: a `leaveField` replacement (cause `opponentEffect`) whose substitute is the K.O. of this Character; the same open question (M6b-Q7) about Marco replacing his own removal applies.
- **OP16-015 Monkey.D.Luffy**, "If your Leader's card name includes "Ace" and you have 6 or more DON!! cards on your field, give this card in your hand -2 cost." `nameIncludes: 'Ace'` on the Leader (G13; a Leader such as [Ace & Newgate] matches) and `donOnField >= 6` (given DON!! count). "[On Your Opponent's Attack] You may trash 1 Character card with 8000 power from your hand: Your Leader and this Character's base power becomes 7000 during this turn": two `setBasePower` steps (Leader, self), duration `thisTurn`; it can lower a base above 7000.
- **OP16-017 LittleOars Jr.**, "If you have no Characters with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates" and a cost of 8 or more give this Character -4000 power." `none` over your Characters on current cost, so a cost-7 Whitebeard Character (printed or reduced) does not remove the penalty, and nor does a cost-8 Character without the type.
- **OP16-018 Rockstar**, "[Once Per Turn] If your {Red-Haired Pirates} type Character would be K.O.'d, you may trash 1 Character card with 6000 power or more from your hand instead." A `ko` replacement with no cause (battle and effects), `oncePerTurn`, the substitute a hand cost (Rockstar protects herself). Once Per Turn is spent when the replacement is used.
- **OP16-019 Let's Show 'Em What We're Made Of!!**, "[Main] Play up to 2 Character cards with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates" and 8000 power from your hand." `power: eq 8000`; costs 9. [Trigger] "Your Leader gains +1000 power during this turn": no pick.
- **OP16-020 If You're Coming with Me... Kiss Your Lives Goodbye!!**, "[Main] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards and reveal 1 Character card with 8000 power from your hand: Draw 1 card." Both are one optional cost (`costOptional`, two cost entries). "[Counter] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +3000 power during this battle", also `costOptional`.
- **OP16-021 Moby Dick (Stage)**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Whitebeard Pirates} type, look at 3 cards ... add up to 1 card to your hand" is an `if` step over `lookAtTop` with no filter and no reveal (the text does not reveal). "[Activate: Main] You may trash this Stage: Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters": `trashSelf` cost with no `costOptional` (the optional-cost check forbids it on a non-Event; an activation is the player's choice already); the target is chosen exactly 1 and one rested DON!! is given when one exists, otherwise nothing, and the Stage is still trashed.
- **OP16-022 Monkey.D.Luffy (Leader)**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If the only Characters on your field are {Impel Down} type Characters, set up to 2 of your DON!! cards as active." The leading "If" before the Activate text is an activation condition (not offered when false, Once Per Turn not spent). Read as `none` of your Characters that are not {Impel Down}, so it is also true with no Characters at all. Open: whether "the only Characters ... are" needs at least one.
- **OP16-025 Bunkov / OP16-029 Antlerkov**, "[When Attacking] If you have [Antlerkov], play up to 1 Character card with a cost of 2 or less from your hand." An `if` step (as OP17-003); the partner may be rested; the play is of printed cost 2 or less and only Characters (an Event of cost 1 is never offered).
- **OP16-026 Emporio.Ivankov**, "Look at 3 ...; reveal up to 1 {Impel Down} type card, add it to your hand and place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. Then, play up to 1 Character card with a cost of 2 or less from your hand." The play is a separate step ("Then"), not conditional on the take, and the card just added to hand is eligible.
- **OP16-027 Jinbe**, "[DON!! x1] This Character gains +2000 power." A permanent with `donGiven: 1`.
- **OP16-030 Trafalgar Law**, "[On Play] Up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase." `skipRefresh` on a rested pick (G26 reading: it ends after that Refresh Phase). "[End of Your Turn] Set all of your green Characters with a cost of 5 or less as active": all matching, no pick; Law (cost 8) is not set.

## Tooling note (cards/src, not changed)

`cards/test/optional-cost.test.ts` fails for OP16-011: its `[DON!! x1] [When Attacking]` unit is not split from the [On Play] unit before it ("no text unit found for this ability"), because `HEADERS` in `cards/src/optional-cost.ts` does not list the `[DON!! xN]` tag as a unit start and the lookbehind keeps `[DON!! x1] [When Attacking]` together. Adding `DON!!\\s*x\\d+` to `HEADERS` should fix it.
