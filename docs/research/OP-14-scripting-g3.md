# OP-14 scripting, group 3 of 4: readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP14-062, 063, 064, 065, 067, 068, 069, 070, 071, 072, 074, 075, 076, 077, 078, 079, 080, 081, 082, 083, 084, 085, 086 (23 cards, all `verified: true`, all scripted; none needed a wave 4+ gap or a design item). Scripts in `cards/effects/OP-14/`, tests in `engine/test/cards/OP14-0*.test.ts` (helpers in `helpers-op14g3.ts`). The audit's gaps map as: B4-G1 = G4 `mill` (wave 1), B4-G17 = G1 cost on your own Characters (wave 1), B4-G2 = G2 `cannotBeRested` (wave 1), B4-G7 = G24 `rested` timing (wave 3), B4-G38 = G27 `returnDon` for the opponent (wave 3). All merged. One line per reading; "no ruling" means the reading is the scripter's.

- **OP14-062 Gladius**, "[On K.O.] DON!! -1: K.O. or rest up to 1 ... base power 6000 or less": a DON!! -1 cost (always optional, RL-52), then a choose-one of K.O. or rest, each with its own "up to 1" target (the mode is chosen before the target). Base power, inclusive at 6000 (a 0-power Character is in range).
- **OP14-063 Sugar**, "[On K.O.] If your opponent has 6 or more DON!! cards on their field": counts the opponent's cost area plus given DON!! (rules-digest: the field includes the cost area), tested at 6 and 5 and with given DON!!. The hand pick is asked (max 0 with no candidate) and never gated by an `exists` over the hand (no leak, OP15-002 fix round).
- **OP14-064 Giolla**, "Add up to 1 DON!! ... and rest it. Then, K.O. up to 1 ... base power of 0": "Then" is sequential, not "If you do": the K.O. happens with an empty DON!! deck or after declining the add (tested). `basePower: eq 0`.
- **OP14-065 Senor Pink**, "Your opponent returns 1 DON!! card from their field": G27, the opponent chooses which (active, rested or given class); it is not a cost, so no decline. Open M6b-Q45 is about the active-only variant and is not needed here.
- **OP14-067 Dellinger**, DON!! add and the look are sequential steps of one effect; the look happens even with the DON!! declined. "reveal up to 1 {Donquixote Pirates} type card": any category (Event, Stage, Character), the rest to the bottom in any order. A deck of fewer than 5 looks at what is there.
- **OP14-068 Trebol**, "[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When a DON!! card on your field is returned to your DON!! deck, if your Leader has {Donquixote Pirates}": the Leader type is a trigger-time condition on the ability; any return of your DON!! counts (your own DON!! -X on your turn is excluded only by the opponent's-turn condition; a return by an opponent's effect, G27, counts). The DON!! returned by the opponent's own effects from their own field does not trigger it. The audit labelled this card "keywords only"; the text is a real trigger (M6b audit slip, no engine impact).
- **OP14-069 Donquixote Doflamingo**, "[On Play] DON!! -3: Choose one": the DON!! -3 is an optional cost; both modes may be chosen with the other Leader types (E-Q6: mode 1 then does nothing). "until the end of your opponent's next End Phase" on your own turn is the engine duration `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`. "Up to 3 ... cannot be rested" restricts rests by effects and the Character's own attack/block (G2). Cost 8 / cost 7 inclusive; costs read as current cost, Characters only (a Leader is not a target of either mode, the text says Characters).
- **OP14-070 Buffalo**, "When this Character becomes rested by your opponent's Character's effect, you may return 1 DON!! ...: If you do, set this Character as active": `rested` timing (G24, M6b-Q10, Q30) with `by: 'opponentEffect'` and a Character `sourceFilter`: an attack declaration, a block, a cost, your own effect and an opposing Leader, Event or Stage effect do not trigger it (tested). The return is a `may` step plus `ifDid`, not a cost; with no DON!! on your field the return cannot happen and it stays rested.
- **OP14-071 Pica / OP14-074 Monet [On Play] / OP14-076 Ever White [Main]**, "If your Leader has {Donquixote Pirates}": a leading "If" is an effect-level condition; for Ever White it follows the colon, so the 2 DON!! are rested even when the check fails (tested). Ever White's cost is `costOptional` (Event, B-1); with fewer than 2 active DON!! left after the Event is paid the accept is illegal.
- **OP14-072 Baby 5**, "[On K.O.] DON!! -1: Add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards": a single top card is asked as a yes/no ("up to 1" of one candidate), as in EB04-058. Declining the DON!! -1 skips everything.
- **OP14-074 Monet [On K.O.]**, "Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand. Then, add up to 2 DON!! cards ... and rest them": the draw and the trash are mandatory (exactly 1, the drawn cards are candidates); the count of DON!! is asked (0 to 2).
- **OP14-075 Lao.G**, "Then, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -2000 power during this turn": sequential; the opposing attacker is itself a legal target (it is a Character of theirs).
- **OP14-077 Penta-Chromatic String**, "Then, if your opponent has a Character with 6000 power or more": independent of whether a target was chosen; current power, inclusive at 6000, opposing Characters only (their Leader is not a Character).
- **OP14-078 Bullet String**, "DON!! -1: If your Leader has {Donquixote Pirates}, up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +2000 power during this battle. Then, that card gains an additional +2000 power during this turn": the DON!! -1 is the optional DON!! -X cost (declining skips the effect, the Event is still paid and trashed); "Then, that card" is read inside the "If", and the card is bound once so both bonuses go to the same card (no target: neither is given). After the battle the +2000 of the turn stays.
- **OP14-079 Crocodile (Leader)**, "All of your opponent's Characters cannot be removed from the field by your effects": a restriction on the opponent's Characters (`cannotBeRemovedByOpponentEffects` seen from them, i.e. against the controller's effects); battle K.O.s and their own effects are unaffected (tested with a control Leader). It covers K.O., trash and return to hand, but not a K.O. cost on your own Characters. "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may K.O. 1 of your Characters with a type including 'Baroque Works': ... Then, you may trash 2 cards from the top of your deck": the K.O. is the cost (the Leader's activation is the choice, so no `costOptional`), a "Former Baroque Works" type counts (`typeIncludes`), with one candidate it is paid with that card without a prompt; the -10 cost is "up to 1" and a current cost floors at 0; the mill is its own "you may" step, not a cost.
- **OP14-080 Gecko Moria (Leader)**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may K.O. 1 of your {Thriller Bark Pirates} Characters": the Once Per Turn belongs to the Activate only (the printed line has no line break before [When Attacking]); the K.O.'d Character is gone before the +1000, so it gets no bonus; the +1000 goes to the Leader and every Character you have, until the end of the turn. "[When Attacking] You may trash 3 cards from your hand": an optional hand cost (secret; the accept is illegal with fewer than 3 cards) followed by an "up to 1" top-of-deck add (yes/no).
- **OP14-081 Spider Mice**, "[On Play] Trash 3 cards from the top of your deck": plain `mill` (a deck of 3 or fewer is milled empty and RL-26 applies); "[On K.O.] ... a base cost of 1": `baseCost: eq 1`, inclusive, a base-2 Character reduced to 1 is not a target and a base-1 Character raised to 2 is (tested).
- **OP14-082 Oinkchuck**, "All of your {Thriller Bark Pirates} type Characters gain +4 cost until the end of your opponent's next End Phase": split on whose turn it resolves, because the single engine duration `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` would last a whole extra turn when the K.O. happens on the opponent's turn (the usual case, a battle K.O.): on the opponent's turn the cost lasts `thisTurn` (their End Phase is this turn's), on your own turn `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` (tested both). Worth review: if the intended reading is always the following opponent turn, the `if` should go. The [Trigger] "Play ... from your trash rested": `play { rested: true }`, cost 2 inclusive; with no candidate nothing is asked.
- **OP14-083 Ms. Wednesday**, "[Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: Give up to 1 of your opponent's 0 cost Characters -3000 power": the trash is the cost (paid even when no target exists); "0 cost" is the current cost, `cost: eq 0` (no printed-0 Character exists in the pool, so the test lowers costs with a planted `cost` effect; a printed cost of 1 is out of range).
- **OP14-084 Ms. All Sunday**, "play up to 1 Character card with a type including 'Baroque Works' and a cost of 4 or less and up to 1 ... a cost of 1 from your trash": two separate "up to 1" picks, cost-4-or-less first, then cost 1; a cost-1 card may fill either slot but not both (M6b-Q6: "and" means one of each, not one in total). A player who takes the cost-1 card with the first pick forfeits the second pick if no other cost-1 card exists. The Leader's type is checked with `typeIncludes` (a Leader of "Former Baroque Works" would count).
- **OP14-085 Miss.Goldenweek(Marianne)**, same shape as OP15-087: draw 2 then trash exactly 2 (mandatory; the drawn cards are candidates).
- **OP14-086 Miss Doublefinger(Zala)**, "If you have 7 or more cards in your trash, this Character gains +1000 power, and all of your Characters with a type including 'Baroque Works' gain +2 cost": one live permanent with a trash-count condition (both effects share it); she is Baroque Works herself so her own cost rises by 2 (tested at 6 and 7 and as the trash grows).

## Engine notes

- No engine bug found. Observations only: a cost-modified Character's current cost floors at 0 (OP14-079 test), and a K.O. cost or optional hand cost with a single legal candidate is paid without a prompt.
