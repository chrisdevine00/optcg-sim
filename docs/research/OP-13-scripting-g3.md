# OP-13 scripting, group 3 of 4: readings and ambiguities

Cards scripted (22, all `verified: true`): OP13-059, 060, 061, 062, 063, 065, 066, 067, 068, 069, 071, 072, 074, 075, 077, 078, 080, 081, 082, 083, 086, 087. Skipped as instructed: OP13-079 Imu (design item D1) and OP13-084 (unverified, B4-V2). No card needed a wave 4+ gap, a design item or `[DON!! xN] [On K.O.]`. The audit's gaps map as: B4-G15 = G11 `ko` replacement with a cause (OP13-060, wave 2), B4-G1 = G4 `mill` (OP13-087, wave 1). Scripts in `cards/effects/OP-13/`, tests in `engine/test/cards/OP13-0*.test.ts` (helpers in `helpers-op13g3.ts`). One line per reading; "no ruling" means the reading is the scripter's.

- **"Then" inside the "If" (OP13-061, 066, 067)**: "If you have any DON!! cards given, add up to 1 DON!! ... Then, K.O. ..." is one gated effect (EB05-033, audit Q10): with no DON!! given neither part happens; once inside, "Then" is not "If you do" (a declined or empty DON!! add still lets the K.O. through, tested). OP13-067 Gaban's "Then, add up to 1 DON!!" follows the same convention: the draw 2 / trash 1 / DON!! add are all behind the Leader check.
- **OP13-059 Brilliant Punk**, "You may return 1 of your Characters to the owner's hand: Return up to 1 Character with a cost of 6 or less": the cost is a real return of one of your Characters (G1), optional because it is an Event (B-1: asked after the Event is played, accept illegal with no Character of yours). The target is any Character on either field, current cost, inclusive at 6; the Character paid as the cost is gone and cannot be the target. With a single Character the cost is paid without a prompt. [Trigger] draw 1 is plain.
- **OP13-060 Amatsuki Toki**, "If your Character with a type including "Roger Pirates" would be K.O.'d by your opponent's effect, you may trash this Character instead": a `ko` replacement with cause `opponentEffect` (G11). There is no "other than this Character", so Toki may protect herself (trashing herself instead of being K.O.'d; it only avoids the K.O. event itself). Battle K.O.s, K.O.s by your own effects and a return to hand are not replaced (tested).
- **OP13-062 Crocus**, "[When Attacking] Return up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base power of 3000 or less": base power, inclusive at 3000, unaffected by boosts or reductions (tested both ways); Characters only, no Leader target.
- **OP13-066 Silvers Rayleigh**, "add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active at the end of this turn": read as one delayed step (the count is asked at the end of the turn and the DON!! enters active), not "add now (rested), set active later". Both readings leave the DON!! active for the next turn; they differ only if the DON!! deck or the field changes during the turn. Worth review if a ruling says the add is immediate.
- **OP13-068 Douglas Bullet**, "If you have 8 or more DON!! cards on your field": the cost area (active and rested) plus DON!! given, live (rules-digest); inclusive at 8.
- **OP13-069 Tom**, "[On Play] DON!! −1: Add up to 1 Stage card with a cost of 3 or less from your trash": DON!! −1 is the optional DON!! −X cost (RL-52); it can be paid with a rested DON!! (after the play cost every DON!! may be rested, tested). Only Stages are candidates (not Events), by current cost, inclusive at 3 (the pool has no cost-4 Stage, so the tests pin 3 in and 6 out).
- **OP13-071 Nekomamushi**, "K.O. up to 1 ... with 3000 base power or less": base power, inclusive at 3000; DON!! count as OP13-068.
- **OP13-072 Buggy**, "If your Leader's type includes "Roger Pirates" and you have any DON!! cards given": both must hold (each failing alone does nothing); a DON!! given to a Character counts, not only to the Leader.
- **OP13-074 Hera**, "Play up to 1 [Homies] type Character card with 3000 power or less from your hand": the exact type {Homies}, printed power (a card in hand has no boosts), inclusive at 3000, Character cards only; played without paying its cost, active. The hand pick is asked (max 0 with no candidate), never gated on a hand condition.
- **OP13-075 Guess We'll Have Another Scrap**, "[Main] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards: If your Leader is [Gol.D.Roger] and you have any DON!! cards given, add ...": the conditions follow the colon, so the DON!! is rested even when they fail; "any DON!! given" is checked after the rest (the rested DON!! is not given). [Counter] has no cost, +3000 during this battle.
- **OP13-077 Go All the Way to the Top!!**, "K.O. up to 1 ... 4000 base power or less and up to 1 ... 3000 base power or less": two separate "up to 1" picks in printed order (4000 first, then 3000), M6b-Q6 reading: a 3000 Character taken by the first pick is not available to the second, so choosing it first forfeits the second pick when no other 3000-or-less Character exists (tested). [Counter] is "during this turn": +3000 stays after the battle (tested).
- **OP13-078 Oro Jackson**, "[Once Per Turn] When your Character with a type including "Roger Pirates" is removed from the field by your opponent's effect": `removed` timing (G29) with cause `opponentEffect`, filtered to Character cards whose printed type includes Roger Pirates; K.O., trash and return to hand all count; a battle K.O. and your own effects do not; the use is spent only when it fires (a second removal in the same turn adds nothing, tested). M6b-Q20 (filter reads the card where it now is) applies.
- **OP13-080 St. Ethanbaron V. Nusjuro**, "If you have 7 or more cards in your trash, this Character cannot be removed from the field by your opponent's effects and gains [Rush]": one permanent with both clauses (the printed keyword list is empty; Rush is conditional, so with 6 cards it cannot attack the turn it is played, tested). A protected Character can still be chosen as a target; the removal then does nothing. "[When Attacking] If you have 10 or more cards in your trash, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn": effect-level `if`, Characters only, inclusive at 10 (9 asks nothing).
- **OP13-081 Koala**, "If your Leader has the "Revolutionary Army" type, this Character gains +3 cost": the exact type element. The Activate's cost is one card from the trash to the deck bottom (nothing to order); not offered with an empty trash. The effect gives one rested DON!! to the Leader or 1 Character, as OP14-005 (n 1: no count is asked, the target is chosen "exactly 1"); with no rested DON!! the cost is still paid and nothing is given.
- **OP13-082 Five Elders**, "[Activate: Main] If your Leader is [Imu], you may rest ... and trash ...: Trash all of your Characters and play up to 5 ...": the leading "If" before the colon is an activation condition (not offered without an [Imu] Leader, nothing paid); the "you may" is the player's choice to activate. The hand trash is asked even with one card (hidden zone). "Trash all of your Characters" includes Five Elders itself; the Characters just trashed are candidates for the play when they are "Five Elders" with 5000 power (printed, exact type). "Different card names" is a distinct-names pick (two copies of one name cannot both be chosen, tested). All five distinct 5000-power Five Elders can come back (tested).
- **OP13-083 St. Jaygarcia Saturn**: as OP13-080 for the protection (inclusive at 7); "reveal up to 1 "Five Elders" type card" is the exact type, any category (a {Five Elders} Event qualifies, a {Celestial Dragons}-only card does not), the rest to the bottom in any order.
- **OP13-086 Saint Shalria**, "reveal up to 1 "Celestial Dragons" type card other than [Saint Shalria] and add it to your hand. Then, trash the rest and trash 1 card from your hand": the rest of the 3 are trashed (not the bottom); the hand trash is mandatory and sequential (the card just taken is a legal choice), never "if you do"; with an empty hand nothing is asked.
- **OP13-087 Saint Charlos**, "[On Play] Trash 1 card from the top of your deck": mandatory `mill` of your own deck (G4); a one-card deck is milled empty (RL-26 applies at the next draw).
- **OP13-063 Kouzuki Oden / OP13-065 Shanks / OP13-067 Gaban**: "add up to 1 DON!!" always asks a count (0 or 1); "a type including "Roger Pirates"" is `typeIncludes` (a {Former Roger Pirates} card qualifies); OP13-065's "other than [Shanks]" excludes every card named exactly Shanks.

## Engine notes

- No engine bug found. Two validator observations, not blocking: `validateScript` accepted a `power` step with the unknown duration `'turn'` and a selector with an empty `filter: {}`; the first crashed at runtime (`addModifier`), the second silently matched nothing. Both were scripter errors caught by the author tests; worth a `dsl-validate.ts` check (duration must be one of the `Duration` values; a `Filter` object must have exactly one key).
- A Character protected by "cannot be removed from the field by your opponent's effects" is still offered as a target of an opposing "up to 1" selection; the effect then does nothing. Consistent with OP14-079's script, noted for the reviewer.
