# Block 5 starter decks, scripting group 1: readings

ST-30, ST-31, ST-32, ST-34 (runbook `new-set.md` step d). 21 cards scripted, 1 left (ST32-003, gap G24). Method and lessons from the EB-05, OP-17 and OP-16 batch reviews (`docs/reviews/m6b-*.md`). One line per reading, with the card text.

## Left unscripted

- **ST32-003 Dracule Mihawk**: "[Your Turn] When this Character becomes rested, draw 1 card and trash 1 card from your hand." Needs timing `rested` (audit G24 = B5-G17, wave 3). The [On Play] half (play a Character with cost 5 or less that is [Perona] or `<Slash>`) needs nothing new, but the card is scripted whole or not at all.

## Readings

- **ST30-002, ST30-017** "reveal up to 1 Character card with 6000 power": read on the card in the deck, so its printed power exactly 6000 (`power eq`), not "or more". "Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order": `rest: 'bottomAnyOrder'` (the owner orders, engine `order` decision). Tested: 5000 and 7000 are not candidates, the 6th card is not looked at.
- **ST30-003** "All of your Characters with 6000 base power gain +1000 power": includes Edward.Newgate himself (printed 6000); reads base power (a 5000 Character with a given DON!! is not boosted); [Your Turn] is a `yourTurn` condition; Leaders are not Characters.
- **ST30-004** "You may reveal 2 Character cards with 6000 power from your hand: Draw 3 cards and trash 2": the revealed cards stay in hand and may be trashed; "and" is a sequence, so the trash is of the hand after drawing.
- **ST30-007** [On Play] `restDon 1` then [Rush] this turn (as ST30-012). [When Attacking] "-1000 power" to "up to 1" opposing Character: the Leader is not a candidate.
- **ST30-008 Marco** "play this Character card from your trash rested": `play` of `self` with `rested: true`. The hand Character is the cost; nothing else conditions it.
- **ST30-009, ST30-011** "If your Character with 6000 base power would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect": `leaveField` replacement, cause `opponentEffect` (G14), filter base power exactly 6000. The replacing card itself (2000 and 1000 base) is never protected by its own text. A battle K.O. is not replaced. ST30-011: "rest this Character instead" is the `restSelf` cost form, so a rested Buggy cannot pay and the replacement is not offered. ST30-009: the replacement's payload is an effect (trash self from `characters`, then draw 1), because a cost list cannot carry the draw.
- **ST30-010 Crocodile** "will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase": `skipRefresh`, once; the Character is active again in the following Refresh Phase (tested).
- **ST30-014 Mr.3** "Give up to 2 of your Characters with 6000 base power up to 2 rested DON!! cards each": `giveDon each` with `upTo: 2` targets (G16). The engine caps the targets by the DON!! available (3 rested DON!! reach one target of 2 each, per the G16 planted test); the DON!! are the rested cost-area cards. "You may rest this Character" is the activation cost; Mr.3 cannot be activated while rested.
- **ST30-015** [Counter] "If you have 2 or more Characters with 6000 base power": the condition is an `if` step read as the effect resolves (EB05-008 precedent), so with fewer the Counter is played and does nothing. Counts base power. [Trigger] "K.O. up to 1 ... with 6000 power or less" reads current power.
- **ST30-016** "Then, if you have [Portgas.D.Ace] and [Monkey.D.Luffy] Characters with 6000 base power, draw 1": "Then" is an independent step (OP16 review), so choosing no target for "up to 1" still draws. The two names are exact `name` filters, base power 6000 each.
- **ST31-002 Jinbe** "Draw 1 card and play up to 1 {Straw Hat Crew} type card with a cost of 1 from your hand": a sequence; the drawn card is a candidate. No category filter: only a Character (or Stage) can be played, so a {Straw Hat Crew} Event of cost 1 is never offered (tested).
- **ST31-003 Brook** "a total of 3 or more given DON!! cards": `donGiven you` counts DON!! given to the Leader and every Character, not only to Brook. [Opponent's Turn] is an `opponentsTurn` condition. On its owner's turn nothing applies.
- **ST32-001 Kin'emon** "rest your <Slash> attribute Leader or 1 of your DON!! cards": `oneOf` cost (G19): a Leader without the attribute, or a rested Leader, leaves only the DON!! alternative; with neither payable the optional is not offered. The attribute name was restored from the official list (data commit 8b2351b).
- **ST32-002 Oden** "cannot be rested until the end of your opponent's next End Phase": unscoped `cannotBeRested` (rules-digest 57, G2): the Character cannot attack, block or be rested by an effect. Tested through attack legality on the opponent's turn and its end. "Draw 1 card and ..." is a sequence: the draw happens with no target.
- **ST32-004 Rayleigh** "If your Leader has the `<Slash>` attribute, this Character gains [Rush: Character]": a permanent with a `leader` condition (no printed keyword, so none in `keywords`). The [On Play] is not conditional.
- **ST32-005 Zoro** printed [Rush: Character] (`keywords`); [On Play] "If your Leader has the `<Slash>` attribute, rest ...": an `if` step, nothing is asked without the attribute.
- **ST34-001 Katakuri** "[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When a DON!! card on your field is returned to your DON!! deck, if your Leader has the {Big Mom Pirates} type": `donReturned` auto with `yourTurn` and the Leader type as conditions, so the Once Per Turn is spent only when they hold (an intervening if). "Add up to 2 DON!! ... and rest them" asks a count (`addMost` in tests: options 2, 1, None); the count is capped by the DON!! deck, which holds the returned card. [On K.O.] "Play up to 1 Character card with 8000 power or less from your hand" reads current power on a hand card (printed). The opponent's-turn exclusion is not driven in a test (no opponent effect returns your DON!! yet, G27).
- **ST34-002 Cracker** "If your Leader has {Big Mom Pirates}, add up to 1 DON!! ... Then, K.O. ...": "Then" sits inside the leading "If" (OP16 review, M4b-5): without the Leader type neither step happens. The K.O. does not depend on a DON!! having been added (no "If you do"), so an empty DON!! deck still K.O.s.
