# EB-04 group 2 of 2: scripting readings

Cards EB04-028 to EB04-060 (23 scripted). EB04-048 Rob Lucci is unverified (B4-V1) and was skipped. The cards of the set not listed (group 1, or already scripted) are outside this group. No card of this group needs a wave 4+ gap, a design item or `[DON!! xN] [On K.O.]`. The only gaps in the audit for the group were B4-G1 and B4-G15 (EB04-043, unified G4 `mill` and G11 `ko` with a cause, waves 1 and 2, both merged). One line per reading.

- EB04-028 Ice Time "[Main] You may trash 1 card from your hand: If your Leader has the {Navy} type, up to 2 ...": the cost is optional (`costOptional`, an Event) and is paid before the Leader check, so without a {Navy} Leader the card is still trashed and nothing else happens. The power limit is current power (10000 inclusive). "until the end of your opponent's next End Phase" is `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`. [Trigger] "Return up to 1 Character with a cost of 5 or less to the owner's hand": either side's Characters (`owner: 'any'`), current cost, goes to its owner's hand.
- EB04-029 "[Main] If your Leader is [Sanji], look at 3 ...; reveal up to 1 [Sanji] or Event card and add it to your hand. Then, trash the rest.": the "If" gates the look (a public Leader check). "[Sanji] or Event" is name Sanji or category Event, any card. [Counter] "Up to 1 of your [Sanji] cards": Leader or Character, name exactly Sanji (the Leaders named Sanji qualify); `battle` duration.
- EB04-033 "DON!! −1: If you have 3 or more {Foxy Pirates} type Characters, K.O. ...": the DON!! −1 is paid whatever the count; the count includes this Character (it is on the field when [On Play] resolves). The limit is base power (current power is ignored both ways).
- EB04-034 "[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand: If you have 4 or more Events in your trash, ...": the Events are counted after the cost, so a trashed Event counts. The target is "Leader or Character", up to 1 by the usual rule; `battle` duration. Once Per Turn is per card.
- EB04-035 "[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When a DON!! card on your field is returned to your DON!! deck, if your Leader has the {Kid Pirates} type, add up to 1 DON!! ... and rest it": `donReturned` with `owner: 'you'`, the Leader type is an ability condition (checked when it triggers), any cause counts (own DON!! −X, an effect of either player). "add up to 1" asks a count (0 or 1), rested.
- EB04-036 Foxy "[On Play] DON!! −1: If your Leader has the {Foxy Pirates} type, draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand. Then, rest ...": the whole sentence is inside the guard (a "Then" after a failed "If" does not resolve, M4b-5); the DON!! −1 is paid regardless. "cost of 9 or less" is current cost, so a Leader is never a target. [Activate: Main] "add up to 1 DON!! card ... and rest it": asks a count.
- EB04-037 Porche "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Foxy Pirates} type, look at 5 ...": the Leader check gates the look. "reveal up to 1 {Foxy Pirates} type card": any category, so an Event or Stage of the type qualifies. The rest go to the bottom in an order the owner picks.
- EB04-039 Kid "[On Play] Add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active": a count is asked (0 or 1). [Activate: Main] "You may trash this Character:" is a plain `trashSelf` cost (no `costOptional` on an Activate, as the optional-cost check requires). The play is from the hand only, active, free; with no legal card the hand pick is asked at max 0 (hand contents are hidden, so the prompt is not gated).
- EB04-041 Stealth Black "If your Leader is [Sanji] and you have 4 or more DON!! cards on your field": DON!! on the field means the cost area plus given DON!! (rested ones count, so paying the Event's own cost does not lower the count). "play up to 1 [Sanji] with 6000 power or less from your hand or trash": one choice over both zones, printed power for cards off the field, Characters only. [Trigger] draws before it trashes.
- EB04-042 Alpha "You may trash 3 cards from the top of your deck: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −1 cost during this turn": an optional `mill` cost; with fewer than 3 cards in the deck the cost cannot be paid and no prompt is shown (the engine skips an unpayable optional cost on a Character). `thisTurn` duration.
- EB04-043 Kaku "[Once Per Turn] If your black Character with a base cost of 5 or less would be K.O.'d by your opponent's effect, you may place 3 cards from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order instead.": `replacement` of `ko` with `cause: 'opponentEffect'`, matching any of your Characters that is black and has base cost 5 or less (Kaku included); "black" is the color filter, so a multicolor card with black also qualifies. The cost is paid by a `move` of exactly 3 trash cards to the deck bottom (an `order` decision follows). Once Per Turn is per Kaku card, and is used up only when the replacement is accepted. [On Play] "Trash 2 cards from the top of your deck" is the `mill` action (not optional, no cost).
- EB04-045 Ginny "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: If there are 2 or more Characters with a cost of 8 or more, ...": the rest is a cost (paid whatever the count); "there are" counts Characters on both fields, Ginny included, current cost, Leaders excluded. The target is "{Revolutionary Army} type Leader or Character", up to 1.
- EB04-046 Doll "[Opponent's Turn] All of your {Navy} type Characters gain +2 cost.": a permanent on the opponent's turn over your {Navy} Characters, Doll included; each Doll adds +2.
- EB04-047 Helmeppo "[Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: Play up to 1 {SWORD} type Character card with a cost of 3 or less other than [Helmeppo] from your hand or trash.": one `from: ['hand','trash']` choice; the exclusion is by name, so any other Helmeppo is excluded; the trashed Helmeppo is in the trash as the pick is made and is excluded by name too.
- EB04-049 Finger Pistol Yellow Lotus "[Main] You may trash 2 cards from the top of your deck: K.O. up to 1 ... base cost of 5 or less": an optional `mill` cost on an Event (asked even when it cannot be paid, only decline is legal); base cost, so a raised cost 5 Character is still a target. [Trigger] "Activate this card's [Main] effect" asks the optional cost again.
- EB04-051 Emet "This Character cannot attack unless there is a Character with 12000 base power or more.": a permanent `cannotAttack` on itself under a `none` condition over Characters on both fields (base power, Leaders excluded: a Leader is not a Character). [Trigger] "Give all of your opponent's Characters −3000 power during this turn. Then, if you have 0 Life cards, play this card.": the Life check is at resolution time; the card is already out of Life when the Trigger resolves, so it reads the Life left after the damage. The "Then" is independent of the power step (all targets, no choice).
- EB04-052 Sanji "[When Attacking] This Character's base power becomes the same as your opponent's Leader during this turn.": `setBasePower` from the opposing Leader's base power (M6b-Q9, proposed reading: base power). DON!! given and other modifiers still add on top. [On K.O.] "If you have 2 or less Life cards, play up to 1 yellow Character card with 6000 power or less from your hand": printed power from the hand (the pick is asked at max 0 when no card qualifies, hand contents are hidden).
- EB04-053 Sentomaru "[On Block] If you have 2 or less Life cards, draw 1 card.": a public Life check, no prompt.
- EB04-054 Kuma "[On Play] If you have 2 or less Life cards, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards.": an `up to` single card from the deck top, offered as an accept/decline. "[On K.O.] Add up to 1 card from the top of your opponent's Life cards to the owner's hand": the card goes to the opponent's hand (its owner), with no Life condition; asked as accept/decline.
- EB04-055 Kuma "[On K.O.] Play up to 1 {Revolutionary Army} type Character card with a cost of 4 or less from your hand." Printed cost in hand. [Trigger] "If your Leader has the {Revolutionary Army} type and you and your opponent have a total of 5 or less Life cards, play this card.": `life: 'both'`, read when the Trigger resolves (the revealed Life card is already out of the Life area).
- EB04-056 Pacifista "If you have [Jewelry Bonney] and you have 0 Life cards, this Character gains [Blocker].": `exists` over your Leader or Characters named [Jewelry Bonney] (the opponent's does not count), and 0 Life. The printed keywords stay empty (the Blocker is granted, not printed).
- EB04-057 Vegapunk "If you have 2 or less Life cards, all of your yellow {Scientist} type Characters cannot be removed from the field by your opponent's effects.": `cannotBeRemovedByOpponentEffects` on your yellow {Scientist} Characters (Vegapunk itself included), your own effects still remove them. "[DON!! x1] This Character gains [Blocker]" is a separate permanent with no Life condition.
- EB04-060 Gum-Gum Hawk Gatling "[Main] You may add 1 card from the top or bottom of your Life cards to your hand: Add up to 1 {Egghead} type Character card from your hand to the top of your Life cards face-up. Then, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −1000 power during this turn.": the cost asks top or bottom; the "Then" does not depend on the Egghead having been added (an "up to" with no pick is not a failure). Event-card Eggheads are not offered (Character cards only).
