# OP-15 scripting, group 2 of 3: readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP15-047, 048, 050 to 057, 060, 063 to 065, 068, 070, 072, 073, 079 to 083 (23 listed; 23 scripted). Nothing is left unscripted: the only gaps in the audit's Gap column for these cards (B4-G1 `mill`, B4-G17 own-Character costs and the replacement payload, B4-G21 activate own [On K.O.]) are G4, G1 and G5 of `m6b-runtime-audit.md` §2, all wave 1 and merged. No design item D1 to D9 is needed. Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text.

- **OP15-047 Sanji**, "[Blocker] [On Play] Up to 1 of your Characters gains [Unblockable] during this turn." Printed [Blocker] from the override. "Your Characters" includes Sanji himself.
- **OP15-048 Chinjao**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 Event from your hand: Draw 2 cards. [Opponent's Turn] [On K.O.] Your opponent places 1 card from their hand at the bottom of their deck." Auto with a cost (optional by construction). The cost's secrecy follows the hand rule: with no Event in hand the prompt is still shown (hand contents are not revealed) but cannot be accepted. The K.O. effect is an `onKO` auto with `opponentsTurn`, the opponent chooses their own card; a single hidden card needs no order. "Opponent's Turn" is the turn of Chinjao's controller's opponent (a battle K.O. on that turn, or any effect K.O. by them); a K.O. on your own turn (for example by your own cost) does not fire.
- **OP15-050 Bobby Funk**, "If you have [Kelly Funk], this Character gains +3000 power." Permanent, condition `exists` your Characters named [Kelly Funk] (a Character card). Not the opponent's, not in hand.
- **OP15-051 Monkey.D.Luffy**, "[Opponent's Turn] If your Leader has the "Dressrosa" type, this Character gains +3000 power." Permanent with `opponentsTurn` and a Leader type condition.
- **OP15-052 Leo**, "If your Character with 7000 base power or less would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may place 1 of your Characters at the bottom of the owner's deck instead." A `leaveField` replacement (cause `opponentEffect`), the same as OP15-069; the replaced set is your Characters with `basePower <= 7000` (base, not current; Leo included). The payload is the G1 own-Character `move` to the deck bottom, exactly 1 of any of your Characters: it may be the Character that would have been removed (then it is not K.O.'d) or Leo or another one. Battle K.O.s and your own effects are not replaced.
- **OP15-053 Rebecca**, "[DON!! x1] This Character gains [Blocker]. [On Play] Look at 3 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 {Dressrosa} type card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." `donGiven: 1` condition on a keyword grant (printed keywords are empty: the [Blocker] is conditional). The look is the standard `lookAtTop` with up to 1 {Dressrosa} card of any category; "Then, place the rest" is its `bottomAnyOrder` step (the owner is asked an order).
- **OP15-054 And No One Else Can Have It! It's Our Memento of Him**, "[Main] If your Leader is [Lucy], choose one: • Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand. Then, play up to 1 {Dressrosa} type Character card with a cost of 4 or less from your hand. • Return up to 1 Stage to the owner's hand." The "If" gates the whole choose-one (no colon), so the Event is played and spent, and no mode is asked, without a [Lucy] Leader (name exact: a different {Dressrosa} Leader fails). Mode 1: "Then" is read as a separate step, so the trashed card may be one the draw just brought, and the Character played may be one just drawn (tested). Mode 2: a Stage of either player, returned to its owner's hand (`owner: any`, an opposing Stage goes to the opposing hand); "up to 1", so none is allowed.
- **OP15-055 Go Ahead and Use 'Em, Mr. Luffy!!!**, "[Main] Choose one: • Draw 2 cards. • Up to 1 of your {Dressrosa} type Characters gains [Blocker] until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." Plain choose-one (no condition). Duration `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` (as OP16-009 / OP16-073). No printed [Blocker] (the audit's keyword list is the grant).
- **OP15-056 Would You Let Me Eat the Flame-Flame Fruit?**, "[Main] Draw 2 cards. Then, your [Lucy] Leader gains [Double Attack] and +3000 power during this turn. [Trigger] Draw 2 cards." "Your [Lucy] Leader" is a Leader with the exact name [Lucy] (no condition, no choice: with another Leader the grant does nothing, the draw still happens). "Then" is not an "If you do". [Trigger] is a plain draw 2.
- **OP15-057 Dressrosa Kingdom**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Dressrosa} type, draw 1 card. [On Your Opponent's Attack] You may rest this Stage and trash 1 Event or Stage card from your hand: Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +2000 power during this battle." Both abilities are autos; the second has the cost `restSelf` plus a hand trash of exactly 1 Event or Stage, so it is optional by construction. The cost involves a hand card so the prompt is asked even with no Event or Stage in hand (hidden hand) but cannot be accepted. A side effect of scripting this Stage: `OP16-043.test.ts` used it as inert scenery, so that test now uses OP04-096 (an unscripted {Dressrosa} Stage).
- **OP15-060 Enel**, "If you have 6 or less DON!! cards on your field, this Character cannot be removed from the field by your opponent's effects and gains +2000 power. [Activate: Main] DON!! −1: This Character gains [Blocker] until the end of your opponent's next End Phase. Then, trash 1 card from your hand." The permanent has two applications under one condition (DON!! on the field counts the cost area, rested and active, plus given). `cannotBeRemovedByOpponentEffects` stops opposing K.O. effects, not a battle K.O. The activation is not Once Per Turn. "Then, trash" is an independent step (done even with a Blocker already granted; skipped with an empty hand).
- **OP15-063 Gedatsu**, "[On Play] DON!! −1: Draw 1 card. [On K.O.] If you have 6 or less DON!! cards on your field, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 2000 power or less." DON!! -1 as the auto's cost (always optional, RL-52); the DON!! rested to play Gedatsu are on the field and may be the one returned. The K.O. clause checks the current power (2000 or less, DON!! on the opponent's own turn do not add) and the DON!! count when the effect resolves. "Up to 1": may be none. It fires on any K.O. (no turn restriction).
- **OP15-064 Kotori** and **OP15-072 Hotori**, "[Activate: Main] DON!! −2, You may rest this Character: If you have [Satori] and [Hotori] (Hotori: [Kotori] and [Satori]), rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 5000 power or less (Hotori: give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −3000 power during this turn)." Cost `donMinus 2` plus `restSelf`; the "you may" on an `[Activate: Main]` cost is not a `costOptional` (optional-cost checker: activation is already the player's choice). The "If" is after the colon, so the cost is paid even when the other two names are not on your field. "Have" is read as on your Characters (all three are Characters). Kotori's limit is current power 5000 or less. Hotori's "up to 1" Character is any opposing Character (no filter).
- **OP15-065 Goro**, "[On Play] Reveal 1 card from the top of your deck. If the revealed card has a cost of 2 or less, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and rest it." `reveal` with `bind`, then an `if` on the bound card's cost (<= 2); the card stays on top. "Up to 1" asks the count (`addMost`); a card with no cost does not qualify (Leaders cannot be on top of the deck anyway). The DON!! is added rested.
- **OP15-068 Heavenly Warriors**, "If you have 6 or less DON!! cards on your field, this Character gains [Blocker]." A conditional keyword grant (printed keywords empty). The Blocker is real: legal to block with 6, not with 7.
- **OP15-070 Fuza**, "All of your [Shura] cards and this Character gain [Unblockable]. [Opponent's Turn] All of your [Shura] cards' base power and this Character's base power become 6000." The same shape as OP15-071 Holly / Ohm: a selector over your Characters named [Shura] or Fuza himself. "Cards" is read as Characters on the field (a [Shura] in hand is not on the field). A given DON!! adds nothing on the opponent's turn.
- **OP15-073 Yama**, "[Blocker] [On Play] Play up to 1 [Heavenly Warriors] with a cost of 1 or up to 1 {Vassals} type Character card with a cost of 1 from your hand." Reading M6b-Q6 (open, no ruling): ONE card in total, from either description (a single selector, `upTo: 1`, over an `any` of [Heavenly Warriors] and a {Vassals} Character, with a cost of exactly 1; nothing in the text says "and"). With the other reading (one of each) a second card could be played, which the script does not allow.
- **OP15-079 Absalom**, "[On K.O.] Add up to 1 {Thriller Bark Pirates} type card from your trash to your hand. [Trigger] Activate this card's [On K.O.] effect." The K.O.'d Absalom is in the trash when the effect resolves and is itself a {Thriller Bark Pirates} card, so it may be chosen (tested). When activated by the [Trigger] it is not yet in the trash, so only other cards are candidates. Pattern: `activate: onKO of self` (G5), as OP16-102/103.
- **OP15-080 Oars**, "If you have [Gecko Moria] with 10000 power or more on your field and there are no other [Oars] cards, this Character gains +7000 power. [On K.O.] You may place 3 cards from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order: Play this Character card from your trash." Two readings: "no other [Oars] cards" is read on YOUR field (the clause continues "on your field"; an opposing [Oars] does not count) and as Characters only; [Gecko Moria] may be your Leader or a Character, and the 10000 is the current power (a 9000 Moria with a DON!! on your turn qualifies). Cost: the G1/wave 1 `move` from the trash to the deck bottom, exactly 3 (the owner is asked an order). The play is scripted as `from: trash` with `isSelf`, not `from: 'self'`, so that if Oars itself was one of the 3 cards placed it is not played (see ENGINE below). Oars comes back active.
- **OP15-081 Sanji**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Straw Hat Crew} type, trash 5 cards from the top of your deck." Wave 1 `mill`; a deck of 5 or fewer is emptied and the game is lost at once (RL-26; same as OP16-099).
- **OP15-082 Charlotte Lola**, "[On Play] Trash 3 cards from the top of your deck. [On K.O.] Add up to 1 of your Character cards with a cost of 8 or less from your trash to your hand." Lola herself (cost 4) is in the trash when the effect resolves and may be chosen.
- **OP15-083 Spoil**, "[On Play] Trash 3 cards from the top of your deck. [Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: If you have 15 or more cards in your trash, give up to 1 rested DON!! card to 1 of your Leader or Character cards." Cost `trashSelf`; the count is read after the cost, so Spoil herself counts as one of the 15. The "If" is after the colon: the cost is paid even with 14. One rested DON!! to exactly 1 target (the same shape as OP16-094).

## ENGINE

- **`play` of `from: 'self'` plays the card wherever it now is.** With OP15-080 scripted as `play { what: { from: 'self', owner: 'you' } }`, a trash of exactly Oars plus 2 other cards, and the player placing all 3 (Oars included) at the deck bottom as the cost, Oars ended up on the field (played from the deck bottom). The text says "from your trash", so it should not be played. Other `from: 'self'` plays in the repository (OP17-015, OP16-014, ST30-008) pay a hand cost, so the card cannot leave the trash. The script avoids it with `from: trash` and `isSelf: true`; the test "paying with Oars itself leaves nothing to play" pins the intended result. A fix would make `self` check the zone the text names (or the validator reject `self` for a trash play).
