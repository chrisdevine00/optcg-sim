# EB-03 scripting, group 1 of 2: readings and ambiguities

Cards: EB03-004, 005, 010, 011, 012, 013, 014, 015, 016, 017, 019, 020, 021, 022, 023, 024, 025, 026, 027, 028, 029, 032 (22 scripted, none left out; every card is `verified: true` in `cards/overrides/`). Gaps from `docs/research/m6b-audit/block4.md`, mapped through `m6b-runtime-audit.md` §2: B4-G2 = G2 (EB03-017, wave 1), B4-G17 = G1 (EB03-026, wave 1); both merged. No wave 4 gap, no design item D1 to D9, no `[DON!! xN] [On K.O.]`, no double-event replacement in this group. Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. No new rulings were invented; M6b open questions stay open.

No prompt is gated on a hidden-zone condition: the plays from hand and the deck looks are plain "up to 1" picks. The only gates read public facts (the opponent's hand size for EB03-026, the Leader, a Character count).

- **EB03-004 Carina**, "[Blocker] [Opponent's Turn] If your Leader is multicolored and you have no Characters with 6000 base power or more, this Character gains +4000 power." A permanent with `opponentsTurn`, a multicolored Leader and a `none` over your Characters with base power 6000 or more. The check reads BASE power (a boost never turns it off) and counts Carina herself (2000 base). Opposing Characters are not counted. The text says "gains +4000 power", scripted as a power modifier on self.
- **EB03-005 Sugar**, "[On Play] If your Leader is [Sugar], play up to 1 {Donquixote Pirates} type Character card with 6000 power or less from your hand rested." The "If" has no colon: an `if` step on the Leader name. The pick is an unguarded "up to 1" on the hand (asked at max 0 with nothing eligible); 6000 is the printed power in hand, boundary 6000 in, 7000 out; the card is played rested.
- **EB03-010 Monet**, "[Blocker] [On Play] Look at 5 cards ...; reveal up to 1 Character card with 1000 power or less or up to 1 Event card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." One take of up to 1 card (a Character with 1000 power or less, or an Event), not one of each: the text's two "up to 1" are alternatives of a single reveal. A 0-power Character qualifies; the rest are ordered by the owner.
- **EB03-011 But If We Ever See Each Other Again...**, "[Counter] If your Leader is [Nefeltari Vivi], up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +4000 power during this battle. [Trigger] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -2000 power during this turn." The "If" has no colon, so it is an `if` step: the Counter is played and does nothing for another Leader. The Leader name is checked as the Counter resolves.
- **EB03-012 Otama**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: Rest up to 1 of your opponent's DON!! cards or {Animal} or {SMILE} type Characters with a cost of 3 or less." One selector over the opponent's Characters and cost-area DON!!: the type and cost filter qualifies the Characters only (a DON!! card has neither), as the engine reads a mixed card/DON!! selector (M4b E-Q3). Only an active DON!! can be rested. The cost (resting Otama) is paid first; "up to 1" may rest nothing.
- **EB03-013 Carrot**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If this Character was played on this turn, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with a cost of 5 or less. Then, play up to 1 [Zou] from your hand." The "If" has no colon, so it is a step condition, not an activation condition: the ability can be activated on a Character that was not played this turn, the Once Per Turn is spent and nothing happens. The "Then, play" sits inside the guard (M4b-5). "Then" is not "If you do": [Zou] is played with no K.O. target. [Zou] is a Stage, so the play pick has no category filter (matched by name only). The play is an unguarded "up to 1" pick.
- **EB03-014 Kuina**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: Give up to 2 rested DON!! cards to your <Slash> attribute Leader." The <Slash> attribute is the Leader's printed attribute (Q1 in the audit noted the text was damaged; the verified text is used). With another Leader the cost is paid and nothing is given. "Up to 2" asks a count (2, 1, 0) with 2 or more rested DON!!; with 1 rested the Leader is offered as an "up to 1" target (the engine's DON!! give always lets the player pick none).
- **EB03-015 Camie**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to 1 of your {Fish-Man} or {Merfolk} type Leader or Character cards. Then, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 2 or less." Camie is {Merfolk}, so she can receive the DON!! herself. The give target is "up to 1" (the engine lets the player pick none). "Then" is not "If you do": the rest happens even when no DON!! was given (none rested, or a target declined).
- **EB03-016 Kouzuki Hiyori**, "[On Play] If your Leader is [Kouzuki Oden], draw 1 card. [Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your {Land of Wano} type Leader." The On Play "If" is an `if` step on the Leader name (a [Kouzuki Oden] Character does not count). The Activate cost is trashing herself (`trashSelf`); with a non-{Land of Wano} Leader or no rested DON!! the cost is still paid and nothing is given.
- **EB03-017 Jewelry Bonney**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Supernovas} type, set up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active. Then, up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 8 or less cannot be rested until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." A failed "If" stops the "Then" (M4b-5): both steps are inside the Leader-type guard. Within the guard "Then" is not "If you do": the restriction applies even when no DON!! was set. "Set up to 1" asks a count (1 or none); the DON!! rested to pay for Bonney can be the one set. `cannotBeRested` (G2) with `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` also stops the Character attacking and blocking (rules-digest 57). The cost filter reads the current cost.
- **EB03-019 Wanda**, "[Blocker]". Keywords only, no abilities (as OP16-044).
- **EB03-020 There You Are, Sore Loser!**, "[Counter] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +2000 power during this battle. Then, if you have 2 or more {FILM} type Characters, that card gains an additional +2000 power during this battle. [Trigger] Set up to 1 of your Characters as active." The first boost binds its target; the second reads "that card" through the binding, so choosing nothing gives no second boost either. The {FILM} count is of Characters (the Leader is not one) and is read after the first boost (which cannot change it). The Event is not in the field, so it never counts as a {FILM} Character.
- **EB03-021 Alvida**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Place up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 4000 base power or less and up to 1 Character with a base cost of 3 or less at the bottom of the owner's deck." Two sequential "up to 1" moves: first the opponent's Characters with base power 4000 or less, then any Character (either side, including one of yours) with base cost 3 or less. They are separate picks, so a Character that fits both is taken by one of them, never twice; power and cost are BASE values (a lowered current power does not make a 5000-base Character eligible). Each card goes to its owner's deck bottom; the two cards are placed one after the other, so no order is asked even if both belong to the same player (open: if the printed "and" means a simultaneous placement the owner would choose the order; the final deck order differs only in which of the two is lower). With no other card in hand only declining the optional cost is legal (as every optional cost the engine cannot pay).
- **EB03-022 Isuka**, "[Blocker] [On Play] Place up to 1 Character with a cost of 4 or less at the bottom of the owner's deck." Any Character on either side, current cost; Isuka (cost 6) is never a candidate. With no candidate nothing is asked (the board is public).
- **EB03-023 Kaya**, "[On Play] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck and place them at the top or bottom of the deck in any order." `lookAtTop` with no take and `topOrBottomAnyOrder` (as OP17-050 Streusen, QA-OP17): all the looked-at cards go to the top, or all to the bottom, in an order the owner chooses. Reading: "place them at the top or bottom" is one choice for the whole group, not a per-card split (the engine's shape for this wording).
- **EB03-024 Nefeltari Vivi**, "[Blocker] [On Play] Play up to 1 {Alabasta} or {Straw Hat Crew} type Character card with a cost of 5 or less from your hand. Then, you cannot play any Character cards on your field during this turn." The second sentence is read as "you cannot play any Character cards during this turn" (the same `cannotPlay` Character restriction as OP14-024 Kin'emon; "on your field" is read as the words for the cards you would play to the field). "Then" is not "If you do": it applies when nothing was played. The play comes first, so the restriction does not stop it.
- **EB03-025 Hina**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Return up to 1 Character with 6000 base power to the owner's hand." Exactly 6000 BASE power, any Character on either side, Hina herself included (6000); the card goes to its owner's hand.
- **EB03-026 Boa Hancock**, "[On Play] If your opponent has 5 or more cards in their hand, your opponent places 1 card from their hand at the bottom of their deck. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may place 1 of your Characters at the bottom of the owner's deck: Give your Leader and 1 Character up to 1 rested DON!! card each." The hand-size gate is public; the opponent chooses the card (`chooser: opponent`). The cost may place Hancock herself; then the "1 Character" is chosen among the others (none: only the Leader is given one). "Your Leader and 1 Character ... each" is select-then-give (as OP13-042): the Character is chosen exactly 1 after the cost, then one rested DON!! to each; with a single rested DON!! the player chooses which gets it (an "up to 1" prompt), with none nothing is given. Review: the engine always lets a fixed target receive none (OP13-R5, severity 1).
- **EB03-027 Marguerite**, "[On Play] Return up to 1 Character with 7000 base power to the owner's hand." As Hina with 7000.
- **EB03-028 Yu**, "[On Play] Trash 1 card from your hand. [Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: If you have 4 or less cards in your hand, draw 2 cards." The On Play trash is mandatory (exactly 1; an empty hand does nothing). The Activate's "If" follows the colon, so it is an `if` step checked after the cost (trashing Yu does not change the hand size); boundary 4 draws, 5 does not.
- **EB03-029 Insolent Fool!! Stand Down!!**, "[Main] You may rest 4 of your DON!! cards: If your Leader is [Boa Hancock], play up to 1 {Amazon Lily} or {Kuja Pirates} type Character card with a cost of 6 or less from your hand. [Counter] Up to 1 of your [Boa Hancock] cards gains +3000 power during this battle." The Main cost is an optional cost on an Event (`costOptional`, CR 8-3-1-4, B-1): declining it plays the Event with no effect. The Leader check after the colon is an `if` step. [Boa Hancock] cards for the Counter are the Leader or Characters named exactly [Boa Hancock]; with none, nothing is asked.
- **EB03-032 Charlotte Flampe**, "[Your Turn] [On Play] Up to 1 of your [Charlotte Katakuri] cards gains +2000 power during this turn." An auto with a `yourTurn` condition (a Flampe played on your turn always meets it); [Charlotte Katakuri] cards are the Leader or Characters with that exact name; the opponent's are never candidates.
