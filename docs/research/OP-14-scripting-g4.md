# OP-14 group 4: scripting readings

Cards OP14-087 to OP14-120 (21 scripted; OP14-094 and OP14-103 skipped as unverified, B4-V3 and B4-V7). No card needed a wave 4+ gap or a design item: the audit's only gaps for the group were B4-G39 (G18, wave 2), B4-G2 (G2, wave 1) and B4-G7 (G24, wave 3), all merged. One line per reading.

- OP14-087 / 099 / 097 ("reveal up to 1 ... other than [this card]"): the name exclusion uses the printed name. "type including Baroque Works" is `typeIncludes`, so "Former Baroque Works" counts (tested with ST30-013).
- OP14-087: the leading "If your Leader's type includes ..." gates the whole look (a step `if`), so with another Leader nothing is looked at or trashed.
- OP14-088: "If your Leader's ... draw 1 card and K.O. up to 1 ..." gates both halves. The Stage filter is `cost eq 1` (current cost), `ko` on `stage` (G18).
- OP14-089 / 113 ("draw 2 and trash 2" / "trash 1 from your hand"): the trash is `exactly`, a plain unconditional step after the draw or look ("and" / "then" is not "if you do"). With 2 cards in hand the player still confirms the pick.
- OP14-090: "can attack Characters on the turn in which it is played" is the printed Rush: Character wording, so a conditional permanent grant of `Rush: Character`. The condition ("there is a Character with a cost of 0 or with a cost of 8 or more") reads current cost on either field, the Leader excluded (a Leader has no cost). Cost 0 is only reachable through a cost reduction; the test uses a planted -1 cost.
- OP14-091: the card text prints the excluded name as "[Mr.2.Bon.Kurei.(Bentham)]" (extra dot); the script excludes the card's own printed name "Mr.2.Bon.Kurei(Bentham)" (ST30-013 shares it). "from your hand or trash" is one `from: ['hand','trash']` selection: one card total, one choice across both zones. The hand part of the pick shows hand contents to the opponent only through a prompt that is asked in any case.
- OP14-092: `replacement` of a K.O. of itself (any cause, battle included), conditioned on the opponent's turn, Once Per Turn, optional. "in any order" is the order the owner picks (DECISIONS 2026-11-16, bottom placement); an `order` decision follows the pick. With fewer than 3 trash cards the cost cannot be paid and no choice is offered. Once Per Turn is per card (a second Mr.3 has its own use).
- OP14-093: "[Blocker]" via `keywords`; the On K.O. add can pick the K.O.'d Babe itself (a cost 4 Baroque Works Character in the trash).
- OP14-098: the Main condition is checked once at resolution, over either field. "+3 cost until the end of your opponent's next End Phase" is `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` applied to the Baroque Works Characters present at resolution (not later arrivals). The Counter has no condition.
- OP14-100: the look is "reveal up to 1" with no category (a Thriller Bark Pirates Event or Stage would qualify); the rest go to the bottom in an order the owner picks.
- OP14-102 / 106 / 109 / 110 / 111 / 117 / 089 / 100 [Trigger] "Play up to 1 ... from your trash rested": `rested: true`, cost <= 4 inclusive, Characters only.
- OP14-106: [Trigger] "Play this card." has no condition and plays the card from the Life area (declining adds it to hand).
- OP14-110: "a cost of 4 or less and a [Trigger] other than [Dr. Hogback]": `hasTrigger` is the printed [Trigger] text; the K.O.'d Hogback itself is excluded by name. The play is active (not rested) from the trash. With nothing legal in the trash no prompt is shown (empty public zone).
- OP14-111 / 120 ("cannot attack until the end of your opponent's next End Phase"): `restrict cannotAttack` with `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` (as OP15-097), `upTo: 1`. OP14-120: the "Then, if your opponent has a Character with a cost of 0 or with a cost of 8 or more, draw 1" runs even if no target was chosen.
- OP14-116: "Then, play up to 1 ..." is independent of the power pick (declining the +2000 still plays the Character). Event cost 4 is paid by the normal Counter play.
- OP14-119: [Your Turn] "When this Character becomes rested" is the wave-3 `rested` timing with `isSelf`, any cause (an attack counts), only on your turn. "cannot be rested" is `restrict cannotBeRested` until the end of the opponent's next End Phase (M6b-Q30 is open for the cause scope). [On Your Opponent's Attack] "You may trash 1 card from your hand:" is an optional cost; the prompt is asked even with an empty hand (hand size is public) but cannot be accepted.
- OP14-120 [On K.O.]: "play this Character card from your trash" uses `from: 'self'`; safe here because the cost trashes a hand card, not the source (docs/BACKLOG.md §5). Replayed from the trash, its [On Play] fires again.
