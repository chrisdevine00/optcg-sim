# Wave 7 scripting, group 1 of 2: readings and ambiguities

Cards (10 scripted): EB02-009, EB02-053, EB02-057, EB03-006, EB04-023, OP09-101, OP10-022, OP10-116, OP11-101, OP11-116. Every card is `verified: true` in `cards/overrides/`. Engine gaps (docs/research/m6b-wave7.md): G65 move a given DON!! (EB02-009), G64 look at Life (EB02-053, OP10-116), G61 Character into Life (EB02-057, OP09-101, OP11-101, OP11-116), G62 Leader power cost (EB03-006, EB04-023), G67 total cost of a selector with waves 6's G53 reveal-and-play (OP10-022). All wave 7 or earlier and merged; no card needed a wave 5+ gap that is missing or a design item. One line per reading, with the card text. Nothing was settled: M6b-Q76 to M6b-Q85 are the readings used. The quoted `"X" type` of the data is the printed `{X}` type: an exact type element (`type: 'X'`), never a substring.

- **EB02-009 Thousand Sunny**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Stage: Give up to 1 of your currently given DON!! cards to 1 of your "Straw Hat Crew" type Characters." A plain `restSelf` cost with no `costOptional` (the optional-cost check forbids it on a non-Event [Activate: Main]: activating is the choice), so the Stage is rested whatever the effect then finds to move. G65 `moveDon`: the source is "up to 1" card of yours (Leader or Character) that has DON!! given, so choosing none is legal and the Stage stays rested; the destination is exactly 1 {Straw Hat Crew} Character and is never the source card (M6b-Q82). With one candidate for either choice nothing is asked. No DON!! changes state, and none is moved when no card has any given or no {Straw Hat Crew} Character can receive.
- **EB02-053 Myskina Olga**, "[On Play]/[On K.O.] Look at up to 1 card from the top of your or your opponent's Life cards and place it at the top or bottom of the Life cards." One ability with two timings. G64 `lookAtLife { owner: 'any' }`: you choose whose Life (only the areas that hold cards are offered, plus "none" for "up to 1"), then top or bottom (not asked with one Life card); the card keeps its face state and stays in the same Life area, only you see it (M6b-Q81). The ids offered depend on Life counts only, never on the cards.
- **EB02-057 Mad Treasure**, "[When Attacking] You may add 1 card from the top or bottom of your Life cards to your hand: Add up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 3 or less to the top or bottom of your opponent's Life cards face-up." Auto with `lifeToHand { from: 'topOrBottom', n: 1 }` as the optional cost (payable with at least 1 Life card; you choose the end). The move is G61: the Character goes to its owner's (the opponent's) Life at the end you choose, face-up (M6b-Q78); "up to 1" may pick none and the Life card is spent anyway. The Character is removed from the field by your effect, so "cannot be removed by your opponent's effects" stops it.
- **EB03-006 Nami**, "[On Play] You may give your active Leader −5000 power during this turn: Draw 1 card." G62 `power` cost on your Leader (`state: 'active'`, so a rested Leader cannot pay, M6b-Q79); the cost lowers power below 0 freely and effects add up. An auto with a cost is optional by construction.
- **EB03-006 (cont.)**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If your Leader has the {Alabasta} type, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −1000 power during this turn." Colon-less "If": an effect condition (B3A-R1), so the ability is activated, Once Per Turn is spent and nothing happens when the Leader is not {Alabasta}. `leader: { type: 'Alabasta' }` is the exact type element. The −1000 target is "up to 1", chosen on resolution.
- **EB04-023 Chaka & Pell**, "[Double Attack] [On Play] You may give your active Leader −5000 power during this turn: Draw 2 cards." `keywords: ['Double Attack']`; same cost as EB03-006 (G62, M6b-Q79). Declining the cost draws nothing; any active Leader qualifies (no type is asked).
- **OP09-101 Kuzan**, "[On Play] Place 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 3 or less at the top or bottom of your opponent's Life cards face-up: Your opponent trashes 1 card from their hand." The text has no "you may", and `cards/test/optional-cost.test.ts` refuses an auto with a cost unless the text says "you may" (an auto cost is optional by construction), so the placement is the first step of the effect, mandatory, not a cost. G61 `move` of exactly 1 Character (cost 3 or less, owner `opponent`) to the top or bottom of their Life, face-up, at the end you choose (M6b-Q78). The trash is guarded by a public check, that a Character of cost 3 or less exists, and after the move that the chosen Character has left the field (`none` over the bound card): a protected Character ("cannot be removed by your opponent's effects") is not placed and then nothing is trashed, which is what the cost reading gives (an unpayable cost); the engine still asks the end before the removal is tried. `ifDid` is not used because a `do` that chose a card counts as done even when the removal was stopped. The opponent chooses the card to trash (exactly 1); with an empty hand nothing is trashed. If review prefers the cost form, the checker needs an allowance for an auto cost without "you may".
- **OP10-022 Trafalgar Law**, "[DON!! x1] [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If the total cost of your Characters is 5 or more, you may return 1 of your Characters to the owner's hand: Reveal 1 card from the top of your Life cards. If that card is a "Supernovas" type Character card with a cost of 5 or less, you may play that card." The "If" before the colon is an activation condition (B3A-R1): the ability is offered only while `totalCost` of your Characters is at least 5 (G67, current costs, M6b-Q84) and 1 DON!! is given to the Leader; the condition is not read again after the cost (returning a Character may drop the total below 5). The cost has no `costOptional` (optional-cost check; activating is the choice), exactly 1 of your Characters, which may be one that counted. G53: the revealed Life card is shown to both players; "If that card" has no colon, so it is an effect condition on the revealed card, and the `may` play is gated on public facts only: a "Supernovas" Character of cost 5 or less (cost 6, another type or an Event is not played and the card stays on top of Life). A played Life card is not damage (no [Trigger]).
- **OP10-116 Damned Punk**, "[Main] Look at up to 1 card from the top of your or your opponent's Life cards and place it at the top or bottom of the Life cards. Then, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less." G64; the "Then" is a following step: the K.O. happens even when you looked at none ("up to 1"), since looking cannot fail and nothing is "if you do". "[Trigger] Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand." Two sequential steps; the trash is exactly 1 (after drawing, a hand always holds the drawn cards).
- **OP11-101 Capone"Gang"Bege**, "[Blocker] [Once Per Turn] If your "Supernovas" type Character other than [Capone"Gang"Bege] would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may add it to the top of your Life cards face-down instead." `replacement` with `replaces: { leaveField, cause: 'opponentEffect' }` (any K.O., trash, return to hand or deck by the opponent's effect); `type: 'Supernovas'` exact element, "other than [Capone"Gang"Bege]" is by name (`not: { name }`), so Bege himself and any other copy of that name are excluded (Bege never shields himself). The payload is the G61 move of the replaced card (`ref: 'replaced'`) to your Life top face-down (a face-down Life card you put there is known to you, M6b-Q78). Optional (`optional: true`); Once Per Turn is spent only when the replacement is used (a declined one does not spend it). Your own effects are not replaced.
- **OP11-116 Merman Combat Ultramarine**, "[Main] Add up to 1 Character with a cost of 6 or less to the top or bottom of the owner's Life cards face-up." G61, `owner: 'any'`: your own Character goes to your Life, an opponent's to theirs, at the end you choose, face-up; a cost of 7 is not a candidate; "up to 1" may pick none. "[Trigger] Add up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less to the top or bottom of the owner's Life cards face-up." Opponent's Characters only, cost 4 or less, to their Life.
