# Wave 6 scripting, group 1 of 2: readings and ambiguities

Cards (11 scripted): EB02-022, EB02-039, EB03-001, EB03-003, EB03-007, EB03-009, EB03-039, OP09-008, OP09-018, OP09-022, OP10-026. Every card is `verified: true` in `cards/overrides/`. Gaps (block3.md, mapped through `m6b-runtime-audit.md` §2): G49 printed effects (EB02-022, EB03-001, -003, -007, -009, -039), G54 `sameNameAs` (EB02-039), G52 place this card as a cost (OP09-008, OP10-026), G55 total power (OP09-018), G50 played rested (OP09-022). All are wave 6, merged; no card needed a wave 5+ gap or a design item. One line per reading, with the card text. No ruling was settled: M6b-Q70 to Q75 are the readings used.

- **EB02-022 Usopp**, "[On Play] If you have 2 or less Characters with 5000 power or more, play up to 1 Character card with 6000 power or less and no base effect from your hand." A colon-less "If", so an `if` step. The count is of current power (a boosted 4000 counts, a 4000 does not) and includes Usopp himself (5000) once he is on the field. The hand pick is "up to 1", asked at max 0 whatever the hand holds (no hidden gate); `hasBaseEffect: false` reads the printed text (a keyword-only card has none, a [Trigger] counts as one, M6b-Q70).
- **EB02-039 GERMA 66**, "[Main] You may trash 1 "GERMA 66" type Character card with 4000 power or less from your hand: If the number of DON!! cards on your field is equal to or less than the number on your opponent's field, play up to 1 Character card with 5000 to 7000 power and the same card name as the trashed card from your trash." The quoted type is the exact `type: 'GERMA 66'`. `costOptional` (an Event) with the cost bound as `trashed`; "If" after the colon is an effect condition, so the cost is paid even when the DON!! comparison fails and nothing is played. The DON!! counts are the whole field (cost area plus given). The trashed card itself is never a target (at most 4000 power). The trash is public, so when nothing qualifies there is no prompt (not a hidden-gate case).
- **EB03-001 Nefeltari Vivi**, "[Once Per Turn] If your Character with a base cost of 4 or more would be K.O.'d, you may trash 1 card from your hand instead." A `ko` replacement of any cause (battle and effect), `baseCost >= 4` (a cost boosted or lowered by an effect does not change it), Characters only. The optional prompt is shown with an empty hand but accept is then illegal (as OP12-048). A declined replacement does not spend Once Per Turn. The trash is any 1 card (`exactly: 1`).
- **EB03-001 (cont.)**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Leader: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn. Then, up to 1 of your Characters without a [When Attacking] effect gains [Rush] during this turn." "You may" on a non-Event activation has no `costOptional` (the activation is the choice). "Then" after an "up to 1" pick is not "if you do": the Rush step happens with no opposing Character or when the power pick chose nothing. "Without a [When Attacking] effect" is `not: { hasTiming: 'whenAttacking' }` on printed text (keyword-only, [On Play] and no-text Characters qualify; M6b-Q70 [Trigger] is irrelevant here). The Rush can go to a Character that has already attacked, or to a Character that was played earlier (it is only useful on a Character played this turn).
- **EB03-003 Uta**, "[On Play] If your Leader is [Uta], draw 2 cards. Then, play up to 1 Character card with 6000 power or less and no base effect from your hand." Colon-less "If" (effect condition); the "Then" is inside the guard (M4b-5), so with another Leader nothing is drawn or played. `leader: { name: 'Uta' }` is the Leader area only (a Character named Uta does not count). The play pick is made after the draw (a drawn card may be played) and is asked at max 0.
- **EB03-007 Baccarat**, "[Blocker] [On K.O.] Play up to 1 Character card with 6000 power or less and no base effect from your hand." Same filter as EB03-003; `keywords: ['Blocker']`; fires on any K.O. of Baccarat herself (battle or effect), asked at max 0.
- **EB03-009 Makino**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: Up to 1 of your Characters with no base effect gains +2000 power during this turn." `restSelf` cost, no `costOptional`. Makino has a base effect (this one), so she can never be the target. A [Trigger]-only Character has a base effect (M6b-Q70) and is not a target. With no eligible Character the pick is skipped (the board is public) and she is still rested.
- **EB03-039 Ulti**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Animal Kingdom Pirates} type, draw 1 card and trash 1 card from your hand. Then, play up to 1 Character card with 6000 power or less and no base effect from your trash." Colon-less "If"; the draw, the trash and the "Then" play are all inside the guard. `leader: { type: 'Animal Kingdom Pirates' }` (exact element). The trash is mandatory (`exactly: 1`, the hand holds the drawn card at least) and the card just trashed is in the trash when the play is chosen, so it may be the one played.
- **OP09-008 Building Snake**, "[Activate: Main] You may place this Character at the bottom of the owner's deck: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −3000 power during this turn." Cost `move` from `self` to the deck bottom (G52): a cost removal, so DON!! given to Snake return; "the owner's deck" is p1's deck (Snake is always p1's Character here). The power pick is "up to 1" and the cost is paid whether or not anything is chosen.
- **OP09-018 Get Out of Here!**, "[Main] K.O. up to 2 of your opponent's Characters with a total power of 4000 or less." `pick: { upTo: 2, totalPower: { lte: 4000 } }`, current power (M6b-Q71): exactly 4000 in total is allowed, a boosted or weakened Character counts for its current power. Choosing 1, 2 or none is legal.
- **OP09-022 Lim**, "Your Character cards are played rested. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may rest 3 of your DON!! cards: Add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and rest it, and play up to 1 "ODYSSEY" type Character card with a cost of 5 or less from your hand." `playRested` on Character cards only (a Stage is not rested; M6b-Q74), applying to every play from any zone, so the Character this ability plays enters rested. "Add up to 1 DON!!" asks a count (0 or 1) and the play is a second step of the same effect, not conditional on the DON!! (an unconditional "and"). The quoted type is the exact `type: 'ODYSSEY'`; cost `<= 5` is the current cost in hand. The cost rests 3 DON!! first, so the DON!! added by the effect cannot have paid for it. Several existing tests use OP09-022 as an inert {ODYSSEY} Leader; see the run notes in the report.
- **OP10-026 Kin'emon**, "[Activate: Main] You may place this Character and 1 [Kin'emon] with 0 power from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order: Play up to 1 [Kin'emon] with a cost of 6 from your hand." Two `move` costs in the order written (M6b-Q73): this Character first, then the [Kin'emon] of exactly 0 power from the trash, so the order is not asked and the deck bottom ends `[this card, the trashed card]`. `power: { eq: 0 }` is the printed power in the trash (the card itself is 1000 and does not qualify; OP10-027 is the only 0-power [Kin'emon]). With two candidates the player chooses which one. The play is `name: "Kin'emon"` and `cost: { eq: 6 }`, "up to 1", asked at max 0 (hand).
