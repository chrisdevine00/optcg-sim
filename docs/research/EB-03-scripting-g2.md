- **EB03-035 Charlotte Pudding**, "[On Play] If the number of DON!! cards on your field is equal to or less than the number on your opponent's field, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and rest it." `donOnField` counts the cost area plus given DON!!, rested included, read after the 4 DON!! for Pudding are rested (the count does not change). `addDon` upTo 1 asks a count (1 or 0), rested.
- **EB03-036 Baby 5**, "[On Play] DON!! -1: K.O. up to 2 of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 3 or less." Auto with a `donMinus: 1` cost (optional by construction); the targets read the BASE cost (a raised cost-3 Character is a target, a lowered cost-5 one is not).
- **EB03-037 Lim**, "[On Play] If you have 7 or more DON!! cards on your field, all of your {ODYSSEY} type Leader and Character cards gain +1000 power until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." Leading "If" is an `if` step; Lim herself ({ODYSSEY}) is boosted; only the cards on the field when it resolves; plain `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`.
- **EB03-038 Thanks for the Treat. ♡**, "[Main] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards: If the number of DON!! ... and you only have Characters with a type including "GERMA", add up to 2 DON!! cards from your DON!! deck and rest them. [Counter] Your Leader gains +3000 power during this battle." `costOptional` `restDon: 1` on the Event (asked after the Event is played). "You only have Characters with a type including GERMA" is `exists` a Character and `none` that is not `typeIncludes: 'GERMA'`, so with no Characters it is false (as OP13-095, the OP-13 g4 reading). The DON!! comparison is read after the rest cost (the count does not change). Review: with no Characters at all the strict reading could be "true" (vacuous).
- **EB03-041 Kujyaku**, "[Opponent's Turn] All of your {SWORD} type Characters with a cost of 6 or less gain +2000 power. [On Play] You may trash 1 {Navy} type card from your hand: Draw 2 cards." A permanent on the opponent's turn, current cost (Kujyaku herself, cost 4, is boosted); a `trash` cost of exactly 1 {Navy} card of any category. The hand cost is asked even when it cannot be paid (hand contents stay secret): only declining is legal.
- **EB03-042 Koala**, "If your Leader has the {Revolutionary Army} type, this Character gains +4 cost. [Opponent's Turn] [On K.O.] Play up to 1 {Revolutionary Army} type Character card with a cost of 6 or less other than [Koala] or up to 1 [Nico Robin] with a cost of 6 or less from your hand or trash." A permanent `cost` +4 on self conditioned on the Leader type; the [On K.O.] is an auto conditioned on the opponent's turn (a K.O. on your own turn plays nothing). "Up to 1 ... or up to 1 [Nico Robin]" is ONE pick over the union of both filters, from hand and trash together (at most one card is played, not one of each): the "or" separates the two alternatives of one play. Costs read are the printed costs in the hand or trash (Koala's own +4 is gone from the trash).
- **EB03-043 Stussy**, "[Blocker] [On Play] You may place 2 cards with a type including "CP" from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order: K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less." A `move` cost (exactly 2, `typeIncludes: 'CP'`, trash to deck bottom); the engine asks the owner the order (bottom-order.md). With exactly 2 {CP} cards the trash is public and nothing is picked (the order is still asked); with fewer than 2 the effect is skipped without a prompt. K.O. reads the current cost.
- **EB03-044 Black Maria**, "If your Leader is multicolored, this Character gains [Blocker]. [On Play] Look at 5 cards ...; reveal up to 1 [Onigashima Island] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order and play up to 1 [Onigashima Island] from your hand." A conditional [Blocker] grant (keywords stay empty); `lookAtTop` 5 with a named take, rest `bottomAnyOrder`, then an unguarded "up to 1" play pick from the hand (asked at max 0 when none is held: a hand condition is never a gate), so an [Onigashima Island] already in the hand is playable too.
- **EB03-045 Perona**, "[Blocker] [On Play] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters. Then, if you have 10 or more cards in your trash, play up to 1 {Thriller Bark Pirates} type Character card with a cost of 2 or less from your trash rested." `giveDon` n 1 rested to exactly 1 of Leader or Characters (herself included): once the effect runs the DON!! is given (the "up to 1" cannot be declined, as OP14-005 Killer; at least 4 DON!! are rested from paying for her). "Then, if" is checked whether or not a DON!! was given (as EB03-053 Nami); the trash count is public; the play is rested.

- **EB03-048 Rebecca**, "[Blocker] [On Play] Look at 5 cards ...; reveal up to 1 {Dressrosa} type Stage card and add it to your hand. - **EB03-049 I Knew You People Were Behind This.**, "[Main] You may rest 7 of your DON!! cards: If your Leader is [Perona], play up to 1 {Thriller Bark Pirates} type Character card with a cost of 6 or less and up to 1 {Thriller Bark Pirates} type Character card with a cost of 4 or less from your hand or trash. - **EB03-050 Conis**, "[On Play] Up to 1 of your {Sky Island} type Characters gains [Double Attack] during this turn." `grant` this turn; Conis herself ({Sky Island}) is a legal target; the opponent's Characters are not.
- **EB03-052 Shirahoshi**, "[Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: If your Leader is [Shirahoshi], add 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards. Then, all of your {Neptunian} type Characters gain +1000 power during this turn." `trashSelf` cost (on a Character the activation is the choice, so no `costOptional`); the "Then" is inside the Leader guard (M4b-5, RD 121-122): with another Leader she is trashed and nothing else happens. The Life card is face-down on top. The {Neptunian} boost covers the Characters on the field after she left (herself is gone and is {Merfolk} anyway).
- **EB03-056 Belo Betty**, "[On Play] You may turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-up: K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 3 or less." Auto with a `turnLifeFaceUp: 1` cost; the engine requires the top card to be face-down, so with the top card already face-up the cost cannot be paid (the text says "1 card from the top", so a face-up top card might be skipped on a looser reading; review). - **EB03-058 Lilith**, "[Your Turn] [On Play] If you have 2 or less Life cards, draw 1 card. [Trigger] If your Leader is [Vegapunk], play this card." The [Your Turn] is an ability condition and the Life check an `if` step (a public count): played by the [Trigger] on the opponent's turn she draws nothing. Trigger: `if` Leader named [Vegapunk] then play self; with another Leader the card is simply trashed.
- **EB03-059 S-Snake**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Egghead} type and you have 2 or more Life cards, add up to 1 Character card with a [Trigger] from your hand to the top of your Life cards face-up. [Trigger] Up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 6 or less other than [Monkey.D.Luffy] cannot attack during this turn." Gate on public facts only (Leader type, Life count); the hand pick is "up to 1" asked at max 0 when none qualifies. The card goes to the top of Life face-up (no [Trigger] fires as it is not damage). The Trigger is a `cannotAttack` this turn on one Character (cost 6 inclusive, [Monkey.D.Luffy] excluded by name); it does not stop the attack already declared.
- **EB03-060 Will You Be My Servant?**, "[Main] If your Leader is [Nami], look at 4 cards ...; reveal up to 1 card with a cost of 2 to 8 and add it to your hand. "A cost of 2 to 8" is `cost >= 2` and `<= 8` on any category (an Event of cost 3 qualifies). The Trigger `activate`s the [Main] of the card itself; with another Leader it does nothing.
- **EB03-061 Uta**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Set up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active. Then, rest up to 1 of your opponent's DON!! cards or Characters with a cost of 4 or less. [End of Your Turn] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards: Set up to 1 of your {FILM} type Characters as active." `setActive` over the cost area asks a count (1 or 0; nothing is asked with no rested DON!!); "with a cost of 4 or less" limits the Characters, any opposing active DON!! is a candidate (the engine applies the filter to cards only, as OP12-037). The [End of Your Turn] is an auto with a `restDon: 1` cost (optional by construction); Uta herself ({FILM}) may be set active.
- **EB03-062 Trafalgar Law**, "[Rush] [Activate: Main] You may trash 1 card from your hand and trash this Character: Add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards. Then, play up to 1 [Trafalgar Law] with a cost of 7 or less from your hand." Costs: a hand trash (exactly 1, asked even with one card) and `trashSelf`; "Then, play" is independent of whether a card was added (the Life move may be declined). A [Trafalgar Law] of cost 8 (another copy of this card) is not eligible; the trashed hand card may be a Law, which then cannot be played.

## Engine notes (no bug found)

- A hand-trash cost on an auto/Event prompts even when it cannot be paid (hand contents are secret), while a public-zone cost (trash cards, DON!!) on an auto is skipped silently when unpayable and an Event "You may" cost still prompts (EB03-041 versus EB03-043; EB03-049).
- A single-candidate "exactly N" pick from a public zone is made without asking (EB03-043: exactly 2 {CP} cards in the trash).
