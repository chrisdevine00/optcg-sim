# OP-10 scripting, group 4 of 4 (block 3): readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP10-018, 019, 020, 021, 039, 040, 041, 059, 060, 061, 078, 079, 080, 096, 097, 098, 112, 113, 115, 117, 119 (21 scripted); OP10-118 held back (see below). Every card is `verified: true` in `cards/data/sets/OP-10.json` with an override in `cards/overrides/`. Gaps from `docs/research/m6b-audit/block3.md`, mapped through `m6b-runtime-audit.md` §2: B3-G4 = G11 (OP10-118, wave 2), B3-G8 = G30 (OP10-112, wave 4). The other 20 cards needed no gap. No card needed a wave 5+ gap or a design item.

Method notes: no prompt is gated on a hidden-zone condition. The deck looks (OP10-039, 059, 078) are plain "up to N" picks that are asked at max 0 when nothing matches; the hand pick of OP10-119 is the same. Every count gate reads public facts (Life, hand size, DON!! on the field, trash size, Character counts). A [Main]/[Counter] Event is one `activate` ability with `when: ['main', 'counter']` whose id is `main` (OP15-021).

## Leaders and Characters

- **OP10-112 Eustass"Captain"Kid**, "[On Play] You may rest this Character: Trash up to 1 card from the top of your opponent's Life cards. [End of Your Turn] If your opponent has 2 or less Life cards, draw 1 card and trash 1 card from your hand." `restSelf` cost; the Life trash is a `move` from the opponent's `lifeTop` to the trash (G30): face up, not damage, no [Trigger]. "Up to 1" is asked as a second yes/no after the cost. The [End of Your Turn] "If" has no colon, so it is an effect `if` (B3A-R1): the ability fires either way, and with 3 or more opposing Life nothing happens. "Draw 1 and trash 1" are both inside the guard; the trash is mandatory.
- **OP10-113 Roronoa Zoro**, "If you have less Life cards than your opponent, this Character gains [Rush]. [Trigger] You may trash 1 card from your hand: If your Leader has the "Supernovas" type, play this card." Permanent `grant Rush` under `life you < life opponent` (strictly less: equal Life gives no Rush). The [Trigger] "If" is after the colon: the hand trash is paid first and the play does nothing for a non-{Supernovas} Leader (the trash is lost). Hand-trash cost is `exactly 1`, so an empty hand cannot pay it.
- **OP10-119 Trafalgar Law**, "[On Play] Reveal up to 1 "Supernovas" type Character card from your hand and add it to the top of your Life cards face-down. Then, give up to 1 rested DON!! card to 1 of your "Supernovas" type Leader." One `move` hand to Life top (face-down) with a {Supernovas} Character filter, then `giveDon` rested n 1 to the Leader if its type is {Supernovas}. "Then" is not "If you do": the DON!! is given even when no card was added. The reveal is NOT modelled: the engine moves a hand card to Life without a reveal event, so the opponent is not shown the card. To review (it changes what the opponent sees only). The DON!! target is asked as "up to 1" (declinable), as OP11-016.
- **OP10-118 Monkey.D.Luffy** (held, not registered). "Once per turn, this Character cannot be K.O.'d by your opponent's effects. [When Attacking] You may place 3 cards from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order: If your opponent has 5 or more cards in their hand, your opponent trashes 1 card from their hand." Scripted as the M6b-Q36 shape: a mandatory Once Per Turn `ko` replacement, `cause: 'opponentEffect'`, empty `instead`; the [When Attacking] cost is `move` trash (exactly 3) to deck bottom, the hand check is an effect `if` (after the colon), the opponent chooses the card. **Why it is not in the commit:** `cards/test/optional-cost.test.ts` fails with "OP10-118.protect: no text unit found for this ability", because `unitFor` in `cards/src/optional-cost.ts` finds a replacement's text only by `/would (?:be|take)/` and this wording is "cannot be K.O.'d". I may not edit `cards/src`. The script and its 5 tests (all green with the checker aside) are kept in `docs/research/a10g4-held/OP10-118.ts.txt` and `OP10-118.test.ts.txt`; to land it: make the replacement regex also accept `cannot be K\.O\.'d`, move the two files to `cards/effects/OP-10/OP10-118.ts` and `engine/test/cards/OP10-118.test.ts` (and import `./helpers-a10g4.ts`), run `npm run cards:effects-index` and `cards:measure`.

## Events and the Stage

- **OP10-018 Ten-Layer Igloo**, "[Counter] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +3000 power during this battle. Then, give up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Character cards −2000 power during this turn. [Trigger] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +1000 power during this turn." "Then" is sequential, not "If you do"; the +3000 lasts the battle, the −2000 the turn.
- **OP10-019 Divine Departure**, "[Main] You may rest 5 of your DON!! cards: K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 8000 power or less. [Counter] Up to 1 of your Leader gains +3000 power during this battle." `costOptional` with `restDon 5`; the 5 are taken after the Event's own cost of 1. The power filter is current power. The Counter target is "up to 1" of the Leader (asked, though only one candidate).
- **OP10-020 Gum-Gum UFO**, "[Main] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −4000 power during this turn. Then, if you have 2 or less Life cards, up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +1000 power during this turn. [Trigger] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 3000 power or less." The "if" is an effect condition read as the effect resolves, independent of whether the −4000 was given.
- **OP10-021 Punk Hazard** (Stage), "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Stage: If your Leader is [Caesar Clown], give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." `restSelf` cost (no `costOptional` on a Stage); the Leader check is after the colon, so the Stage is rested even when the Leader is another card. Leader check is by exact name `Caesar Clown`.
- **OP10-039 Gum-Gum Dragon Fire Pistol Twister Star**, "[Main] If your Leader has the "ODYSSEY" type, look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 2 "ODYSSEY" type Character cards and add them to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Trigger] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less." The Leader-type "If" has no colon: effect `if` around the whole look. "Then, place the rest" is part of the look (`rest: bottomAnyOrder`). Only {ODYSSEY} Characters are takeable.
- **OP10-040 The Weak Do Not Have the Right to Choose How They Die**, "[Main]/[Counter] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with a cost of 7 or less." One ability with two timings. As a [Counter] the attacker is rested, so it is a legal target.
- **OP10-041 Radio Knife**, "[Main] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 6 or less. Then, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with a cost of 5 or less. [Trigger] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less." "Then" is sequential: the K.O. may take any rested Character with cost 5 or less (including one just rested by the first step, or one already rested), not only the one just rested.
- **OP10-059 Fo...llow...Me...and...I...Will...Gui...de...You**, "[Main] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Dressrosa" type Character card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Trigger] Activate this card's [Main] effect." `activate self main` for the Trigger (OP14-097).
- **OP10-060 Barrier-Barrier Pistol**, "[Main] Place up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 6000 power or less at the bottom of the owner's deck. [Trigger] Activate this card's [Main] effect." Current power; a single card to the owner's deck bottom asks no order.
- **OP10-061 Special Long-Range Attack!! Bagworm**, "[Main] Draw 1 card. Then, return up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 2 or less to the owner's hand. [Trigger] Return up to 1 Character with a cost of 2 or less to the owner's hand." The Main only reaches the opponent's Characters; the [Trigger] text says "1 Character" without an owner, so it reaches either side's (`owner: 'any'`, as OP11-059).
- **OP10-078 I Do Not Forgive Those Who Laugh at My Family!!!**, "[Main]/[Counter] Look at 3 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Donquixote Pirates" type card other than [I Do Not Forgive Those Who Laugh at My Family!!!] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." Any category of {Donquixote Pirates} card, excluded by exact name.
- **OP10-079 God Thread**, "[Main] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost 5 or less. Then, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active. [Trigger] Add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active." The printed "a cost 5 or less" is read as "a cost of 5 or less" (a typo in the printing). "Then" is sequential: the DON!! is added even with nothing K.O.'d. The DON!! count is asked (up to 1: 0 or 1).
- **OP10-080 Little Black Bears**, "[Counter] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +4000 power during this battle. Then, if you have 7 or more DON!! cards on your field and 5 or less cards in your hand, draw 1 card. [Trigger] Add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active." `donOnField` counts the cost area (active and rested) plus given DON!!; the hand is counted after this Event has left it (so 5 others in hand is the boundary).
- **OP10-096 There's No Longer Any Need for the Seven Warlords of the Sea!!!**, "[Main] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's "The Seven Warlords of the Sea" type Characters with a cost of 8 or less. [Trigger] ... a cost of 4 or less." Exact type `The Seven Warlords of the Sea`. The cost-8 boundary is not exercised in the tests (no vanilla {Seven Warlords} Character with cost 8 or 9); cost 6 and the cost-4 Trigger boundary are.
- **OP10-097 Gum-Gum Rhino Schneider**, "[Main] Up to 1 of your "Dressrosa" type Characters gains +2000 power during this turn. Then, if you have 10 or more cards in your trash, that card gains [Banish] during this turn. [Trigger] Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand." This Event is already in the trash when it resolves, so it counts toward the 10. "That card" is the Character chosen for the +2000 (bound `buffed`); if none was chosen nothing is granted.
- **OP10-098 Liberation**, "[Main] If the number of your Characters is at least 2 less than the number of your opponent's Characters, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 6 or less and up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 4 or less. [Trigger] Negate the effect of up to 1 of each of your opponent's Leader and Character cards during this turn." Condition: opponent's Character count minus yours is at least 2, checked at resolution. The two K.O.s are two sequential "up to 1" picks, the base cost 6 one first; `pick groups` is not implemented, so a player who picks a cost-4 Character for the first K.O. cannot then also K.O. a cost-6 one (they could have chosen the cost-6 first). To review: with exactly one target of each cost the order of the two picks is a trap the real card does not have. Base cost is read (a cost raised by an effect does not matter; pinned). The Trigger is two negates: one Leader pick and one Character pick.
- **OP10-115 Let's Meet Again in the New World**, "[Counter] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +4000 power during this battle. Then, if you have 0 Life cards, draw 1 card. [Trigger] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost equal to or less than the number of your opponent's Life cards." The Trigger reads the opponent's current Life count when it resolves; with 0 Life only a cost-0 Character could qualify.
- **OP10-117 ROOM**, "[Counter] If you have 1 or less Life cards, up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +3000 power during this battle. Then, set up to 1 of your Characters with a cost of 5 or less as active. [Trigger] Draw 1 card." The leading "If" has no colon, so it guards the whole Counter; "Then" after a failed "If" does not resolve (M4b-5): with 2 or more Life neither part happens.
