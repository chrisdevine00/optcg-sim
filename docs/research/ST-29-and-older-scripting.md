# ST-29 and older block 4 scripting: readings and ambiguities

Cards (26 scripted, none left out): ST29-001, 002, 003, 004, 005, 007, 008, 009, 011, 012, 013, 014, 015, 016, 017; OP01-039; OP02-005, 068; OP03-044, 048, 072, 097; OP04-077, 096; ST02-007; ST06-008. Every card is `verified: true` in `cards/overrides/`. ST29-006, 010 are not in this group. Gaps from `docs/research/m6b-audit/block4.md`, mapped through `m6b-runtime-audit.md` §2: the only gap among these cards is B4-G15 = G11 (ST29-008, wave 2, merged); every other shape existed before the waves. No card needs a wave 4+ gap, a design item D1-D9, `[DON!! xN] [On K.O.]` or G14b. One line per reading, with the card text. No new rulings were invented; M6b open questions stay open.

- **ST29-001 Monkey.D.Luffy**, "[When Attacking] If you have 2 or less Life cards, draw 1 card and trash 1 card from your hand." Leading "If" on an auto is a condition (public Life count, no hidden gate), checked at the trigger and at resolution. Draw and trash are both mandatory; the trash may take the card just drawn.
- **ST29-002 Usopp**, "[On Play] / [When Attacking] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost equal to or less than the number of your opponent's Life cards." Two timings, one ability. The bound is the opponent's Life as the effect resolves (`cost: { lte: { life: 'opponent' } }`), Characters only. An already rested Character is a legal no-op target.
- **ST29-003 Kaku**, "If the number of your Life cards is equal to or less than the number of your opponent's Life cards, this Character gains +1000 power. [Trigger] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 3 or less." A permanent with a Life comparison (<=, so 0 and 0 counts), active on both turns. The Trigger K.O. is "up to 1".
- **ST29-004 Sanji**, "[On Play] Look at 4 ...; reveal up to 1 {Straw Hat Crew} type card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Trigger] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Play this card." Any {Straw Hat Crew} card of any category may be taken. The Trigger has a hand-trash cost (a cost, not a gate); with an empty hand it cannot be activated. The Sanji himself is not in the hand when the cost is paid (he is revealed from Life).
- **ST29-005 Jinbe**, **ST29-009 Nico Robin**, "[Trigger] If your Leader is [Monkey.D.Luffy], play this card." An `if` step with a Leader-name check (the Leader only; a [Monkey.D.Luffy] Character does not count). When the check fails, the activated Trigger card is trashed (rules: a Trigger card that is not played goes to the trash). ST29-009 prints [Blocker].
- **ST29-007 Tony Tony.Chopper**, "[On K.O.] You may add 1 card from the top or bottom of your Life cards to your hand: Add up to 1 card from your hand to the top of your Life cards. [Trigger] Up to 1 of your [Monkey.D.Luffy] cards gains +2000 power during this turn." The On K.O. is an optional-cost auto (`lifeToHand` top or bottom, a choice of end is asked). The card taken from Life is in the hand when "up to 1 card from your hand" is chosen, so it may be put straight back; the added card is face-down on top. With 0 Life the cost cannot be paid. "[Monkey.D.Luffy] cards" are the Leader and Characters of that name.
- **ST29-008 Nami**, "If your {Egghead} type Character would be K.O.'d by your opponent's effect, you may turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-up instead. [Trigger] If your Leader is [Monkey.D.Luffy], play this card." A `ko` replacement with cause `opponentEffect` (G11): a battle K.O., your own effect or a bounce is not replaced. Not Once Per Turn. The payload `turnLifeFaceUp: 1` is the engine's reading of "1 card from the top" (the top Life card, which this test pins as face-up afterwards). Nami covers herself ({Egghead}). Review: M6b open question on `turnLifeFaceUp` when the top card is already face-up is the engine's.
- **ST29-011 Brook**, **OP04-077 Ideo**: [Blocker] only; the script is the keyword line with no abilities.
- **ST29-012 Monkey.D.Luffy**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to 1 of your [Monkey.D.Luffy] cards. [Trigger] Play this card." `giveDon` n 1, rested; target exactly 1 of the Leader and Characters named [Monkey.D.Luffy] (this Character is one itself, so a target always exists). As OP14-005 the engine asks no count at n 1.
- **ST29-013 Rob Lucci**, "[Trigger] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost equal to or less than the total of your and your opponent's Life cards." `life: 'both'`, read as the Trigger resolves: your Life no longer includes the card that carried the Trigger (it has left Life).
- **ST29-014 Roronoa Zoro**, "[Rush: Character] [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card with a [Trigger] from your hand: Draw 1 card and give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader or 1 of your Characters." The cost filters the hand by `hasTrigger` (a cost, not a gate). Activate on a Character: no `costOptional`. "Draw 1 and give" are two mandatory steps: with no rested DON!! the card is still drawn and the cost is spent.
- **ST29-015 Raw Heat Strike**, "[Counter] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +2000 power during this battle. Then, if you have 1 or less Life cards, give up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Character cards -2000 power during this turn. [Trigger] Draw 1 card." "Then, if" is its own condition (M4b-5): the +2000 may be declined and the -2000 still applies. Life is checked as the second part resolves.
- **ST29-016 Kizaru!! ...**, "[Main] Your [Monkey.D.Luffy] Leader gains [Unblockable] during this turn. [Counter] Your Leader gains +3000 power during this battle." The Main grants the keyword to the Leader only when it is named [Monkey.D.Luffy] (the Event is played and does nothing otherwise). The Counter has no name restriction and asks no target (the Leader).
- **ST29-017 Iai Death Lion Song**, "[Counter] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +4000 power during this battle. Then, if you have 2 or less Life cards, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 3 or less. [Trigger] Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand." As ST29-015 for "Then, if"; the Trigger draw 2 and trash 1 are both mandatory.
- **OP01-039 Killer**, "[Blocker] [DON!! x1] [On Block] If you have 3 or more Characters, draw 1 card." `donGiven: 1` (1 or more) and a Character count (Killer himself is one), both conditions at the trigger and at resolution.
- **OP02-005 Curly.Dadan**, "[On Play] Look at up to 5 cards ...; reveal up to 1 red Character with a cost of 1 and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom ... in any order." Printed cost of the card in the deck; "red" includes a multicolor card with red. "Look at up to 5" is scripted as 5 (a shorter deck is looked at in full).
- **OP02-068 Gum-Gum Rain**, **OP03-072 Gum-Gum Jet Gatling**, **OP03-097 Six King Pistol**, "[Counter] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +3000 power during this battle." As OP04-016: an optional cost (`costOptional`), declining gives nothing, and with no other card in hand it cannot be paid. Triggers: OP02-068 returns up to 1 Character of cost 2 or less, from either side, to its owner's hand; OP03-072 adds up to 1 DON!! from the DON!! deck as active (a count 1 or 0 is asked, "add up to N asks a count"); OP03-097 "Draw 1 card. Then, K.O. up to 1 ..." has two mandatory steps ("Then" is not "If you do").
- **OP03-044 Kaya**, "[On Play] Draw 2 cards and trash 2 cards from your hand." Both mandatory; any 2 cards of the hand, the drawn ones included.
- **OP03-048 Nojiko**, "[On Play] If your Leader is [Nami], return up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less to the owner's hand." Leader named exactly [Nami]; a Nami Character does not count.
- **OP04-096 Corrida Coliseum**, "If your Leader has the [Dressrosa] type, your [Dressrosa] type Characters can attack Characters on the turn in which they are played." Scripted as a permanent grant of [Rush: Character] to your {Dressrosa} Characters, conditioned on the Leader type (exact element). Applies to Characters played before or after the Stage. A Stage with an existing test fixture (`helpers-w3s.ts` STAGE) still works as an inert hand card (it only matters whether it is a Stage).
- **ST02-007 Jewelry Bonney**, "[Activate: Main] (1) You may rest this Character: Look at 5 ...; reveal up to 1 {Supernovas} type card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest ...". Costs: rest 1 DON!! and rest self, both paid on activation (no `costOptional` on a Character). Any {Supernovas} card of any category.
- **ST06-008 Hina**, "[On Play] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters -4 cost during this turn." `cost` action, `thisTurn`, opponent's Characters only. The resulting cost is 0 for a cost-4 Character (not negative on the view).

## Engine notes (no bug found)

- An activated Trigger card that is not played (a failed "If your Leader is ...", or a Trigger with no play) is trashed, not added to the hand.
