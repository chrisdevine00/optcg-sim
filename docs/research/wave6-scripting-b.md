# Wave 6 scripting, group 2 of 2

Cards: OP10-027, OP11-102, OP12-058, OP12-075, OP12-080, OP12-109, OP12-113, OP13-106, OP13-119, PRB01-001, ST16-002 (11 scripted, none left out). Every gap is merged: G49 (PRB01-001), G51 (OP12-075, OP13-119), G52 (OP10-027, OP12-080), G53 (OP12-058), G56 (OP11-102, OP13-106), G57 (OP12-109, OP12-113), G58 (ST16-002), with wave 4's G30 (OP11-102). No card needs a wave 5+ gap or a design item D1 to D9. Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. Official text conventions: the printed `"X" type` is the exact type {X}, `type including "X"` is a substring match (CR 2-4-3-1).

- **OP10-027 Kin'emon**, "[Activate: Main] You may place this Character and 1 [Kin'emon] with 1000 power from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order: Play up to 1 [Kin'emon] with a cost of 6 from your hand." Two costs in the order written (G52, M6b-Q73): this Character first, then the trash card, so the trash card is the last card of the deck; "in any order" is not asked. "With a cost of 6" is exactly 6 (`eq`); "with 1000 power" is the trash card's printed power (this card has 0, so it never pays for itself). The hand play is "up to 1", asked at max 0 (the hand is hidden). The paired OP10-026 is scripted by another agent (M6b-Q73): the tests use it only as an inert 1000-power [Kin'emon] in a trash.
- **OP11-102 Camie**, "[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] This effect can be activated when your opponent activates an Event or [Trigger]. If your opponent has 2 or more Life cards, trash 1 card from the top of each of your and your opponent's Life cards." One `auto` with two timings (`eventActivated` and `triggerActivated`, both `by: 'opponent'`) sharing the Once Per Turn, `yourTurn` as its condition (M6b-Q72). The "If" has no colon before it: an effect condition, so the ability fires and the Once Per Turn is spent with the opponent at 1 Life. The Life count is read when the effect resolves, i.e. after a [Trigger] and its damage. Both Life cards go face up to the trash in one `move` with `owner: 'any'` (G30, yours first); with no Life of your own only the opponent's is trashed. A [Trigger] added to the hand is not an activation.
- **OP12-058 I Will Make Whitebeard the King of the Pirates**, "[Main] If your Leader's type includes "Whitebeard Pirates", reveal 1 card from the top of your deck. If that card is a Character card with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates" and a cost of 9 or less, you may play that card. If you do, that Character gains [Rush] during this turn. [Trigger] Draw 1 card." Both "If" clauses are effect conditions (no colon): the Event is paid and trashed whatever the Leader is. Both "includes" are substring matches (a {Whitebeard Pirates Allies} or {Former Whitebeard Pirates} card counts, tested). The revealed card is public, so the "you may" is the only prompt (G53); cost 9 is played and cost 10 is not; an unplayed card stays on top of the deck; [Rush] goes to the played card only.
- **OP12-075 Ms. All Sunday**, "[On Play] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 3 or less. Then, your opponent may add 1 DON!! card from their DON!! deck and set it as active. [Trigger] DON!! −1: Play this card." "Then" is not "If you do" (M4b-5): the opponent is asked even when nothing was K.O.'d (`chooseOne` with `chooser: 'opponent'`, G51, M6b-Q75). The [Trigger] is an activation cost (`donMinus` on the trigger): not offered with no DON!! on the field, declining adds the card to the hand.
- **OP12-080 Baratie**, "[Activate: Main] You may place this Stage at the bottom of the owner's deck: If your Leader is [Sanji], look at 3 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 Event and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Trigger] Play this card." The "If" comes after the cost colon, so it is an effect condition: with another Leader the Stage is placed and nothing else happens (DECISIONS 2026-11-24, B3A-R1). The Stage goes to the bottom first (G52), the rest below it. [Sanji] is the Leader's exact name (any of the three [Sanji] Leaders).
- **OP12-109 Pacifista**, "[Trigger] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 1 or less and add this card to your hand." "And" is a plain sequence: the card goes to the hand (G57, `move` of `self`) whether or not a Character was K.O.'d; the K.O. is "up to 1", asked at max 0 when it has no target.
- **OP12-113 Roronoa Zoro**, "[On K.O.] If your Leader has the "Supernovas" type, play up to 1 "Supernovas" type Character card with a cost of 4 or less from your hand rested. [Trigger] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 1 or less and add this card to your hand." The quoted "Supernovas" is the exact type for the Leader and the card (the printed `"X" type` form); the "If" is an effect condition; the hand play is "up to 1" asked at max 0 (hidden hand, the gate is the public Leader type); the card enters rested. The [Trigger] is the same sentence as OP12-109's in the verified data (the override matches the card data and the limitless cross-check): flagged for a re-check against the official list, scripted as the text reads.
- **OP13-106 Conney**, "[Opponent's Turn] When a [Trigger] activates, this Character gains [Blocker] during this turn. [Trigger] Play this card." `triggerActivated` with `by: 'any'` and `opponentsTurn` (G56, M6b-Q72). The [Blocker] arrives after the [Trigger] and the damage, so it does not help against the attack that dealt the damage, but a second attack the same turn can be blocked (tested). A Conney played by its own [Trigger] was not on the field at the activation and gets no [Blocker] (tested; the engine queues the watcher at the activation).
- **OP13-119 Portgas.D.Ace**, "If you have 3 or less Life cards, this Character gains [Rush]. [On Play] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader. Then, you may return up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less to the owner's hand. If you do, your opponent plays up to 1 Character card with a cost of 4 or less from their hand." [Rush] is a live permanent condition on your Life (3 is in, 4 is out, 0 is in). The give is the Leader pick with `upTo: 1` (asked and skippable, as OP13-005). "Then, you may return" is a plain sequence; "If you do" is the labelled `move` having moved a Character (so declining plays nothing and asks the opponent nothing). The opponent chooses and plays free from their own hand (G51, M6b-Q75), the returned Character is one of their candidates (tested), and "up to 1" lets them decline. Ace's own 6-cost payment rests 6 DON!! so a rested DON!! always exists for the give.
- **PRB01-001 Sanji**, "[Activate:Main] [Once Per Turn] Up to 1 of your Characters without an [On Play] effect and with a cost of 8 or less gains [Rush] during this turn." `not hasTiming onPlay` (G49, M6b-Q70) reads the printed text: a [Trigger] only card and a [Blocker] only card have no [On Play] and qualify, a printed [On Play] disqualifies even when unscripted; cost 8 is in, cost 9 is out. "Up to 1" may be declined and the Once Per Turn is still spent.
- **ST16-002 Gordon**, "[Blocker] [On Your Opponent's Attack] You may trash any number of "Music" type cards from your hand. Your Leader or 1 of your Characters gains +1000 power during this battle for every card trashed." No colon: an effect, not a cost (G58); the pick `any` binds the trashed cards and `refCount` scales the bonus. The gate is the public hand size (at least 1 card), not "the hand holds a Music card" (hidden-gate rule, as OP15-002); with a non-empty hand and no {Music} card the pick is asked at max 0. The target (Leader or Character, `exactly: 1`) is asked only when at least one card was trashed (public information). The quoted "Music" is the exact type.
