# OP-09 scripting, group 4 of 4 (block 3): readings and ambiguities

Cards (22 scripted): OP09-019, 020, 021, 039, 040, 041, 057, 058, 059, 060, 079, 080, 096, 097, 098, 099, 112, 114, 115, 116, 117, 118. Every card is `verified: true` in `cards/data/sets/OP-09.json` with an override in `cards/overrides/`. Gaps from `docs/research/m6b-audit/block3.md`, mapped through `m6b-runtime-audit.md` §2: B3-G1 = G4 `mill` (OP09-059, wave 1), B3-G17 = G29 `removed` (OP09-080, wave 3), B3-G55 = G35 `winGame` (OP09-118, wave 4). The other 19 cards needed no gap. No card needed a wave 5+ gap or a design item; nothing is left unscripted.

Method notes: no prompt is gated on a hidden-zone condition (the deck looks and the hand pick of OP09-116's [Trigger] are plain "up to" picks that the engine asks at max 0 when nothing matches, DECISIONS "Empty picks"). Every count gate reads public facts (Life, rested Characters, Character power and cost). Every "If" without a cost colon is an effect `if` (DECISIONS 2026-11-24, B3A-R1); an "If" after the cost colon (OP09-021, OP09-060) is an effect `if` too and the cost is paid either way. A "Then" after an "If" sits inside the guard (M4b-5); "Then" is never "If you do".

## Characters

- **OP09-112 Belo Betty**, "[On Play] If you have 2 or less Life cards, draw 1 card. [Trigger] If your Leader has the "Revolutionary Army" type and you and your opponent have a total of 5 or less Life cards, play this card." Both "If"s are effect conditions. The [Trigger] total is read after the damage that revealed the card (the card is out of the Life area), as OP10-100; when it fails the card follows the engine's normal [Trigger] outcome. "You have 2 or less Life cards" counts only your own Life (0 counts).
- **OP09-114 Lindbergh**, "[On Play] If you and your opponent have a total of 5 or less Life cards, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with 2000 power or less. [Trigger] If you and your opponent have a total of 5 or less Life cards, play this card." Total of both Life areas, `<= 5`; "2000 power or less" is current power (a Character lowered to 2000 qualifies, one raised above does not); "up to 1" is asked at max 1. The [Trigger] needs no particular Leader.
- **OP09-118 Gol.D.Roger**, "[Rush] When your opponent activates [Blocker], if either you or your opponent has 0 Life cards, you win the game." `Rush` plus an `auto` on `blockerActivated` by the opponent, with an effect `if` (either Life is 0) then `winGame`. Only the opponent's [Blocker] counts (not your own) and the text names no turn. The condition is read when the effect resolves, after the block was declared. Win against a pending defeat: M6b-Q50. Tested: a block with 0 Life on either side wins at once; 1 Life each does not; no block with the opponent at 0 Life is just the damage.

## Events

- **OP09-019 Nobody Hurts a Friend of Mine!!!!**, "[Main] If your Leader has the "Red-Haired Pirates" type, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −3000 power during this turn. Then, if your opponent has a Character with 5000 or more power, draw 1 card. [Trigger] Draw 1 card." The whole [Main] needs the Leader type. The second "if" reads current power after the −3000 (a 8000 Character lowered to 5000 still counts, a 7000 one at 4000 does not) and holds when no Character was chosen.
- **OP09-020 Come On!! We'll Fight You!!**, "[Main] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Red-Haired Pirates" type card other than [Come On!! We'll Fight You!!] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Trigger] Draw 1 card." Any category with the type, excluded by exact name; the rest go to the bottom with an order choice.
- **OP09-039 Gum-Gum Cuatro Jet Cross Shock Bazooka**, "[Counter] If your Leader has the "ODYSSEY" type and you have 2 or more rested Characters, up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +2000 power during this turn. [Trigger] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with a cost of 4 or less." The printed duration is "during this turn" (not battle), so the +2000 outlasts the battle. The 2 rested Characters are yours (Characters only, not the Leader or Stage).
- **OP09-040 Thunder Lance Flip Caliber Phoenix Shot**, "[Main] If you have 2 or more rested Characters, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less. [Trigger] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less." No Leader condition. The [Trigger] has no rested-Character condition either.
- **OP09-041 Soul Franky Swing Arm Boxing Solid**, "[Counter] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +2000 power during this battle. Then, if your Leader has the "ODYSSEY" type and you have 2 or more rested Characters, set up to 2 of your Characters as active. [Trigger] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 4 or less." The "Then, if" is independent of whether the power was given. "Set up to 2 of your Characters as active" offers every one of your Characters (not only rested ones, the text does not say "rested"; an active one is a pointless but legal pick), never the Leader.
- **OP09-057 Cross Guild**, "[Main] Look at 4 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Cross Guild" type card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Trigger] Activate this card's [Main] effect." No name exclusion: another copy of itself may be taken.
- **OP09-058 Special Muggy Ball**, "[Main] Your opponent chooses 1 of their Character with a cost of 6 or less and return to the owner's hand. [Trigger] Return up to 1 Character with a cost of 3 or less to the owner's hand." The opponent chooses (`chooser: opponent`), exactly 1 (no "up to": they cannot decline when one is eligible), current cost; only their own Characters. The [Trigger] names no owner: either side's Character (`owner: any`, as OP10-061/OP11-059).
- **OP09-059 Murder at the Steam Bath**, "[Counter] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +3000 power during this battle. Then, trash up to 2 cards from your hand. Trash the same number of cards from the top of your deck as you did from your hand. [Trigger] Draw 1 card." `trashFromHand` up to 2 bound as `trashed`, then `mill` `{ refCount }` (G4, wave 1). The same number is the number actually trashed (0 trashes none); a short deck trashes what it holds. The hand pick is asked even when nobody got the +3000.
- **OP09-079 Gum-Gum Jump Rope**, "[Main] DON!! −2 (You may return the specified number of DON!! cards from your field to your DON!! deck.): Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less. Then, draw 1 card. [Trigger] Add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active." `donMinus` 2 (optional, as OP15-074: the engine asks "use?" after the Event's own cost of 2). "Then, draw 1" is not "If you do": it happens when no Character was rested. The DON!! count is asked (up to 1: 1 or 0).
- **OP09-096 My Era...Begins!!**, "[Main] Look at 3 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Blackbeard Pirates" type card other than [My Era...Begins!!] and add it to your hand. Then, trash the rest. [Trigger] Activate this card's [Main] effect." `rest: 'trash'`: every looked-at card not taken is trashed face up, including when none was taken.
- **OP09-097 Black Vortex**, "[Counter] Negate the effect of up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Character cards and give that card −4000 power during this turn. [Trigger] Negate the effect of up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Character cards during this turn." The negate target is bound (`neg`) and the −4000 goes to that card; with none chosen nothing happens. The −4000 and the negation both last the whole turn.
- **OP09-098 Black Hole**, "[Main] If your Leader has the "Blackbeard Pirates" type, negate the effect of up to 1 of your opponent's Characters during this turn. Then, if that Character has a cost of 4 or less, K.O. it. [Trigger] Negate the effect of up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Character cards during this turn." Characters only for the [Main] (not Leaders); the K.O. filter is `ref` of the negated card plus `cost <= 4`, read after the negate (so a cost bonus the card gave itself is gone) and as current cost. The "Then" is inside the Leader "If".
- **OP09-115 Ice Block Partisan**, "[Main] K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 3 or less and a [Trigger]. [Trigger] Draw 1 card." `hasTrigger` on the printed [Trigger] text, current cost.
- **OP09-116 Never Underestimate the Power of Miracles!!**, "[Counter] Up to 1 of your Leader or Character cards gains +2000 power during this battle. [Trigger] Play up to 1 "Revolutionary Army" type Character card with a cost of 4 or less from your hand." Played active, free; no Leader condition. With a hand that holds no match the choice is still asked at max 0 (the hand is hidden).
- **OP09-117 Dereshi!**, "[Main] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 2 cards with a [Trigger] other than [Dereshi!] and add them to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Trigger] Draw 1 card." Any category with a [Trigger] (an Event counts), excluded by exact name; up to 2.

## Stages

- **OP09-021 Red Force**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Stage: If your Leader has the "Red-Haired Pirates" type, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −1000 power during this turn." `restSelf` cost (no `costOptional` on a Stage); the Leader check is after the colon, so with another Leader the Stage is rested and nothing happens.
- **OP09-060 Emptee Bluffs Island**, "[Activate: Main] You may place 2 cards from your hand at the bottom of your deck in any order and rest this Stage: If your Leader has the "Cross Guild" type, draw 2 cards." Cost: `move` exactly 2 from the hand to the deck bottom (the owner is given the order, DECISIONS, OP16-R3) and `restSelf`; the draw is inside the Leader "If", so a failing Leader pays the cost and draws nothing. The draw comes from the top, so the placed cards cannot be drawn back. Not offered with fewer than 2 cards in hand.
- **OP09-099 Fullalead**, "[Activate: Main] You may trash 1 card from your hand and rest this Stage: Look at 3 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Blackbeard Pirates" type card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order." Trash cost `exactly 1`; no Leader condition and no name exclusion. Not offered with an empty hand.
- **OP09-080 Thousand Sunny**, "[Opponent's Turn] You may rest this Stage: When your "Straw Hat Crew" type Character is removed from the field by your opponent's effect, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and rest it." `auto` on `removed` (owner you, a Straw Hat Crew Character, `cause: opponentEffect`) under `opponentsTurn`, with a `restSelf` cost (an optional offer, so declining leaves the Stage active); "removed" is any move off the field (a K.O., a bounce, a trash), not a battle K.O. The Stage already rested cannot pay and is not offered. The DON!! count is asked (up to 1).
