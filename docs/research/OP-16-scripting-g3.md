# OP-16 scripting, group 3 of 4: readings and ambiguities

Cards: OP16-058, 059, 060, 063, 066, 068, 069, 070, 071, 072, 073, 074, 075, 076, 079, 080, 081, 082, 083, 084, 085, 087, 088, 089, 090, 091 (26 cards; 22 scripted, 3 left unscripted, 1 keyword-only needing no script). Scripts in `cards/effects/OP-16/`, tests in `engine/test/cards/OP16-*.test.ts`. One line per reading; "no ruling" means the reading is the scripter's.

- **OP16-058 The Prisoners Are Rioting!!**, "[Main] If you have 10 DON!! cards on your field, all of your [Prisoner of Impel Down] cards' base power becomes 7000 during this turn." The count is the DON!! on the field after the Event's own cost is paid (rested DON!! count, as digest: the field includes the cost area); "all of your [Prisoner of Impel Down] cards" is read as the Characters on your field (the only zone where base power matters), a snapshot at resolution. "[Counter] Up to 1 of your [Buggy]" is read as your Leader or Characters named Buggy (no zone in the text).
- **OP16-059 We'll Change This Mission...**, "[Main] You may rest 7 of your DON!! cards: Look at 5 ...; play up to 2 {Impel Down} type Character cards with 6000 power or less." Uses wave 2 G17 (`take.to: 'play'`). The 6000 limit is read on the printed power of a card in the deck (inclusive). The 7 DON!! must still be active after the Event's own cost (the optional cost is asked after the Event is played, B-1). The played Characters are not rested; the rest go to the bottom in any order.
- **OP16-060 Sengoku (Leader)**, "[Activate: Main] You may return 8 of your active DON!! cards to your DON!! deck: Play up to 3 {Admiral} type Character cards with different card names from your hand." Uses wave 1 G9 (`returnDon` active only) and G15's `pick.distinctNames`. Not Once Per Turn (the text has no limit). The "You may" cost on an Activate is the ordinary activation choice. The three played Characters' [On Play] abilities all trigger (the engine orders them).
- **OP16-063 Kuzan**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] DON!! −1: Up to 1 of your opponent's Characters cannot activate [Blocker] during this turn." Uses G10 (`cannotActivateBlocker`, timed): the target is a snapshot at resolution (wave 1 reading 6), so a Character played afterwards is not affected. Once Per Turn is spent on activation even when no target is chosen.
- **OP16-066 Sengoku / OP16-070 Rosinante / OP16-075 Garp**, "If your Leader has the {Navy} type, add up to N DON!!...". The Leader type is an `if` in the effect: for OP16-070 the "rest 2 DON!!" before the colon is paid and wasted with another Leader type. For OP16-066 "Then, draw 2 cards and trash 2 cards" is read as inside the Leader-type `if` (same convention as OP17-103, audit Q10, no ruling).
- **OP16-068 Trafalgar Law**, "[When Attacking] If your Leader has the {Donquixote Pirates} type, this Character gains +3000 power during this turn." Plain effect-level `if`; the +3000 lasts the turn.
- **OP16-069 Doflamingo**, "[On Play]/[When Attacking] Add up to 1 DON!! card ... set it as active." One ability with two timings (`when: ['onPlay', 'whenAttacking']`), no Leader condition.
- **OP16-071 Benevolent King of the Waves**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Add up to 1 DON!! ... rest it. [On K.O.] Add up to 1 DON!! ... rest it." The trash is the cost (not payable with an empty hand); [On K.O.] has no cost.
- **OP16-073 Borsalino / OP16-075 Garp**, "add up to 1 DON!! card and set it as active, and add up to 1 additional DON!! card and rest it": one `addDon` action with two entries (active, then rested), each asking its own count (E-Q11). OP16-073's "[End of Your Turn] DON!! −2: Set this Character as active. Then, this Character gains [Blocker] until the end of your opponent's next End Phase": `DON!! −2` is the optional cost; "Then" is not "If you do", but both steps only run after the cost is paid. "Until the end of your opponent's next End Phase" is the engine's `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` (the Blocker is gone once the opponent's turn ends; tested).
- **OP16-072 Hannyabal / OP16-082 Kin'emon / OP16-091 Nami**, "reveal up to 1 ... and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom / trash the rest": "up to 1" may be empty; Kin'emon and Nami's look is inside the Leader-type `if` (the whole sentence is conditional); Nami "other than [Nami]" excludes any card named Nami. Only the cards looked at are trashed.
- **OP16-081 Otama**, "[Activate: Main] You may rest this Character: If you have a Character with a cost of 8 or more, give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn." The rest is the cost (paid even when the condition fails); the check is the current cost of any of your Characters, Otama included (she is cost 2, so she never satisfies it herself).
- **OP16-082 Kin'emon**, "This Character gains +3 cost." A permanent cost bonus, field only (RL-43 via the OP17-085 precedent): in hand the cost is the printed 4.
- **OP16-083 Kouzuki Oden**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 Character card with a cost of 8 or more from your hand: Draw 2 cards." The trash is the cost, judged on printed cost in hand; Blocker is a printed keyword.
- **OP16-084 Kouzuki Momonosuke (cost 5)**, "[Activate: Main] You may trash this Character with a cost of 20 or more: If you have 9 or more DON!! cards on your field, play up to 1 [Kouzuki Momonosuke] with a cost of 9 from your trash." "With a cost of 20 or more" is an activation condition on this Character (`conditions: [{ self: { cost: { gte: 20 } } }]`), so it is not offered at a lower cost (the only source is OP16-087's +20, hence the test chains it); the 9-DON!! check is after the cost, so with fewer the Character is trashed for nothing. The target is read on the printed cost 9 in the trash (OP16-085), by name.
- **OP16-085 Kouzuki Momonosuke (cost 9)**, "play up to 1 {Land of Wano} type Character card with a cost of 6 or less other than [Kouzuki Momonosuke] from your trash." Name exclusion is `not name`; a cost 6 [Kouzuki Momonosuke] (P-064) is excluded. Blocker is a printed keyword.
- **OP16-087 Shinobu**, "[On Play] You may trash this Character: If your Leader has the {Land of Wano} type, draw 1 card and up to 1 of your [Kouzuki Momonosuke] gains +20 cost during this turn." The trash is the cost (paid even with another Leader type); both effects are conditional; "up to 1 of your [Kouzuki Momonosuke]" is read as your Characters (a Leader has no printed cost to change).
- **OP16-089 Dracule Mihawk / OP16-090 Tony Tony.Chopper**, "Draw 2 cards and trash 2 cards from your hand. Then, ...": "Then" runs regardless (no "if you do"); the second step is "up to 1" so may be empty. Rush: Character is a printed keyword. K.O. "cost of 1 or less" is the current cost.

## Not scripted

- **OP16-074 Magellan**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Impel Down} type, your opponent returns 1 DON!! card from their field to their DON!! deck. [On K.O.] Your opponent returns 4 DON!! cards ...": needs **G27** (Action `returnDon` for the opponent; wave 3, audit B5-G14).
- **OP16-079 Yamato (Leader)**, "When a {Land of Wano} type Character card is played from your trash, that Character gains [Rush]": needs **G25** (`cardPlayed` origin qualifier, wave 3) and **D9** (acting on the event's subject card).
- **OP16-080 Marshall.D.Teach (Leader)**, "[On Your Opponent's Attack] ... Change the target of that attack": needs design item **D3** (redirecting an attack).

## Keyword only

- **OP16-088 Shimotsuki Ushimaru**, "[Blocker]" only: counted as scripted through its override's `keywords` (effect-dsl E-D7); no script file is needed.
