# OP-10 scripting, group 3 of 4: readings and ambiguities

Cards scripted (25, all `verified: true`): OP10-075, 076, 077, 081, 082, 083, 085, 086, 087, 088, 090, 091, 092, 093, 094, 095, 100, 102, 103, 106, 107, 108, 109, 110, 111. Left unscripted: none. Gap map (audit `block3.md` to unified ids): B3-G1 = G4 `mill` (OP10-081, 087, 088, 091, 095, wave 1); B3-G8 = G30 Life to the trash (OP10-109, wave 4). No card needed a wave 5+ gap or a design item.

- **OP10-075 Foxy**, "[Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: If the number of DON!! cards on your field is equal to or less than the number on your opponent's field, draw 1 card.": the cost (trashing itself) is paid first; the "If" follows the colon, so it is an effect condition. "DON!! cards on your field" is the cost area (active and rested) plus DON!! given, for both players (rules-digest); read when the effect resolves.
- **OP10-076 Baby 5**, "[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: If your Leader has the "Donquixote Pirates" type, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active.": the "If" follows the cost colon (an effect condition): without the Leader type the hand card is still trashed and nothing is added. "add up to 1 DON!!" asks a count (0 or 1).
- **OP10-077 Bellamy**, "[On Block] You may rest 2 of your DON!! cards: Add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active.": `restDon: 2` (active DON!! only); the count is asked.
- **OP10-081 Usopp / 095 Roronoa Zoro / 091 Brook**, "You may rest 1 of your "Dressrosa" type Leader or Stage cards: K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of N or less. Then, trash 2 cards from the top of your deck.": the cost rests an active Leader or Stage with the {Dressrosa} type (the engine takes the only candidate; with both it asks). "Then" is not "If you do": the 2 cards are trashed whether or not a Character was K.O.'d ("up to 1" may pick none). The K.O. filter reads the current cost.
- **OP10-082 Kuzan**, "This Character cannot be removed from the field by your opponent's effects.": `cannotBeRemovedByOpponentEffects` on itself (your own effects still K.O. it). "[Activate: Main] You may trash this Character: Draw 1 card. Then, play up to 1 "Blackbeard Pirates" type Character card with a cost of 5 or less other than [Kuzan] from your trash.": the play happens after the draw whatever the draw did ("Then" is not "If you do"); the trashed Kuzan is in the trash but excluded by name, as is any other [Kuzan].
- **OP10-083 Kouzuki Momonosuke / 087 Tony Tony.Chopper / 088 Nami**, "You may rest this Character and 1 of your "Dressrosa" type Leader or Stage cards:": two rest costs (`restSelf` and the Leader or Stage); both must be active to activate.
- **OP10-083 Momonosuke**, "Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2 cost during this turn.": `cost` -2, this turn; the view floors the cost at 0 (no ruling).
- **OP10-085 Jesus Burgess**, "[DON!! x1] If you have 8 or more cards in your trash, this Character gains [Rush].": a permanent grant with both conditions (at least 1 DON!! given; trash of 8 or more); it is live while the trash holds 8.
- **OP10-086 Shiryu**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If your Leader has the "Blackbeard Pirates" type, and this Character was played on this turn, K.O. up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 3 or less.": no colon before the "If", so it is an effect condition (DECISIONS 2026-11-24, B3A-R1): the Activate is always offered and spends the Once Per Turn, and does nothing on a later turn or without the Leader type. "Played on this turn" is entering the field this turn, by any play. "Base cost" is the printed cost (a raised cost still qualifies, a lowered one does not). "[Opponent's Turn] +2000" is a permanent power buff live on the opponent's turn only.
- **OP10-087 Tony Tony.Chopper**, "If your opponent has 5 or more cards in their hand, your opponent trashes 1 card from their hand. Then, trash 2 cards from the top of your deck.": gated on the hand size (public); the opponent chooses. The "Then" follows the "If" sentence, so it sits inside the guard (M4b-5): with 4 or fewer cards in their hand nothing is trashed from your deck either. The cost is paid regardless.
- **OP10-090 Franky**, "[On K.O.] Play up to 1 "Dressrosa" type Character card with a cost of 3 or less from your trash rested.": any K.O. (battle or effect); Franky is in the trash but costs 4 and is not matched.
- **OP10-092 Perona**, "You may place 2 "Thriller Bark Pirates" type cards from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order:": a `move` cost of exactly 2 cards of any category with the type, to the deck bottom; the owner orders them. Unpayable with fewer than 2. "Other than [Perona]" excludes every Character named [Perona], not only this one. The boost is "up to 1".
- **OP10-093 Saint Homing**, "You may trash this Character: Up to 1 of your black Characters gains +3 cost until the end of your opponent's next turn.": `cost` +3 with the plain `untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn` duration; the trashed Saint Homing cannot be the target (tested through two turns).
- **OP10-094 Ryuma**, "[DON!! x1] This Character gains [Double Attack].": permanent grant while at least 1 DON!! is given.
- **OP10-100 Inazuma**, "[DON!! x1] [When Attacking] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost equal to or less than the total of your and your opponent's Life cards.": `life: both` is the sum, read when the effect resolves (this attack's damage not yet dealt). "[Trigger] If your Leader has the "Revolutionary Army" type and you and your opponent have a total of 5 or less Life cards, play this card.": no colon, so an effect `if`; the Life count is read after the damaged Life card left the area (so 3 + 3 before the damage reads 5 afterwards and plays).
- **OP10-102 Emporio.Ivankov**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Up to 3 of your "Revolutionary Army" type Characters gain +1000 power during this turn. Then, add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand.": the Life card is added whether or not any Character was chosen ("Then" is not "If you do"; the text has no "up to" on the Life card, so it is not optional; with no Life nothing happens). Ivankov is a Revolutionary Army Character and may boost himself.
- **OP10-103 Capone"Gang"Bege / 107 Jewelry Bonney**, "You may add 1 card from the top or bottom of your Life cards to your hand: Add up to 1 "Supernovas" type Character card [with a cost of 5] from your hand to the top of your Life cards face-up.": the cost is `lifeToHand topOrBottom`; the card just added is in the hand when the effect resolves and may itself be put back. Bonney's "a cost of 5" is exactly 5.
- **OP10-106 Killer**, "[On K.O.] If your Leader has the "Supernovas" type, look at 3 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Supernovas" or "Kid Pirates" type card and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order.": the "If" is an effect condition (no colon); the card may be of any category; "the rest" go to the bottom in an order the owner chooses.
- **OP10-108 Scratchmen Apoo**, "If you have a yellow "Supernovas" type Character other than [Scratchmen Apoo], this Character gains [Blocker].": a permanent grant conditioned on your own Characters; any other card named [Scratchmen Apoo] does not count.
- **OP10-109 Basil Hawkins**, "[On K.O.] Trash up to 1 card from the top of your opponent's Life cards.": a `move` from the opponent's Life top to the trash, asked as an optional yes or no (the engine's "up to" on Life); not damage, so a [Trigger] on the trashed card does not activate (tested). The Trigger "Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand." draws first, then asks which card (any in the hand, the drawn ones included).
- **OP10-110 Heat & Wire**, "[On Play] Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost equal to or less than the number of your opponent's Life cards.": the opponent's Life only. "[Trigger] If you have 2 or less Life cards, play this card.": an effect `if`; read after the Life card was taken as damage.
- **OP10-111 Monkey.D.Luffy**, "[On Play] Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck; reveal up to 1 "Supernovas" type card other than [Monkey.D.Luffy] and add it to your hand. Then, place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order.": "other than [Monkey.D.Luffy]" is by name (any other copy is excluded); any category of card.
