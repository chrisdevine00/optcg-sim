# EB-05 scripting, group 1 of 3: readings and ambiguities

Cards: EB05-001, 002, 004 to 014, 016 to 020 (18 cards, all scripted). Method: runbook `new-set.md` step d. One line per reading, with the card text. No new rulings were invented; where the text is silent the nearest reading on file is used.

- **EB05-001 Jewelry Bonney**, "[On Play] If your Leader has the {Supernovas} type, draw 2 cards and play up to 2 Character cards with 2000 power or less from your hand." Read as one `if` step: both the draw and the play need the Leader type (one sentence, no "Then"). The 2000 power filter reads printed power in hand.
- **EB05-001**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Give up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Characters with 6000 power or more -1000 power until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." The 6000 filter is current power when the target is chosen; the -1000 lasts through the opponent's turn (`untilEndOfOpponentsNextTurn`).
- **EB05-002 Doll**, "... then place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. Then, trash 1 card from your hand." The trash is unconditional (m4b-runtime-audit §7: "Then" is not "If you do"), and it also covers the cards just added. With an empty hand nothing is trashed.
- **EB05-004 Baby 5**, "[On K.O.] Play up to 1 Character card with 6000 power or less from your hand rested." "Rested" is the `rested` flag of `play`: it enters the field rested. The reveal cost of [On Play] keeps the 2 Events in hand.
- **EB05-005 Belo Betty**, "Up to 3 of your Characters with both the {Revolutionary Army} type and a [Trigger]". `hasTrigger` is the printed [Trigger] line. Betty has none, so she cannot pick herself. "You may trash this Character:" on a non-Event [Activate: Main] is part of the activation cost (DECISIONS B-1), so the card is trashed even when the opponent has no Character.
- **EB05-006 Miss Buckingham Stussy**, "Give up to 1 Character +3000 / -4000 power". "Character" with no owner is any Character on the field, yours or the opponent's, never a Leader. The Life card goes to the hand with no [Trigger] (not damage). The mode is chosen after the cost; a mode may end with no target.
- **EB05-007 Monet**, "reveal a total of 3 Event or {Punk Hazard} type cards". Any mix of the two, 3 cards, all staying in hand. The [End of Your Turn] +5000 lasts to the end of the opponent's next End Phase, so the Character is 6000 during their turn.
- **EB05-008 Luffy, Now's Your Chance**, "[Main] You may give 1 active DON!! card to 1 of your Leader and reveal 2 red Events from your hand". The Event's own cost is paid first (it is played and trashed), so the DON!! must be one still active afterwards. "Red Event" is `color: Red` plus category Event; the played card is no longer in hand. Both cost parts are one optional cost (B-1), which can be declined.
- **EB05-008**, "[Counter] If you have no Characters with a cost of 5 or more, up to 1 of your Leader or up to 1 of your Characters gains +4000 power during this battle." The condition is an `if` in the effect, checked as it resolves: with a Character of cost 5 or more the Counter is played and does nothing. The audit's "1 of your Leader" is the Leader; the second "up to 1 of your Characters" is read as one target in total (the usual "Leader or Character" wording, Q13 of block5.md).
- **EB05-009 Fight and Die with Me!!!**, "All of your Characters with 4000 base power or less gain +1000 power until the end of your opponent's next End Phase." Characters on the field at resolution only; the filter is base power, so DON!!-boosted Characters still count.
- **EB05-010 Nico Robin (Leader)**, "[Once Per Turn] When one of your Characters with the \"Wisdom\" attribute and without [Blocker] is K.O.'d, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards." Q14 of block5.md: the `koed` event reads the K.O.'d card with printed values (OPEN-QUESTIONS M4b-2), so a Character that has [Blocker] only from an effect counts as "without [Blocker]", and one that had it only until K.O. is judged by its printed text. "Up to 1" is an optional prompt (accept or decline). Once Per Turn is spent when the ability triggers.
- **EB05-010**, "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Set up to 1 of your Characters with the \"Wisdom\" attribute and 6000 power or less as active." Current power is read when the target is chosen.
- **EB05-011 Otohime**, "[On Play] If your Leader is [Shirahoshi], you may turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-down: Rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less." The Leader condition comes before the "you may ... :" cost, so it is an ability condition (cost not offered when false). `turnLifeFaceDown` needs a face-up top Life card (wave 1 G8); with a face-down top card the cost cannot be paid and the effect does not run. This makes the card rare in practice (face-up Life cards are uncommon): a Q&A check on "turn face-down" with an already face-down card is worth doing.
- **EB05-013 Shirley**, "[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards: Your {Fish-Man} or {Merfolk} type Leader gains +2000 power during this battle." The type check is on the target, not a condition: with a Leader of neither type the cost can still be paid (and is wasted). Once Per Turn is spent on activation.
- **EB05-014 Shirahoshi**, "reveal up to a total of 2 [Megalo] or {Neptunian} type cards and add them to your hand. Then, trash the rest." One pick of up to 2 over name Megalo or type Neptunian; the remaining looked-at cards go to the trash (no order). [Activate: Main] "You may rest this Character:" is an activation cost (B-1); the Rush target is chosen after it is paid, and "6000 power or less" is current power.
- **EB05-016 Nico Robin**, "[Your Turn] [On Play] Play up to 1 Character card with a cost of 5 or less and the \"Wisdom\" attribute from your hand." `yourTurn` ability condition; it matters only if the card enters the field on the opponent's turn, which the printed [Trigger] does not do (the [Trigger] only rests; the card is then trashed, not played).
- **EB05-017 Mermaid Cafe Dancers**, "You may rest 1 of your DON!! cards and trash this Character: ...+4000 power during this battle." Both parts are paid as one cost; the Character is trashed even with a Leader of neither type.
- **EB05-018 Rebecca**, "Up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters with a cost of 6 or less will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase." `skipRefresh` on a rested target, which skips exactly the next Refresh (DECISIONS G26).
- **EB05-019 I Choose to Open This Country...**, "[Counter] You may rest 1 of your cards:" read as OP15-035 does: the Leader, a Character, the Stage or an active DON!! (only active cards can be rested). Can rest the Leader that then receives the +4000. [Trigger] "If your opponent has 3 or less Life cards" is an `if` checked as the Trigger resolves.
- **EB05-020 My name is Shirahoshi!!**, "[Main] You may rest your Leader [Shirahoshi]: Draw 2 cards." Optional cost on an Event (B-1): the cost is asked after the card is played and can be declined; with no active [Shirahoshi] Leader accepting is illegal and nothing is drawn.

## Not scripted

None. All 18 cards validate against the implemented shapes.

## Engine notes (no bug found)

- An "up to N" selector over a hidden zone (a hand or a looked-at set) is always asked, even with no candidate (`min 0, max 0`); over public zones with no candidate nothing is asked. Not a defect, but tests must answer it (`none`).
