# Enel (Yellow)

**Leader:** OP05-098 Enel. Yellow, 4 Life, 5000 power, Special, type Sky Island.
**Leader text** [CARD]: "[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When your number of Life cards becomes 0, add 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards. Then, trash 1 card from your hand."
**Standing** [ML]: 8 top-8s in the window (1 OP16-era, 4 pre-ban, 3 post-ban), 1.1% share over the window and 2.0% after the ban. opmetagame tier A [OPM]. Shonen TCG: tier A with three paper Standard Battle wins (Australia, Germany, Brazil) and 15-11 online in 2026-10-12 to 10-15 [SH-TIER]; tier 2 at 31-27 (53.4%) from 14 decks in Limitless Play events 2026-10-12 to 10-27 [SH-BEST]; 23-19 against other leaders in 2026-10-05 to 10-15 [SH-MUC].

**How to read this guide.** Turn numbers are *your own* turns: T1 is your first turn. DON!! available on each of your turns is 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10 going first and 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 going second (CR 6-4-1). Tags in square brackets resolve in `docs/content/sources.md`. *(inference)* marks our reasoning rather than a sourced claim.

**About the lists.** [SH-YEN] analyses two pre-ban first-place lists from 2026-09-30, tracked on OPMetagame; it was last reviewed 2026-10-09 and calls its A-tier rating pre-ban. Our three lists are post-ban Limitless Play top-8s [ML]. They share the core but differ in flex: ours play Charlotte Smoothie (OP17-106) in all three and Kingdom Come or Black Rope Dragon Twister in one, while the Shonen lists play Charlotte Mont-d'or and The Ark Maxim, which ours do not [SH-YEN][ML].

## What the deck is trying to do

Yellow Enel is a Life-gain deck that takes damage on purpose and buys it back [SH-YEN]. The leader refills one Life card the first time you hit 0 on each of the opponent's turns, at the cost of a hand card, while Borsalino, Sweet 3 Generals, Marshall.D.Teach and Charlotte Linlin add Life on your own turn [SH-YEN][CARD]. The OP17 Big Mom Pirates fill the middle: 4000-power Characters with Triggers that become 8000-power attackers on your turn once Charlotte Linlin (OP17-112) is on the field, and whose "[Trigger] Play this card" puts bodies on your board when the opponent hits your Life [SH-YEN][CARD]. Charlotte Pudding's draw 3 keeps the hand full enough to pay the leader [SH-YEN].

## Mulligan

- **Sourced baseline:** none of the opened sources gives a Yellow Enel mulligan rule.
- **Keepable hand** *(inference, built on [SH-YEN] and the S9 mulligan questions in strategy-concepts.md)*: Charlotte Pudding plus a spare Trigger card to trash for it, or Streusen to find Pudding, plus two counter cards (Daifuku, Zeus and Streusen carry +2000).
- **Mulligan** *(inference)*: hands with no play under 4 cost, or hands of 8–10 cost cards only.
- **Do not keep for Linlin** *(inference)*: she is a T6 card; the opening hand's job is draw and defence.

## What it is weak to

- **Running out of hand cards.** Each leader refill costs a hand card, "so the deck needs card draw to keep paying" [SH-YEN]. Hand-trash effects such as Rocks' Charlotte Linlin (OP17-049) and Smoothie-style discards hurt *(inference)* [CARD].
- **A sample too small to name a bad matchup.** [SH-MUC] lists no best or worst pairing with 8+ games, and every OP17 result in [SH-YEN] is pre-ban.
- **Banish** *(inference from card text)*: Nico Robin's leader trashes your Life card without its Trigger [CARD], which turns off the Big Mom "[Trigger] Play this card" bodies and the event's 0-Life refill.
- **Your own-turn Life loss does not trigger the leader.** Zeus's Life cost and Kingdom Come's Life trash happen on your turn, so the leader cannot refill them [SH-YEN][CARD].
