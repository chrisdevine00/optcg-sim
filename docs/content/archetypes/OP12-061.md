# Donquixote Rosinante (Purple/Yellow)

**Leader:** OP12-061 Donquixote Rosinante. Purple/Yellow, 4 Life, 5000 power, Special, types Navy/Donquixote Pirates [CARD].
**Leader text** [CARD]: "[Once Per Turn] If your [Trafalgar Law] would be K.O.'d, you may add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand instead. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] DON!! −1: The next time you play [Trafalgar Law] with a cost of 4 or more from your hand during this turn, the cost will be reduced by 2." (optcgapi drops the minus in "DON!! −1"; the Limitless page has it.)
**Standing** [ML]: 10 top-8s in the window (5 OP16-era, 2 pre-ban, 3 post-ban), 2.2% share over the window and 4.4% after the ban. opmetagame tier A [OPM]. Shonen TCG puts it in its tier 2, "quietly positive", at 40-36 (52.6%) from 17 decks in Limitless Play events 2026-10-12 to 10-27 [SH-BEST]; its paper-and-online tier list has it at B with one Standard Battle win in Spain and an online record one game under even (22-23) [SH-TIER].

**How to read this guide.** Turn numbers are *your own* turns: T1 is your first turn. DON!! available on each of your turns is 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10 going first and 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 going second (CR 6-4-1). Tags in square brackets resolve in `docs/content/sources.md`. *(inference)* marks our reasoning rather than a sourced claim.

**Format note.** EB05 (*ONE PIECE Heroines Edition vol.2*) releases in English on 2026-10-30 and OP18 on 2026-11-20 (meta-landscape.md). Every list, record and plan here predates both, so recheck this guide once EB05 cards appear in results.

**About the main strategy source.** [SH-ROS] was written for OP-16 (published 2026-06-18, updated 2026-09-16) and carries its own correction notice. It gives no decklist, and parts of it look dated or wrong against card text: it says Koby discards a card (OP16-064 does not; Tsuru OP16-067 does) [CARD], and it talks about balancing "energy access to both colors", but costs are paid by resting any active DON!! (CR 2-7-2, rules-digest item 74), so there is no coloured resource to balance *(inference from the rules)*. It also says Law has to be found "through the deck's other draw and search choices"; in our three post-ban lists that job falls to OP12-108 Donquixote Rosinante, which searches for [Trafalgar Law] by name [CARD][ML]. We cite it only for the leader plan and the piloting steps that agree with card text.

## What the deck is trying to do

Rosinante is a purple/yellow midrange deck built to put [Trafalgar Law] cards on the board early and keep them there [ML][SH-ROS]. The leader makes every Law costing 4 or more 2 cheaper once per turn for DON!! −1, and once per turn swaps a Law's K.O. for a Life card to hand, so the opponent has to remove a Law twice [SH-ROS][CARD]. The Laws in the lists are mostly Blockers (P-093, ST10-010 and the 6-cost EB04-038 Rosinante & Law, which counts as a [Trafalgar Law]), and two of them add DON!! when your DON!! count is at or below the opponent's, which can refund the DON!! −1 the discount cost *(inference from card text)* [CARD]. A Navy package (Koby, Marineford, Borsalino, Sakazuki) supplies search, Life refill and removal, and the deck closes with power-reduction removal such as Sakazuki's −6000 [SH-ROS][CARD].

| T2 | 3 / 4 | Leader DON!! −1, then a 4-cost Law (P-093, ST10-010 or P-088) for 2. P-093's On Play adds a rested DON!! if your DON!! count is at or below the opponent's, which the DON!! −1 helps you meet *(inference from card text)* [CARD]. |
| T3 | 5 / 6 | EB04-038 Rosinante & Law for 4 after the discount: Blocker, draws 1 and adds an active DON!! under the same DON!! condition [CARD]. A second Law or Borsalino (refills Life at 2 or less) fills the rest *(inference)*. |
| T4 | 7 / 8 | Sakazuki: DON!! −1 for −6000 on an opposing Character until the end of their next End Phase; its Activate: Main rests 1 DON!! to add 2 active DON!! because the leader is Navy [CARD][SH-ROS]. |
| T5+ | 9–10 / 10 | Keep Laws protected and spend removal on real threats only; [SH-ROS] calls answering minor early plays with removal "the most common mistake in this deck". Divine Departure (−8000 for 5 rested DON!!, if you have DON!! given) and Mamaragan (DON!! −2: draw 1, rest a 5000-or-less Character) are the late tools [CARD]. |

[SH-ROS] puts the Law discount window at "turns 5 and 6". With 4-cost Laws costing 2 after the discount, the post-ban lists can land a Law much earlier, so treat that timing as dated *(inference from card text)*.

## Mulligan

- **Sourced baseline:** none. [SH-ROS] refers to the general mulligan guide and frames the opening around colour access, which does not apply (see the note above).
- **Keepable hand** *(inference, built on card text and the S9 mulligan questions in strategy-concepts.md)*: a 1-cost searcher (Koby, OP12-108 or Marineford) plus a 4-cost Law or EB04-038, so the discount has a target by T2–T3; a counter card or a second Law is a bonus.
- **Mulligan** *(inference)*: no searcher and no Law; hands of only 5+ cost cards with no early play.
- **Going second** *(inference)*: the extra DON!! makes the T2 discounted Law easier, so a hand with a Law and no searcher is fine.

## What it is weak to

- **Luffy & Ace (ST30-001):** 3-8 in the first post-ban week [SH-MUC]; Luffy & Ace beat both Rosinante and Purple Enel 8-3 [SH-MUC].
- **Fast aggression early.** [SH-ROS] names decks that present "four or five meaningful threats in the first three turns" and decks that answer Law before the leader's protection matters.
- **Removal that is not a K.O.** The leader replaces only a K.O., so bottom-of-deck, bounce and rest effects get around it (Rocks' Gloriosa and Rocks Pirates event, for example) *(inference from card text)* [CARD].
- **Life pressure.** Each protection costs a Life card from a 4-Life leader, so a board that forces repeated K.O.s also drains Life *(inference)*.

## Playbook seeds

Measurable game-plan assertions for `ai/playbooks/OP12-061.json`. Turn numbers are the AI's own turns.

1. **Discount before every eligible Law.** When the AI plays a [Trafalgar Law]-named card with cost 4 or more from hand and the leader's discount is unused that turn, it activates the discount first in at least 90% of such plays. Source: [SH-ROS] step 3, leader text [CARD].
2. **A Law on board early.** Has at least one [Trafalgar Law]-named Character on the field by the end of T3 in at least 60% of games. *(inference from card text; threshold to tune)*
3. **Protect Laws while Life allows.** Uses the leader's replacement when a Law would be K.O.'d and Life is 2 or more in at least 90% of eligible K.O.s. *(inference)*
4. **Removal on real threats.** At most 20% of Sakazuki, Gum-Gum Lightning and Divine Departure targets are Characters with cost 2 or less. Source: [SH-ROS] "Hold Purple removal for real threats".
5. **Meets the DON!! condition.** P-093 and EB04-038 are played with their "DON!! count at or below the opponent's" condition met in at least 70% of plays. *(inference)*
