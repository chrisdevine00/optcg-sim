# Monkey.D.Luffy (Black, OP17-079)

**Leader:** OP17-079 Monkey.D.Luffy. Black, 5 Life, 5000 power, Strike, types Elbaph/The Four Emperors/Straw Hat Crew.
**Leader text** [CARD]: "All of your Characters with a cost of 12 or more gain [Blocker]."
**Standing** [ML]: 14 top-8s in the window (0 OP16 era, 9 pre-ban, 5 post-ban); 6.8% share over the window and 10.2% after the ban, the third-largest post-ban share. opmetagame tier S; it won the Utrecht Regionals per opmetagame [ML][OPM]. Shonen TCG: third most-played leader after the ban, 75-69 (52.1%) over 36 pilots from 10-12 to 10-27, "steady third choice" [SH-BEST]; B tier at 31-30 in its first post-ban week [SH-TIER].

**How to read this guide.** Turn numbers are *your own* turns: T1 is your first turn. DON!! available on each of your turns is 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10 going first and 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 going second (CR 6-4-1). Tags in square brackets resolve in `docs/content/sources.md`. *(inference)* marks our reasoning rather than a sourced claim. Not the OP16-022 Green/Blue Luffy [SH-BLU].

## What the deck is trying to do

Black Luffy is a board deck of cheap Blockers that also attack. No Character in the lists is printed at cost 12; OP17's Giants add to their own cost instead: Rodo and Gerd gain +12 cost under an Elbaph leader, and Loki and Jaguar.D.Saul gain +12 with no condition, so a 1-cost Rodo is a cost-13 Blocker the moment it lands [SH-BLU][CARD]. Once a cost-12+ Character is out, the Elbaph Straw Hats switch on: Usopp, Sanji, Zoro, Robin and Franky get +3000, and the 8-cost Luffy OP17-093 gains Rush and replays a cheap Giant from the trash [SH-BLU][CARD]. Nami and Gerd keep the hand and trash stocked so Giants keep coming back [SH-BLU]. Those Giants also dodge cost-based removal such as Gloriosa and the Rocks Pirates bounce [SH-XEB].

## Mulligan

- **Sourced baseline:** the first step is landing a cheap Giant early [SH-BLU].
- **Keepable hand** *(inference)*: Rodo or Gerd, plus at least one of Usopp, Sanji, Zoro or Nami OP17-086. Rodo, Gerd and Zoro each carry a +2000 Counter, so an early-Giant hand is usually also a defensive hand [CARD].
- **Mulligan** *(inference)*: no Rodo or Gerd and no way to dig for one (Usopp, Saul, Nami). A hand of 2-cost Straw Hats with no Giant leaves them as 2000-power bodies.

## What it is weak to

- **Luffy & Ace (ST30-001):** 16-37 over 2026-08-20 to 10-14 (SH-LA's 37-16, reversed) and 2-9 in post-ban events from 10-12 to 10-23 [SH-BLU]. Shonen TCG would leave the deck at home if your store is full of Luffy & Ace [SH-BLU].
- **Sabo (OP13-004):** 26-37 over 2026-08-20 to 10-18 (SH-SAB's 37-26, reversed).
- **Removal on its Giants.** The Straw Hats' +3000 needs a cost-12+ Character on the field, so removing the Giants turns the bonuses off; keep more than one Giant out [SH-BLU].
- **Blocking is not free.** A Blocker becomes the new target and can still be K.O.'d in battle, so pick blocks where losing the Giant is worth it [SH-BLU].
- *(inference)* Power-based removal (−X power, K.O. by power) ignores the +12 cost that protects Giants from cost-based removal.
