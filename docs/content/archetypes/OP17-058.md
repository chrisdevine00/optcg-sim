# Kaido (Purple)

**Leader:** OP17-058 Kaido. Purple, 5 Life, 5000 power, Special, types The Four Emperors / Animal Kingdom Pirates.
**Leader text** [CARD]: "[When Attacking]/[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] DON!! −1: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn."
**Standing** [ML]: 15 top-8s in the window (0 / 11 / 4 by era), 6.1% share over the window and 7.9% after the ban; opmetagame tier A, 5.9% top-cut share [ML][OPM]. Pre-ban tracker: 101 decklists, 50.2% [SH-KAI]. Post-ban it has a losing record online: 47-53 (47.0%) across 13 events from 2026-10-12 to 10-27 [SH-BEST] and 37-49 (43%) in the 10-05 to 10-15 chart [SH-MUC].

**How to read this guide.** Turn numbers are *your own* turns: T1 is your first turn. DON!! available on each of your turns is 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10 going first and 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 going second (CR 6-4-1), before this deck's ramp. Tags in square brackets resolve in `docs/content/sources.md`. *(inference)* marks our reasoning rather than a sourced claim. **Confidence is low:** the only deck-specific source [SH-KAI] discusses the leader and two support cards and does not describe the lists in [ML]; most of the game plan below is read from card text.

## What the deck is trying to do

Kaido is a purple Animal Kingdom Pirates deck whose leader is "a cheap combat trick usable in both directions": once per turn, for DON!! −1, it gives an opposing Character −2000, either when Kaido attacks or when the opponent attacks [SH-KAI][CARD]. Shonen TCG frames it as "a removal-and-defense build" rather than ramp [SH-KAI]. *(inference from [CARD] and [ML])* The tournament lists pair that with a DON!! engine: King, Queen (EB04-032), Basil Hawkins and Yamato all add DON!! from the DON!! deck, and the top end spends it back with DON!! −X costs (Lead Performers, Queen OP17-065, Kaido OP17-063, Charlotte Linlin ST34-004, Mamaragan, Gum-Gum Lightning). Kaido OP17-062 refunds part of that: on your turn, when a DON!! is returned, it adds an active DON!! and sets one active [CARD]. The deck aims to land 9–10-cost bodies that stop attackers, K.O. or zero out threats, and add Life, while the leader taxes one opposing Character every turn *(inference)*.

## Mulligan

- **Sourced advice:** "Mulligan for early blockers and Purple counter density over a single Animal Kingdom Pirates piece. An opening hand needs to survive first." [SH-KAI]
- **Keepable hand** *(inference)*: a T1–T2 play that ramps or draws (Queen EB04-032, Hawkins, Yamato, OP07-077) plus two or more counter cards (Hawkins and the 2000-counter flex Characters, Mamaragan). A 10-cost in hand is fine if the hand also ramps.
- **Mulligan** *(inference)*: hands of only 9–10 costs, or hands with no ramp piece and no counter.

## What it is weak to

- **Black Luffy (OP17-079)**: 2-11 in the post-ban chart, Kaido's worst pairing [SH-MUC]. *(inference)* Their Giants are cost 13+ and big, so −2000 rarely changes a battle, and cost-based K.O.s (Kaido OP17-063: cost 6 or less) cannot reach them.
- **Luffy & Ace (ST30-001)**: 12-22 [SH-LA]. *(inference)* Rush Luffys attack before Kaido's 9–10 drops land.
- **Purple Enel (OP15-058)**: 12-17 [SH-PEN].
- **A losing overall record after the ban** [SH-BEST][SH-MUC]; Shonen TCG: "We'd skip it unless you know the room is Rocks and nothing else" [SH-MUC].
- *(inference)* Every DON!! −X spends tempo: a turn spent ramping and paying DON!! −X leaves fewer DON!! for counter events and plays.

## Playbook seeds

Measurable game-plan assertions for `ai/playbooks/OP17-058.json`. Turn numbers are the AI's own turns.

1. **Uses the leader's DON!! −1 on the opponent's attacker when it is legal and changes the battle result** (attacker power minus 2000 falls below the defender's), in at least 80% of such attacks. Source: ARCHITECTURE.md §4.6 example, [SH-KAI] "use the trigger defensively".
