# Nico Robin (Purple/Yellow)

**Leader:** OP09-062 Nico Robin. Purple/Yellow, 4 Life, 5000 power, Strike, type Straw Hat Crew.
**Leader text** [CARD]: "[Banish] (When this card deals damage, the target card is trashed without activating its Trigger.) [When Attacking] You may trash 1 card with a [Trigger] from your hand: Add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and rest it."
**Standing** [ML]: 23 top-8s in the window, almost all before the ban (0 / 21 / 2 by era); 7.8% share over the window but 2.6% after the ban. opmetagame tier S [OPM]. In person before the ban it was 2nd at the EU Finals, won three NA Coliseum blocks, and was "the 2nd most popular Leader" at Dallas [ML][TCGP-DAL]. Post-ban online it has thinned out: 8 decks and a 15-19 record across 13 events from 2026-10-12 to 10-27 [SH-BEST]. See "Disagreements" in meta-landscape.md.

**How to read this guide.** Turn numbers are *your own* turns: T1 is your first turn. DON!! available on each of your turns is 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10 going first and 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 going second (CR 6-4-1), before this deck's ramp. Tags in square brackets resolve in `docs/content/sources.md`. *(inference)* marks our reasoning rather than a sourced claim.

## What the deck is trying to do

Robin is a purple/yellow ramp deck running the OP17 Big Mom Pirates package. Each leader attack pitches a [Trigger] card from hand to add a rested DON!!, which is next turn's DON!!, not this turn's [SH-ROB]; Yamato adds another [CARD]. The deck races to 10 DON!! for Charlotte Linlin (OP17-112), which on your turn turns every Trigger Character with 4000 base power into an 8000 base-power attacker, and whose On Play either adds a Life card or bounces the top of the opponent's Life to hand [CARD][SH-ROB]. Along the way Charlotte Pudding draws three, and roughly 29 of the 50 cards carry a [Trigger], so taking hits also develops the board [SH-ROB]. The leader's Banish denies the opponent's own Triggers on every leader hit [CARD][SH-ROB].

## Game plan by turn

| Your turn | DON!! (1st / 2nd, before ramp) | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | 1 / 2 | A 1-cost searcher: Streusen (OP17-113) or Charlotte Brulee, each looking at 3 for a Big Mom Pirates card [SH-ROB][CARD]. |
| T2 | 3 / 4 | Yamato (Blocker, adds a rested DON!!) the first turn it is affordable [SH-ROB]. Attack with the leader and pitch a Trigger card to ramp: "stack it with the leader and you ramp twice in one turn" [SH-ROB]. |
| T3 | 5+ / 6+ | Charlotte Pudding, trashing a Trigger card to draw 3: "fire her on turn 3 over almost anything else" [SH-ROB]. Leader attack and ramp again. |
| T4–T5 | 10 reachable | Charlotte Linlin. Shonen TCG's simulation (100,000 games, no extra draw counted) has Linlin castable by T4 in 22% of games going first and 55% going second, and by T5 in 56% / 60% [SH-ROB]. |
| Linlin turn onward | 10 | Attack with the 8000-power Trigger bodies; choose Linlin's Life gain when you are under pressure, the Life bounce when racing *(inference)*. Sweet 3 Generals and Smoothie (rest 2 DON!!) add Life and shrink or disrupt [CARD]. |

## Mulligan

- **Sourced baseline:** Shonen TCG's simulation took one mulligan when the hand had nothing costing 3 or less [SH-ROB]. With 29 Trigger cards, 99% of opening hands hold at least one Trigger to feed the leader [SH-ROB].
- **Keepable hand** *(inference, built on [SH-ROB])*: a 1-cost searcher or Yamato for T1–T2, plus a Trigger card you can afford to pitch on T2, plus Pudding or a +2000 counter. Linlin in the opening hand is a bonus, not a requirement; Pudding and the searchers find her.
- **Mulligan** *(inference)*: hands with no card costing 3 or less, or hands whose only Trigger cards are the ones you need to keep (Teach, Pudding).
- **Going second** is the big swing for this deck: two ramps reach 10 DON!! on T4 [SH-ROB].

## What it is weak to

- **Luffy & Ace (ST30-001)** is Robin's worst common matchup "by a distance": 19-32 [SH-ROB]. Luffy & Ace's own guide has it 32-19 the other way [SH-LA].
- **Banish and Trigger denial cut both ways** *(inference)*: Robin's defence leans on Trigger bodies coming off Life, so an opponent who strips Life cheaply or wins before 10 DON!! takes away the payoff turn.
- **The post-ban field.** It lost its best common matchup when Mihawk was banned (78-55) and its online results since the ban are losing (15-19) [SH-ROB][SH-BEST]. Shonen TCG calls it "a solid second-tier pick, not September's top deck" [SH-ROB].
- **The DON!! from the leader comes in rested**, so ramp turns do not add plays that turn [SH-ROB]; a fast deck can punish a slow T2–T3 *(inference)*.
