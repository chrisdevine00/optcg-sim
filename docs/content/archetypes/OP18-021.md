## What the deck is trying to do

Franky is a Stage-engine deck. The Leader lets you play a Stage of cost 5 or less from hand for DON!! −1 instead of paying its cost, and each of the four 5-cost Stages (OP18-075 Thousand Sunny, OP18-076 Shark Submerge 3, OP18-077 White Hobby Horse 1, OP18-078 Mini-Merry) draws 1 card and adds 1 DON!! from the DON!! deck as rested when played [CARD-18]. *(inference)* So a Stage put down with the Leader's ability returns 1 DON!! and brings back 1 rested DON!! while drawing a card, which is close to a free card each turn. Only 1 Stage can be on the field: playing another trashes the old one (CR 3-8-5), and Tom OP18-070 and the Trigger of OP18-074 return Stages from the trash to the hand [CARD-18]. In hand every Stage is a +3000 Counter, so the deck defends with the cards it draws [CARD-18] *(inference)*. A Stage on the field switches on the rest of the deck: Franky OP18-034 plays a cost-5-or-less Franky Family or Straw Hat Crew card from hand, then rests a cost-6-or-less opposing Character if you have a Stage of cost 5 or more, and OP18-074 gives +4000 on the same condition [CARD-18]. The Franky Family OP18-072 and Zambai OP18-066 K.O. one of your own Stages with cost 5 or less as a cost, for −3000 on an opposing Character and Rush respectively [CARD-18]. Searchers (Iceburg OP18-061, Nami, Tom) keep Stages and Straw Hat Crew cards flowing [CARD-18][CARD].

## Mulligan

There is no written source for this deck: no deck guide, article or video was read for it. The keep rule below is our reasoning *(inference)*, from card text [CARD-18]:

- **Keepable hand** *(inference)*: an opening searcher (Iceburg OP18-061, Nami EB02-017 or PRB02-012, Tom OP18-070), at least one Stage (OP18-075 to 078), and counters. A Stage in hand counts as a +3000 Counter with this Leader, so Stages double as defence and the counter requirement is usually met by the Stage itself.
- **Going second** *(inference)*: one piece short is acceptable, because you have 2 DON!! on T1 and an extra draw.
- **Going first** *(inference)*: the searcher is required, since the deck has no other way to find Stages or the next play.
- **Send back** *(inference)*: a hand of only Characters of 5 or more cost, or a hand with no Stage and no searcher, which leaves the Leader's ability and the +3000 Counter with nothing to work on.

## What it is weak to

- **Hands with no Stage** *(inference)*: the Leader's ability, the +3000 Counter and the conditions on OP18-034's rest and OP18-074's +4000 all need a Stage, and only 1 can be on the field (CR 3-8-5) [CARD-18].
- **A thin Blocker line** *(inference)*: the lists show one Blocker, Franky Family OP18-072 (3/0/4), so a wide attack meets hand Counters, not blocks [ML-ADD].
- **Gloriosa and bounce on its bodies** *(inference)*: its searchers and Blockers cost 1 to 6, within reach of cost-based removal, though a Stage is not a Character and cannot be bottomed or bounced by Character-only effects [CARD].
- **Banish** *(inference)*: its Life Triggers (OP18-074, OP18-038, PRB02-012) are skipped by Banish (CR 10-1-3), and its defence is hand Counters, not Life [CARD-18].
- **Hand-size pressure** *(inference)*: a Stage engine draws extra cards, which can feed hand-trash effects such as ST10-010's, which needs a hand of 7 or more [CARD].
