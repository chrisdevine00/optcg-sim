## What the deck is trying to do

Luffy is a Straw Hat Crew tempo deck that uses its Leader to lend itself DON!! and keeps its hand full with searchers [CARD-18][ML-ADD]. The Leader's DON!! −2 returns 2 DON!! and sets up to 2 DON!! active, so on the turn you use it you have the same DON!! to spend as before; the cost is 2 fewer DON!! on the field afterwards, which is a loan rather than a gain *(inference)*. The only-Straw-Hat-Crew condition is checked on your Characters, so a Character of another type (the lists' only one is Shanks OP17-022) switches the ability off while it stays on the field [CARD-18][CARD]. The engine reads an empty board as not met as well, and the 2 DON!! are returned before the condition is read, so the Leader's DON!! −2 needs at least one Character on your field, all of them Straw Hat Crew; otherwise it is DON!! spent for nothing [CARD-18] *(inference)*. Sanji & Pudding EB02-035 pays part of the loan back: once per turn, when 2 or more DON!! are returned to the DON!! deck, it adds 1 DON!! and sets it active, which the Leader's DON!! −2 sets off [CARD]. Nami (EB02-017, PRB02-012), Zoro-Juurou ST18-004 and Shoot Down That Flag. OP18-038 each look at 5 cards for a Straw Hat Crew card [CARD-18][CARD]. The Counter events OP09-078 Gum-Gum Giant (DON!! −2, trash a card: +4000, draw 2) and OP15-078 Mamaragan (DON!! −2: draw 1 and rest a 5000-or-less Character; Counter +1000) pay in DON!! (Gum-Gum Giant in its Counter, Mamaragan in its Main; Mamaragan's Counter costs nothing), so they sit well beside a Leader that refunds DON!! [CARD] *(inference)*. The Leader's +1000 lasts only until the end of the opponent's next turn, so it is a defensive 6000 [CARD-18].

## Mulligan

There is no written source for this deck: no deck guide, article or video was read for it. The keep rule below is our reasoning *(inference)*, from card text [CARD-18][CARD]:

- **Keepable hand** *(inference)*: a searcher (EB02-017, PRB02-012, OP18-038 Shoot Down That Flag. or Zoro-Juurou ST18-004) and at least two cards with a Counter. Every searcher finds a Straw Hat Crew card, and the Leader's ability needs spent DON!! to return, so a hand that can develop and still defend is the target.
- **Going second** *(inference)*: one piece short is acceptable, because you have 2 DON!! on T1 and an extra draw.
- **Going first** *(inference)*: the searcher is required, since the deck has no other way to find its next play.
- **Send back** *(inference)*: a hand of only 5 or more cost cards or only events, which leaves nothing to play on T1 and T2.

## What it is weak to

- **Fewer DON!! on the field** *(inference)*: each DON!! −2 (Leader, Gum-Gum Giant, Mamaragan) takes 2 DON!! off the field, so the deck falls behind the normal DON!! count unless Sanji & Pudding refunds it [CARD].
- **A non-Straw-Hat Character on the field** *(inference)*: Shanks OP17-022 is not Straw Hat Crew and turns the Leader's ability off while it stays [CARD-18][CARD].
- **Small bodies** *(inference)*: most Characters are 1 to 4 cost and 1000 to 3000 power (Nami, Sanji, Usopp), within reach of K.O. effects such as El Thor (3000 or less) and Wyper's −2000 sweep [CARD].
- **Rest effects on its Blockers and DON!!** *(inference)*: its defence is Counter events that cost active DON!! (Gum-Gum Giant costs 1 and DON!! −2, Demon Aura 1); effects that rest DON!! (such as OP12-037) take the active DON!! those events pay with, though Mamaragan's Counter costs nothing [CARD].
- **Banish** *(inference)*: its Triggers (OP18-038, PRB02-012) are skipped by Banish (CR 10-1-3) [CARD-18][CARD].
