# Rocks.D.Xebec (Blue)

**Leader:** OP17-039 Rocks.D.Xebec. Blue, 5 Life, 5000 power, Slash, type Rocks Pirates [CARD].
**Leader text** [CARD]: "[When Attacking] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Reveal 1 card from the top of your deck. If the revealed card's type includes "Rocks Pirates", draw 2 cards."
**Standing** [ML]: 32 top-8s in the window (most of any legal leader), 11.2% share over the window and 16.1% after the ban. opmetagame tier S [OPM]. Top 4 at the Dallas Finals [TCGP-DAL]. Shonen TCG counts it as the most-played leader of 2026-09-28 to 10-12, 109 of 587 Standard lists (18.6%), winning 51.2% of non-mirror matches on Limitless Play [SH-XEB]. That window runs from after the ban was announced (09-24) to the day it took effect (10-12); meta-landscape.md counts the 09-28/29 events as pre-ban and later ones as post-ban or ban-adopting, so it is a mixed sample, not a post-ban one.

**How to read this guide.** Turn numbers are *your own* turns: T1 is your first turn. DON!! available on each of your turns is 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10 going first and 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 going second (CR 6-4-1). Tags in square brackets resolve in `docs/content/sources.md`. *(inference)* marks our reasoning rather than a sourced claim.

**Format note.** EB05 (*ONE PIECE Heroines Edition vol.2*) releases in English on 2026-10-30 and OP18 on 2026-11-20 (meta-landscape.md). Every list, record and plan here predates both, so recheck this guide once EB05 cards appear in results.

## What the deck is trying to do

Rocks is a blue card-advantage midrange deck built almost entirely from OP17 Rocks Pirates [ML][SH-XEB]. Every leader attack trades one hand card for a reveal that draws two when the top card is Rocks Pirates, and with a list where every card is Rocks Pirates the reveal cannot miss, so each attack is a two-for-one [SH-XEB]. The deck spends that surplus on tempo tools that stop attackers (Miss Buckingham Stussy), send threats to the bottom of the deck (Gloriosa) or bounce them (the Rocks Pirates event), and finishes with the 10-cost Rocks.D.Xebec, which draws and plays two more Rocks Pirates from hand [SH-XEB][CARD]. Its Characters print no Counter, so its defence is the eight 0-cost +2000 counter events, Buckin, and the hand itself [SH-XEB].

## Mulligan

- **Sourced baseline:** Shonen TCG's simulation mulliganed any opening hand without a 1- to 3-cost Character, which happened 15% of the time [SH-XEB].
- **Keepable hand** *(inference, built on [SH-XEB] and the S9 mulligan questions in strategy-concepts.md)*: one of Streusen, Kyo, Buckin, Stussy or Don Marlon for turns 1–2, plus either a counter event or a card that does something at 5–7 DON!! (Linlin, Gloriosa, Shiki). Going second you can keep a hand that is one piece short, because the leader's reveal starts refilling from T2.
- **Mulligan** *(inference)*: hands of only 6+ cost cards, or hands with no 1–3 cost Character going first.
- **Do not chase OP17-118 in the opening hand** *(inference)*: the deck digs two cards per attack, and 76–77% of games have it by 10 DON!! anyway [SH-XEB].

## What it is weak to

- **Black Luffy (OP17-079)** is the one clearly bad common matchup: 18-38 (32.1%) [SH-XEB]. Elbaph Giants such as OP17-094 Rodo gain +12 cost with an Elbaph leader, and Black Luffy gives cost-12+ Characters [Blocker]. Gloriosa (cost 5 or less) and the Rocks Pirates bounce (cost 6 or less) cannot touch them; Stussy still can, because it checks base cost [SH-XEB].
- **Few printed counters.** Defence rests on eight +2000 events, Buckin and OP17-118 [SH-XEB]. Hand size is life: every reveal and every Newgate activation costs a card, so over-paying on a turn the opponent can pressure leaves you exposed *(inference)*.
- **Rosinante (OP12-061)** 7-14 on a small sample [SH-XEB].
- Before the ban, Mihawk was its worst matchup ("an abysmal matchup into the most popular Leader") [TCGP-DAL]; that is gone since 2026-10-12 (meta-landscape.md).
