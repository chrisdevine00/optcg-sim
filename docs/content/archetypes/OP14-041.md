# Boa Hancock (Blue/Yellow, OP14-041)

**Leader:** OP14-041 Boa Hancock. Blue/Yellow, 4 Life, 5000 power, Special, types The Seven Warlords of the Sea/Kuja Pirates.
**Leader text** [CARD]: "[Opponent's Turn] When you play a Character, draw 1 card. [DON!! x1] [Once Per Turn] When one of your {Amazon Lily} or {Kuja Pirates} type Characters with 5000 base power or more is K.O.'d, add up to 1 card from the top of your opponent's Life cards to the owner's hand."
**Standing** [ML]: 10 top-8s in the window (5 OP16 era, 3 pre-ban, 2 post-ban); 2.5% share over the window, 5.6% after the ban. opmetagame tier A [OPM]; A tier with 47 decklists on 2026-10-08 per Shonen TCG's reading of OPMetagame [SH-HAN]. Online since the ban it has the worst record of any deck with 20+ pilots: 28-43 (39.4%) over 20 pilots, 2026-10-12 to 10-27 [SH-BEST]; C tier in Shonen TCG's post-ban list [SH-TIER]. No OP-17 result above store level was found by Shonen TCG [SH-HAN].

**How to read this guide.** Turn numbers are *your own* turns: T1 is your first turn. DON!! available on each of your turns is 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10 going first and 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 going second (CR 6-4-1). Tags in square brackets resolve in `docs/content/sources.md`. *(inference)* marks our reasoning rather than a sourced claim.

## What the deck is trying to do

Hancock is a Yellow-heavy Trigger deck that turns the opponent's attacks into card draw. Its Life Triggers play Kuja Pirates Characters for free, and the leader draws a card each time a Character is played on the opponent's turn; when the opponent then K.O.s one of those 5000+ Kuja bodies, the leader (with a DON!! attached) sends the top of their Life to their hand, so attacking and removing both cost them something [SH-HAN][CARD]. The deck also sculpts its own Life: Zoro OP15-113 adds Life, Kouzuki Hiyori puts a known Trigger on top, and the 9-cost Boa Hancock adds one of your Life and takes one of theirs [SH-HAN][CARD]. *(inference)* In our three lists, 16 Characters play themselves from Life (Marigold, Gorgon Sisters, Shakuyaku, Ran) and 8 more have a Trigger that plays a Character (Boa Hancock OP14-112, Gecko Moria) [ML][CARD].

## Mulligan

- **Sourced baseline:** mulligan for Charlotte Pudding or Roronoa Zoro; a hand with neither is the one to send back [SH-HAN].
- **Keepable hand** *(inference)*: Pudding or Zoro, plus at least one Trigger Character to feed Pudding's cost or to plant with Hiyori.
- **Do not trash every Trigger to Pudding**: each one trashed is one fewer card that can play itself from Life later [SH-HAN].

## What it is weak to

- **Banish (Nico Robin, OP09-062).** Banish trashes the Life card without its Trigger (CR 10-1-3), which switches off this deck's engine *(inference)*; the record is 2-12 (SH-ROB's 12-2, reversed).
- **Removal that is not a K.O.** The Life-removal half of the leader fires only on K.O.s; bounce and rest effects do not trigger it [SH-HAN].
- **Early pressure before the Blockers come online** *(inference)*: Boa Marigold is a Blocker only at 2 or less Life [SH-HAN], and there are no 1-cost plays.
- **The current online field.** 28-43 since the ban [SH-BEST]; Shonen TCG advises waiting for post-ban results before taking it to a Regional [SH-HAN].
