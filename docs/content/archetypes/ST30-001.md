# Luffy & Ace (Red/Green)

**Leader:** ST30-001 Luffy & Ace. Red/Green, 4 Life, 6000 power, Strike/Special, types Impel Down / Whitebeard Pirates / Straw Hat Crew [CARD].
**Leader text** [CARD]: "If you have a Character with 7000 base power or more, give this Leader −2000 power. [Opponent's Turn] All of your [Portgas.D.Ace] and [Monkey.D.Luffy] cards gain +3000 power."
**Standing** [ML]: 20 top-8s in the window (2 / 10 / 8 by era), 6.2% share over the window and 12.3% after the ban, the second-largest post-ban share. opmetagame tier S [OPM]. Not among TCGplayer's five strongest leaders at the Dallas Finals [ML]. After the ban it has the best record of the popular decks: 121-91 (57.1%) across 13 Limitless Play events from 2026-10-12 to 10-27 [SH-BEST], and 105-66 (61%) across the seven OP17.5 events of 10-05 to 10-15 [SH-MUC].

**How to read this guide.** Turn numbers are *your own* turns: T1 is your first turn. DON!! available on each of your turns is 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10 going first and 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 going second (CR 6-4-1). Tags in square brackets resolve in `docs/content/sources.md`. *(inference)* marks our reasoning rather than a sourced claim.

**Format note.** EB05 (*ONE PIECE Heroines Edition vol.2*) releases in English on 2026-10-30 and OP18 on 2026-11-20 (meta-landscape.md). Every list, record and plan here predates both, so recheck this guide once EB05 cards appear in results.

## What the deck is trying to do

Luffy & Ace is a red/green Straw Hat aggro deck built on very high search density: fourteen 1-cost searchers plus four Thousand Sunny stages, eighteen searchers in all [SH-LA]. The searchers find ST31-001 Sanji, whose On Play draws and plays a Straw Hat Crew Character costing 5 or less for free, ideally the Rush Luffy ST21-014, on the 5–6 DON!! turn [SH-LA][CARD]. The 6000-power leader swings without needing DON!!, because no Character in the played lists has 7000 base power, so its −2000 clause never turns on [SH-LA]. On the opponent's turn every Monkey.D.Luffy gets +3000, so a 6000 Luffy defends at 9000 and the board protects itself; despite the name, the lists play no Portgas.D.Ace [SH-LA].

## Mulligan

- **No sourced keep rule** for this deck was found. Shonen TCG's simulation plays every 1-cost searcher on T1–T2, taking Sanji first [SH-LA].
- **Keepable hand** *(inference, built on [SH-LA])*: at least one 1-cost searcher or Thousand Sunny, plus Sanji or a Luffy, or two searchers. With eighteen searchers a hand with one is very likely to find the T3 Sanji turn.
- **Mulligan** *(inference)*: hands with no 1-cost searcher and no Sanji, or hands of only events.
- **Going first versus second** *(inference)*: the T3 combo odds are higher going second (94% vs 88%) and the extra DON!! pays for ST30-012's Rush [SH-LA].

## What it is weak to

- **Rocks (OP17-039)** is "the one to practice": 32-42 over the full window [SH-LA]; post-ban samples have it close to even (20-24 from Xebec's side [SH-XEB]; "close to a coin flip" [SH-MUC]). Shonen's advice: make them answer an 8000-power attack every turn, and save Demon Aura and I Know You're Strong for turns their board is rested [SH-LA]. By card text that fits I Know You're Strong; Demon Aura rests *active* cards, so against Rocks it is for an active Blocker such as Gloriosa or Kaido OP17-042 [CARD] *(inference)*.
- **Sabo (OP13-004)**: 19-27 [SH-LA], though only 5-6 in the post-ban chart [SH-MUC].
- **Kalgara (OP08-098)**: 5-9, its one losing post-ban pairing with eight or more games [SH-MUC].
- **Cost.** About $294, and the EB02 Nami and the two Rush Luffys are two-thirds of it [SH-LA] (not a game weakness, but it limits who can pilot it).
- The +3000 only applies to Luffy cards and only on the opponent's turn, and never to the leader (RL-49); Nami, Bonney, Sanji and the leader defend at printed power, so removal and attacks aimed at Sanji or the leader bypass it *(inference from card text)*.

## Playbook seeds

Measurable game-plan assertions for `ai/playbooks/ST30-001.json`. Turn numbers are the AI's own turns.

1. **Plays a 1-cost searcher or Thousand Sunny on T1** when holding one, in at least 90% of games. Source: [SH-LA] simulation plays every searcher on T1–T2.
2. **Plays Sanji into a free Luffy on T3** in at least 80% of games (simulated 88% going first, 94% going second) [SH-LA].
3. **Attach every spare DON!!.** On leader attacks from T2 to T4, every active DON!! not used for a play that turn and not held for a [Counter] Event in hand is given to the leader or an attacker before the attack, in at least 80% of such attacks. Each +1000 raises the counter the defender needs (CR 7-1-4-1). *(inference; replaces an earlier reading of [SH-LA] "keep swinging without giving it DON!!")*
4. **Never plays a Character with 7000+ base power** (which would switch on the leader's −2000). Source: [SH-LA], [CARD].
5. **I Know You're Strong on a rested board.** Casts OP13-040's [Main] only when the opponent has at least one rested Character with cost 7 or less (100% of [Main] uses). Source: [SH-LA] "save … for turns their board is rested"; [CARD].
6. **Demon Aura on active cards.** Casts OP12-037's [Main] only when the opponent has at least one active Character or active DON!!, and before that turn's attacks, in 100% of [Main] uses. *(inference from [CARD])*
