# Monkey.D.Luffy (Red/Green, OP13-001)

**Leader:** OP13-001 Monkey.D.Luffy. Red/Green, 4 Life, 5000 power, Strike, types Supernovas/Straw Hat Crew.
**Leader text** [CARD]: "[DON!! x1] [On Your Opponent's Attack] If you have 5 or less active DON!! cards, you may rest any number of your DON!! cards. For every DON!! card rested this way, this Leader or up to 1 of your {Straw Hat Crew} type Characters gains +2000 power during this battle."
**Standing** [ML]: 15 top-8s in the window, but 12 of them came in the OP16 era (before 2026-08-21); 3.3% share over the window and 1.5% after the ban. opmetagame lists "Red/Green Luffy" in S tier on a 1.5% share [OPM]. Not top tier by our two-source rule [ML]. Shonen TCG's post-ban tier list rates it B: 3 online pilots, 13-5 (72%), with snorlax1cynda's 6-0 win at ChinoizeCup #121 on 2026-10-12 [SH-TIER][SH-BEST]; that win is list 2 below. Before the ban, OPMetagame had it in S tier at a 46.4% win rate over 18 OP-17 decklists [SH-RGL].

**How to read this guide.** Turn numbers are *your own* turns: T1 is your first turn. DON!! available on each of your turns is 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10 going first and 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 going second (CR 6-4-1). Tags in square brackets resolve in `docs/content/sources.md`. *(inference)* marks our reasoning rather than a sourced claim. Not to be confused with ST30-001 Luffy & Ace, which is a separate archetype [SH-RGL].

## What the deck is trying to do

This is a Straw Hat Crew midrange deck whose leader defends rather than attacks: on the opponent's turn it rests your active DON!! for +2000 power each on the leader or one Straw Hat Character, so the deck wants active DON!! during the opponent's turn, which most decks spend on their own [SH-RGL]. A dense package of cards sets DON!! active again (Sanji OP13-027, Usopp OP14-022, Luffy OP13-118, Charlestone; plus Nami OP14-031 in our lists), so you can develop on your turn and still defend on theirs [SH-RGL][CARD]. Searchers (Nami, Jewelry Bonney, Thousand Sunny) keep the hand stocked, and Roronoa Zoro EB04-007 and the big Luffys close [SH-RGL][CARD].

## Mulligan

- **Sourced baseline:** SH-RGL's first instruction is to search on turns 1 and 2 with a Nami, Bonney or Thousand Sunny [SH-RGL]. Our lists run 12 of these (1-cost searchers plus the Stage); with 12 copies you see at least one in the opening five 76.3% of the time, 94.4% with the mulligan [SH-MUL].
- **Keepable hand** *(inference)*: one T1 searcher, plus one restand piece (Usopp, Sanji or Nami OP14-031) and a couple of +1000/+2000 counter cards. Going second, a hand with no searcher but Usopp and Sanji is still keepable.
- **Mulligan** *(inference)*: hands of only 6+ cost cards (Zoro, Luffys, Shanks) with no searcher or restand piece; going first, be stricter [SH-MUL].

## What it is weak to

- **Rocks (OP17-039) and Kaido (OP17-058).** Shonen TCG's call is to skip the deck if your locals run Rocks or Kaido, based on player reports rather than tracked records [SH-RGL]. No head-to-head records were found.
- **Decks that out-scale it.** The deck wins by surviving and swinging with Zoro, and struggles against decks that go bigger over a long game [SH-RGL].
- **Light printed counters.** By SH-RGL's count its sample list has 18 Characters with +1000, 8 with +2000 and 13 with none; the leader's DON!! rest is the rest of the defence [SH-RGL]. *(inference)* If your DON!! are all spent or more than 5 are active, the leader does nothing, so a turn spent tapping out leaves you exposed.
- **Effects that rest DON!! or deny Refresh** *(inference)*: the leader only works with active DON!!.
- **Small sample.** Only 3 online pilots after the ban [SH-TIER]; treat every plan below as a hypothesis.

## Playbook seeds

Measurable game-plan assertions for `ai/playbooks/OP13-001.json`. Turn numbers are the AI's own turns.
