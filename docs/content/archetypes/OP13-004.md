# Sabo (Red/Black)

**Leader:** OP13-004 Sabo. Red/Black, 5 Life, 5000 power, Special, types Dressrosa / Revolutionary Army.
**Leader text** [CARD]: "If you have 4 or more Life cards, give this Leader −1000 power. [DON!! x1] If you have a Character with a cost of 8 or more, your Leader and all of your Characters gain +1000 power." (optcgapi drops this minus sign and the second paragraph; the Limitless page has both.)
**Standing** [ML]: 16 top-8s in the window (0 / 11 / 5 by era), 9.8% share over the window and 7.3% after the ban. opmetagame tier S on its tier list (A on its decks page) and the #2 top-cut share at 11.7% [ML][OPM]. Top 8 at the Dallas Finals [TCGP-DAL]. After the ban: 62-56 (52.5%) across 13 Limitless Play events, 2026-10-12 to 10-27 [SH-BEST]; 45-41 in the post-ban chart, with no pairing of eight or more games lost by more than one game [SH-MUC].

**How to read this guide.** Turn numbers are *your own* turns: T1 is your first turn. DON!! available on each of your turns is 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10 going first and 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 going second (CR 6-4-1). Tags in square brackets resolve in `docs/content/sources.md`. *(inference)* marks our reasoning rather than a sourced claim.

## What the deck is trying to do

Sabo is an aggressive red/black deck that borrows the OP17 Elbaph Straw Hats: "an aggressive Straw Hat deck that turns its 2-cost bodies into 5000-power attackers" [SH-SAB]. Jaguar.D.Saul (cost 4) and Loki (cost 6) each "gain +12 cost", so on the field they count as cost 16 and 18 [CARD][SH-SAB]. Once one is out, Usopp, Nico Robin, Sanji, Zoro and Jinbe from OP17 get +3000 each, and with a DON!! on Sabo the leader's second line adds +1000 to everything, so each 2-cost swings for 6000 [SH-SAB][CARD]. Pirates Docking Six (+6 cost, so cost 11) turns on Sabo's line but not the Elbaph +3000 [SH-SAB]. The low event count is deliberate: the 2-cost Elbaph cards are both counters and attackers [SH-SAB]. TCGplayer: Sabo "has been given a new life with OP17's Elbaph archetype" [TCGP-DAL].

## Mulligan

- **Sourced keep rule:** "Keep a hand with a 1-cost dig and either Saul or a 2-cost Elbaph body. Two expensive cards and no dig goes back." [SH-SAB]
- **Odds:** Saul and Loki are 8 cards; you hold one in the opening five about 60% of the time, and in about 84% of games counting the redraw [SH-SAB].
- **Keepable hand** *(inference, built on [SH-SAB])*: Nami or Usopp, plus Saul or two 2-cost Elbaph bodies. Loki alone is not a T3 enabler.

## What it is weak to

- **Rocks (OP17-039)** is "the problem": 38-63 over 2026-08-20 to 10-18 [SH-SAB]. Xebec's guide has Sabo 13-18 [SH-XEB]. The post-ban chart is the outlier at 10-8 for Sabo [SH-MUC]. *(inference)* Stussy stops attackers by base cost, which a 2-cost Elbaph body always fails.
- **Purple Enel (OP15-058)**: "worse on a smaller sample" [SH-SAB]; Enel's guide has it 25-11 for Enel [SH-PEN].
- **Removing the cost-12 Character** *(inference)*: Saul and Loki carry the whole board's power; an answer to them drops every 2-cost back to 2000. Zoro OP17-095 exists to protect against effect removal [CARD].
- **High Life** *(inference from leader text)*: at 4–5 Life the leader is 4000 and its buff needs DON!!, so early turns are weaker than the board suggests.
