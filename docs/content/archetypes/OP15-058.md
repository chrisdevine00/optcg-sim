# Enel (Purple, OP15-058)

**Leader:** OP15-058 Enel. Purple, 5 Life, 5000 power, Special, type Sky Island [CARD].
**Leader text** [CARD]: "Under the rules of this game, your DON!! deck consists of 6 cards. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If it is your second turn or later, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active, and add up to 4 additional DON!! cards and rest them. Then, give up to 4 rested DON!! cards to 1 of your Characters."
**Standing** [ML]: 14 top-8s in the window (5 OP16 era, 8 pre-ban, 1 post-ban); 5.3% share over the window, 4.1% after the ban. opmetagame tier S on /tier-list/ (B on /decks/) [OPM]. In person it won the EU Finals (Utrecht) and was 2nd at the Dallas Finals [ML]; TCGplayer said Enel "returned with a vengeance" with "a whole toolbox of tech cards" [TCGP-DAL]. Online it has slid since the ban: 212-209 over 2026-08-20 to 10-14, under 50% after 09-28 [SH-PEN]; 31-38 (44.9%) over 16 pilots from 10-12 to 10-27 [SH-BEST]; C tier in Shonen TCG's post-ban list [SH-TIER].

**How to read this guide.** Turn numbers are *your own* turns: T1 is your first turn. **This deck's DON!! curve is not the usual one**: its DON!! deck is 6 cards (leader text), so you can never have more than six DON!! on your field [SH-PEN][CARD]. Tags in square brackets resolve in `docs/content/sources.md`. *(inference)* marks our reasoning rather than a sourced claim.

**Format note.** EB05 (*ONE PIECE Heroines Edition vol.2*) releases in English on 2026-10-30 and OP18 on 2026-11-20 (meta-landscape.md). Every list, record and plan here predates both, so recheck this guide once EB05 cards appear in results.

## What the deck is trying to do

Purple Enel is a DON!!-cycling tempo deck [SH-PEN]. With a 6-card DON!! deck, every card that says "if you have 6 or less DON!! cards on your field" is always on: Ohm always shrinks a Character when he attacks, Shura always has Rush, and the 6-cost Enel finisher can never be removed by the opponent's effects [SH-PEN][CARD]. Almost every card pays with DON!! −1 or −2, which sends DON!! back to a DON!! deck that the DON!! Phase and the leader refill next turn, so the deck spends DON!! to draw and gets it back for free [SH-PEN]. From your second turn the leader tops you up to six and loads up to 4 rested DON!! onto one Character, which then swings big on your turn [CARD][SH-ENC]. A pile of 0-cost Sky Island events draws cards, removes small Characters, and doubles as +2000 counters for the leader [SH-PEN].

## Mulligan

- **Sourced baseline:** keep the opener cheap. With fourteen 1-cost Characters you open with at least one 82% of the time, and 97% if you mulligan every hand without one; going second, the turn-1 draw takes the no-mulligan figure to 88% [SH-PEN].
- **Keepable hand** *(inference)*: a 1-cost Character plus any two of: another 1-drop, a 0-cost Sky Island event, Law or Holly. The leader guarantees six DON!! from T2, so expensive cards are less of a problem than missing early bodies.
- **Mulligan:** hands with no 1-cost Character [SH-PEN].

## What it is weak to

- **The current top of the field.** Since the ban news it loses to the three leaders it sees most: Rocks, Black Luffy and Luffy & Ace [SH-PEN]. Black Luffy is the worst at 10-18 [SH-PEN].
- **Nico Robin** 17-26 [SH-PEN].
- **Answers to the finisher.** Its immunity covers only the opponent's *effects*: a battle K.O. while it is rested still works, effects that negate its text (Black Hole OP16-117) or lower its power (Sakazuki OP16-065, −6000) get round it, and on the opponent's turn it has 10000, not 14000 [SH-ENC]. *(Note: SH-ENC discusses the OP15-060 printing; OP15-118, the one in these lists, has the same immunity line [CARD].)*
- **Big boards** *(inference)*: its cheap removal tops out at 3000–6000 power (El Thor, Mamaragan, Lightning Dragon, Gum-Gum Lightning). Shonen TCG would try a second Gum-Gum Lightning into Black Luffy and Rocks for more ways to deal with big bodies, labelled as a guess [SH-PEN].
