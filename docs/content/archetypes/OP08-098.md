# Kalgara (Yellow)

**Leader:** OP08-098 Kalgara. Yellow, 5 Life, 5000 power, Slash, types Jaya/Sky Island/Shandian Warrior.
**Leader text** [CARD]: "[DON!! x1] [When Attacking] Play up to 1 {Shandian Warrior} type Character card from your hand with a cost equal to or less than the number of DON!! cards on your field. If you do, add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand."
**Standing** [ML]: 5 top-8s in the window (4 of them post-ban), 0.6% share over the window and 2.9% after the ban, including a 6-0 win at ChinoizeCup #122 on 2026-10-13. opmetagame tier S on 2 tracked decklists [OPM]. Shonen TCG: the best online record of any leader with 10+ players in the first post-ban events, 33-20 (62%), and the only winning record against Luffy & Ace over 8+ games [SH-MUC]; 30-19 (61.2%) from 10 decks, 2026-10-12 to 10-27, "best win rate in the field … small sample" [SH-BEST]; tier A with no paper results yet [SH-TIER].

**How to read this guide.** Turn numbers are *your own* turns: T1 is your first turn. DON!! available on each of your turns is 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10 going first and 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 going second (CR 6-4-1). Tags in square brackets resolve in `docs/content/sources.md`. *(inference)* marks our reasoning rather than a sourced claim.

**About the lists.** [SH-KAL] compares two pre-ban lists by Nightingale (1st, 2026-09-30) and BMKid (3rd, 2026-09-28), tracked on OPMetagame, and warns that "two lists is a thin sample". The same two players have post-ban lists among our three (BMKid 1st on 10-13, Nightingale 8th on 10-12), and the core is unchanged: 4 each of Borsalino, both 5-cost Kalgara/Wyper pairs, Wyper OP08-110, Kalgara OP15-101, Mont Blanc Noland (3–4) and Upper Yard [ML][SH-KAL].

## What the deck is trying to do

Kalgara is a yellow midrange deck that spends Life to cheat Characters into play, then buys the Life back [SH-KAL]. Each leader attack with a DON!! attached plays a Shandian Warrior from hand for free, up to the number of DON!! on your field, and moves your top Life card to hand [CARD]. The deck is built around 20-plus Shandian Warriors the leader can play, a searcher (Kalgara OP15-101) to find them, a draw engine that fires when Life cards move (Kalgara OP12-099), and Life refills from hard-cast cards (Mont Blanc Noland, Borsalino, Sweet 3 Generals) [SH-KAL][CARD]. Wyper OP15-114's −2000 sweep, played free off the leader's attack and stacked with Sweet 3 Generals' −3000, clears small boards [SH-KAL].

## Mulligan

- **Sourced baseline:** none of the opened sources gives a Kalgara mulligan rule. [SH-KAL] advises keeping 12–13 Characters with a +2000 Counter in the list.
- **Keepable hand** *(inference, built on [SH-KAL] and the S9 mulligan questions in strategy-concepts.md)*: a T1–T2 setup card (Upper Yard, Nami OP15-108, Wyper OP08-110 or Kalgara OP15-101) plus one Shandian Warrior the leader can play by T3.
- **Mulligan** *(inference)*: no setup card and no Shandian Warrior; hands of only hard-cast cards (Noland, Borsalino, Sweet 3 Generals) with nothing for the leader to play.
- **Going second** *(inference)*: the extra DON!! lets the leader drop Wyper OP08-110 on T2, so a hand with any 4-cost-or-less Shandian Warrior is fine.

## What it is weak to

- **Its own Life clock.** Every free play costs a Life card, and the refills have conditions: Noland needs a Kalgara Character, Borsalino needs 2 Life or less, Sweet 3 Generals needs 2 spare DON!! [SH-KAL]. A fast opponent can punish a turn spent playing for value *(inference)*.
- **No clearly bad matchup in the data,** but the sample is small: [SH-MUC] lists no worst pairing with 8+ games, from 11 players.
- **Pre-ban, Mihawk.** Bandai banned OP14-020 partly because it "significantly restricts the viability of Leader cards with the {Slash} attribute", and Kalgara is a Slash leader [SH-KAL].
- **Banish** *(inference from card text)*: Nico Robin's leader trashes your Life cards without Triggers, removing Life the leader needs to spend.

## Playbook seeds

Measurable game-plan assertions for `ai/playbooks/OP08-098.json`. Turn numbers are the AI's own turns.

2. **Best free play.** The free play is the highest-cost legal Shandian Warrior in hand in at least 75% of uses. *(inference from [SH-KAL] "play the best Shandian Warrior")*
3. **Upper Yard early.** Has Upper Yard on the field by the end of T2 in at least 60% of games. Source: [SH-KAL] step 1; threshold *(inference)*.
4. **Stack before the sweep.** When Sweet 3 Generals and Wyper OP15-114 both resolve in one turn, Sweet 3 Generals resolves first in at least 80% of such turns. Source: [SH-KAL] step 5.
5. **Kingdom Come only to win.** Plays EB01-059 only on turns where its lethal search finds a win that turn (100%). Source: [SH-KAL] step 6.
