# Review 1: archetype guides and curriculum

The Phase 3 gate review (PROMPT.md Track G) was done by a subagent acting as a competitive OPTCG player. It returned 20 findings. Each finding was checked against card text, the rules digest, the rulings and the lists before anything was changed.

- **Card text:** the Limitless card pages for the six cards the findings turn on (OP17-058, OP14-031, OP05-098, EB04-031, OP17-054, OP12-037), fetched on 2026-10-28. Their robots.txt allows crawling, and requests were spaced 2 seconds apart. Every other card was read from the optcgapi dump (`.cache/track-b/raw/optcgapi-allSetCards.json`, `optcgapi-allSTCards.json`). None of the claims checked there depends on a minus sign.
- **Copy counts:** from `docs/research/meta-lists.json`, shown as list 1 / list 2 / list 3.
- **Severity:** the reviewer's scale. 3 is wrong enough to teach a losing play, 2 is wrong or unmeasurable but contained, and 1 is stale or cosmetic.
