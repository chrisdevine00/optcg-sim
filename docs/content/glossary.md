# Glossary

Plain-language definitions of every term a new player meets. **Rule** gives the Comprehensive Rules v1.2.1 number (via `docs/research/rules-digest.md`; "item N" is the digest's numbered item). Rules terms come from the CR; player slang and strategy terms point to a concept slug in `docs/research/strategy-concepts.md` or to a lesson in `docs/content/curriculum.md` instead, because the CR does not define them. The app's rules reference (PROMPT.md Learning layer §6) is built from this file.

## Cards and card parts

| Term | Plain meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Leader | The card that starts face-up in play and stays there all game. It sets your deck's colours, your Life, and usually your deck's plan. It can attack but is never "played", so it can attack on your first turn that allows battle. | CR 2-2-2, 2-2-3-1, 3-6-3, 5-2-1-3; item 80 |
| Character | A card you play to your Character area. It can attack, block (if it has [Blocker]) and be K.O.'d. In hand it can be used as a counter if it has a Counter value. | CR 2-2-2, 2-2-4-1, 2-2-4-2 |
| Event | A one-shot card. You pay its cost, trash it, and do what it says. [Main] Events are used on your turn; [Counter] Events during the opponent's attack. | CR 2-2-2, 2-7-3, 8-4-2 |
| Stage | A card that stays in your Stage area and gives an ongoing or activated effect. You can have one; a new one replaces the old. | CR 2-2-2, 3-8-5, 3-8-5-1 |
| DON!! card | Your resource. You do not draw it; two arrive each turn from your DON!! deck. | CR 2-2-2, 6-4-1 |
| Cost | The number in a card's top-left corner: how many DON!! you rest to play it. Only Characters, Events and Stages have one. | CR 1-3-9-1, 2-7-5, 2-7-2 |
| Power | A Leader's or Character's strength in battle. It can go below zero. | CR 2-6-2, 1-3-6-1 |
| Life (value) | The number on a Leader that says how many Life cards you start with. | CR 2-9-1, 2-9-3, 5-2-1-7 |
| Counter value | The +1000 or +2000 printed on the left edge of many Characters. Trash the card from your hand during the opponent's attack to add that much power to a defender for that battle. | CR 2-10-2, 2-10-4, 7-1-3-1-1 |
| Colour | Red, green, blue, purple, black or yellow. Your deck may only use colours your Leader has. | CR 2-3-3, 2-3-5, 5-1-2-2 |
| Type | The trait line on a card, such as {Straw Hat Crew} or {Rocks Pirates}. Effects often say "a {Type} card" (exact) or a type "including" some text (partial match). | CR 2-4-2, 2-4-3, 2-4-3-1 |
| Attribute | Slash, Strike, Ranged, Special, Wisdom or "?". Some effects care about it. | CR 2-5-2 |
| Card number | The ID such as OP17-039. Alternate arts share a number, and the four-copy limit counts by number. | CR 2-14-2, 5-1-2-3; item 39 |
| Base power / base cost | The number as printed, before any changes. | CR 4-9-2 |
| Trigger | A Life-card ability. When that card would go from Life to your hand because you took damage, you may reveal it and use the [Trigger] instead. | CR 10-1-5, 10-1-5-1 |
| Block symbol | The small number in a card's lower-right corner. It does nothing in a game but decides Standard legality. | CR 2-12-1 to 2-17-1 (no effect on play); topics/013.php |

## The board (areas)

| Term | Plain meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Deck | Your 50-card face-down pile. Nobody may look at it or change its order. | CR 3-2-2, 5-1-2 |
| DON!! deck | Your separate 10-card DON!! pile. Both players may look at it. | CR 3-3-2, 5-1-2 |
| Hand | Your cards, hidden from the opponent. There is no hand-size limit. | CR 3-4-2; item 53 |
| Trash | Your face-up discard pile. Either player may look through it. | CR 3-5-2 |
| Leader area | Where your Leader sits. | CR 3-6-2 |
| Character area | Where your Characters go. At most 5; to play a sixth you first trash one of yours (that is not a K.O.). | CR 3-7-6, 3-7-6-1; item 78 |
| Stage area | Where your one Stage goes. | CR 3-8-5 |
| Cost area | Where your DON!! sit after they arrive. You rest them here to pay costs. | CR 3-9-2, 3-9-3 |
| Life area | Your face-down Life cards. Nobody may look at them. | CR 3-10-2 |
| Field | Your Leader, Character, Stage and cost areas together. "If you have…" means on the field unless stated. | CR 3-1-2, 3-1-2-1 |
| Public information | How many cards are in every area, the trash's contents, and what was played. | CR 3-1-4; TRM §4.11 (item 136) |

## Card states

| Term | Plain meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Active | Upright. Active cards can attack, block, or be rested to pay costs. | CR 4-4-1 |
| Rested | Turned sideways. Rested Characters cannot block and can be attacked. | CR 4-4-1; item 91 |
| Given DON!! (attached) | A DON!! placed under your Leader or a Character. It is neither active nor rested. | CR 4-4-2, 6-5-5-1 |
| "Cannot attack the turn it is played" | Players call this summoning sickness. A Character you play this turn cannot attack unless it has [Rush]. | CR 3-7-4, 10-1-1-1 |

## Setup and turn

| Term | Plain meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| First / second player | A random method picks who chooses; that player picks going first or second. | CR 5-2-1-4, 5-2-1-5 |
| Opening hand | The 5 cards each player draws at the start. | CR 5-2-1-6 |
| Mulligan (redraw) | Once, before Life is set, you may shuffle your whole hand back and draw 5 new cards. All or nothing; the first player decides first. | CR 5-2-1-6, 5-2-1-6-1 |
| Refresh Phase | Start of your turn: "until the start of your next turn" effects end, start-of-turn effects happen, given DON!! come back, and all your rested cards stand up. | CR 6-2-1 to 6-2-4 |
| Draw Phase | Draw 1 card. The first player skips this on their first turn. | CR 6-3-1 |
| DON!! Phase | Put 2 DON!! from your DON!! deck into your cost area (1 on the first player's first turn). | CR 6-4-1 |
| Main Phase | Your action phase: play cards, use effects, give DON!!, and attack, in any order. | CR 6-5-2 |
| End Phase | End-of-turn effects happen, "this turn" effects end, and the turn passes. | CR 6-6-1 |
| First-turn rule | Neither player may attack on their own first turn. | CR 6-5-6-1 |
| Turn player | The player whose turn it is. When both players must choose or act at once, the turn player goes first. | CR 1-3-4, 1-3-10 |

## DON!!

| Term | Plain meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Rest DON!! (pay a cost) | Turn DON!! in your cost area sideways to pay for a card or effect. | CR 2-7-2, 8-3-1-5 |
| Give DON!! (attach) | Move an active DON!! under your Leader or a Character for +1000 power during your turn only. It returns at your next Refresh Phase. | CR 6-5-5-1, 6-5-5-2, 6-2-3 |
| ① (circled number) | An effect cost: rest that many active DON!!. | CR 8-3-1-5 |
| DON!! −X | An effect cost: return X DON!! from your field (cost area or given) to your DON!! deck. They can come back in later DON!! Phases. | CR 8-3-1-6, 10-2-10; item 86 |
| [DON!! xX] | A condition: the effect works only while this card has X or more DON!! given to it. | CR 8-3-2-3, 10-2-9-1 |

## Battle

| Term | Plain meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Attack | Rest your active Leader or Character to attack the opponent's Leader or one of their rested Characters. | CR 7-1, 7-1-1-1, 7-1-1-2 |
| Attack Step | The attack is declared; [When Attacking] and [On Your Opponent's Attack] effects happen. | CR 7-1-1 |
| Block Step | The defender may use one [Blocker] to make it the new target. | CR 7-1-2-1 |
| Counter Step | The defender may trash Characters from hand for their Counter value and use [Counter] Events, as many as they like. | CR 7-1-3-1 |
| Damage Step | Compare power once. The attacker wins if its power is greater than **or equal to** the defender's. | CR 7-1-4-1 |
| Ties go to the attacker | Equal power means the attack succeeds. A counter that only matches the attacker does nothing. | CR 7-1-4-1 |
| Damage | Each point moves your top Life card to your hand (or lets you use its Trigger). | CR 4-6-1, 4-6-2, 7-1-4-1-1-2 |
| K.O. | A Character trashed by losing a battle or by an effect. Other ways of trashing it are not a K.O. | CR 10-2-1-1, 10-2-1-3 |
| End of the Battle | "During this battle" boosts end and play returns to the Main Phase. | CR 7-1-5 |
| Counter (Event) | An Event with [Counter]: pay its cost and trash it during the opponent's Counter Step. | CR 7-1-3-1-2, 10-2-4-1 |

## Keywords

| Term | Plain meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| [Rush] | This Character can attack the turn it is played. | CR 10-1-1-1 |
| [Rush: Character] | This Character can attack the opponent's Characters, not the Leader, the turn it is played. | CR 10-1-6-1 |
| [Double Attack] | A hit on the Leader deals 2 damage. Against 1 Life it does not win: Life goes to 0 and the game goes on. | CR 10-1-2-1; item 95 |
| [Banish] | Life cards this card's damage removes go to the trash, not to hand, and their Trigger does not happen. | CR 10-1-3-1 |
| [Blocker] | When another of your cards is attacked, rest this to become the new target. Once per battle. | CR 10-1-4-1, 7-1-2-1 |
| [Unblockable] | The opponent cannot use [Blocker] against this attacker. | CR 10-1-7-1 |
| [Trigger] | See "Trigger" above. | CR 10-1-5 |
| [On Play] | Happens when the card is played. | CR 10-2-6 |
| [When Attacking] | Happens when this card attacks. | CR 10-2-5 |
| [On Block] | Happens when this card's [Blocker] is used. | CR 10-2-15-1 |
| [On K.O.] | Happens when this card is K.O.'d. | CR 10-2-17-1 |
| [On Your Opponent's Attack] | Happens when the opponent attacks, after their [When Attacking]. | CR 10-2-16-1 |
| [Activate: Main] | An ability you choose to use in your Main Phase, outside battle. | CR 10-2-2-1 |
| [Main] | An Event effect you use in your Main Phase, outside battle. | CR 10-2-3-1 |
| [Once Per Turn] | Usable once per turn, per card. | CR 10-2-13-1, 10-2-13-2 |
| [Your Turn] / [Opponent's Turn] | The effect works only during that player's turn. | CR 10-2-11-1, 10-2-12-1 |
| [End of Your Turn] / [End of Your Opponent's Turn] | Happens in the End Phase of that turn. | CR 10-2-7, 10-2-8, 6-6-1-1 |
| Trash (as a cost) | Choose a card from your hand and put it in the trash. | CR 10-2-14-1 |

## Effect wording

| Term | Plain meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Card text beats the rules | If a card and the rules disagree, the card wins. | CR 1-3-1 |
| "Up to" | Choose that many or fewer, including zero. | CR 4-8-1, 8-4-4-1 |
| "If … then" | If the "if" part fails, what follows does not happen. | CR 4-10-1, 4-10-2 |
| "Instead" | A replacement effect: something else happens in place of the event. | CR 8-1-3-4 |
| Activation cost | What you pay before the colon to use an effect. If you cannot pay all of it, you cannot use it. | CR 8-3-1, 8-3-1-3 |
| Reveal / look at | Reveal shows the card to both players; looking shows it only to you. | CR 11-2-1, 11-3-1 |
| Remove | Move a card from its area to another. | CR 4-11-2 |
| Set power to 0 | Lower the card's power by its current power for the effect's duration. | CR 4-12-1 |
| "During this turn" / "until the end of the turn" | Two slightly different end points in the End Phase. | CR 6-6-1-2, 6-6-1-3 |
| Rule processing | Automatic checks (like losing at 0 deck) that happen immediately, even mid-effect. | CR 9-1-2, 9-2-1 |

## Winning and losing

| Term | Plain meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Win by damage | Hit the opponent's Leader while they have 0 Life cards. | CR 1-2-1-1-1, 7-1-4-1-1-1 |
| Deck-out | A player whose deck reaches 0 cards loses. | CR 1-2-1-1-2, 9-2-1-2 |
| Concede | You may give up at any time. | CR 1-2-3 |
| Draw (game result) | Both players lose at once, or an endless loop nobody can stop. | CR 9-2-1, 11-1-1-1 |

## Deck building and format

| Term | Plain meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Deck rules | 1 Leader, 50 cards of the Leader's colours, at most 4 per card number, and 10 DON!!. | CR 5-1-2, 5-1-2-1 to 5-1-2-3 |
| Standard Regulation | The main English tournament format since 2026-04-01. Until 2027-03-31 it allows block numbers 2 to 5, plus listed exceptions. | topics/013.php; item 42 |
| Extra Regulation | A format that allows all blocks. | topics/013.php; item 42 |
| Banned card | Not allowed in a deck at all. Current list (effective 2026-10-12) includes OP14-020 Dracule Mihawk. | news/restriction.html; item 41 |
| Banned pair | Two cards that may not be in the same deck, such as OP07-115 with EB04-058. | news/restriction.html; item 41 |
| Restricted card | Limited copies. There are none on the current list. | news/restriction.html |
