## Findings

### B3A-R1 (2) OP11-041 Nami: the Once Per Turn is spent by an "If" that fails at resolution
- **Card:** "[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] This effect can be activated when a card is removed from your or your opponent's Life cards. If you have 7 or less cards in your hand, draw 1 card."
- **Script:** `oncePerTurn` on the ability; the hand check is an effect-level `if` (`hand <= 7`), so it is read at resolution.
- **Probe (scratch, real script):** Nami, 8 cards in hand, my Character hits the opponent's Leader: nothing is drawn and the use is spent. I then play a card (7 in hand) and a second Character hits: still nothing drawn (hand 7 before and after). Under the other reading (the "If" as an activation condition, the use kept) the second hit would draw.
- **Judgement:** defensible and probably right (CR 8-3-3 "text after a failed if does not resolve"; rules-digest 126 "activates and resolves once per turn"; the timing phrase is the activation, the "If" is part of the effect). It differs from how the same batch encodes a leading "If" with no Once Per Turn (OP11-036, 075, 076 use `conditions`; OP11-040, 047 use an effect-level `if`; harmless there). No test pins the 8-then-7 case. Keep, record it in OPEN-QUESTIONS as an open reading, and add a test (author wrote `8 in hand: nothing is drawn` only).

### B3A-R2 (2) "Turn 1 card from the top of your Life cards face-down" needs a face-up top card: unrecorded, five cards depend on it
- **Cards:** OP11-100 Otohime, 103 Long-Jaw Neptunian, 104 Shirley, 107 Topknot Neptunian, 108 Neptune.
- **Engine:** `payable` for `turnLifeFaceDown` requires every turned card to be face-up (`engine/src/effects/eval.ts:364`); `turnLifeFaceUp` requires face-down (`eval.ts:360`). Life is face-down by default, so these five are usable only after something turned the top card face-up (OP11-022, OP11-117, OP12-102's replacement).
- **Judgement:** consistent with the design (the set pairs "turn face-up" with "turn face-down") and with M4b-3 for the face-up half, but the face-down mirror has no entry in OPEN-QUESTIONS or DECISIONS (M4b-3 names only "face-up"; the independent DISAGREEMENTS-c says "no official ruling for the face-down mirror itself"). The other reading (any top Life card may be "turned face-down", a no-op on a face-down one) makes the five cards freely usable in any deck, a large play difference. Also `docs/research/OP-11-scripting-g1.md` says of OP11-022 "the cost needs a Life card"; the engine needs a face-down top card (the author test "cannot be activated without Life cards" does not cover it). No leak: the face state is public and the prompt follows it.
- **Fix (docs):** add an OPEN-QUESTIONS entry (face-down mirror) and correct the g1 line. No script change.

### B3A-R3 (2) OP12-098 author boundary test is mislabelled; the cost-8 boundary survives
- **Test:** `engine/test/cards/OP12-098.test.ts` "boundary: a cost-8 Character that is not Revolutionary Army, or a Revolutionary Army one of cost 7, does not count" uses `C8` and `RA2` (`EB05-041`, cost 2), never a cost-7 {Revolutionary Army} Character. The mutant `cost >= 8` to `cost >= 7` survives the author suite and is killed by the independent suite (its "an RA Character with cost 7 gives no bonus").
- **Fix:** use a printed cost-7 {Revolutionary Army} Character (or Morley at 4 + 4 minus one) in the author test.
