## Findings

### LF-R1 (3) EB02-030: the replacement covers a Character played after the Counter (official answer: it does not)
- **Card:** `[Counter] If any of your Characters would be K.O.'d in battle during this turn, you may trash 1 card from your hand instead. [Trigger] Draw 1 card.`
- **Ruling:** official Q&A (`qa_eb02.txt`, printed above the card number EB02-030): "During my opponent's turn, can I trash 1 card from my hand to prevent a Character I played after activating this Counter effect from being K.O.'d in battle? **No, you cannot.**" The effect covers the Characters you have when it resolves.
- **Script:** `addReplacement { replaces: { ko: { from: 'characters', owner: 'you' }, cause: 'battle' }, optional, instead: trash 1, duration: 'thisTurn' }`. The filter is live, so every Character on the field at the K.O. is covered. The script's own ruling says so ("including one played after the Event"), contradicting the Q&A. `collectReplacements` (`flow.ts`) evaluates `pool(c, rp.ko)` at the K.O.
- **Probe:** p1 (defender) holds the Event, 5 active DON!!, and OP14-106 Salome (Blocker, [Trigger] "Play this card") as the top Life card. p2's first Thatch attacks the Leader; p1 uses the Event in the Counter Step; the damage reveals Salome, p1 activates the Trigger (Salome is played after the Event); p2's second Thatch attacks the Leader; Salome blocks and is K.O.'d in battle. **The engine asks `optional` ("use the replacement instead of the K.O.?")** and a card trashed from hand saves Salome. Official: no prompt, Salome is K.O.'d. (Control, existing author test: a Character on the field before the Event is covered.)
- **Why it changes play:** a Blocker played from a Trigger, a [Counter] play or an [On Block]-style effect after the Event gets a free save the card does not give; the choice also alters the Counter decisions of the opponent.
- **Fix:** capture the Characters of the controller on the field when `addReplacement` resolves and test membership in `collectReplacements` (for example a `targets: InstanceId[]` on the replacement modifier, or resolve a `{ ref }` filter at creation), keeping the live `from: 'characters', owner: 'you'` pool for printed replacements. Rewrite the script ruling. Test (fails on the old code): the Salome probe above, plus the control. M6b-Q54 (every battle K.O. of that turn is offered, each time) is untouched by this and stays a reasonable reading: the text has no Once Per Turn, and the Q&A answer reads like a fixed set of Characters, not a single use. EB02-030 is the only script that uses `addReplacement`.

### LF-R2 (3) ST24-005 X.Drake: the end-of-turn DON!! set joins the [End of Your Turn] order prompt (official answer: it comes after all of them)
- **Card:** `[On Play] If your Leader has the "Supernovas" type, rest up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less. Then, set up to 1 of your DON!! cards as active at the end of this turn.`
- **Ruling:** official Q&A (`qa_st-23-28.txt`, above the card number ST24-005): "Does this [On Play] effect set 1 of my DON!! cards as active before my [End of Your Turn] effects activate, or after? In this case, this [On Play] effect will set 1 of your DON!! cards as active **after all of your [End of Your Turn] effects have activated and resolved**." (The same file's Smoker entry, no set at the end of a turn in which a Character may not set DON!!, passes: probe below.)
- **Script / engine:** `delayed: { at: 'endOfThisTurn' }`. `fireDelayed` (`flow.ts`) puts the delayed effect in the same End Phase `batch` as the [End of Your Turn] autos, so the controller gets an `order` decision with both and may resolve the DON!! set first.
- **Probe:** p1 Leader ST01-001 ({Supernovas}), EB03-018 Tashigi rested on the field, 5 active DON!!, X.Drake in hand. Play Drake (5 DON!! rested, 0 active), end the turn. The engine asks "Several of your effects activated at once", options **Tashigi [End of Your Turn]** and **X.Drake (at the end of this turn)**. Choosing Drake first sets 1 DON!! active, then Tashigi's optional cost (rest 1 DON!!, trash 1 card) is offered and payable. Official: Tashigi's cost is not payable (no active DON!!) and Drake's DON!! is set afterwards.
- **Why it changes play:** any [End of Your Turn] effect that rests or spends an active DON!! (Tashigi EB03-018 here; others with a DON!! cost) can be paid with the DON!! that Drake sets, and an effect that reads the active or rested DON!! count sees the wrong number.
- **Affected:** `endOfThisTurn` delayed effects are shared: EB02-015, OP08-074, OP11-092, OP11-107, OP13-024, OP13-038, OP13-066, OP14-031, OP15-025 and ST24-005. Only the ST24-005 entry is in the Q&A, but the cards that set DON!! active at the end of the turn have the same shape and no ruling that goes the other way; check each (a delayed bottom-of-deck or return is not order-sensitive).
- **Fix:** queue the delayed `endOfThisTurn` effects in a later batch than the [End of Your Turn] autos of the same controller (or resolve them after the batch without an order choice among themselves and the autos), keep several delayed effects ordered among themselves as now. Test (fails on the old code): the Tashigi probe (no prompt for Tashigi's cost, 1 DON!! active at the end), and a control with only Drake. The Smoker case needs no change: with OP10-030 Smoker's restriction active the end-of-turn set does nothing (probe: 6 active, 2 rested, Smoker sets 1, Drake played: 2 active, 6 rested after the turn).
