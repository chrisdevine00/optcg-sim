## Findings

### W57-R1 (3) OP09-101 Kuzan: an [On Play] with a cost may be declined; the engine forces the placement (D-1 settled: rules-digest 112 is right)
- **Card:** `[On Play] Place 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 3 or less at the top or bottom of your opponent's Life cards face-up: Your opponent trashes 1 card from their hand.`
- **Script:** a public `exists` guard, then a mandatory `move` of exactly 1 opposing Character into their Life (the end is asked), then, if it left the field, the opponent trashes 1. No `cost`, because `cards/src/optional-cost.ts` refuses an auto with a cost whose text has no "you may".
- **Ruling:** official general-rules Q&A (`qa_rules.txt`, "Can I play a Character card with an [On Play] effect without activating this [On Play] effect?"): you must activate it whenever possible, "However, if the effect has a cost, you can choose not to pay that cost and play the card without activating the effect." The text before the colon is the cost. The OP09 Q&A for this card agrees it is a cost ("Can I still activate this [On Play] effect even if it is not possible to place 1 of my opponent's Characters ...? No"). CR 8-1-2 and 8-3-1 as digested in rules-digest 112 and 120. The engine/DECISIONS reading (a cost without "you may" is mandatory when payable) is not in any of these and contradicts the Q&A.
- **Probe:** temporarily rewrote the script as `cost: [{ move: { from: 'characters', owner: 'opponent', filter: { cost: { lte: 3 } }, pick: { exactly: 1 } }, to: { zone: 'life', position: 'topOrBottom', faceUp: true } }]` with the trash as the only effect step. The engine asks `optional` ("pay move cards to use this effect?"), declining plays Kuzan with nothing placed, and **the `it.fails` "declining the cost" in `w7/OP09-101.test.ts` turns into an unexpected pass**. The other independent tests of the card fail only because their `place` helper does not answer the new `optional`; `cards/test/optional-cost.test.ts` fails the OP09-101 row. Restored.
- **Why it changes play:** the placement gives the opponent a Life card. A player facing a near-lethal race may rightly decline; the engine cannot.
- **Affected:** exactly **1 scripted card**. Scan of every scripted unit: an auto timing, a colon cost clause, no "you may", not DON!!-only, gives OP09-101 only (OP17-049 and OP17-112 are "choose one" false positives; ST29-004, OP14-036 and the other "You may ... :" cards carry their `cost`). The unscripted OP-01 to OP-08 cards with a bare "①:" cost (about 25, for example OP04-025, OP05-029, OP07-019) have the same shape when they are scripted, so the checker rule is the real defect.
- **Fix:** script Kuzan with the cost above and the trash as the effect; change `optionalCostProblems` so that an auto whose text has a colon cost clause is accepted without "you may" (keep rejecting a script with a cost where the text has no clause, and a "you may ..." clause with no cost); update the `place` helper of the independent test to accept the `optional`, turn D-1 into a plain test, add a decline test and a "protected Character: not payable" test. The top-or-bottom question asked before a protected removal (the lead's question) disappears with the cost form (the cost is not payable); as built it leaks nothing (protection is a public board fact), it is only a pointless prompt.

### W57-R2 (3) OP09-018 Get Out of Here!: negative power counts as 0 in the total (G55, M6b-Q71), against the official answer
- **Card:** `[Main] K.O. up to 2 of your opponent's Characters with a total power of 4000 or less.`
- **Ruling:** official Q&A (`qa_op09.txt`): "Can I use this [Main] effect to K.O. 1 Character with −2000 power and 1 Character with 6000 power? Yes, you can." (−2000 + 6000 = 4000.) Rules-digest 17: power can be negative.
- **Script / engine:** `engine/src/effects/run.ts` `selectCards` builds `limits.costs` with `Math.max(0, power)` ("never below 0"); `g55-total-power.test.ts` pins "power below 0 counts as 0, so a card cannot pay for another", and Q71 records it. The Q&A answer is the opposite.
- **Probe:** opposing Characters of 6000 and (3000 lowered by 5000 to) −2000, planted `ko { upTo: 2, totalPower: { lte: 4000 } }`: only the −2000 Character is offered first, and after choosing it the 6000 Character is not offered (0 picks). Removing the floor alone is not enough: I tried it and the pick is still refused, because `checkPick` in `actions.ts` rejects a pick whose running total exceeds the bound and picks must follow candidate order, so a 6000 card listed before the −2000 card can never be reached.
- **Fix:** drop the floor in `selectCards` and make the running-total test in `checkPick` feasibility-aware: reject only if the total plus the most negative contributions still available from later candidates (up to `max - picked - 1` of them) exceeds the bound; do not prune a candidate "over the bound on its own" at list time while a later candidate has negative power. Replace the pinned "counts as 0" test with the Q&A case in both orders of candidate listing.

### W57-R3 (3) OP13-002 Ace with OP14-053 Vista: the K.O.'d card is read from the trash with printed values, not its last known base power (G43 / `koed`, official answer)
- **Card (Ace):** `[DON!! x1] [Once Per Turn] When you take damage or your Character with 6000 base power or more is K.O.'d, draw 1 card.` Script: `koed` with filter `basePower >= 6000`.
- **Ruling:** official Q&A (`qa_op14_eb04.txt`, OP14-053): Leader OP13-002, Vista K.O.'d on the opponent's turn with 7 or less cards in hand, "since this card was K.O.'d when its base power had become 6000? Yes, you can." Also EB03-004 Q&A: a base power set by an effect is the base power.
- **Engine:** `runKo` in `flow.ts` fires the `koed` watchers with `matches(..., nid, w.filter)` on the card as it now is in the trash; its comment says "no meta card reads that". Ace and Vista (both in this batch) now do.
- **Probe:** p1 Leader OP13-002 with 1 DON!! given, Vista in play, p2's turn (Vista's power reads 6000), Vista K.O.'d: **no draw** (hand stays empty). Control with a printed-6000 Character: draws.
- **Fix:** keep the last-known snapshot `leaveField` already builds (`lki`: power, basePower, cost, types) and evaluate `koed` filters (and any filter on the K.O.'d card) against it. The author tests "buffed 5000 does not count, debuffed 6000 does" still hold, since both read the layered base power. Add the Vista case.

### W57-R4 (3) OP12-081 Koala: a Character played by its own [Trigger] counts as "using a Character's effect" (official answer: it does not)
- **Card:** `... or when your opponent plays a Character using a Character's effect.`
- **Ruling:** official Q&A (`qa_op12.txt`, OP12-081): "Can I activate this [Once Per Turn] effect when my opponent plays a Character using a Character card's [Trigger] effect? No. Playing a Character using a [Trigger] effect is not considered playing it using a Character effect."
- **Engine:** `viaOf` in `effects/run.ts` returns `characterEffect` whenever the effect's source card is a Character, so "[Trigger] Play this card." on a Character card qualifies.
- **Probe:** p1 Leader OP12-081 attacks; p2's top Life card is OP13-106 Conney (base cost 1, "[Trigger] Play this card"); p2 activates it. Result: Conney is played and **Koala fires**: p2 ends at 2 Life with a Life card in hand (4 Life, 1 taken by damage, 1 more moved to hand).
- **Fix:** `viaOf` returns `effect` when the frame's ability is a `trigger`. Add a test with a Character [Trigger] play (no fire) and with an [On Play] play (fire). Dozens of Characters print "[Trigger] Play this card.", so the error is common against this Leader.

### W57-R5 (3) "This effect can be activated when ..." autos are mandatory (OP12-081, OP11-102): "can" makes them optional
- **Cards:** OP12-081 Koala ("[Once Per Turn] This effect can be activated when your opponent plays ..."), OP11-102 Camie ("[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] This effect can be activated when your opponent activates an Event or [Trigger]. ... trash 1 card from the top of each of your and your opponent's Life cards").
- **Ruling:** rules-digest 112 (an effect with "can" or "may" is optional) and the official OP12-081 answer: "You can choose not to activate the [Once Per Turn] effect of [Koala] when Sanji is played, and then choose to activate it in response to [Charlotte Linlin] being played."
- **Scripts:** both are `auto` with no `optional` and no cost, so they fire at the first qualifying event with no question and spend the Once Per Turn. Probe (Koala, above): fires with no prompt. The independent and author tests never assert a decision.
- **Why it changes play:** Camie trashes the controller's own Life card too; Koala's use can be saved for a better moment. 9 other scripted cards share the wording and are outside this batch (OP09-001, OP10-042, OP11-040, OP11-041, OP11-043, OP13-100, OP16-041, OP16-048, PRB02-009): check each.
- **Fix:** `optional: true` on `watch` (OP11-102) and `opp-play-life` (OP12-081) (the engine already asks `optional` autos, `run.ts` `optionalAuto`), decide and test that a declined use does not spend the Once Per Turn, and teach `optional-cost.ts` that "can be activated" means optional. The Q&A also puts Koala's resolution after Linlin's [On Play]; check the order with a test.

### W57-R6 (2) G46 (Q68): a K.O. source that left the field before the K.O. is not recognised, but an [On K.O.] source is

### W57-R7 (2) The nightly leak property goes red on these cards (test model, not a leak)
- `leaks.test.ts` "a handle never links a card across a zone hidden from the viewer" failed at 182 runs (seed 1823274767, biased decks): p1's OP11-073 reveals the top card of p2's deck (G44, handle `t14crs`), 14 steps later OP12-086 looks at p2's top 3 and trashes them, showing the same card with the same handle. The viewer saw that card on top, so nothing new is learned; the tracker exempts a hand reveal and a Life reveal in place (`revealedInHand`, `revealedInLife`) but not a deck reveal in place. Without the fix the nightly standard-deck run will fail now that OP11-066 to OP11-081 are scripted. **Fix:** that one line plus a unit test in `link-tracker.test.ts`.

### W57-R8 (2) `cardsVersionOf` leaves `baseEffect` and `printed` out: a text correction changes play with no version change
- Wave 6 reads the verified text of every card (scripted or not) for `hasBaseEffect` and `hasTiming` (EB02-022, EB03-001/003/007/009/039, PRB01-001), and leaves those two fields out of `cardsVersionOf` "so no record's version moves". An unscripted card has `scripts-` hash input `null`. A later override that edits the text of such a card (routine: `textFixes`) changes which hand card Usopp may play while engine, cards and scripts versions all stay equal, so an old record replays differently and is not detected. By code reading (`record.ts`, `defs.ts`); no probe run. **Fix:** include `baseEffect` and `printed` in the hash only for games whose scripts use `hasBaseEffect` or `hasTiming` (all other games keep their hash), or bump the card-data version once.
