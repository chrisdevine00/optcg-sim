## 2026-11-06 — L fix round 2: a search-free refutation, draws in the attackers bound, honest Trigger wording

- **The refutation bound (`ai/src/search/lethal/bound.ts`, `refutedByBound`).** Reduce the defender to its hidden hand only, every card a plain +P Counter (a mode the placeholder always has, at no cost); ignore Blockers, board effects and the cards each hit gives it. That only weakens it, so a lethal that fails against it fails against the real defender. With `R` attacks landing out of `m` possible and `L` Life, `need = L+1` (`ceil(L/2)+1` with Double Attack) attacks must land, so `k = m - need + 1` must be stopped. The reduced defender is an online strategy: it stops an attack whenever stopping costs at most `t = floor(H/k)` cards, so `k` stops fit in `H` cards. What is left is `need` attackers each facing at least `Leader + P*t`. The attackers' power comes from `attackerPotential`, an over-estimate read from the typed scripts of every card they can reach (never text): attackers and their power, DON!! (with `addDon`, `giveDon`), a permanent per-attack gain (`gain`), and the power the used abilities add in all over the turn (`gainTotal`, shared out over attackers: sum of max(0, a_i - g_i) >= max(0, sum a_i - sum g_i)). If the shortfall in DON!! exceeds the DON!! there is, no lethal. Any op, form or `kind` the scan does not classify returns `null` and the search runs.

## 2026-11-05 — L fix round (AI half): sound hand costs, a cheap proof path, a conservative attackers bound

Context: the L review (`docs/reviews/l-review.md`) failed the milestone on L-01 and L-02 (false claims) and L-03 (the detector rarely answers). This entry records the AI-side fixes.

- **L-01, the attackers bound is decided from keywords and scripts, never text.** `attackersPossible` counted a hand Character only if its text had "[Rush", so EB04-030 ("gains Rush", printed without brackets) was missed and a lethal turn read "No lethal, any hand". It now counts legal attackers, hand Characters with the printed Rush keyword, and for every card the seat can reach (hand, Leader, Characters, Stage) the most attacks its script could add: cards it untaps (`setActive`), plays (`play`) or gives Rush (`grant`), each as many as its pick allows (a `forEach` counts 5); Double Attack comes from keywords or a granting script. So the bound can only err toward searching. The earlier A7 sentence ("errs only toward searching … a rare lethal could show as refuted") is replaced by this: it errs only toward searching. Planted test: Kaido Leader, EB04-030 in hand, 10 DON!!, Rocks at 1 Life with nothing: proven (`public` any-hand, `actual`), the line wins when played, and `reviewMissedLethal` flags the turn.
- **L-02, the placeholder pays whatever a real prior card could pay.** The hidden hand is now a placeholder Event carrying the union of every type and colour in the defender's prior, so "trash 1 card with a type including Rocks Pirates" (Shiki) can be paid and Shiki's −3000 fires. Costs and filters the model cannot meet with one card (a name, a category, a Counter value, distinct names, a total cost in a `from: hand` selection on a card of the defender's public board) are model gaps. A prior card with no data, or a prior Event with no script, is a gap too. A gap drops a proof from `any-hand` to `needed-hidden-info` (chip "Depends on their hand"); review then calls it hindsight, not severe. Derived from the prior's scripts, not by hand (`profile.ts`, `handCostGaps`).
- **L-03, DON!! costs are modelled, and proofs are found by a cheap path first.** The defender's DON!! is public, so a Counter that costs DON!! −N is a mode guarded by "N or more DON!! on your field" that returns N DON!! (the engine checks and spends the real DON!!). Plain +power modes are collapsed to the biggest one. A power boost aimed at a card not in the battle is pruned (one battle only). The seat's own plays and activations are searched only for cards that matter (Rush, a permanent or replacement ability, or an op other than drawing, looking at the deck, milling or revealing: its own deck draws blanks, A3). The search then tries, in order: (1) a plan in which **every attack lands** against every answer, found per order of attackers with the fewest DON!! for each attack (more DON!! never hurts an attack, so the first that lands is the least), choosing the plan with the fewest DON!! in all and then the smallest for the earliest attack (the same line the tidy order gave, E20's 2 / 5 / 0, in thousands of nodes); (2) the adaptive AND-OR search, which decides; (3) for an adaptive proof, a fixed plan with a bait attack within what is left of the cap.
- **A soundness harness.** `ai/test/lethal-sound.test.ts` samples positions (Rocks with Shiki, Newgate or nothing on its board, and Kaido; 1 to 2 Life, 0 to 3 real cards from the sourced lists, 0 to 10 DON!!), takes the `public` proof, and replays it against **every** defender answer with the real cards of the prior in hand, Life and deck, on the position's own hand and on re-rolled hands. A broken proof fails the test. Result: 0 of 180 checks broken with the corrected model (120 positions); 22 of 168 broken with the superseded model (`model: 'power-only'`, which has no types and no rest or draw), so the harness has teeth. `lethal-games.test.ts` now replays `actual` proofs against every answer, replays oblivious `public` proofs against the real hand, and fails if fewer than 30% of the positions it searches are decided.
- **L-05.** A turn where the seat played the proof's attacks (same attackers, same order, each at least as strong as the line made it, or the next needed attacker could no longer attack) and still did not win is not flagged: something outside the model (a Life Trigger, a card not in the lists) stopped it. Planted record test in both directions.
- **L-04, L-08.** The polite text carries the Trigger caveat; copy says "their usual lists run …" (the prior is not their deck). `eventCounterOf` reads `when` as a list too (`hasCounterTiming`). Additive exports: `FALLBACK_COUNTER`, `defenceBound(priorCardIds, cards, defenderDon, fallback)` (the most one hidden card adds, given the DON!! on their field, and whether their Counters can rest or draw) and `counterProfile`. `facts.maxPerCard` is now DON!!-aware, so an Unknown can still say "hits needed, attackers, the public defence bound".
- Alternatives rejected: dropping DON!! costs only for modes with a trash cost (the trash cost is dropped and errs toward the defender, but DON!! is public and was inflating the space); a per-hand-composition search; trusting the text of a card for any rules fact.

- **Measured the reviewer's way** (`.cache/l-review/rate.test.ts`: Rookie-versus-Rookie, all four Rocks and Kaido pairings, `public` mode at the 1.5 s app cap, in Node on the same machine; this run gave 829 Main-phase decisions). E20: proven, any-hand, in about 120 ms and 3,066 nodes (it was unknown at 3 s; E01 proven in about 20 ms). Unknown rate: **353 of the 580 positions that need a search (61%; reviewer: 86%)**, which is 43% of all decisions because 249 are answered at once by the attackers bound. Against a Rocks defender 78 of 235 searched are unknown (33%; reviewer 65%); against Kaido 275 of 345 (80%; reviewer 99.5%). The target (under about 30%) is **not reached**, and mostly for Kaido: with DON!! on their field its +4000 cards, rest and draw modes multiply the defender's answers, and the sound model cannot drop them. The levers left: a fast upper bound on total defence that proves or refutes without search (it needs a complete, sound list of what a defender's board can add, which the scripts do not yet give), and a cap on each attack's DON!! at the point where it is unstoppable. What the UI can show on Unknown without a search: `facts.hitsNeeded`, `facts.attackers`, and `defenceBound(...)` (the most one hidden card adds given their DON!!, and whether it can rest or draw). Differential: 276 re-rolls of the opponent's hidden cards, no difference.

## 2026-11-05 — L: what `public` lethal assumes, and how the search is built

Context: ARCHITECTURE §4.4 says the `public` defender's hand is "bounded by public information" and a hidden Life [Trigger] is a chance node; nothing says how to drive the real engine with cards nobody can name.

- **Method.** The search runs on a real `GameState` built with `determinize` from the requesting seat's `SearchView` alone (it cannot read a game: `publicState(view, cards)`), so Blockers, DON!! allocation, on-attack effects (Kaido's −2000, Newgate's +3000), Counters, Triggers and "each hit gives the defender a card" (CR 7-1-4-1-1-2) all come from `reduce`, `legalActions` and `queries`. Hidden defender cards become a **placeholder Character** that costs nothing, has no effect and carries the biggest Counter in the defender's archetype prior (a Character's printed Counter or a [Counter] Event's +power: `biggestCounter`); every other hidden card is a blank. `public` therefore assumes: **(A1)** each hidden defender card is worth at most that one number in defence and nothing else. A Counter Event's DON!! cost and any other effect on it (a K.O., a draw) are ignored, so a proof is at least as strong as one against the real deck, and a refutation may be pessimistic about a real hand. **(A2)** Hidden Life cards are never [Trigger]s inside the search; the chance is a label (`triggerRisk`: C(50−T, k) / C(50, k) averaged over the prior's lists by recency weight, with k the face-down Life cards, all of which a winning line takes). A refutation ignores it (a Trigger can only help the defender). **(A3)** The seat's own deck draws blanks, so an effect that draws is searched but finds nothing. **(A4)** With no sourced list for their Leader the biggest Counter is assumed to be +4000 and the facts say so (`noPrior`). The leak property: `publicState` takes the view and public card data and nothing else, a differential test changes every hidden card of both sides and demands an identical answer (up to handle names), and no hidden card id may appear in the answer.
- **Search.** AND-OR over the engine: the seat's decisions are OR nodes, the defender's every legal answer an AND node, with a transposition table on `summaryHash`, interchangeable cards (same id and state) as one choice, moves largest swing first, and node caps (tests) or wall-clock caps (the app). The Main Phase moves are **macros**, "give k DON!! to X, then attack T with X": **(A5)** DON!! is never given to a card that does not attack next, so a DON!! condition on a Character that helps another card is not searched; `playCard` (only cards with a script or [Rush]: a card the engine knows nothing about does nothing but spend DON!!) and `activate` stay single steps; attacks on Characters are searched only for a Blocker or a scripted Character (**A6**). A refutation is exhaustive under A5 and A6; a cap is `unknown`, never "no lethal".
- **The line.** First a **fixed plan** that wins against every defender answer (a person can follow it without adapting): the search carries the set of states the defender's answers lead to, over simple moves (Leader attacks, "may" effects declined), tightening the DON!! budget until no plan is left and then taking the first plan in order of fewest DON!!, each attack's DON!! from none upward and smaller attacks first. That is why E20's split is 2 / 5 / 0 and E01's is "all 4 on the Leader". If no fixed plan exists the adaptive AND-OR search decides, and a proof there is labelled `oblivious: false` (the line depends on their answers). Alternatives rejected: the fully adaptive search alone (its first proof gave the Leader 2 DON!! after the defender had spent its cards, which is true but cannot be followed or checked as "split 2 / 5 / 0"); a model of the defender's hand as a number (it would restate the rules the engine already knows, and miss Blockers, Newgate and Kaido).
- **A7.** `attackersPossible` (a bound on attackers against Life, counting [Rush] Characters in hand and [Double Attack] anywhere) answers "no lethal, too few attackers" at once and lets review skip hopeless turns. It does not count effects that untap a Character or add an attack, so such a card could make a rare lethal show as refuted; the bound errs only toward searching otherwise.
- **Cost.** About 30 µs a node (the hash and `reduce`). E20 (a proof with 10 DON!!, 3 attackers, a Newgate) takes about 78,000 nodes, 2.3 s, for the plan search; positions with a full hand of scripted playable cards usually hit the cap (`unknown`) at 8,000 to 25,000 nodes. - **Engine.** No engine change. Additive only on the AI package: `lethal`, `explainLethal`, `tier1`, `tier2`, `guidedCount`, `checkGuidedCount`, `reviewMissedLethal`, `answerLethal` and the request type.

## 2026-10-29 — M4a effects slice: runtime and API decisions

Context: M3 review 1 found four severity-4 leaks in hotseat (M3-01 to M3-04). Behind the handoff curtain and during a veiled defence, the board, log, live regions, staged-Counter breakdown and rewind list all rendered from the private view of the incoming or defending seat.

Decision: the app session builds a **public view** from the two player views, keeping only cards both seats can see, with no actions and generic decision wording. It also builds a **public log**, keeping only event fields both projections share. The hotseat screen renders the public view unless a seat's reveal is active (holding R, or press-and-hold on touch). The hotseat log and live regions are always public, and they are cleared when a reveal ends. Everything behind the curtain is `inert`. Rewind is off while a seat is veiled, and a hotseat rewind needs the other player's agreement. The cost: a player's own draws no longer appear by name in the hotseat log.

Rejected:
- masking each widget separately, which is how the leaks happened;
- adding a public projection to the engine, which would change the frozen API for something the app can derive from two views.

Also decided in this round: with a Blocker chosen, Enter blocks without playing Counters that would be wasted, and "Play anyway" is a separate button (M3-05). Carried: 44 px lane chips on tablets (M3-18), because they would use up the cross-platform height margin; the chips pass WCAG 2.5.8 through the spacing exception.

## 2026-10-28 — M2 engine implementation decisions

Context: the M2 engine author made these choices while implementing the frozen API.
1. **View handles.** A view handle is a hash of the viewer, the instance id and a hidden per-card key. The key is re-rolled when the card enters a hidden zone and on every shuffle. Instance ids stay stable except under the new-object rule (CR 3-1-6). Rejected: re-keying the instance ids themselves (breaks ARCHITECTURE §2.2 and the tests).
2. **Concession.** `legalActions` lists only the decider's actions, their own concede included. `reduce` accepts a concede from either player (CR 1-2-3).
3. **Unscripted Events cannot be played** (E-D8), rather than playing as a no-op.
4. **Deck-out** is checked when an operation draws from or touches an empty deck (CR 9-2-1-2 via rule processing), so scenarios may start with empty decks. Rejected: an immediate state check, which ends every minimal scenario.
5. **Choices** complete automatically at `max` picks; `done` is only for stopping early.
6. **State internals.**
   - DON!! are counts by location class, not instances.
   - Card definitions are copied into state, because `reduce` takes no lookup.
   - Instance ids use a separate RNG stream.
   - `summaryHash` hashes board positions, so internal refactors keep golden hashes.
7. **Additions to the API** (no frozen shape changed): `autoSkip`, `projectEvents`, `actionFromView`, `ProjectedView`, `startRecord`/`appendAction`, `ENGINE_VERSION`, `validateDeck`, plus the root scripts `test:nightly` and `bench`.
8. **Ordering and preview.** When the turn player has two or more replacement effects, they resolve in canonical order until M4 adds the `order` decision. `preview` is keyed by instance ids; M3 adds a handle-keyed preview for the app as an addition.

Alternatives rejected are noted per item.

## 2026-10-28 — Frozen engine API amended after the independent test author's report

Context: the independent test author (branch `m2-independent-tests`: 132 tests and 183 todos, written without seeing engine source) found three gaps in the API frozen earlier today. First, `Omit<Action, …>` does not distribute over the `Action` union, so the fixtures' documented `g.act({ type: 'attack', … })` did not typecheck. Second, `PlayerView` exposed only the decision and actions, so visibility rules (CR 3-1-4 public zone counts, CR 3-3-2 DON!! deck) could not be tested. Third, event names were not frozen. It also found a data gap: OP05-022, a Leader with printed [Blocker], had no keywords, because only meta cards had verified overrides.

Decision:

- **Events.** Export a frozen `GameEventType` union. A trash that is not a K.O. never emits `KO`.
