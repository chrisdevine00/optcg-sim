# OP-15 group A: observation and setup gaps

- **G1 (OP15-004 Sea Cat, OP15-013 Pincers): no way to put a Leader at 0 power or less in the owner's own Main Phase.** The scenario spec has no power modifiers, no Leader with 0 printed power exists, and no scripted card on file gives your own Leader a negative modifier you can use on your own turn (the old Alabasta cards OP04-002, OP04-006, OP07-006, EB03-006, EB04-023 are not scripted yet and act as vanilla). Sea Cat's "condition true" branch (give -3000) is therefore untested; only the false side (5000 and 4000 Leaders) is pinned. Pincers is reached on the opponent's turn only, through OP15-009 Koby's replacement (-2000 each) on an OP13-004 Sabo Leader. Once the Alabasta Leader-minus cards are scripted, add Sea Cat tests with Leader at 0 and below 0 and a play-from-hand test for Pincers at cost 2.
- **G2 (OP15-034 Yorki): the "[Your Turn]" gate cannot be probed.** No scripted card plays a Character from hand during the opponent's turn, so Yorki's [On Play] on the opponent's turn (which must do nothing) is untested.
- **G3 (OP15-024 Usopp, OP15-029 Kuma): "cannot be rested" by an opposing Leader effect is untested.** No scripted Leader rests opposing Characters; only a Character effect (EB05-012 Camie) and an Event (OP17-038) were available.
- **G4 (OP15-024 Usopp [On K.O.], "Leader or Character with a cost of 7 or less"):** whether the opposing Leader (which has no cost) is a legal target of the cost filter is a rules question (not in rulings.md); the tests only assert on Characters and never on the Leader.
