# ST-26 / ST-27 independent card tests: API gaps and observations

- **Colon-less "If" after a cost colon (ST26-005, ST27-001, ST27-002).** Asserted per the current convention (effect condition): the cost is paid and the Once Per Turn is spent even when the "If" fails. For ST27-001 the Once Per Turn assertion is confounded, because the only [Fullalead] (a Stage) is rested by the same cost, so "not offered again" has two causes; the next-turn test shows the ability returns.
- **Activation costs that rest or trash a card are not asked.** "You may rest 1 of your [Fullalead] cards" and "You may trash this Character" resolve at activation without an `optional`; the "you may" is the choice to activate. Tests therefore do not expect an `optional` after `activate` for ST27-001, ST27-002 and ST27-005.
- **Empty "up to" picks on the field or trash are tolerated either way** (`skipEmpty`): the engine currently asks an empty `choose` with `done`; no ruling on file says whether it must. No hidden card is involved in these.
- **Cost of a Character is read through `queries.card(...).cost`** (ST26-001 in hand, ST27-002 and ST27-004 on the field). There is no separate view of the cost to play. ST27-004 asserts that the static +1 cost per 4 trash cards is on the field only (play cost 4 with 8 cards in trash): the card text has no "in your hand" clause, which is a reading of the text, not a ruling on file.
- **"DON!! on their field" for ST26-005** is counted as cost area (active and rested) plus DON!! given to cards, via `queries.don`. Given DON!! counting is asserted (2 active + 2 rested + 1 given = 5).
- **Rest "DON!! cards or Characters" (ST26-002)** is observed as a `choose` offering Characters and DON!! class picks (`{from: 'costActive'}`); the opposing DON!! is offered only when active. The test for "only rested DON!! is offered no active class" asserts the absence of an active class and no state change, and accepts either no prompt or an empty one.
