# OP-13 group D: observation and setup gaps

1. **OP13-097 (open question, not tested).** "If the only Characters on your field are {Celestial Dragons} type Characters" with no Character at all on my field: OPEN-QUESTIONS M6b-Q21 / E-Q4 (the precedent says false, the auditor proposed true). No test asserts either reading; a position with only the opposing Characters is not probed.
2. **OP13-099 "black" filter.** Every {Five Elders} Character card in the pool is black, so "black {Five Elders} type Character" cannot be told apart from "{Five Elders} type Character" by any position. The colour half of the filter is untested.
3. **Base cost versus current cost of an opposing Character (OP13-091, -095, -097, -117, -102).** The scenario has no cost modifier, and the only scripted card that raises an opposing Character's cost on my turn is the opponent's own OP13-120 Sabo ("until the end of your opponent's next turn"). The tests use it (`afterOpponentSabo` in `kit-op13d.ts`: Sabo is used on p2's turn, then p1's turn starts), which makes the tests depend on OP13-120 working. ST14-001 (a static +1 cost Leader) is not scripted.
4. **Unscripted [Trigger] scenery.** OP01-104 Speed, OP04-111 Hera, OP04-113 Rabiyan, OP06-105 Genbo and OP06-108 Tenguyama Hitetsu are stats-only, so a [Trigger] on them cannot be activated from the Life area (the damage decision offers no activate). Tests that need a live [Trigger] use OP17-107 Daifuku (cost 3), OP13-109 (cost 5) and OP14-105 Gorgon Sisters (cost 6); the unscripted ones are used only for "has a [Trigger]" from card data (OP13-100, OP13-110).
5. **OP13-100 [Your Turn] gate.** Probed only by a Character with a [Trigger] played from my own Life on the opponent's turn. No scripted card of the opponent plays or makes me play a Character in another way, and the once-per-turn behaviour after choosing "0 DON!!" (is the use spent?) is not asserted.
6. **Multi-pick lists (OP13-093, OP13-095).** An "exact N" or "up to N" pick lists only the picks that start a canonical selection (instance order), not every candidate, so "which cards are offered" is read through `reachablePickSets` and `pickSet`, never from the first listing.
7. **OP13-104 "add up to 1 card from the top of your deck".** The engine asks it as a yes/no `optional` (the card is hidden), not as a pick; "up to 1" is read as accept or decline.
8. **OP13-114 [Trigger] with an empty hand.** Either the activation is not offered (the hand size is public) or it is offered and the cost is unusable; the test accepts both.
9. **OP13-102 / OP13-114 / OP13-099 / OP13-120 activations.** Choosing to activate an [Activate: Main] pays its "you may" cost at once (no separate yes/no); declining is not activating. The tests assert the state after activation, not a decline of an already paid cost.
