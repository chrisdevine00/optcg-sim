# OP-09 group A: observation and setup gaps

- **G1 (scenery must be scripted): only scripted cards have effects in the engine, and only cards with a verified override carry `keywords`.** Older sets (OP-01 to OP-08, ST-01 to ST-10) are mostly unscripted, so a Blocker, "cannot be K.O.'d" card or a leader with a static modifier from them is inert. Scenery used: OP09-091 Vasco Shot, OP09-007 Heat, OP09-015 Lucky.Roux, OP12-106 Trafalgar Law (Blockers with keyword data), ST30-001 Luffy & Ace (a Leader at 4000 for OP09-007), OP04-083 Sabo (effect-K.O. protection), OP09-077 Gum-Gum Lightning (opposing effect K.O.), OP10-029 Dracule Mihawk (sets a rested {ODYSSEY} Character active). No other scripted card with an unconditional "cannot be K.O.'d by effects" was found.
- **G2 (OP09-022 Lim, OP09-023 Adio): the {ODYSSEY} Leader's "Your Character cards are played rested" was not scripted when these tests were written** (no OP09-022 script). The Adio tests use Lim only for the {ODYSSEY} type and do not assert whether Adio or any other played Character enters active or rested. All other tests use a Leader without the type.
- **G3 (OP09-012 Monster): only a K.O. by an opposing effect (OP09-077) is exercised.** The text covers a K.O. by any effect, including my own; no scripted card in reach K.O.s my own Character from my side, so that half is untested.
- **G4 (OP09-001 Shanks): whether declining the "up to 1" target (choosing nobody) spends the Once Per Turn is not asserted.** The text does not say, and an "up to 1" resolved with 0 targets cannot be told apart by any later observation without a second attack that the test could compare; the tests pin only that a second attack after a real use asks nothing.
- **G5 (OP09-023 Adio): "set up to 3 DON!! as active" with fewer than 3 rested DON!! cannot be set up.** Playing Adio (cost 9) always leaves at least 9 rested DON!! in the cost area, so the "fewer rested than the maximum" boundary is unreachable; only the 0..3 count choice is tested.
- **G6 (exact-N picks): `offeredIds` of an exact-2 `choose` lists only the cards that can start a canonical selection** (the highest-id card is not offered as a first pick). OP09-024 therefore asserts every pair of the three cards in hand is reachable (`reachablePickSets`) instead of listing the offers.
- **G7 (forced cost picks): a cost with exactly one candidate (Trafalgar Law as my only Character, "return 1 of your Characters") is paid by the engine without a pick.** The OP09-030 test accepts either (pick asked, or paid on its own) and asserts the outcome.
