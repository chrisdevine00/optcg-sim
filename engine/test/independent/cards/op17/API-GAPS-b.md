# API gaps: OP-17 independent tests, group B

None of these stopped a test from being written. Each says what the public API leaves open and how the tests cope.

1. **Exact-N picks list only the canonical-first candidates.** For "trash 2 cards from your hand" (OP17-043, 068, 078), "rest 4 of your cards" (OP17-038) and the like, the `choose` decision lists only the picks that can start a canonical selection, so a later card appears only after the earlier ones are picked, and picking in another order dead-ends. `kit-op17b.ts` picks incrementally (`payHand`, `pickCards`, `pickInOrder`: always the first listed pick that is wanted). Wanted: a way to read the whole candidate set, or an order-free pick.
2. **A "you may" cost on [Activate: Main] and on [Trigger] has no separate "use?" question.** Activating (OP17-053 Barbell, OP17-101 Caribou) or choosing `trigger` with `activate: true` is choosing to pay; the cost `choose` follows at once. Declining is not activating. With an unpayable cost (empty hand) the Trigger action `activate: true` is simply not legal (OP17-101). Auto effects ([On Play], [When Attacking], [On Your Opponent's Attack]) do ask `optional`, and when the cost cannot be paid they ask it with `accept` unavailable (kit `unusable`).
3. **A "may" that belongs to the opponent is a `mode` choose.** OP17-117 [Trigger] "Your opponent may trash 3 cards from their hand. If they do not, K.O. ...": p2 gets a `choose` (purpose `mode`) with options "Trash 3 cards from your hand" / "Do not trash ...", not an `optional`. With fewer than 3 cards the trash option is not selectable. Tests assert the outcome through the option ids "0" and "1".
4. **The controller's blind pick from the opponent's hand is not observable.** OP17-099 mode 2 ("Trash 1 card from your opponent's hand") is resolved by the engine without a decision (E-Q5: a random card); tests assert only that the opponent's hand shrinks by one, their trash grows by one, and the card was one they held.
5. **Scenery must be scripted.** Unscripted Events cannot be played and unscripted [Trigger] cards cannot activate, so the tests use scripted scenery: EB01-059 and OP06-058 (opposing K.O. / bottom-deck effects against OP17-043 and OP17-053), OP05-021 and OP05-117 (Stages for OP17-116), OP01-104, OP01-009, OP17-107 and OP07-077 ([Trigger] cards for OP17-105 and OP17-116), OP17-074 (a [Blocker] with a keyword entry). Three tests use other cards of this set as scenery and break with them: OP17-097 (lowers costs, for OP17-036 / 041 / 071), OP17-085 Dorry (a +12 cost Character, for OP17-066 / 067), OP17-066 Orochi (a 0-power Character, for OP17-069 / 103).
6. **Cost modifications can only be made by scripted cards.** Only OP17-097 lowers a cost, so the "current cost vs base cost" checks (OP17-036, 041, 071) depend on it.
7. **The opponent's drawn card pollutes hand checks.** After `endTurn` the opponent's Draw Phase has already run (OP17-047 checks); the tests remove the known filler card (`P-015`) from the hand they compare.
8. **Simultaneous [End of Your Turn] effects ask the turn player for an `order`.** Two OP17-047 Shikis produce an `order` decision for p1 before p2's choices (tests answer it explicitly).
9. **Revealing is observable only through `CardRevealed` events.** The "reveal up to 1" step of OP17-032 / 033 / 037 is checked with `hasEvent`; whether the opponent's projection shows the revealed card is not checked here (projection tests cover it).
10. **[Once Per Turn] on [On Your Opponent's Attack] is exercised across two attacks** (OP17-033, 057, 064 and 072 use a Leader attack then a Character attack), because a Character attacks once per turn and the scenario API cannot untap it.
