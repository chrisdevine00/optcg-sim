# Leftovers group B: observation and setup gaps

- **G1 (ST22-005 Oden, ST22-012 Marco): only K.O. and bounce by an opposing effect are exercised.** The scripted opposing removal that reaches a cost-7 / 8000-power Character is OP10-019 Divine Departure (K.O.) and OP14-048 Shiryu (return to hand). No scripted card places a cost-7 Character at the bottom of the deck or trashes one by an effect (OP01-070 Mihawk and EB01-039 are not scripted), so "removed from the field" by bottom-of-deck or by trash is untested.
- **G2 (ST22-005 Oden, ST22-012 Marco): "removed by your opponent's effect" is not separated from "removed by my own effect".** No scripted own-side removal of Oden or Marco was available as scenery, so the "must not happen" cases cover a battle K.O. and (for Oden) the activation's own return of another Character only.
- **G3 (ST22-012 Marco): one [Once Per Turn] use against two Characters K.O.'d at once is untested** (the readings of the shared use are not settled, M6b-Q6).
- **G4 (ST22-001, ST22-011, ST22-017, ST23-003): the cost prompt's behaviour with a hidden hand is read through the available actions only.** The tests assert what the owner is asked (an `optional`, or for an [Activate: Main] the cost pick itself) and that an unpayable cost is not offered or is offered with accept illegal; they cannot read what the opponent's view would show.
- **G5 (ST22-007 Squard, ST23-005 Yasopp): which DON!! card moved cannot be read.** DON!! are counts (`queries.don`: active, rested, given); the tests assert the counts and the +1000 on the target.
- **G6 (ST22-011 Whitey Bay): "[Your Turn]" cannot be probed from the opponent's turn.** Characters cannot be played on the opponent's turn without an effect that no scripted card provides here, so only own-turn play is tested.
- **G7 (ST22-002 Izo, ST22-003, ST22-006, ST22-012: reveal):** the reveal is read from the `CardRevealed` event; the pick lists for a "reveal N from hand" cost only list the picks that can start a canonical selection, so the tests read the completable pairs with `reachablePickSets` instead of the offered ids.
