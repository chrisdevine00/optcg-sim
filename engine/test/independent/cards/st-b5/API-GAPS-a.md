# ST-block-5 group A independent card tests: API gaps and observations

- **Readings left unasserted (no answer on file).** OP01-120 Shanks (M6b-Q12, open): whether "cannot activate a [Blocker] that has 2000 or less power" reads power when the block is declared or when the effect resolves; only base-power boundaries (2000 and 1000 barred, 3000 allowed) are asserted, never a Blocker whose power changes after the attack. OP04-083 Sabo (M6b-Q18, partly answered): whether a Character that arrives after the [On Play] resolves is covered by "none of your Characters can be K.O.'d by effects until the start of your next turn"; only Characters on the field when it resolves (and Sabo itself) are asserted, and the end of the protection at my next Refresh (RL-10). OP04-083 and EB05-049 (RL-16): whether a protected Character is offered as a K.O. cost and the effect then fizzles, or the cost is simply unavailable; the test accepts either encoding and asserts the same outcome (Character stays, no -3 cost).
- **"Treat this card's name as [Usopp]" (OP03-122 Sogeking) is not tested.** The only scripted cards that read a name through it need a [Usopp] Leader or Character that this test pool cannot place without testing other cards' scripts, and `CardView` has no name field to read the substituted name from.
- **Opposing K.O. and bounce by effect come from borrowed scripted cards.** `KO_SRC` in `kit-stb5a.ts` uses OP16-008 Squard (needs EB02-004 Don Accino on the field to trash, 5 DON!!) and `BOUNCE` uses OP17-056 Rocks Pirates (rest 5 DON!!, cost 6 or less); ST01-015 Gum-Gum Jet Pistol, the obvious "K.O. 6000 or less", is not scripted in this checkout. A fault in either borrowed script would show up in several cards' tests at once.
- **Forced picks are not asked.** An exact-N cost with exactly N candidates (a lone 6000-power card in hand for ST30-006 or ST30-008) may be resolved by the engine; `payHandIfAsked` in `kit-stb5a.ts` answers the cost prompt only when it is pending. Resting 5 DON!! (OP17-056) is also paid without a prompt when only active DON!! can pay.
- **Every Life card taken asks a `trigger` decision**, even without a [Trigger]; tests that count Life after a hit answer it with `takeDamage` or `noDefence`.
- **Event [Counter] boosts are read through `TestGame.power` of the boosted card during the Counter Step** (ST30-015, ST30-016); they are visible only while the battle lasts.
- **A hand-cost reduction is read through `queries.card(...).cost` on the hand instance** (EB04-061), as in earlier groups.
- **Scenario DON!! budget.** `mine` puts 10 active DON!! in the cost area, so a scenario with DON!! given to a card must also lower `costArea` (total 10); tests do this explicitly.
