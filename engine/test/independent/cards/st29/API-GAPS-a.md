# ST-29 and older block-4 cards (group A): observation and setup gaps

- **G1 (OP03-044 Kaya and any exact-N pick of 2 or more): the offered picks are not the candidate set.** The engine lists only picks that can start a canonical (instance-id ordered) selection, so the last-ordered candidate is not offered as a first pick. Tests read the candidates with `reachablePickSets` (every selection the decision can end with) and pick in whatever order the engine accepts.
- **G2 (ST29-013 Rob Lucci): whether the revealed Life card still counts in "your Life cards" while its [Trigger] resolves is not documented.** The tests assert only what both readings share (limits of N or N+1). Observed, for the record: the engine counts the card as already gone (p1 with only the Lucci Life card and the opponent with 0 Life has a limit of 0).
- **G3 (ST06-008 Hina): the exact value of a cost pushed below 0 is not asserted.** CR 1-3-6-2 treats a negative cost as 0 outside calculations; the test asserts `max(0, cost) = 0` and tests the lowered cost through another card's cost comparison (ST29-002 Usopp).
- **G4 (ST29-008 Nami, ST29-007 Chopper): "turned face-up" and "placed on top of Life" are read through `CardView.faceUp` and the Life zone order of the owner's view.** The opponent's view of face-down Life cards cannot be used for this, so the tests read the owner's full-state queries.
- **G5 (ST29-008 Nami): a simultaneous K.O. of two {Egghead} Characters by one opponent effect is untested.** No scripted card in the test pool K.O.s two Characters at once and QA-OP17 for OP17-023 (same shape) is the only guide; the single-K.O. cases are covered.
- **G6 (OP04-096 Corrida Coliseum): the Stage leaving the field is tested by replacement with another Stage only.** No scripted opposing Stage-removal card was used as scenery.
