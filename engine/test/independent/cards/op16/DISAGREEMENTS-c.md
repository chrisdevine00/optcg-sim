# Disagreements: OP-16 independent tests, group C

No disagreements found: all tests for the 31 cards in this group pass against the engine, so there is no `it.fails` here.

Readings that were checked against the engine and held (each is a test, listed because the printed text or the rules leave room):

- OP16-101 Mahoroba: the Event is trashed before its effect resolves (CR 2-7-3), so "10 or more cards in your trash" counts Mahoroba itself; 9 cards before the play K.O.s, 8 does not.
- OP16-105 Moria / OP16-111 Boa [Trigger] "1 or less" / "2 or less Life": the Life card taken is already out of the count (RD 101-102), so Moria with 1 more Life card plays, with 2 more does not.
- OP16-116 Zehahahahaha!: with 9 DON!! the "Then" Life step does not resolve (RD 121-122); with 10 DON!! and no [Marshall.D.Teach] to play the "Then" still resolves.
- OP16-118 Ace: "up to 1 [Monkey.D.Luffy] or up to 1 card with a type including ..." is one card in total (M6b-Q26 has no ruling on file; this is the reading tested).
- OP16-098 Yamato: "You may trash this Character" can be paid with no black cost-8 [Yamato] in the trash (the cost does not depend on the effect resolving).
